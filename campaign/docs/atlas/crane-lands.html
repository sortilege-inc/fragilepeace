Crane Lands

The Crane Lands are characterized by rolling hills and picturesque rice paddies, known for their lush landscapes and elegant architecture. The temperate climate supports a rich agricultural environment. The region is inhabited by the Crane Clan, recognized for their artistry, diplomacy, and adherence to the Way of the Crane. The capital, the City Of The Rich Frog, is a hub of commerce and culture, featuring teahouses, curio shops, and the notable Tongbo Temple. The Crane Lands have a history marked by political intrigue and artistic achievement, with clan members actively involved in governance and social affairs.

In the City Of The Rich Frog, several Crane samurai, including Doji Setsuna, Shiba Midori, Bayushi Monban, and Kakita Kazumi, were approached by Daidoji Shin, a Crane agent investigating rogue shugenja and social threats. He warned them of potential dangers in the Dragon District and mentioned the governor, Miya Tetsuya, noting that formal appointments were necessary for a meeting. As dusk fell, the group took the last ferry to the Unicorn side, where they found a small, darkened Curio Shop filled with strange antiques and managed by an elderly proprietor. Inside, they discovered various Gaijin artifacts and Rokugani curios, but The Shopkeeper's demeanor changed when spiritual matters were discussed. Tensions escalated as they sought information regarding a talisman linked to a spirit, resulting in a hurried exchange of receipts and a cautionary warning about local authorities. The Shopkeeper ultimately provided the necessary documentation, and the group left as the shop was closed.
