Tongbo Temple

Tongbo Temple is a significant structure located in the City Of The Rich Frog, recognized for its traditional architecture and serene environment. This temple serves as a spiritual site, frequented by practitioners of mystical arts, including shugenja. Its purpose is to facilitate worship and spiritual practices, contributing to the cultural and religious fabric of the city. The temple is situated amidst urban life, providing a tranquil escape for visitors.

The leadership of the Emerald Magistrates has expressed intent to visit Tongbo Temple to make formal introductions to the governor, Miya Tetsuya, and to gather information regarding local spiritual disturbances. Their plans indicate a focus on exploring the temple and its offerings as part of their investigation into the mysterious events surrounding the city.
