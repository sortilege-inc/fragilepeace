Virtuous Contemplation

Virtuous Contemplation is a secluded garden within the Castle of the Swift Sword, featuring an artificial waterfall that fills a small pond, creating a tranquil setting for reflection and conversation. It provides samurai a serene space away from the hustle of castle life, surrounded by nature's beauty. The garden is accessible to the castle's inhabitants and serves as a venue for quiet meals and discussions.

In a gathering near dawn, Doji Setsuna, Bayushi Monban, Kakita Kazumi, Shiba Midori, and Kitsuko Ayoko shared cold bento and drinks in Virtuous Contemplation. They discussed matters related to the ongoing dispute over the Snow Plain diamond mines, including logistics, historical accounts, and strategies. The garden facilitated private conversations, notably Midori's observations about Ayoko's potential talents and Setsuna's comments that challenged Ayoko's status.
