Guest Wing

The Guest Wing is a section of Miya Tetsuya's mansion, designed to accommodate visitors with individual rooms. It features a dining hall for guests and includes spacious quarters that allow for privacy, though the layout permits sounds to travel easily. The decor consists of tatami mats and traditional Japanese furnishings, which may show signs of disarray following recent events.

The Guest Quarters are located within the City Of The Rich Frog, serving as accommodations for visitors, including dignitaries and magistrates. The quarters are situated in a castle that features a Main Tower with internal chambers designed for privacy and security. Each room has only one door for entry and exit, with no windows present. The area is known for its rich and savory breakfast offerings prepared by servants.

A series of alarming incidents occurred in the Guest Wing, starting with a scream from Miya Misato, whose room was found ransacked. Kakita Kazumi, a Crane shinobi, entered her room to find her terrified, with a shuriken embedded in the wall and a scrap of paper attached. Miya Tetsuya and guards responded, discovering evidence of a break-in and a warning message left behind. Meanwhile, Bayushi Monban, a Scorpion samurai, awoke in a Geisha House, unaware of the turmoil at the mansion. Miya Tetsuya pledged to enhance security for Miya Misato, recognizing the risk to her safety, and discussions of a decoy plan for her protection were proposed.

The situation escalated with the discovery of footprints and signs of a hasty search, indicating that multiple shinobi may have infiltrated the Guest Wing during the night. During a night of chaos, alarms were sounded throughout the castle, prompting guards to check the Guest Wing. The following morning, a breakfast spread was laid out in the Guest Quarters, where several guests convened, including Doji Setsuna, Shiba Midori, and Miya Tetsuya. Shiba Midori recounted an attack that occurred within the quarters, where assailants used her as a weapon and tied up others, including Miya Tetsuya's niece. Miya Tetsuya declared a lockdown of the island, asserting that no one would leave or arrive without his orders until the culprits were found. Investigations were initiated, with the Emerald Magistrates agreeing to conduct a probe into the incident.
