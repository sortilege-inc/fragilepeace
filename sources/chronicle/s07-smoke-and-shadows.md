## Narrative

!lede *That's a woman. Our target is not a woman. Let her go.* They had hold of [[Doji Setsuna]] on her Utaku warsteed, and they let go the moment they looked at her. They were working from a description and she did not fit it. What came down on the [[Emperor’s Road]] was not a robbery. It was an errand, and the errand was [[Asako Taishi]].

He shouted his protection again and it bought him nothing again. [[Kakita Kazumi]] looked once at the smoke and said he knew the tactic. [[Bayushi Monban]] came down out of the air onto [[Doji Setsuna]]'s warsteed with steel out, put a sheath-strike into one of the men holding [[Asako Taishi]], and did not shift him. The horse did better, rearing and putting a second man in the dust. The archers were blind in their own smoke. They dropped their bows and came in on foot.

[[Kakita Kazumi]] went into the fog and stopped being anywhere. He came out of it long enough to put a *yoroi-dōshi* — the armour-piercer, a blade meant for a man already held — into one of them. [[Asako Taishi]] began screaming that there were assassins on him. A second bomb went in and the road disappeared entirely. [[Shiba Midori]] swung her jitte at a man's hands and lost him in the haze. [[Doji Setsuna]] took the reins of [[Asako Taishi]]'s pony and walked it around until there was a horse between her and whoever was coming.

They dragged him off the road, put a blade into his chest and into his kidneys, and left him with minutes on him. [[Bayushi Monban]] jumped at a man, missed, and was on the ground with rope on his arms before he was up. From inside the ropes he announced that he was an Emerald Magistrate and would not be bound. Then he headbutted one of the men holding him.

Their leader came forward in black armour with a long-chained nunchaku, held a threat over [[Asako Taishi]], and turned to [[Shiba Midori]]. She read him first, for whether he wanted this stopped. The two of them walked into a clearing where the smoke was thinning, bowed to each other, and kept every form of a formal duel. He cut first and cut deep. She answered, broke him, and finished it two-handed. He died on the grass with his men still holding a dying scholar.

After that it came apart. [[Bayushi Monban]] threatened what was left of them. [[Doji Setsuna]] turned her horse for the [[Castle of the Swift Sword]]. [[Kakita Kazumi]] went sideways into the shadows and nobody saw him leave. The survivors closed around [[Asako Taishi]] and carried him off gravely wounded, which was what they had come for and not how they had meant to get it. What the party had left to look at was the clothing: dark blue, matched across all of them, no clan crest on any of it. *I did not expect it to go this way at all*, [[Bayushi Monban]] said. He thanked [[Doji Setsuna]], and they agreed to reassemble at the castle.

## Learned

- Masked Assailants: A disciplined body in matching dark blue with no clan crest on any of them. They fought with smoke bombs, fouling arrows and numbers, took Monban with rope rather than steel, and released Setsuna the instant they saw she was a woman — they had a description and were working to it. They wanted Asako Taishi alive enough to carry, and stabbed him nearly to death getting him.
- Black-Armored Leader: Their commander, in black armour with a long-chained nunchaku. He accepted a formal duel from Midori in the middle of an assassination, bowed, kept the forms, struck first and wounded her badly. She killed him. He kept the observances of a duel he had no need to keep, which is a samurai's habit and not a bandit's.
- Asako Taishi: Stabbed in the chest and the kidneys, bleeding out, and carried away alive by the men who did it. Still under the party's promised protection and now in their hands.
- Kakita Kazumi: Recognised the smoke-bomb pattern as something he had seen before. Fights unseen — Skulk into cover, yoroi-dōshi into held men, Crossing Blade to put them down — and left the field without anyone noticing him go.
- Shiba Midori: Carries a jitte and will use it to disarm rather than to kill. She reads an opponent for whether he wants the bloodshed to stop, and she took a formal duel from an armoured man twice her size, was cut deep, and won it two-handed.

## Setsuna

They had a description. It specified a man, and it was exact enough that they let go of a Doji on an Utaku warsteed rather than take the wrong person. Someone briefed them on [[Asako Taishi]] and did not brief them on the party; they did not know an Emerald Magistrate's escort would be on that road.

Dark blue, matched, no crests. That is a company with a paymaster who can outfit it and a reason to keep the outfit anonymous. [[Kakita Kazumi]] has seen the smoke tactic somewhere before, and where is worth asking him.

Two Emerald Magistrates asserted Imperial authority on the [[Emperor’s Road]] four times between them and it stopped nothing at all. The one thing that moved these men was a sword. She should not plan on the badge working in open country again.

[[Asako Taishi]] is alive, taken, and still owed the protection she gave him in as many words. Whatever he was carrying, someone wanted it enough to spend a trained company and their commander on it. The commander died on the grass without anyone learning his name.
