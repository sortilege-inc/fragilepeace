Otosan Uchi

Otosan Uchi, the capital city of the Empire of Rokugan, stands as a vital administrative and military hub within the territory of the Lion Clan. Strategically located just a day's ride from the imposing Loyalty Castle, this city serves as a center for clan activities, political maneuvering, and military logistics, reflecting the martial culture that defines the Lion Clan. Its austere architecture and labyrinthine layout create an atmosphere thick with the complexities of clan interactions, where the sounds of troop training echo through the streets, a reminder of recent military victories and the ongoing tensions with the Unicorn Clan.

The city is marked by significant locations, including the shrine adorned with Lion Clan markings, dedicated to those who have awakened from death, drawing visitors seeking a connection with the kami. Otosan Uchi has been the backdrop for key events that have shaped its reputation, such as the burning of the Crane Clan embassy and the subsequent seven-year peace deal between the Lion Clan and Crane Clan clans. Influential figures like Doji Setsuna engage in delicate missions that could tip the scales of power, mediating disputes involving the Scorpion Clan and Unicorn Clan.

The annual Go tournament at Loyalty Castle provides a rare social occasion amid the prevalent tensions, attracting notable figures such as the soothsayer Kitsu Family Saemi and the Badger Clan smith Ichiro Kan. The road leading to the castle is meticulously paved to clan standards, symbolizing the city’s commitment to order and readiness for the challenges posed by ongoing conflicts and alliances.

Recently, the arrival of Shinjo Mono, an Emerald Magistrate, brought news of a successful military engagement, coinciding with an incident involving Bayushi Monban, who narrowly escaped an attack by Lion Clan bushi over an insult regarding their lord's cuisine. This disturbance unfolded during the chaos surrounding Shinjo Mono's arrival, leading to a duel in the castle courtyard between Kakita Kazumi and Matsu Koda, which concluded with Kazumi yielding to Koda. Following the duel, discussions at a dinner in Loyalty Castle revolved around Shinjo Mono's status and the political implications of their presence in Lion Clan territory, laying plans for further travel toward the Castle of the Swift Sword.

Otosan Uchi also serves as a center for historical documentation and dispute resolution, featuring impressive architecture and beautifully landscaped gardens, alongside chambers dedicated to scholarly pursuits like the Kitsu Archives and military libraries. The climate is generally mild, with cool summer nights fostering an environment conducive to contemplation and discussion.

The castle acts as a gathering place for significant figures, including Emerald Magistrates and ambassadors from various clans, facilitating negotiations over historical claims and territorial rights. Recently, Otosan Uchi has become the focal point of a dispute over Diamond Mines in the Snow Plain, with conflicting claims from the Scorpion Clan and Unicorn Clan clans. The arrival of Kitsuki Kaage, another Emerald Magistrate, has sparked discussions on the historical accounts of the Battle of the Snow Plain and missing histories that could impact upcoming negotiations. The gardens of the castle have served as a backdrop for strategic discussions and personal reflections, as preparations for critical meetings with envoys continue, solidifying Otosan Uchi's role as a site of historical inquiry and political maneuvering.
