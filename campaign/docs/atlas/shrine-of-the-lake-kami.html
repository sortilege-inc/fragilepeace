Shrine of the Lake Kami

The Shrine of the Lake Kami is situated at a post road station on the border of the Kitsu Family and Akodo provinces, surrounding a small lake. It is jointly administered by the Akodo and Kitsu Family clans. The area features samurai quarters for lodging and a dignitary tea house by the lake, where ceremonies and gatherings occur. The lake's water is known for its pleasant quality, particularly at night, enhancing the shrine's serene atmosphere.

Ceremonies at the shrine include tea gatherings, where Kitsu Family samurai hosts discuss various topics with guests. The lakeside path offers a place for fresh air, where supernatural presences may be sensed. Rituals typically involve offerings of rice, sake, and candles, guided by a resident monk. Notable ancestral invocations can occur, such as calling upon Matsu Tomoe, signifying the importance of ancestral respect. The shrine's atmosphere may change with manifestations like golden ginkgo leaves and the scent of jasmine, underscoring its spiritual significance.
