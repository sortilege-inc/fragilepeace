## Narrative

!lede [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]] wanted his friend saved. [[Tonbo Higuchi]] is a noble author of standing, and something is inside her. He put [[Asahina Nao]] at the magistrates' disposal; [[Shiba Midori]] had the ritual instructions. They were still planning when the palace produced a crash from the [[Palace Kitchen]].

She was in there in a purple dress, dishevelled, feeding logs into the furnace one after another and not feeling the heat at all. [[Kakita Kazumi]] went to her and got nothing but confusion back. Then he saw the pendant at her throat, took it off her, and put it in water. [[Bayushi Monban]] recognised the engraving — script out of [[Burning Sands|the Burning Sands]]. That was the binding.

They needed a place and a container. [[Asahina Nao]] produced a fresh vessel; [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]] gave them the [[Central Garden]] and had the servants shut the entrances. The woman wandered to the [[Central Garden Fish Pond|fish pond]], reached in, and ate a goldfish raw.

[[Bayushi Monban]] laid a Threshold Barrier around the pond — and felt his own demon wound light up while he cast it. He tried to pull her through the barrier to separate her from the thing riding her. It did not work. She went out through the ward and the spirit stayed where it was.

[[Tonbo Kuma]] arrived with his bodyguard to help. [[Shiba Midori]] reached for the Heart of Amaterasu to blunt the spirit's grip and the working would not do what she needed. So they changed the order of operations: drive it out first, seal it second. [[Bayushi Monban]] called on [[Lady of Decay|the Lady of Decay]] — his clan tattoos moving on his skin as he did it — went up on Riding the Clouds, carried the vessel and the instructions to [[Shiba Midori]], and the two of them ran a Cleansing Rite of ash and fire together.

The [[Ifrit]] came out of her enormous, inside the barrier, furious.

[[Kakita Kazumi]] went at it with a bucket of water in one hand and put an iaijutsu cut into it that landed properly. It hit him back hard enough to put him on the ground unconscious and bleeding. [[Asahina Nao]] kept working the sealing rite. [[Bayushi Monban]] went in and dragged [[Kakita Kazumi]] out.

And then, with the thing raging in a cage of their making, [[Doji Setsuna]] offered it a parley.

It talked. Furious and grieving at once, and willing to leave its host on terms. *Do I have to go back into a person?* it asked [[Shiba Midori]]. And then: if there is no other choice, it would go. [[Shiba Midori]] put protection up, [[Doji Setsuna]] held out the new vessel, and the [[Ifrit]] — worn out — went into it and collapsed, and [[Asahina Nao]] closed the seal.

[[Tonbo Higuchi]] was left stable and badly shaken. [[Bayushi Monban]], still carrying the mark of [[Lady of Decay|the Lady of Decay]], turned on [[Doji Setsuna]] in front of everyone for walking up to a burning spirit to talk to it, and the two of them had it out where the whole garden could hear.

## Learned

- Tonbo Higuchi: The noble author who writes as Hana no Ame — the same woman whose pen name is on the receipt in Setsuna's custody, and Governor Miya Tetsuya's friend. She was the possessed guest — pendant at her throat, feeding a furnace without feeling it, eating a goldfish raw out of the pond. Freed, stable, badly shaken.
- Ifrit: A fire spirit out of the Burning Sands, bound into a foreign pendant. Enormous, and it fought. It also negotiated — asked whether it had to go back into a person, said it would go if there was no other choice, and went into the jar with what looked like grief.
- Asahina Nao: Provided the replacement vessel and worked the sealing rite through the whole fight, including while Kazumi was down.
- Tonbo Kuma: Came with his bodyguard when called, and helped.
- Bayushi Monban: His demon wound burned while he laid the Threshold Barrier. He called on the Lady of Decay to do the cleansing, and his clan tattoos moved on his skin when he did. Then he rebuked Setsuna publicly for approaching the Ifrit.
- Governor Miya Tetsuya: Gave the magistrates the Central Garden, the servants, Asahina Nao, and their friend's life to save.
- Lady of Decay: What Monban calls on when a spirit has to be driven out. It leaves a mark on him.

## Setsuna

**The parley worked, and it is the only thing in this whole affair that has.** Every technical approach failed in sequence — the Threshold Barrier did not separate them, the Heart of Amaterasu would not take, the extraction had to be reordered mid-fight, and [[Kakita Kazumi]] put a sword into the thing and was carried off unconscious for his trouble. Then she talked to it, and it left.

**Because it is a person, and everybody has been treating it as cargo.** *Do I have to go back into a person?* It asked that. It agreed to be sealed because there was no other choice offered, and it went into the jar grieving. [[Shiba Midori]] has been calling this trade blasphemy for three sessions on principle; tonight the principle answered a direct question in front of witnesses.

Which makes the vessel in [[Asahina Nao]]'s hands a **prisoner**, not a trophy, and the party is now holding one. They have done exactly what the smugglers do — moved a bound spirit from a spent vessel into a fresh one — with better manners and the governor's blessing. She should be clear-eyed that the difference is consent and a promise, and that they have not yet kept any promise, because none was made.

**She should find out what it wants.** It is the only witness in this city who has been inside the traffic from the cargo's side. It knows who bound it, who carried it, and who put it into [[Tonbo Higuchi]].

**The pendant is the physical evidence.** Foreign script from [[Burning Sands|the Burning Sands]], recovered off the host's neck — and a receipt in [[Hana no Ame]]'s pen name for exactly such a talisman is already in her custody. That is a chain from a named buyer to a possessed author in the governor's own house.

Which closes the circle nobody had drawn yet. **[[Hana no Ame]] is a pen name, and it belongs to [[Tonbo Higuchi]].** The novelist the Lion daimyō wants silenced, the buyer on the receipt in Setsuna's own custody, and the possessed woman in the governor's kitchen are one person — and the receipt she signed was for the pendant that was hanging round her neck tonight.

So the question is no longer who she is. It is whether she bought that thing knowing what was in it, or was sold it deliberately by someone who did. A novelist researching how people interact with spirits is exactly the customer you steer a bound Ifrit toward if you want a noble author walking around a governor's palace with a fire spirit inside her — and both a [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] and a [[Lion Clan|Lion]] samurai were already hunting her before it happened.

One last thing, and it is not a small one. [[Bayushi Monban]] shouted at her in a garden full of people. He was frightened, he was marked by [[Lady of Decay|the Lady of Decay]], and he was not wrong about the risk. He was wrong about the place.
