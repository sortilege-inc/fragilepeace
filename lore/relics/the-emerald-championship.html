The Emerald Championship

The tournament that fills the office of Emerald Champion, the Emperor's own magistrate and the highest martial appointment in the Empire. It has been vacant since Doji Satsume died in the first weeks of 1123 — natural causes entered on the record, and no account of the death produced to go with it — and every clan holding a duellist has been doing arithmetic ever since.

It was expected to be convened while the Lion and the Unicorn were at war, then rumoured delayed because the Champions who would have to attend were committed to that war. Miya Satoshi settled the question at the signing: it is put off until next summer, because the Emperor will not have his Winter Court made the venue for something that acrimonious, and because tradition holds it in summer regardless. The Ruby Champion continues in the role until it can be held.

Which means it will be argued about all winter by everyone who intends to enter it, in the same court where the Lion–Unicorn settlement, the Phoenix claim and Toshi Ranbo are all to be heard.
