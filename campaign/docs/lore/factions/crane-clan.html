Faction

Crane Clan

The Crane Clan, a venerable faction within the Emerald Imperial Houses, is celebrated for its courtly elegance, artistic pursuits, and masterful political maneuvering. Their influence permeates the upper echelons of society, with Doji Setsuna, a distinguished Emerald Magistrate, leading their political and legal affairs. Recently, she has faced personal turmoil following her husband’s capture during the Battle of the Hanto River, a backdrop that complicates her role in the clan's intricate political landscape. The Crane Clan's presence is particularly notable at Loyalty Castle, a military stronghold within Lion Clan territory, where they engage in cultural exchanges and strategic exercises. Their grand Go tournaments serve as both displays of skill and arenas for political maneuvering, reinforcing their alliances and influence. Marital ties with the Lion Clan, notably through Doji Setsuna's connection to Shinjō Family Mono via her husband, Shinjō Harunobu, further enhance their sway in courtly matters and military readiness. Among the clan's notable members is Kakita Kazumi, a yōjimbō to Doji Setsuna and a recent Topaz Champion, who investigates disturbances affecting their interests, including the burning of their embassy in Otosan Uchi. Kakita Kazumi is also recognized for her high-status dueling skills and her critical role in legal proceedings, particularly in the ongoing disputes over territorial claims. Another prominent figure is Kakita Sugimasa, known as "the Plucked Crane," who serves as an observer-courtier, contributing to the clan's reputation for strategic insight. The Crane Clan's interactions extend to other factions, including the Scorpion Clan, where Shiba Midori's pragmatic shrine-tending reflects their commitment to honoring ancestors and maintaining order. This involvement often leads to complex relationships, as illustrated by Shiba Midori's intervention in a conflict involving Scorpion Clan samurai, Bayushi Monban, who faces troubling news regarding his betrothed. The Crane Clan is currently embroiled in a legal dispute regarding a secret treaty allegedly created by the Scorpion, which includes claims over Diamond Mines. The court proceedings have featured multiple document presentations and significant accusations exchanged between Crane and Scorpion representatives. Doji Setsuna advocates for damages to be paid to the Empire due to delays and unpressed claims by the Scorpion, referencing precedents from past cases affecting the Crane. As the Crane Clan prepares for their journey to the Castle of the Swift Sword, they remain vigilant, anticipating further challenges and duels arising from ongoing conflicts with the Unicorn Clan and other factions. Their dedication to self-defense and martial readiness, combined with their artistic values, positions them as a formidable force within the realm's political landscape. The clan emphasizes honor, tradition, and decorum in social interactions, as demonstrated by Doji Setsuna's appeals to custom in conflict resolution, ensuring that the principles of honor and formal resolution are upheld in all matters. The clan's goals include maintaining peace between clans and stability within the Imperial Houses, navigating the complex interplay of honor and duty. They engage in various traditions and rituals that reflect their values, including participation in tea ceremonies and discussions about duty, family, and the beauty of Rokugan. The Crane Clan pays close attention to their lineage and the expectations placed upon them, exemplified by their respect for ancestors such as Matsu Family Tomoe, maintaining a complex relationship with honor and the responsibilities associated with their roles in society. Recently, Doji Setsuna and her fellow Emerald Magistrates were summoned to Kitsu Keep by Lady Kitsu Takeko to address a dispute over Diamond Mines with the Scorpion Clan and Unicorn Clan, linked to ancient agreements from the Battle of Snow Plain. Kitsu Takeko presented conflicting claims regarding ownership of the mines, with the Scorpion Clan citing a historical agreement not supported by official records. The Crane Clan's approach in these negotiations involves pursuing evidence to strengthen their claims and mediating between the clans to maintain stability. Doji Setsuna's knowledge of clan dynamics, including the Lion Clan's diplomatic challenges and the Scorpion Clan's rising influence, informs her strategies for securing favorable outcomes for the Crane Clan. In the context of the Snow Plain dispute, the Crane Clan's representatives, particularly Doji Setsuna, aimed to frame their arguments to uphold legal principles advantageous to their clan. Doji Setsuna highlighted the importance of allowing the Scorpion to assert their claims in good faith, suggesting that denying such opportunities could undermine the Unicorn's territorial rights. Kakita Kazumi criticized the evidence presented by the Scorpion, advocating for the Diamond Mines in question to be placed into Imperial custody due to insufficient proof from either side. The Crane Clan's legal strategies sought to reinforce their standing while addressing the broader implications of the case for all clans involved. The Crane Clan emphasizes the importance of maintaining their court position while addressing threats from the aggressive and ambitious Lion Clan. They seek to strengthen alliances and mitigate conflicts, ensuring their significant role in the political landscape of the Emerald Imperial Houses. Their diplomatic efforts include arranging meetings and leveraging connections to gather necessary documentation and support for their claims, while also engaging in investigations into suspected infiltrators, such as Akodo Kayamayako from the Scorpion Clan. The culmination of their efforts often manifests in ceremonial gatherings, such as tea ceremonies with influential figures like Shosuro Imako, a Scorpion Clan operative, to ensure that their contributions to negotiations are recognized and valued. In a recent court dispute concerning the historical accounts of the Battle of Snow Plain, Doji Setsuna testified to the valor of the Lion, while Kakita Kazumi argued for the equal valor of the Lion and Unicorn, countering claims of Scorpion influence. The Crane strategically managed courtroom dynamics, with Setsuna orchestrating testimony to focus on their arguments, and Kazumi compellingly asserting the significance of Imperial authority in recognizing the Lion's victory despite allegations of Scorpion dishonor. The Crane encountered challenges from the Unicorn and Scorpion advocates but upheld their claims about the historical integrity and honor of their ancestors. Discussions acknowledged the Scorpion's role in preserving historical documents purportedly hidden for centuries. The Crane illustrated their commitment to justice and honor while addressing accusations from rivals, culminating in the invocation of ancestor spirits, intertwining the Crane's narrative with the ongoing legal discourse. In addition to their political engagements, the Crane Clan has established a presence in the City Of The Rich Frog, with members such as Doji Setsuna, Shiba Midori, Bayushi Monban, and Kakita Kazumi actively involved in the city's political and social dynamics. They are particularly connected to Daidoji Shin, an agent of Doji Uji, who is investigating threats from rogue shugenja, including those responsible for a tsunami in the Crane Lands, suspecting Mantis Clan involvement. Key interactions occur with Miya Tetsuya, Miya Tetsuya of the city, as the Crane members plan to formally introduce themselves to her. During their visit, Daidoji Shin warns them to avoid the Dragon District, where Katsuki is investigating illicit magic, suggesting that the Unicorn District may have leads on suppliers. The Crane members engage in playful banter with Shin, reflecting the social intricacies of their clan, and ultimately decide to investigate The Docks, indicating their commitment as Emerald Magistrates and their pursuit of information regarding magical items distributed throughout the city. The Crane Clan is also actively involved in law enforcement and maintaining order, particularly through investigations related to smuggling and criminal activities. Recently, Shiba Midori, also an Emerald Magistrate, was attacked by two assailants near The Docks. Despite her efforts to defend herself, she was overwhelmed. The Crane, led by Kaeru Haya, searched for Midori, suspecting a link between her attack and a suspected smuggling operation. They discovered that a Mantis ship, captained by Kubota, was seen departing shortly after the incident. The Crane confronted Captain Kubota, who claimed to be transporting ransom cargo related to a kidnapping, denying any wrongdoing and asserting he was an honest merchant. The encounter escalated into a confrontation, illustrating the tension between the Crane and the Mantis as they navigated local politics and smuggling activities. The Crane Clan is represented by the Kakita and Doji families, known for their courtly elegance and skill in diplomacy. They operate primarily within Miya Tetsuya's mansion, engaging in political maneuvering to protect their interests. The Crane emphasize honor and beauty, navigating complex social dynamics to maintain their influence. They are involved in various intrigues, including the protection of Miya Misato, Miya Tetsuya's niece, and the investigation of a recent break-in at her quarters. Kakita Kazumi, a Crane shinobi, infiltrated the room of Shiba Midori to retrieve "The Box," which was confirmed to be empty. Doji Setsuna, a Crane courtier, experienced a violent illness, prompting Shiba Midori to assess her condition. The situation escalated when a scream was heard from the guest quarters, leading to the discovery of a ransacked room belonging to Miya Misato, where a shuriken was found embedded in the wall alongside a warning message. Kazumi and Setsuna worked together to address the threat to Miya Misato, deducing that the intruders were likely after a document bearing the Imperial Seal, which could enable forgeries. To ensure Miya Misato's safety, the Crane proposed a decoy plan to switch her with Shiba Midori during travel to mislead potential thieves. Miya Tetsuya pledged to send guards for protection, acknowledging the grave risk to Miya Misato. Throughout these events, the Crane demonstrated their commitment to honor and the protection of their allies. In their governance of the City of the Rich Frog, the Crane Clan has faced accusations of involvement in shinobi attacks against Miya Misato, the governor's niece, leading to the confinement of two members by the Emerald Magistrates, who are investigating the matter. Doji Setsuna was informed by Shiba Midori about the relocation of Miya Misato due to safety concerns and secured access to the hidden niece by leveraging the governor's authority to bypass security measures. Ikoma Tadayoshi, another Crane, engaged in discussions with the governor regarding the escort of Miya Misato to the Lion and Unicorn camps, aiming to broker peace between the warring factions. The governor tasked Tadayoshi with ensuring Miya Misato's safety and monitoring the Emerald Magistrates. The Crane's involvement in these diplomatic efforts reflects their role in navigating court politics and their commitment to the honor and safety of their allies.
