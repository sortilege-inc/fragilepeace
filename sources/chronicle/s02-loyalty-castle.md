## Narrative

!lede [[Loyalty Castle]] — Shiro Kodo — is a day's ride from [[Otosan Uchi]] and was built to be reached from the capital in that time. It is a training ground and a mustering yard, not a house for guests, and the guest quarters say so: baths, food, a room, and an instruction not to walk the castle after dark. Once a year it holds the largest Go tournament in the Empire, and for that fortnight it keeps a different set of habits.

[[Akodo Ieyasu]] holds it. [[Akodo Masami]], its yoriki, handles guests, and handled these ones well: [[Lion Clan]] travel papers for [[Doji Setsuna]] and her people, and confirmation that the castle's swift messenger had already carried [[Bayushi Monban]]'s letter out. Papers from a Lion officer, through Lion lands, in a Lion war, are worth more than the writ that sent her.

It was at [[Loyalty Castle]], in the ordinary course of a conversation about the war's logistics, that [[Doji Setsuna]] learned her husband had been taken. [[Shinjo Harunobu]] was among the prisoners of the Battle of the [[Hanto River]], captured north of the [[Drowned Merchant River]]. Nobody had sent word. Later an [[Ikoma Bard]] confirmed he was alive and in custody, named [[Akodo Sakura]] as commander of the besieging force, and suggested she could write to him from the [[Castle of the Swift Sword]]. The route and the need arrived in the same conversation, from the same stranger.

The task waiting at the end of the road stayed vague. [[Kitsuki Kaage]] is occupied at the Emerald Champion's castle and had sent her ahead with the shape of a case rather than its particulars: something requiring the Emerald Magistrates, possibly turning on a request to alter a building on the historical register. There is no preparing for a matter described that way.

The castle held people worth marking. [[Kakita Sugimasa]], [[Doji Setsuna]]'s half-brother, was there as an observer-courtier among military men, taking slights from [[Lion Clan]] courtiers and keeping count of them; the court's reading was that his father had sent him to be rid of an unwanted relative. He is waiting for an insult large enough to answer with steel. [[Kitsu Saemi]], a soothsayer of the [[Kitsu Family|Kitsu]], is frail and has been imposed upon during that frailty by people who wanted her authority, and is considering what it would take to get it back. And in the mustering square stood a Shinseist shrine, well kept, with a wax-sealed copy of the Tao on it that has never been opened. Brotherhood pilgrims come for it. The rumour is that a hidden chapter of the Tao is somewhere in these walls, and that whoever finds it gains a lever on the Brotherhood and on the religion of the Empire. The evidence offered is that a fortress of this size has no monastery. Sceptics call it a peasant story. The pilgrims keep arriving.

[[Bayushi Monban]] got worse news than anyone. The castle runners brought him a letter: his betrothed, [[Hida Chizuzu]], overdue from a mission beyond the Wall — captured, tainted, or dead. The [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] chalk seal was correct. He did not believe it. The penmanship was too exact and carried none of the flourishes the hand should have had, and he has been close enough to mahō lately to know what a forgery bought with blood looks like. He asked [[Doji Setsuna]] to compare the hands. Then he decided he would finish at the [[Castle of the Swift Sword]] before riding for the [[Crab Lands|Crab lands]], because the duty was owed first. He also offered to teach [[Ryu]] and [[So]] to write better, on the grounds that a magistrate is served best by servants who can take down what they hear.

Morning came in on a gong. [[Doji Setsuna]] and [[Bayushi Monban]] sparred unarmed in the training square — she landed a clean punch, he slipped most of what followed and answered with a touch. [[Kakita Kazumi]] worked kata and told anyone who asked that he does not go looking for fights and does not need to; [[Kakita Sugimasa]] warned him that a Lion student was circling with a bloodied relative to avenge, and had already challenged Sugimasa by mistake. [[Kitsuki Wataru]] found the [[Togashi Nun]] at her warm-ups with two other monks and asked to be let into the morning chanting; she told him to come back tomorrow, and agreed to guide him.

Then breakfast, and a bowl of soup set in front of [[Bayushi Monban]] with testicles in it. He stood up, took off the scarf, and challenged the room. Lion warriors queued to accept. The bouts were set for later in the day, and the party rode out with those and a formal challenge to [[Kakita Kazumi]] still outstanding.

## Learned

- Shinjo Harunobu: Her husband, taken prisoner at the Battle of the Hanto River, north of the Drowned Merchant River, and alive in custody. Nobody sent word to her; she heard it in passing at a Lion castle. An Ikoma bard says a letter can reach him from the Castle of the Swift Sword.
- Akodo Masami: Yoriki of Loyalty Castle and the officer who handles guests. He issued Lion travel papers for her and her retinue without being pressed, and confirmed Monban's letter had gone out by castle messenger. Useful, and so far willing.
- Akodo Ieyasu: Lord of Loyalty Castle.
- Akodo Sakura: Commander of the Lion force besieging where her husband is held.
- Kakita Sugimasa: Her half-brother, at the castle as an observer-courtier among soldiers, taking slights from Lion courtiers and counting them. Court gossip says his father sent him there to be rid of him. He is waiting for cause to duel, and he warned Kazumi that a Lion student is hunting him over a bloodied relative.
- Kitsu Saemi: A frail Kitsu soothsayer whose authority has been undermined by others while she was weak, and who is weighing whether to fight for it.
- Hida Chizuzu: Bayushi Monban's betrothed, reported overdue beyond the Wall and possibly captured, tainted or dead. Monban believes the letter is a mahō forgery — the seal is right, the hand is too precise and has none of the expected flourishes.
- Kitsuki Kaage: Held at the Emerald Champion's castle by other business, which is why Setsuna was sent ahead without particulars. The case may turn on a request to alter a building on the historical register.
- Togashi Nun: Keeps a morning chanting practice at the castle shrine with two other monks, and has agreed to guide Kitsuki Wataru onto a monk's path.

## Setsuna

She found out her husband was a prisoner from a stranger, in the middle of a conversation about supply lines. No one in the Crane, the Unicorn or the Imperial service thought to tell her. She is on no list that gets told things, and whatever she wants to know about [[Shinjo Harunobu]] she will have to be standing next to when it is said.

Two assets came out of this castle. [[Akodo Masami]] gave her Lion travel papers unprompted, which is the difference between moving through [[Lion Lands|Lion lands]] and being moved through them. And there is now a named route to her husband: a letter from the [[Castle of the Swift Sword]], and a named commander, [[Akodo Sakura]], holding the siege where he is kept.

One liability. Her half-brother [[Kakita Sugimasa]] is at this castle collecting insults and waiting for one big enough to draw on. If he duels a Lion here it lands on the Crane, and it lands on her while she is trying to be read as an Emerald Magistrate rather than as a Doji.
