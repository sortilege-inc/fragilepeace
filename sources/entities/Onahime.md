One of the two Kuni Warden Hunter visages painted on [[Bayushi Monban]]'s own face beneath
the mask — Onahime on one side, the [[Lady of Decay]] on the other. He took the mask off in
the trees in session 6 and showed them to everyone below, who took him for a demon and shot
at him.

**She is not a painting.** When he died he had to be masked to go into [[Jigoku]], and she
kept him alive down there and brought him out — he was eaten and he exploded and he came
through anyway. What she asked in return was to take a piece of him with her when she left,
and that they would stay joined. She has kept her side of it since. **She is not an evil
thing**, which he says plainly and which the record should keep saying, because everything
about how she appears argues the opposite.

**How she shows.** He takes the mask off and calls her — *little sister, time to play*, or
simply *Onahime, rise*. Blackness comes out of the left side of his face, ringed like a
snowflake, sometimes emerald; a second shape comes out of his shoulders and head; what is
left is half a demon and half a rather handsome man with a very wicked smile. Those nearby
hear a woman laughing, childish and soft, because she is enjoying herself. Certain kihō
bring her up whether or not he means it.

**The witness problem.** *The lady Doji is mine, and Onahime will have her doom* — screamed
in a tent with people watching. [[Shiba Midori]] and [[Kakita Kazumi]] have both seen her
now, and possibly [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]]. [[Doji Setsuna]] was knocked down before the change
finished and has not seen it whole.
