Kuni Wardens

Crab — Kuni

The Crab order that hunts what comes over the Wall, and whose Hunter visages — Onahime and the Lady of Decay — have been painted on Bayushi Monban's mask since session 6. He wears their marks on his skin as well, which is a different claim entirely, and one he has shown to exactly one person: he opened his shirt for Akodo Akihito in a Lion camp and closed it again before he left the tent.

Nobody in Scorpion lands is looking for a Kuni Warden. That is the whole of the idea.

The Archivist export was taken before this entry existed. What the page holds is drawn from the sessions themselves.
