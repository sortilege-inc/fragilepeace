One of the grandmothers of the [[Iuchi Family|Iuchi]], seated on the
[[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] delegation for [[Shinjo Altansarnai]]. She is what
[[Akodo Kage]] is to the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] and the two of them know it: high standing,
no authority to bind, and present so that a Champion's weight can be felt in a tent the
Champion is not sitting in. She may be addressed as grandmother without giving offence.

She is meishōdō, and a shugenja, which puts her and [[Shiba Midori]] at the same
understanding of the world by opposite roads. She performed the morning blessing at the
negotiation herself rather than let Midori do it, Midori being a party to the case.

Her method is one question asked until it is answered. What were you sent for, and is what
is happening in this tent getting it? Her reading of the arbiter was that a Crane who would
rather be right than have a peace is a danger to everyone in the room, and that being right
would not bring back her dead grandchildren.
