Faction

Ikoma Family

Lion

The Ikoma Family, a distinguished clan within the Lion Clan, is revered for their vital role as storytellers and historians, with their primary base of operations located at the Castle of the Swift Sword, which they share with the Kitsu Family and the Akodo War College. This castle stands as a center of scholarship, housing extensive libraries that preserve the rich history and knowledge of the realm. The Ikoma Family are deeply engaged in courtly matters and military campaigns, often serving as observers and advisors, thereby influencing both the cultural and strategic dimensions of the Lion Clan.

Historically, the Ikoma Family has been instrumental in documenting significant events, such as the Battle of Kanto Plains and the Battle of the Snow Plain, particularly through their ancestor Ikoma Akuyaku, who played a notable role in that conflict. Their recent involvement in the aftermath of an assault on Doji Setsuna, an Emerald Magistrate, underscores their importance in the political landscape. Following the attack, Kitsu Takeko, the Lion daimyo, initiated a search for the assailants, deeming the incident a serious affront to the Empire. In this context, Ikoma Family scholars, including the notable Kitsu Ayoko, provided essential research assistance and historical insights to support the Emerald Magistrates during the investigation.

The Ikoma Family's influence extends beyond mere documentation; they are key players in the ongoing conflict between the Lion Clan and the Unicorn Clan. Their role as intermediaries in diplomatic and military affairs was exemplified when an Ikoma Family Bard revealed critical information regarding the captivity of Doji Setsuna's husband after the Battle of the Hanto River. This revelation not only shed light on the military situation but also facilitated discussions about potential communication with the besieging forces, showcasing the Ikoma Family's strategic importance.

Their commitment to the preservation of history and the documentation of events significant to the Lion Clan and Rokugan is evident in their recent efforts at the Castle of the Swift Sword, where Shiba Midori sought to authenticate historical documents related to the Battle of the Snow Plain. This effort was prompted by the discovery of a cache of documents, including a draft treaty and historical accounts that could impact the honor and claims of various clans involved in the battle. The family's connection to the past was further emphasized when Shosuro Aishi referenced the summoning of ancestral spirits, including Ikoma Akuyaku, to provide testimony about the events of the battle. The Ikoma's actions underscore their dedication to maintaining the integrity of their clan's history and ensuring that past truths are acknowledged in the current political landscape.

As a family dedicated to the preservation of history and the narration of events, the Ikoma Family continues to play a crucial role in shaping the Lion Clan's identity and actions, merging their scholarly pursuits with the martial responsibilities of their clan.
