Dragon Lands

Dragonlands is a region located east of Morning Glory Castle, characterized by varied terrain, including rivers and access to the Imperial Roads. It is recognized for its scholarly pursuits, particularly due to its proximity to the Ikoma Lands, reputed for their excellent libraries. Dragonlands serves as a passage for travelers moving between different clans, such as the Phoenix Clan and Lion Clan clans, and is marked by historical connections to knowledge and the exchange of ideas, with a notable focus on arcane and spiritual studies.

Asako Taishi, a Phoenix Clan scholar-traveler, is known to have traversed through Dragonlands while journeying from the Shrine of Durogin. His route indicated a tendency to wander rather than pursue a specific destination, leading him toward the libraries of the Ikoma Lands. During the journey, discussions arose regarding Asako Taishi's need for authentication of a potentially dangerous discovery, believed to have caused misfortunes for those aware of it. The party, including Shiba Midori and Bayushi Monban, agreed to escort him to the Castle of the Swift Sword, citing the family's arcane knowledge as a suitable resource for this purpose. The journey included discussions on various spiritual matters, with Shiba Midori sharing her experiences with elemental spirits, further emphasizing the region's connection to spiritual practices.
