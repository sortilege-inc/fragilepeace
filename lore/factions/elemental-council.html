Elemental Council

The Elemental Council is a faction in the realm of Rokugan, known for its influence over elemental magic and regulation of shugenja practices. It operates with the goal of maintaining balance and order in the use of elemental magic, ensuring that shugenja adhere to established protocols and do not disrupt the harmony of the natural world.

The council comprises prominent figures with deep knowledge of elemental forces and their ethical implications. Representatives include Doji Setsuna, Shiba Midori, Bayushi Monban, and Kakita Kazumi. They engage with local officials and investigate illicit magical activities to uphold their mandate. The council also seeks to address potential threats to the realm’s stability, as evidenced by their responses to recent events, such as a tsunami caused by a shugenja's actions.

The council's proactive approach includes gathering intelligence and visiting local shops for items that may aid in their investigations.
