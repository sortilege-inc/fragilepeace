The Ceasefire

The agreement that ended the Lion–Unicorn war, drafted at the negotiating tent and signed on the seventeenth day. Its authority is unusual: it is not a magistrate's ruling but a bilateral agreement between the two clans, which Doji Setsuna witnessed as a representative of the Emperor rather than decided.

Its terms: the borders return to where they stood before the invasion, barring adjustments left for later; five prisoners of high standing are retained by each side until the matter closes at Winter Court, and everyone below that standing goes home; the horses return to the Unicorn for a price; and the Unicorn acknowledge that the Lion conducted themselves throughout as righteous, honourable and correct by the traditions of Rokugani warfare.

What it does not contain is the betrothal. Every question touching it — the fault for the war, the territorial concessions the match was to have brought, the historical grievances, and the wrong done to Asako Ikari — is referred on to the Emperor's administration and to Winter Court, on Doji Setsuna's statement that she could not attest to a document that was untrue by omission.

Doji Setsuna, Shiba Midori and Akodo Akihito each staked ten honour on it holding, which binds their clans through them. The honour is staked, not spent: it is forfeit only by whoever breaks the agreement, and not by anyone whose counterpart broke it first.
