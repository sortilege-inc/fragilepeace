Dran Merchant River

The Dran Merchant River is a significant waterway in the region, integral to trade and transportation. It flows near the Castle of the Swift Sword, which serves as a military and scholarly hub for the Lion Clan. The castle consists of two conjoined fortresses, featuring guest dormitories near kitchens, and the Path of Splinters leading to an Ancestral Shrine. The surrounding area is characterized by obscuring and entangling terrain, particularly in forested regions, complicating travel and movement.

The river is a vital route for important figures, including Magister Kitsuki Kaage, who was traveling upstream but was delayed by unseasonable rains. This underscores the river's importance for military and diplomatic endeavors. The presence of Lion cavalry along the riverbanks further emphasizes its role in securing safe passage. The river and its surroundings support various interactions, including formal reporting of ambushes and strategic discussions regarding troop movements.

The Merchant River flows through a bustling dock area with navigable waters that accommodate various vessels, including a large blue-water ship. The river's banks are lined with wooden structures and warehouses, indicative of its role as a hub for trade and commerce. The climate is temperate, supporting transportation and trade activities. This location has a history intertwined with smuggling operations and local power dynamics, as shown by the presence of law enforcement and various clan interests.

Kaeru Haya, the local head lawman, escorted Bayushi Monban, Doji Setsuna, and Kakita Kazumi to The Docks based on suspicions of a smuggling operation. Haya noted that The Docks are a likely base for smuggling, particularly involving relics from outside Rokugan. The crew of a large ship was in lockup, limiting its operational capacity. Shiba Midori, a Phoenix Emerald Magistrate, was ambushed in a dark alley near The Docks, resulting in a confrontation with assailants wielding wooden weapons. After Midori was incapacitated, her colleagues searched The Docks for her, finding signs of her struggle, including a scrap of her robe.

Bayushi Monban later pursued a departing ship, suspecting it was involved in the smuggling ring. Captain Kubota, the ship's captain, claimed he was transporting ransom cargo for his kidnapped grandniece and insisted he was not engaging in smuggling. The situation escalated as Monban asserted his authority as an Emerald Magistrate, leading to a heated exchange with Captain Kubota, who refused to anchor the ship despite demands. This interaction highlighted the complex interplay of law, commerce, and local power struggles occurring along the Merchant River.
