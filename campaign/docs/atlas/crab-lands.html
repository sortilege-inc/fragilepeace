Crab Lands

The Crab Lands are located beyond The Wall, characterized by rugged terrain and a harsh climate marked by cold winds and unpredictable weather. This region is home to the Crab Clan, whose responsibility is to defend the realm from various threats, including supernatural entities.

A waystation situated on the border of the Kitsu Family and Akodo provinces serves as a significant stop for travelers. This waystation is near a small lake that features a Shrine of the Lake Kami, emphasizing the land's spiritual significance. It accommodates dignitaries and travelers, including Emerald Magistrates, who are welcomed by Kitsu Family samurai and invited to participate in local customs, such as tea ceremonies.

Interactions among the Emerald Magistrates and their Kitsu Family hosts often involve discussions of honor, duty, and respect for ancestors, reflecting the values important in both the Crab Lands and Rokugan at large. The region is seen as a frontier, with inherent challenges and obligations tied to the responsibilities of the Crab Clan.
