Unicorn Lands

The Unicorn Lands feature rolling plains marked by various fortifications and a sparse, poorly maintained road network. The area is strategically significant for trade and military movements, especially during conflicts. The climate is influenced by nearby rivers and forests, with lush landscapes that enhance its natural beauty. The region is encircled by urban development, creating a unique blend of nature and city life. It is inhabited by the Unicorn Clan, notable for their samurai, including Shinjō Harunobu, who are integral to local governance and military efforts. Currently, the lands are affected by hostilities from the Lion Clan, who are besieging Gatherer of Wind’s Castle, creating a tense political atmosphere. The City of the Rich Frog, a crucial trade port, connects the Unicorn, Dragon, and Lion clans. It is located along the route to the Three Sides River, which the Unicorn Clan must cross. Local conditions, including the state of the roads and the threat of conflict, pose challenges to travelers in the region. Recently, a group successfully contained an Efreet, a Ifrit, binding it within a box after a chaotic confrontation. Notable figures such as Shiba Midori and Bayushi Monban participated in discussions regarding the spirit's fate, considering the risks of releasing it in Unicorn Lands compared to other territories. While navigating the river, they recognized that upriver led into Unicorn Lands and downriver toward Scorpion or Lion Lands. The group decided to take an unmanned boat upriver, planning to release the Efreet while deliberating on the safest course of action for the region. The Unicorn Lands are currently experiencing conflict due to a war between the Lion and Unicorn clans, a stark contrast to their previously peaceful reputation. Miya Tetsuya, the governor of the City of the Rich Frog, has a niece named Miya Misato, who is involved in an imperial mission related to the ongoing conflict. The governor has expressed concerns for her safety, especially following multiple shinobi attacks that have occurred while she has been relocated within the castle. An emissary was sent to Unicorn Lands to request an honor guard from the Miya estates, highlighting the need for security in the area. The governor plans for Miya Misato to travel westward to broker peace between the Lion and Unicorn camps, intending for her to join a contingent of Lion soldiers for protection during her journey. To ensure her safety, the governor has tasked Ikoma Tadayoshi with monitoring the Emerald Magistrates, who are also involved in the unfolding events.
