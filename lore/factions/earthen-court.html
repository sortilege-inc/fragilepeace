Earthen Court

Spirit

The court of the earth spirits beneath the mountains west of the Empire, ruled by The Earthen Queen. Its guards are man-shaped boulders that move through solid stone as though it were water. Everything in those mountains is its realm, whether a human knows it or not. Not in the Archivist export.
