The noble author who writes as [[Hana no Ame]], and [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]]'s friend. She
was lodging at the palace, researching how people who are not shugenja reach the spirit
world, and bought a talisman said to hold a wild spirit out of [[Burning Sands|the Burning
Sands]] under her own artist's chop — leaving the handling instructions behind in the shop,
unpaid for. The talisman failed. She wore what came out of it for about a day before the
magistrates took it off her neck.

A [[Lion Clan|Lion]] daimyō wants her next book not to exist, and a [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]
pair have been looking for her as well.
