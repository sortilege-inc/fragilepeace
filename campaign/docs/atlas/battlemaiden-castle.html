Battlemaiden Castle

Battlemaiden Castle is located in the Unicorn Lands, characterized by rolling plains and significant rivers. It serves as a strategic site amid the conflicts between the Lion Clan and Unicorn forces. The castle is considered relevant to military movements and is of interest to the Emerald Magistrates. The surrounding area is currently under siege, with the Lion Clan besieging Gatherer of Wind’s Castle, situated north of the City of the Rich Frog.

The castle plays a crucial role in military strategy and is a point of control in the region's complex political landscape. It is a destination for those engaged in the ongoing conflicts, and it is pertinent for plans to investigate the Lion Clan's actions to ensure that hostilities are authorized, linking their objectives to the importance of Battlemaiden Castle.
