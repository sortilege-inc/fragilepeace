Miya estates

Imperial

Miya estates is associated with Governor Miya Tetsuya, who oversees the City Of The Rich Frog. The estate serves as a residence for the governor and his family, including his niece, Miya Misato. The location has experienced tensions due to multiple shinobi attacks, prompting the governor to relocate his niece within the castle for her safety. The estate is connected to the Imperial Herald, as the governor is a kinsman to this figure. Following the attacks, an emissary was sent to request an honor guard from the Miya estates in Unicorn Lands. Discussions among court members revealed that Miya Misato was on an imperial mission to negotiate peace between the Lion and Unicorn factions, although her presence lacked the typical imperial entourage and banners. The governor expressed concerns about her safety and tasked allies to ensure her protection while she fulfilled her diplomatic duties. Plans were made for her to join a contingent of Lion soldiers traveling nearby, with an emphasis on vigilance against potential threats.
