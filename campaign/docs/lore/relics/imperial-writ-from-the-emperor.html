Imperial Writ From The Emperor

The Imperial Writ from the Emperor is a formal document carried by Miya Misato, a young Miya courtier and niece to Governor Miya Tetsuya. This writ serves as an official order demanding the Lion armies to cease their operations and facilitate negotiations between the Lion and Unicorn clans. Miya Misato received her instructions orally from the Imperial Advisor's servant and is tasked with delivering this message to the clan champions of both factions.

During a tea ceremony at the Governor’s Tea House, Miya Misato revealed her mission to those present, including Shiba Midori, Kakita Kazumi, and Doji Setsuna. As she described her task, she visibly trembled, indicating her nerves and inexperience. The group recognized the gravity of her mission and pledged to accompany her toward the front lines, emphasizing their duty as Emerald Magistrates.
