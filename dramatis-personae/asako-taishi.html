Dramatis Persona

Asako Taishi

Phoenix

What is known

S6A Phoenix scholar, Isawa by blood and raised among the Kaito, travelling the Imperial Roads without a stated destination — Midori reads him as drifting toward the Ikoma libraries. He carries something he will not name and wants it authenticated, and says everyone he has told has come to harm. Wataru finds he has cast no magic in a day but has been targeted by it, or is still inside it. Midori sees a presence overshadowing him that is not malignant; a cleansing kihō gave relief and did not shift it. He was taken in the ambush while under the party's promised protection.
S7Stabbed in the chest and the kidneys, bleeding out, and carried away alive by the men who did it. Still under the party's promised protection and now in their hands.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
