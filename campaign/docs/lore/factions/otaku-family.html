Otaku Family

Unicorn

A family of the Unicorn Clan. Ikoma Tadayoshi argued at the treaty table that they had shamed themselves by withdrawing from the battle on the Snow Plain, and that breaking the Lion afterwards would only finish the Otaku's work for them. Whether the withdrawal was a disgrace or a judgement is a Unicorn question, and the Moto answer it in the way that serves the Moto.

The Archivist export was taken before this entry existed. What the page holds is drawn from the sessions themselves.
