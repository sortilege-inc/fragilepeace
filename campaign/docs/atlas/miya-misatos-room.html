Miya Misato’s Room

Imperial

Miya Misato's Room is marked by disarray, indicative of a recent intrusion. The room shows signs of having been thoroughly searched, with items rifled through and left in chaos. A shuriken is embedded in the wall, pinning a scrap of paper that bears a warning written in dark blue ink, stating "Turn back. Aoi." Miya Misato, The Governor's niece, is found cowering in a corner, visibly terrified by the disturbance.

Kakita Kazumi observes the chaos through the window before entering. Upon his arrival, Miya Misato throws herself at him in fear, expressing her concern about the intruders who did not harm her despite having the opportunity. The Governor and guards soon arrive, retrieving the shuriken and the warning note. Kazumi questions Miya Misato about the location of a document, which she implies is hidden on her person. The Governor vows to send men to protect her, acknowledging the risk to her safety. The room's condition suggests that the intruders were searching for something specific, likely the document, but they did not find it.
