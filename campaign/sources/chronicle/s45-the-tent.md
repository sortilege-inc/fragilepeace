## Narrative

!lede A morning of law in [[Moto Gaheris]]'s tent, and a night that ended with
[[Doji Setsuna]] unconscious on the floor of another one and the Imperial Envoy dying.

[[Moto Gaheris]] served chicken so aggressively spiced it was plainly a test. The
magistrates ate it without comment, which was the correct answer.

Then [[Doji Setsuna]] laid out the law of the betrothal, and it came out better than she
had any right to expect. The contract sits in a grey area. [[Shinjo Altansarnai]],
as [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] Champion, may be able to nullify it unilaterally. The
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] case rests on a contested reading rather than a settled one. And the
whole dispute turns on the difference between precedent that binds and precedent that
merely persuades. Everything afterwards was built on that.

[[Moto Gaheris]] named who he thought had arranged this war. The [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]],
which the party had already got to. And the [[Crane Clan|Crane]] — because the recent
[[Crane Clan|Crane]]–[[Lion Clan|Lion]] treaty happens to have cut the [[Owari Plains]]
off from the reach of the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]'s allies, which is a convenient
accident. [[Kakita Kazumi]] turned it aside with *circumstances we cannot discuss*.
[[Doji Setsuna]], sitting in [[Crane Clan|Crane]] colours in a
[[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] tent, let him do it and said nothing in the Crane's defence.
Her authority in that tent is Imperial, and she kept it Imperial.

[[Bayushi Monban]] — not [[Doji Setsuna]] — asked for the escort west to the border, and
used the Emerald Magistrate's badge to make it a requirement rather than a favour. She
supplied the legal ground; he made the demand.

Then she did the quiet thing. She asked [[Moto Gaheris]]'s guards to carry a message to
*the Shinjo*, without naming which one, and watched them try to pretend there was no such
person in the camp. Half an hour later they came back having decided otherwise, which
confirmed [[Shinjo Altansarnai]] was already there without obliging [[Moto Gaheris]] to
admit it. The message gave her name, mentioned that her husband [[Shinjo Harunobu]] is a
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] prisoner — a credential in that camp — stated what the mission was
for, and asked one question: who else was party to the dissolution of the betrothal? This
was not a stranger writing to a stranger. [[Shinjo Altansarnai]] was a guest at
[[Doji Setsuna]]'s wedding. The outrider's answer came back as *how interesting*, which
is a door left open.

In the evening [[Miya Misato]] asked the party whether any of them had unmarried
relatives, hunting for a substitute bride. [[Doji Setsuna]] said she has a younger
sister, [[Doji Emika]] — and that [[Doji Emika]] is already intended for a different
marriage treaty.

That night the party took tents inside the joint [[Lion Clan|Lion]] and
[[Moto Family|Moto]] camp. [[Miya Misato]] and [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]], her yojimbo, in one;
[[Doji Setsuna]] in the next. In the small hours there was a struggle through the canvas.

There were three armed men in the envoy's tent. One had a knife at [[Miya Misato]]'s
throat and a hand inside her kimono. The other two were holding [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] down
rather than killing him. Nobody was meant to die. This was an extraction, handled
quietly, with the exits kept open.

[[Doji Setsuna]] went in as she was. Sleeping robes, no wakizashi — it was not on her and
she did not stop for it. She was in the tent ahead of [[Bayushi Monban]], who was further
away and said so.

She has one martial skill and it is unarmed. It is the training a high-status court woman
is given so that if she is ever seized she has some chance of getting loose. She used it
on somebody else. Coiling Serpent Style, targeted on the knife rather than the man —
and the three of them had already filed the unarmed woman in nightclothes as not a
threat, which is exactly why it worked. The knife came away from [[Miya Misato]]'s
throat. In the same breath she told [[Miya Misato]] to knee him, and [[Miya Misato]]
did, and he was briefly out of it. Two court women in sleeping robes fighting ugly in the
dark for ten seconds, and it held.

Her fear caught up with her as she came through the flap. She let it, took what it cost
her, and kept going.

Then she made herself the largest thing in the tent. *I am Doji Setsuna, Emerald
Magistrate, and advisor to the Emperor's Ambassador. Drop your blades or my yojimbo here
will ensure your death is slow and dishonorable.* It landed on every one of them. One
broke and ran.

On the third exchange one of them put her on the floor. He tackled rather than cut —
they had worked out that she was worth more alive than dead — and she took a knife badly
enough to open her up. She ended it unconscious and bleeding, past her endurance, and
she lived.

While she was going down, [[Bayushi Monban]] said something that was not [[Bayushi
Monban]]. *The lady Doji is mine, and [[Onahime]] will have her doom.* There was a
shadow ringed like a snowflake across his face — [[Onahime]], one of the two Kuni Warden
Hunter visages he has worn painted on his mask since the road to the
[[Castle of the Swift Sword]]. Later, after another blow landed, [[Shiba Midori]] heard a
child's laughter, a girl's. [[Shiba Midori]] saw it and [[Kakita Kazumi]] saw it, and
perhaps [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] did. [[Doji Setsuna]] was already down.

It ended with bodies in the tent, at least one man away into the dark, brown armour on
the one who had been holding [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]], and [[Miya Misato]] dying on the floor
of a camp guarded by two armies.

## Learned

- Miya Misato: The target. Taken at knifepoint in her own tent by men who wanted her alive and out, not dead. Setsuna got the knife off her throat and she kneed the man herself. She is dying. Earlier that evening she was asking the party for unmarried relatives, looking for a substitute bride.
- Moto Gaheris: Served the audience obnoxiously spiced and named his own suspects — the Scorpion, and the Crane, on the grounds that the new Crane–Lion treaty cut the Owari Plains off from the Unicorn's allies. Had Shinjo Altansarnai in his camp and would not say so; his guards tried to deny she existed and then thought better of it.
- Shinjo Altansarnai: In Moto Gaheris's camp. As Unicorn Champion she may be able to nullify the betrothal on her own authority — which makes her the person who can end this. A back channel to her now exists, opened without Gaheris having to concede anything.
- Doji Emika: Setsuna's younger sister, unmarried, and not available — already intended for another marriage treaty.
- Owari Plains: Cut off from the reach of the Unicorn's allies by the recent Crane–Lion treaty, which is why Moto Gaheris counts the Crane among his suspects.
- Ikoma Tadayoshi: Miya Misato's yojimbo, in her tent when they came. Held down rather than killed. His assailant wore brown armour.
- The three men in the envoy's tent (unpaged): Professionals. Non-lethal handling throughout — Tadayoshi pinned, Setsuna tackled rather than cut once they placed her rank. One fled. They came for the envoy alive.

## Setsuna

**She put herself between them and [[Miya Misato]], not between them and herself.**
Everything she did in that tent was about getting the envoy free — the knife first, then
telling her how to hurt the man holding her. She owns one martial skill, it exists so
that a court woman can save her own skin, and she spent it on someone else while unarmed
and in her nightclothes. A duelist running into that tent is doing their job. She was
doing something nobody had ever asked of her.

They came to take [[Miya Misato]] alive. That is the single most important fact of the
night and it should govern the whole investigation. Nobody in that tent was killed who
could have been. [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] was pinned. She herself was tackled rather than cut
once they had placed her rank — being worth more alive is not a compliment, it is a brief.
An assassination and an extraction are ordered by different people for different reasons.
Whoever sent them wanted the envoy in their hands, not in the ground.

The failure of the escort is the leverage, and it is enormous. Five Imperial persons were
attacked in their tents inside a joint [[Lion Clan|Lion]] and [[Moto Family|Moto]] camp.
There are only two readings. Either one of the two escorting forces did it — a faction
inside an army seizing the Emperor's envoy mid-mission — or a third party walked through
both perimeters, which means both commanders failed the duty they took on. There is no
third reading and no innocent version. Their dishonour is already established; they simply
have not been told yet.

That is the ground she wakes onto. Put to both commanders in the plainest, most courteous
terms available — help her clear their names and run the investigation, or answer for
having let this happen in their custody — it is not a threat but a description of where
bushidō has already placed them, with a way back offered. The courtesy is what makes it
impossible to refuse.

If [[Miya Misato]] dies, the mission does not. It is a charge, and charges pass. She is
the Personal Advisor, an Emerald Magistrate, and the person who has done the substantive
legal work; she can carry it in the envoy's name. Whoever ordered this assumed the mission
was a woman. It is a document.

[[Shinjo Altansarnai]] can probably end this by herself. If a Champion can nullify the
betrothal on her own authority, then the entire war is waiting on one woman's signature,
and that woman is in a camp two tents away and has just been told there is a Crane who
wants to talk — a Crane whose wedding she attended, married into her own clan. That is not
a back channel. That is an acquaintance who has not been called on. The back channel was
the best thing she did all day and it is now the most urgent.

And [[Moto Gaheris]] is not a fool about the [[Crane Clan|Crane]]. He is wrong that they
arranged this war. He is right that a [[Crane Clan|Crane]] treaty has quietly rearranged
the north to Crane advantage at [[Lion Clan|Lion]] expense, and right to notice that the
Crane in the room is the one saying least. She was correct to let [[Kakita Kazumi]] answer
him. She should also register that a man who reads her clan that accurately, from that
little, will read her the same way eventually.
