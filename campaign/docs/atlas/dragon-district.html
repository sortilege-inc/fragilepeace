Dragon District

The Dragon District is located on the north bank of the City Of The Rich Frog, known for its strategic significance and diverse clan influences. It is governed by the Dragon Clan, with Kitsuki Wataru, a magistrate, overseeing local affairs. The district features a blend of residential and commercial structures, serving both the local populace and travelers. The climate is temperate, typical of a riverbank city, facilitating a variety of activities among its inhabitants.

The district faces ongoing issues with smuggling and black market activities, highlighted by the presence of a Dragon magistrate investigating the illicit trade of "Meishodo" artifacts. The risks in the area are emphasized by the mention of The Docks as a place to avoid after dark due to the activity of local criminals. The Dragon District's significance is further underscored by the need for discretion among those navigating its complex social landscape.

Recent developments reveal that the Dragon District is also associated with the Katsuki, who are engaged in illicit magical activities. Daidoji Shin, a Crane agent, has warned that the district is hazardous due to the high concentration of Katsuki involved in these dealings. The atmosphere has grown tense, complicating interactions for newcomers and further entrenching the district's reputation as a hub for illicit activities, which plays a significant role in the events surrounding any investigations in the city.
