Tensions at Loyalty Castle

Session 3·2025-03-25

A provisional magistrate of the Unicorn had not heard that a battle had been fought on his lord's border. The war is not being reported to the people it is happening to.

A Lion Clan shugenja came to Shiba Midori — who won the shugenja competition at the Topaz Championship, and is therefore worth asking — with a problem of observance: the people are not tending the kami properly and she wants that fixed. Shiba Midori answered in the register of a campaign. If it is urgent, treat it as urgent: assign it, resource it, work it. She proposed exchanges between the clans' shrines, and proposed reframing the tending of a shrine as a tribute owed to victorious ancestors rather than a chore of piety. The Lion had not expected a Phoenix to talk that way, and the reframing landed, because it is a Lion argument made about a Phoenix subject.

In the courtyard the other conversation was going worse. Lion Clan samurai had Bayushi Monban with blades out. A Monk walked into it with the Dragon teaching — neither shall I — and offered to take one blow for every blow they gave, which put the Lion in a position where continuing meant beating a monk who had done nothing to anybody. Shiba Midori stepped in beside him. The samurai stalled on the arithmetic. The code that had them drawing swords was the same code that would not let them swing, and there is nothing in it that resolves the case.

Then Shinjo Mono arrived: young, swarthy, foreign blood in him, curly hair pulled to a topknot, a thin trimmed beard, and a provisional magistrate's authority. Doji Setsuna rode Kogarashi across his line of sight to be noticed, and was. She asked whose man he was and got an answer worth the ride — a Shinjo, and the uncle of Shinjo Harunobu. She observed that his lord holds border security for a stretch of country that has just had a battle fought across it. Shinjo Mono had not heard about the battle.

He asked about the horse. She told him it was a wedding gift from her husband, Shinjo Harunobu, and advised him to be careful what he asked and of whom. Then the party turned for the Castle of the Swift Sword.

Setsuna

Shinjo Mono's lord is Shinjo Harunobu's uncle. That is the first connection to her husband's family she has met in person rather than written to, and he is an Emerald Magistrate's provisional — Imperial service, not clan service, which means he can be spoken to without it being a Crane approaching a Unicorn.

He also did not know a battle had been fought on his own lord's border. Word is not reaching the Unicorn officers on the ground. When she asks the Unicorn for anything about the prisoners, she should assume they may not know, rather than that they are refusing.
