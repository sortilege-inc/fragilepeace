## Narrative

!lede The peace was signed and the hostages went home, and then the road south turned out
to run through a city whose river had been howling for five nights.

The last argument in the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] camp was about the road. Every onward journey
goes through the [[City Of The Rich Frog]] first, and past it the choices narrow: the
[[Drowned Merchant River]] and weeks of sailing, or the [[Spine of the World]] and then
the River of Gold — which is the heart of [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] lands. The company
has a Scorpion. He would not answer the question.

They pushed for a long time. [[Shiba Midori]] said she was not asking him to disarm — a
Scorpion's lies are his weapons, and he was welcome to use one — she wanted only an answer
where there had been a silence. *They cannot help you with problems they do not know
about.* What came out in the end was that most Scorpions do not like [[Bayushi Monban]].
Every favour his clan has asked of him since he took the Emerald badge has been refused,
because an Emerald Magistrate's reputation must be untarnished; what he understood as
declining to incur debts, his clan understood as a man who takes credit and never settles.
His own lord does not get on with him. Beiden Pass, which is the short way into
[[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] lands and the way any sane party would take, he would not use:
he could not guarantee their safety through it and could not guarantee his own. Nobody at that table
missed what it costs a man to say so out loud.

He came to breakfast the next morning having not slept at all. The mask was a red rag
knotted at the back of his head, not the lacquered one. He was eating rice, which none of
them had ever seen him do, and getting most of it on the rag. He flinched at every noise
in the camp. No check was required to see it.

He apologised first, formally, to everyone: for abandoning them during the negotiations,
for the fortnight he could not account for, and for the state of himself. Then he put the
rice down and looked at [[Shiba Midori]]. *Big sister. My worst fear has come to pass.*
The letter he handed to [[Doji Setsuna]] had been crumpled and rained on and carried a
long way.

She tried to read him and could not. He told her instead, which is the harder thing.
What he wanted, put plainly and without ornament, was his betrothed: to marry her, to have
children with her, to leave the Empire with her. Everything else was secondary. He had
done what most samurai only wish they could do, he had survived enough pain to earn it,
and she was lost.

The letter is from Lord [[Hida Katsuro]], a commander under the [[Crab Clan|Crab]]
Champion, and [[Hida Chizuzu]]'s father. [[Bayushi Monban]] had written to him weeks ago
telling him not to send his daughter over the [[The Wall|Wall]] for any reason. The
warning arrived days after she had already gone. What came back is a father's letter
wearing a lord's clothes: *how did you know, what do you know, why did you not
tell me, why were you not here to stop it.* Her whole unit has not returned.

[[Doji Setsuna]] knew the name before she finished the page, for a reason nobody in the
tent expected. [[Hida Katsuro]] is the commander [[Shinjō Harunobu]] trained under when he
came of age in the [[Crab Clan|Crab]] lands. *You have a very blessed pain, Monban. I have
only ever read of pain like yours in the books of [[Hana No Ame]].* She could not help him
reach his beloved; what she could do was tell her husband to, and she said she would —
assuming her husband could be got out of [[Castle Of The Swift Sword]] first.

He had no sword, no chop, and no badge. The badge is a jade medallion with the Emerald
chrysanthemum cut into it, which is also a Finger of Jade, and his is at a river shrine in
the west where a fox traded it for the water that kept him alive. It is not retrievable
and it is not in the hands of anyone who would misuse it. So she took off her own and
handed it to him: she rides back to [[Otosan Uchi]] with the Imperial Herald, she does not
need proof of her authority on that road, and she will have it replaced when she arrives.

He is a large man and he got visibly smaller taking it. He has now lost the gift his
Emperor gave him, the blade his lord gave him, the blade the [[Crane Clan|Crane]] gave him
— that one to another Scorpion — and his badge and chop both, and the only thing left to
lose was the right to be trusted with somebody else's. What he did not say, and what the
rest of the table did not hear, is that he holds himself responsible for
[[Miya Misato]]'s death and believes the trust is misplaced.

*You love and trust deeply. You have a way about you that cuts to the quick. I cannot in
any way relate to what you experience. But I admire it, and I want to see you succeed.*

*I may not be worthy of such kindness.*

*Then make yourself worthy. That is my last command to you.*

He could not meet anyone's eye after that, and [[Doji Setsuna]] took herself into the next
room, on the grounds that her staying would aggravate rather than assist.

What he told [[Shiba Midori]] alone was the size of it. His Champion still gives him the
benefit of the doubt. His lord keeps finding new ways to make his life difficult. The
Imperial advisor is as high as trouble goes. And if [[Hida Chizuzu]] is as lost as he
believes, then the [[Crab Clan|Crab]] — the people who have done more for him than his own
clan ever did — become the last allies he has and the next ones to turn on him. They will
extend him the benefit of the doubt as far as a castle by the [[The Wall|Carpenter's
Wall]], and after that it is the general's call.

[[Shiba Midori]] did not offer comfort. She argued. If he cannot marry his betrothed he is
nothing to her, and if he cannot fight for her she has nothing; going south and standing
between her and the first enemy is the only shape his devotion can take that is worth
anything. He answered with the [[The Wall|Wall]]: that he had stood there with her when
there was nothing left to stand for, that the great liar had tried them both and neither
had been deceived, and that if she stood with him again they would not be deceived again.
She took a flash of the mask and the eye and what she had been wearing when she wore them,
and paid for it. He wept, and thanked her, and called it counsel. *I only brought wisdom.
But I will accept your compliment.*

She had been doctoring the camp. [[Akodo Akihito]] is still hurt and still pleasant about
it; [[Doji Setsuna]] is completely healed with no outstanding wounds. [[Shiba Midori]]'s
own major wound cannot be treated by the person who has it, and the offer of
[[Battlemaiden Castle]] on the way past was declined as well-meant and not good enough.

Neither of them noticed the young man at the tent door, a plain black cloth over the lower
half of his face, and neither of them knows how long he had been standing there.
[[Bayushi Monban]] saw him first. [[Shiba Midori]] recognised him: [[Shun]], with
[[Akodo Akihito]]'s regards, and a messenger who had ridden the fastest horse she could
find and gone looking for the armies because that is where the [[Emerald Magistrates]]
would be. The letter she carried bears the seal of [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]], and it asks
for [[Shiba Midori]] by name.

The [[City Of The Rich Frog]] is being haunted by a [[Mayoibune|mayoibune]]. It appeared at the
[[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] docks on the first night, and a crew of Unicorn samurai went
aboard and came back reporting a ship with nobody on it and wailing coming up through the
deck from below; they did not stay to find out what was making it. On the second night it
was in the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] canals, and the Lion soldiers who went aboard did not come
back. On the third night it was at the [[Dragon Clan|Dragon]] docks, and
[[Tonbo Kuma]] went aboard, and the ship and the shugenja both went with the morning
light. It vanishes at dawn and returns about an hour after Lord Moon's light touches the
water, and it is somewhere different every time.

Every ship that can leave has left. Trade has stopped. A city between three clans is
paralysed, and the governor has written to the only shugenja he can think of who is equal
to it. Folded in with the letter is a written pass: the Emerald Magistrates and
[[Shiba Midori]]'s accompaniment may use the Imperial roads at the fastest pace available,
with horses, which ordinarily requires permission nobody gets, and may requisition mounts
from the Unicorn to do it.

[[Bayushi Monban]] asked to see [[Akodo Akihito]] before the camp broke, and [[Shun]]
arranged it. [[Shinjō Miharu]] was in the tent, about to be walked to the armoury to
collect her gear, and she looked as though she were going to say something to her captor
and thought better of it. Then they were alone.

*She seems a fan of yours, my brother.* — *She put a two-inch dent in my kabuto and very
nearly cost me that side of the battle.* — *As I said. A fan.*

[[Akodo Akihito]] means to find out who set Emerald Magistrate against Emerald Magistrate
and make his displeasure known. [[Bayushi Monban]] told him not to: [[Akodo Kage]] sits
very high in the Emperor's favour, and saying anything against him is a way to end a
career. He got the account of the duel he had been refused twice. [[Kakita Kazumi]] was
asked to present the reconstructed writ; he did it as well as it can be done; a room full
of [[Lion Clan|Lion]] generals heard a very convenient story with no ambassador, no letter
and no seal behind it; and [[Akodo Kage]] said out loud what every one of them was
thinking. [[Kakita Kazumi]] took it as a challenge. [[Akodo Akihito]], being the celebrated
hero of that hour, was the one given to answer it.

Then [[Bayushi Monban]] opened his collar and showed him the wound the
[[Phoenix Clan|Phoenix]] lands left on him, which is the only explanation he has for
vanishing. [[Akodo Akihito]] had asked for men to be sent looking and was deployed before
they reported. *We are family, you and I. Family, and first-enemy blood. This may be the
last time you see me.* Their niece's borrowed katana goes back to her when he reaches
Yasuki lands and can buy his own; he is very proud of her; if he does not come back he
hopes great things for her.

The map came out of the desk — the whole of Rokugan, stylised, with their camp on it, and
three lines drawn off it with a finger. The conventional road runs back to the
[[City Of The Rich Frog]], down the [[Drowned Merchant River]] past
[[Castle Of The Swift Sword]], and takes ship across the sea from there: weeks, and more
likely months, and useless to a man whose betrothed is beyond the [[The Wall|Wall]] today.
The fast road crosses the [[Spine of the World]] and goes down the River of Gold — passage
to be bought at Ryōkō Owari Toshi, the City of Lies, or taken off somebody if it cannot be
bought — and comes out at the Port That Never Sleeps in Yasuki country, a short crossing
from the [[Crab Clan|Crab]]. A few days' sailing at most. It is also the length of
[[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] lands, and it would put a hated Scorpion and four foreign
magistrates down the middle of them. The third road crosses the Spine further east and
rides through [[Shinomen Forest]]: the most direct of the three and the most treacherous.

What [[Bayushi Monban]] had wanted was a long arc west and south, keeping east of Beiden
Pass the whole way: down through [[Crane Clan|Crane]] country to Kyūden Ashinagabachi, the
Castle of the Wasps, and then — if [[Kakita Kazumi]] wished it — on to Kyūden Doji. That
is not a route so much as an admission, since it is twice the distance and avoids every
gate he has a right to walk through. It also ends where Winter Court sits this year, and
every one of them is already invited, so he had best get used to the idea.

[[Akodo Akihito]] reached over and took his mask off.

*Wear not your mask. Change your clothes. Do not answer to your name. Sail down the River
of Gold. Walk right through your enemies' front gate without them knowing you.*

That is a passage out of *Lies*, quoted to a Bayushi by a Lion. [[Bayushi Monban]] patted
him on the good side of his face, opened his shirt, and showed him the other thing nobody
has seen: the tattoos of a [[Kuni Wardens|Kuni Warden]]. Nobody in [[Scorpion
Clan|Scorpion]] lands is looking for a Kuni Warden. The height and the mask and the
mannerisms are what make him recognisable, and two of those three can be put down. They
would resupply at the [[City Of The Rich Frog]] and again at Shiro Usagi, the Castle of
the Hare, hire a Falcon guide for the mountain crossing — the slowest part of it — and
arrange a boat waiting on the far side. A small party can use that pass. An army cannot.

*When did you get so wise? Did the Kakita's last strike knock the noodles into you?*

*It taught me one thing: that I am not fast enough.* Their grandmother had told them both;
[[Kakita Kazumi]] took the lesson better. [[Akodo Akihito]] had tried to answer speed with
power, and would have worn him down given the chance, and was not given the chance. He
does not intend to make the mistake twice. There are manuals in the
[[Emerald Magistrates|Emerald Magistrate]] libraries; a lighter weapon might serve; so
might learning to dance. The next time they cross blades he means to take a piece of him
back.

[[Bayushi Monban]] bowed, put the mask back on, lifted it again to stick his tongue out,
and left. Outside, he leaned in to [[Shun]] and said one thing: *protect him. From
himself.*

The ceremony was at noon. Five [[Lion Clan|Lion]] and four [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] stood
up and were formally released on their clans' honour to appear at Winter Court, which is
parole in everything but the word. [[Akodo Akihito]] gave [[Shinjō Miharu]] her horse, her
armour and her weapons back and let her walk to the Unicorn lines. Five Unicorn horses
whose riders will not be claiming them stayed with the Lion, which was read as a courtesy
and meant as one.

The fifth Unicorn was not there to be released. [[Shinjō Harunobu]] is at
[[Castle Of The Swift Sword]] because his wife asked for him to be moved away from the
other four, and the Lion must now go and fetch him. [[Akodo Akihito]] put an Emerald
Magistrate's green over his armour so as to stop being an enemy combatant on that ground,
and so did his escort, and he rode up to [[Doji Setsuna]] with his lord's orders: he will
carry word of the release to [[Castle Of The Swift Sword]] and bring her husband back to
her at the [[City Of The Rich Frog]]. She thanked him and said the rest was better said
face to face. He said he would have word to him within a few days if the river crossing
was kind, and went.

The Lion army marched out demobilised behind him. The magistrates took the Imperial roads
at a pace the pass permits and the law normally does not. [[Kogarashi]] was pleased with
himself. [[Bayushi Monban]] had the black mare he found in the camp on the way back, and
was willing to put [[Ryu]] and [[So]] up behind them until it was explained at length that
putting a peasant on a samurai's horse raises the peasant and lowers the samurai in the
most literal sense available, at which point he gave up and let the baggage train have
them.

They came into the [[City Of The Rich Frog]] from the Unicorn side on the afternoon of the
second day, five days after the first apparition.

[[Bayushi Monban]] is still barred from [[Central Island]] and did not ask for the order to
be lifted. He went to [[The Docks|the Dragon docks]] instead, where [[Tonbo Kuma]] was last
seen. In the middle of the afternoon, on quays that were thronged a fortnight ago, nothing
was moving. The boats are all tied up and nobody will go near them. Everything is shut.
It has the quiet of a city expecting a siege.

He found a ladder and put his hand in the river, and what came up his arm was the sound of
[[Gaki-dō]] — the howling of the hungry dead, close enough to be in the ear, and a shape at
the edge of vision that was gone when he turned his head. The wound the Phoenix lands gave
him burned while he listened. It is not one bad place in the water. The whole river has
been flooded with something, and the balance of the elements in it is wrong.

He filled a jug, stoppered it, wrapped it and sent it across to [[Shiba Midori]] by one of
the governor's rōnin, with orders: nobody in the water, nobody on the docks, nobody aboard
anything until the governor says otherwise, himself included; any ship that tries to make
fast is turned away whether or not it appears to have a crew; and anything that comes out
of the river is an enemy of the Empire, to be killed and burned where it stands. The rōnin
asked whether they should post guards. Yes. Then he walked the waterfront filling ten or
twelve more jugs and marking each with where it was drawn, because the howling does not
stop anywhere along the length of the city — it only gets louder the closer he gets to
[[Central Island]], and to the governor's palace on it.

He had been thrown into this river once. It was not like this.

[[Doji Setsuna]] went to [[Central Island]] instead, with [[Miya Misato]]'s remains and a
servant to carry them. A pair of the governor's rōnin found her a boat; there are no
bridges in the [[City Of The Rich Frog]], which is why there are ferries.
[[Governor Miya Tetsuya]] was not holding court. He received her in a tea room, and the
woman [[Doji Setsuna]] is fairly sure he is conducting an affair with passed her on the
stairs up through the willow looking sombre and was not with him. He looked up and asked
the only question he had: *did you bring the shugenja?*

She said [[Shiba Midori]] was at the docks and would report as soon as she could. Then she
said the other thing, as gently as it can be said: that his niece succeeded in making a
peace on the Emperor's behalf, that the people who wanted her dead kept following and in
the end succeeded, and that her remains were in the room. His face did not crack. He asked
to be told, at some point, how it had come to pass, and whether her assassins had been
brought to justice — *not just the men wielding the knife, but the men who ordered it.*
That, [[Doji Setsuna]] said, is a conversation to have with [[Kakita Kazumi]].

*She succeeded in doing the Emperor's will. That is all we can ask of anyone.* And then,
of the woman who had counselled her: *it is a credit to you. When this is done, you should
be honoured for it.*

There is a go board in the corner of that room with a game on it, untouched since it was
interrupted. It is his standing game with [[Tonbo Kuma]], and the two of them were to have continued
it today. [[Doji Setsuna]] does not play go. She asked leave to look at it anyway, and read
the position: [[Tonbo Kuma]]'s group has one eye, which is not life, and it is their turn.
She put the second eye in. The group cannot be killed now.

*I expected that to be his next move when we rejoin.* He is not a spiritual man and said
so. He had been trying, and failing, not to read the board as an omen — to not feel that
the safety of those stones has something to do with the safety of the man who owns them,
wherever he is. She said she hoped he would be proved right.

## Setsuna

**She gave away her badge.** It is the single most consequential thing she did this
session and she did it in about four seconds, on reasoning that is entirely sound and
entirely beside the point: she travels to [[Otosan Uchi]] with an Imperial Herald, nobody
on that road will ask her to prove anything, and the office will issue another. All true.
What she actually did was put the Emerald chrysanthemum into the hands of the most
compromised magistrate in the Empire, who is about to take off his mask, stop answering to
his name, and walk south under a name that is not his. If he uses it, he uses it as her.
If it is taken off him a second time, it is hers that was taken.

She should be honest that it was not calculation. It was the thing to do for a man who had
just lost everything a samurai is issued, and she did it without weighing it, which is new.
[[Doji Setsuna]] does not usually hand over instruments.

**He told her his ninjō and she should treat that as a debt, not a discovery.** She reached
for it and missed; what she has, she has because he chose to put it in her hand while he
was at the end of himself. Hida Chizuzu, children, and a life outside the Empire — that is
the whole of him, and it is a lever anyone could pull. The court habit is to file it. The
better instinct, and the one she actually showed, is that a thing given at that moment is
given in trust, and using it is a different act from having learned it.

**Her husband's name went out the door ahead of him.** She promised
[[Bayushi Monban]] that [[Shinjō Harunobu]] would help, on the strength of a connection
neither of them knew existed an hour earlier — [[Hida Katsuro]] taught her husband
soldiering, and [[Hida Katsuro]]'s daughter is the woman beyond the [[The Wall|Wall]]. It
is an excellent piece of leverage and she spent it without asking the man whose name it
is. He is still a hostage, he is being fetched by a Lion, and the first thing waiting for
him at the [[City Of The Rich Frog]] is a commitment his wife made on his behalf to march
towards the Shadowlands for a Scorpion.

**There is something under her treaty that she heard for the first time this morning.**
[[The Ceasefire]] contains the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]'s acknowledgement that the
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] conducted themselves throughout as righteous, honourable and correct.
[[Bayushi Monban]] said, in passing, that he and [[Kakita Kazumi]] and a Lion had stood at
a watchtower where the dead got up for want of funeral rites. That clause was not a
throwaway — it was one of the things the Lion were paid in — and this is the first
[[Doji Setsuna]] has heard that there is anything behind it.

The part to sit with is that [[Kakita Kazumi]] was there and did not raise it. He had the
table, he had the standing, and he let the clause go by. It is not proof of anything and
nothing turns on it yet; a watchtower is not an army, and the Lion's conduct was never
going to be relitigated at that tent. But it is now a thing that can be produced at Winter
Court by whoever finds it useful, and it will be produced by a Scorpion if it is produced
by anybody. She should know why her colleague stayed quiet before someone else asks him.

**She is the only person in that city with nothing to bring to what is in the river.**
[[Shiba Midori]] is the one the governor sent for by name. [[Bayushi Monban]] can put his
hand in the water and hear [[Gaki-dō]]. [[Doji Setsuna]] can play go — and, notably, could
not even do that, and did it anyway. Her whole instrument is people: what they will admit,
what they will concede, what they will sign. A ship with no crew on it will not negotiate
and does not care what she is titled. That is worth sitting with, because every other
problem this campaign has handed her has been a problem with a person behind it.

And she did one thing at that go board that was not strategy. She told a man his niece was
dead, took the blow he would not show, and then closed his friend's group so the stones
could not be killed — which does nothing for [[Tonbo Kuma]], who is somewhere aboard a
ship that is not there in daylight, and everything for the man who has to look at the board
until he comes back. [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]] handed her the reading himself and called it
irrational. She did not correct him. It is the kindest thing she has done in the whole
record, and there was no advantage in it at all.

Her binding to [[Miya Misato]] was dissolved last session. She spent this one issuing one
of her own — *that is my last command to you* — to a man who is not hers to command, who
accepted it, and who has now ridden off wearing her authority on a string round his neck.
