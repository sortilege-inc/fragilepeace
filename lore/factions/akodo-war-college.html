Akodo War College

Lion

The Akodo War College is a military institution associated with the Lion Clan, dedicated to training samurai in warfare. Notable members include Akuyaku, Kitsu Somalia, and Torunako, who significantly contribute to military operations and strategy. The college emphasizes practical battlefield knowledge, fostering leaders capable of adapting to various combat scenarios. Its influence is particularly notable during military campaigns, where graduates apply their training in real-world conflicts.

Recently, during a military council led by Matsu Sakura, Akuyaku successfully delayed an early morning attack against the Unicorn Clan by providing critical reconnaissance. His reports revealed extensive fortifications in the Unicorn Clan war camp, including watchtowers and archers, and highlighted the boggy terrain that would complicate movement for both armies. The council deliberated on a cautious approach to gather more intelligence before engaging, with Kitsu Somalia advocating for this strategy. Ultimately, a consensus was reached to proceed with a battle plan allowing Akuyaku to infiltrate the Unicorn Clan camp during the battle, leveraging the distraction to gather crucial information. Akuyaku was authorized to commence his infiltration efforts as the Lion Clan army advanced.
