The previous Champion of the [[Lion Clan]] and [[Akodo Toturi]]'s elder brother. He
arranged the betrothal that began this war — and to clear the way for it he **ordered one
of his own daimyō to set aside and disgrace his wife**, [[Asako Ikari]], the mother of his
child. He is dead: killed by [[Doji Hotaru]]'s arrow at the gates of [[Toshi Ranbo]], with
[[Akodo Toturi]] and [[Matsu Tsuko]] watching. Beyond any justice a magistrate could
demand, and his shame falls on the living. Not in the Archivist export.
