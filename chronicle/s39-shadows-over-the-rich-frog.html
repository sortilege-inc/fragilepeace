Shadows Over the Rich Frog

Session 39·2026-02-17

The spirit agreed to stay inside Bayushi Monban a while, on one condition — that they get off this water-logged island at once. Then the governor sealed the gates for the night.

Governor Miya Tetsuya moved Miya Misato without telling anyone, into the deepest part of the castle they had. Shiba Midori brought Doji Setsuna the news, and the fact that the threats have not stopped.

The party planned. A palanquin for Doji Setsuna on the road; speed; staying together.

Ikoma Tadayoshi kept doing what he had been doing all week — spreading the story of a Crane conspiracy against the Miya while listening to what came back — and then turned it into a proposal. Let him ask Governor Miya Tetsuya for leave to escort Miya Misato west, publicly, as bait: anyone still hunting her will have to come at a moving target. The governor agreed, and attached a condition — Ikoma Tadayoshi is to keep an eye on the Emerald Magistrates themselves, in case their methods put the envoy in more danger than the assassins do.

By the water in the garden, Shiba Midori found Bayushi Monban anxious. He wanted to run his containment plan again. She told him it will not work: the old boxes are spent, it needs new Meishōdō crafted boxes and a Crane shugenja to do it properly.

Doji Setsuna talked her way through the governor's residence and into the guarded room where they were hiding Miya Misato. And then, having got in, she spent the time teaching — how to build an argument, how to concede a point without losing it, how a room is worked.

Out in the garden Ikoma Tadayoshi played his flute and picked up something worth having: an unusual body of Lion Clan passing nearby, which could be an escort. Governor Miya Tetsuya called him in privately afterwards to warn him what the Lion will want for it.

That night Shiba Midori and Bayushi Monban settled the spirit. She undertook to carry it west to the Burning Sands and let it go where it belongs. The Ifrit accepted, on terms: it would stay inside Bayushi Monban for now, provided they left this water-logged castle immediately.

They could not. The governor had sealed the island for the night, and the major-domo said their audience would be in the morning.

So they split. Shiba Midori and Bayushi Monban would handle the spirit; Ikoma Tadayoshi would travel with Miya Misato; and Doji Setsuna sat with the envoy all night and kept coaching her. At first light Shiba Midori and Bayushi Monban went for horses and the west, to keep the bargain, and Ikoma Tadayoshi went to collect the magistrates for the road.

Setsuna

She spent the night teaching. She got past the guards into the room where the Empire had hidden its envoy, and then used the access to coach a young woman in how to argue — not to guard her, not to interrogate her, and not to look at whatever is sealed against her skin. That is the most useful thing anyone did this week and it is the least dramatic. Miya Misato is going to a war with a writ and no experience. What she needs is not another sword.

The spirit is inside her yojimbo and the gates are shut. Bayushi Monban agreed a bargain on the Ifrit's terms — carry it west, free it in the Burning Sands — and the one condition was leave now. Then Governor Miya Tetsuya sealed the island until morning. So the party spends a night inside a locked castle with a fire spirit lodged in the man who has already proved that touching its box makes him irritable and hungry for fire.

If it turns, it turns here, with the envoy in the building.

Ikoma Tadayoshi has an official warrant to watch them now. The governor gave him the escort and told him to keep an eye on the magistrates' own methods. That is not paranoia on the governor's part — it is Bayushi Monban using Cackling Laughter on a room of guests, Kakita Kazumi burgling the party, a poison bag turning up above a Crane bed. They have been assessed and found to be a risk to the person they are protecting, and the assessment is fair.

And the Lion escort has a price. Governor Miya Tetsuya warned him about it privately rather than in front of everyone, which tells her the price is political rather than material. An unusual Lion contingent conveniently near the road west, while she carries an Imperial writ ordering the Lion to stop fighting — she should establish who those troops answer to before the envoy is inside their column.

Her own road is set now: west with Miya Misato, in a palanquin, toward the front where Shinjo Harunobu is held. The giri and the ninjō have finally converged and both of them run through the same gate.
