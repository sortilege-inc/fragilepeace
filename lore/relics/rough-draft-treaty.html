Rough Draft Treaty

The Rough Draft Treaty is a significant document related to the historical conflict between the lion and unicorn clans. It has ties to the Scorpion clan, which possessed the treaty and associated histories for 300 years. The treaty is considered key evidence in arguments, suggesting it includes agreements where the unicorn owed the Scorpion diamond mines due to their alliance during the Battle of Snow Plains. The authenticity of the treaty is disputed, with claims of a forged version presented alongside an authentic one found in a jar.

Advocates use the Rough Draft Treaty to support claims made by the unicorn and Scorpion clans. Bayushi Monban contends that the Scorpion's involvement was essential for the unicorn's victory, citing the treaty as evidence of an obligation the unicorn must fulfill. The treaty exemplifies the complexities of clan interactions and the consequences of historical actions, with its authenticity and implications being central to ongoing legal discussions.
