Matsu Morozane

Lion

Open the sheet & play

Matsu Morozane, a decorated general of the Lion Clan, stands as a formidable warrior, commanding respect and authority amidst the complexities of military strategy against the Unicorn Clan. His imposing stature and unwavering commitment to the martial traditions of his clan have earned him the role of a natural leader, tasked with the critical responsibility of commanding a unit in the impending battle. During a pivotal war council, he reported on the fortified position of the Unicorn Clan, complete with watchtowers and archers, expressing disdain for their decision to leave many horses behind, which elicited laughter from his fellow commanders. Advocating for an aggressive approach, he urged his comrades to "seize reality by the claws," pushing for immediate engagement to test the enemy's resilience.

As the conflict unfolded, Matsu led the left cohort of the Lion Clan, strategically positioned between rice paddies and a central hill. Commanding a unit of Yari Ashigaru, he initiated a Lightning Raid that successfully targeted the enemy, causing panic and revealing the positions of the Unicorn Clan leaders. Despite facing counterattacks that threatened his forces, Matsu maintained discipline among his troops. In a moment of crisis, he invoked the Touchstone of Courage, dispelling panic and bolstering their resolve in the heat of battle, significantly influencing the engagement's dynamics.

Matsu is also recognized for his tactical acumen in mountainous terrain, having selected a unit of mountain-born Ashigaru spearmen for a flanking approach through the mountains. He navigated a disused mountain path to the abandoned Old Diamond Mines, discovering concealed tracks indicating recent use. During an engagement, his cohort crossed a frozen river without casualties, unlike other units. He detached a mountaineer scout element to investigate the Old Diamond Mines while the main force continued its descent. In battle, Matsu executed another Lightning Raid against a Unicorn Clan shugenja-led cohort, causing further panic and inflicting damage.

In a recent confrontation, Matsu faced a familiar Unicorn Clan officer, cleverly deflecting the challenge by unleashing his beast companion, Shiguro Chinmoku, to chase the officer's horse, creating disorder within the enemy ranks. Later, he confronted a Unicorn Clan shugenja wielding two flaming swords. Matsu attempted a Heart-Piercing Strike but failed against the shugenja's defense, suffering a devastating counterattack that left him severely wounded and on fire. Despite his injuries, he rallied himself, providing a brief opportunity for rescue as his troops faltered at the sight of their fallen leader. Kitsu Somalia, a Lion Clan shugenja commander, attempted to aid him with the Extinguish invocation, which initially failed; however, she later stabilized him using Path to Inner Peace and extinguished the flames. An avalanche then divided the battlefield, concluding the engagement with Matsu alive but significantly injured.

Marked by severe injuries, Matsu bears extensive bandaging across his chest, arms, and torso from burns and limps with the aid of a stick, a result of his recovery from the ordeal in the snow. His companion, Shiguro Chinmoku, also has a slight limp but has survived recent confrontations better than their foes. Rescued from the snow by Kitsu Yui, Matsu was treated by Kitsu Somalia before emerging from the Healer’s Tent. In a subsequent war council, he expressed his intent to join a raid on a nearby village, considered a more certain objective than reinforcing the Central Hill.

During the village engagement, Matsu challenged the Unicorn Clan commander Takun Norio to a duel, employing the Battle of the Mind technique to manipulate the duel's dynamics. He prepared to strike the commander's horse, resulting in the steed being spiked, allowing Shiguro Chinmoku to deliver a lethal attack that killed Takun Norio, briefly stabilizing Matsu's cohort amidst heavy casualties. Acknowledging the dire situation, Matsu used the Touchstone of Courage technique to instill discipline among his remaining forces, facilitating a more orderly withdrawal from the village after significant losses.

In addition to his battlefield prowess, Matsu Morozane is recognized for his mangled hand and actively participates in military strategy discussions. He proposes a feigned weakness strategy to lure the Unicorn Clan forces into a trap, despite previous tactical failures. Rallying his troops by invoking their fighting spirit, he suggests a coordinated strike against the enemy. In combat, he employs various maneuvers, including a Command-Void action to lead a breakthrough and a feint with his yari, ultimately closing in on the enemy commander Iuchi Gero. Matsu Morozane beheads Iuchi Gero, inflicting a significant blow to the Unicorn Clan command and causing panic among their ranks, although this victory comes at the cost of a loss of honor due to the brutal execution.

After the engagement, Matsu Morozane detaches with Matsu Hisoka to intercept Unicorn Clan cavalry moving toward Crystal Pass, attempting further assaults to dislodge the Unicorn Clan from their position. He remains focused on the broader campaign, contributing to discussions about troop morale and supply shortages, embodying the spirit and resilience of the Lion Clan. His illustrious military career is marked by extensive records detailing various battles, yet the accounts pertaining to the Battle of the Snow Plain focus predominantly on logistical challenges, such as weather, supply issues, and intelligence failures, rather than combat prowess. Following the battle, Matsu Morozane served as an instructor at the Akodo War College, demonstrating a commitment to military education and leadership within the Lion Clan.

Matsu Morozane's historical significance has been further highlighted through recent investigations into the Snow Plain dispute, prompting discussions among characters, including Shiba Midori, regarding discrepancies in the battle accounts, especially concerning the medic Akodo Kayamayako, suspected to be a Scorpion Clan infiltrator. Conversations have revealed a pattern of omission regarding Kamiyoko's contributions, acknowledged only by common soldiers, raising questions about the reliability of the historical narrative and Matsu Morozane's role within it.

Recently, Matsu, also known as Matsu Morozane, has been recognized for his courageous role in the Battle of Snow Plains, where he faced significant challenges, including poisonings, severe weather, and unexpected mountain obstacles. His forces managed to defeat four out of five enemy generals; however, Imperial intervention ultimately decided the outcome, halting the fighting and adjudicating the results, indicating that Matsu Morozane's efforts alone were insufficient for victory. During court proceedings, Matsu Morozane's testimony was referred to in the context of Scorpion assistance during the battle. Doji Setsuna, speaking for Matsu Morozane, claimed he saw no direct evidence of Scorpion involvement, a statement met with skepticism from advocates of the Unicorn clan, who argued that the Lion could have succeeded without Scorpion aid. Matsu Morozane's account has become a focal point for debates among court members regarding honor, tactics, and clan responsibilities in the conflict.
