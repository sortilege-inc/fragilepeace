## Narrative

!lede They left the bodies where they lay. [[Bayushi Monban]] would not take the heads and would not defile them; [[Kakita Kazumi]] argued the same conclusion from the other end, that heimin should handle the dead and the party should stay clean. Before they went he worked over the corpses with a long stick so as not to touch them, looking for tattoos and marks, and pulled the masks off. Under the leader's he found a strip of cloth carrying the [[Doji Family]] crest, and put it in his sleeve without announcing it. [[Bayushi Monban]] had heard enough of the attackers' taunts to be sure of one thing: they were not [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]]. Four had run. Two were dead.

[[Doji Setsuna]] rode for the [[Castle of the Swift Sword]] and reached the gate after dark. The badge opened it. A majordomo took her straight to a rear tea house, and there she met [[Kitsu Takeko]], the Lion daimyō, who gave her a short and correct ceremony and then let her talk.

She had been attacked on Lion roads while carrying an Imperial writ; the men were trained, uniformly dressed and wore no mon. She did not present this as her misfortune. She presented it as an insult to the Empire committed on [[Lion Clan]] ground, which makes the Lion's honour the injured party alongside the Emperor's, and then asked [[Kitsu Takeko]] for the strength to punish it and for help with her Imperial business. [[Kitsu Takeko]] gave her both: [[Akodo Atsushi]] of the [[Akodo War College]] was ordered to put men across the roads and find four samurai dressed as clan samurai and wearing no mon — *alive*. When [[Doji Setsuna]] asked to ride with him she was refused, on the grounds that she had her own obligations.

Then the tea resumed and the daimyō asked after [[Otosan Uchi]], and after her family. [[Doji Setsuna]] began the formal answer and did not finish it. *I was not able to protect my retinue and those that were with me. I am alone in this place with this huge burden of responsibilities.* Her husband is a prisoner of the clan whose tea she was drinking. [[Kitsu Takeko]] was kind about it, promised to make enquiries after him, and gave orders that her guest be properly kept. [[Doji Setsuna]] said nothing at all about [[Asako Taishi]], or about how badly he had wanted not to be looked at.

[[Akodo Atsushi]] came in near midnight with Lion cavalry, took the account, split his force between escorting the party and holding the ambush ground, and brought them in. [[Bayushi Monban]] demanded to see [[Doji Setsuna]] the moment they arrived and was told she was resting; she came out anyway to let him see she was whole. Then baths, and in the morning Lion healers — [[Bayushi Monban]]'s wounds first, everyone else's tiredness after — and breakfast with the advisor [[Kitsu Yayoi]], who talked about the war going well and the Battle of the [[Kanto Plains]] going better.

At the [[Ancestral Shrine]] a cold gust came in with them and put out several candles. The priests knew what that was: something in the shrine was angry. [[Shiba Midori]] agreed and could locate it. They toured the [[Akodo War College]] and met [[Kitsuko Ayoko]], a scholar who offered to help them look for whatever they were looking for, and in the library [[Doji Setsuna]] found her namesake — a woman who had died gloriously in battle, written down and kept.

Then they made offerings and named the ancestors they wanted, and something came: armoured, carrying a katana, wounded, and plainly holding a message it had not yet been able to give anyone. [[Shiba Midori]] read it as wanting to speak rather than wanting to harm. [[Kitsuko Ayoko]]'s eyes went gold, and she pulled at [[Shiba Midori]]'s sleeve and said she knew the name: [[Katsuki Yui]]. They agreed to call her back properly, later, and went to take afternoon tea with the daimyō.

## Learned

- Kitsu Takeko: The Lion daimyō at the Castle of the Swift Sword. She gave Setsuna a short correct tea and then acted on what she heard: forces on the roads, four mon-less samurai to be taken alive, Akodo Atsushi in command. She refused to let Setsuna ride along. She asked after Setsuna's family, took the answer kindly, and promised to make enquiries about Shinjo Harunobu. She is, so far, the most useful person in the Empire to Setsuna, and she is Lion.
- Akodo Atsushi: Of the Akodo War College; given the hunt for the ambushers. He came at midnight with cavalry, took the account cleanly, and split his force between escort and holding the ground rather than choosing one.
- Kitsu Yayoi: A Lion advisor at the castle. Talks freely at breakfast about the war and the Lion victory at the Kanto Plains.
- Kitsuko Ayoko: A scholar at the Akodo War College who offered her help unprompted. Her eyes turn gold, and when the specter appeared she knew its name without being told.
- Katsuki Yui: An armoured spirit with a katana, wounded, carrying a message it has not been able to deliver. Named by Kitsuko Ayoko. Something in the Ancestral Shrine is angry enough to put the candles out.
- Masked Assailants: Two dead, four fled. Monban is certain from their taunts that they were not Scorpion. Kitsu Takeko wants them alive.

## Setsuna

She got what she came into that tea house for, and she got it by framing. The ambush went to [[Kitsu Takeko]] not as a Crane's grievance or a magistrate's inconvenience but as an insult to the Empire committed on [[Lion Clan]] soil, which made the Lion co-injured and gave the daimyō a reason of her own to spend cavalry on it. It worked on a daimyō in the middle of a war, and it cost nothing.

She was refused the ride along. The hunt is [[Akodo Atsushi]]'s and whatever it turns up will reach her secondhand, if at all, and the standing order is to take them alive — which is what she needs and also what a Lion would want for their own reasons.

She let the formal answer break in front of a Lion daimyō when asked about her family, and said aloud that she is alone and carrying too much. It bought sympathy and a promise of enquiries about [[Shinjo Harunobu]]. It may also have shown [[Kitsu Takeko]] exactly where to press.

She kept [[Asako Taishi]] out of it entirely, including his agitation on the road. That is a live omission in front of the person hunting his attackers.

And [[Kakita Kazumi]] took a strip of cloth bearing her own family's crest off a dead assassin and did not show it to her.
