Lion Roads

Lion Roads are well-maintained thoroughfares that traverse the heart of Lion Clan territory, facilitating swift travel across the region. These roads are known for their direct routes connecting key locations, including Loyalty Castle and the Castle of the Swift Sword. The climate is characterized by hot summers, conducive to travel, and the area experiences a notable absence of banditry due to the Lion Clan's strict enforcement of order. The roads are typically lined with columns of Lion Clan soldiers, indicating a strong military presence.

Visitors may require a letter of passage to travel the Lion Roads. These provisions underscore the organized nature of travel within the territory and the watchful eyes of Lion Clan escorts, who monitor movements in the area.
