Faction

Crab Clan

The Crab Clan is a prominent faction in Rokugan, recognized for its martial prowess and commitment to defending the Empire from external threats, particularly those posed by the Shadowlands. They serve as the bulwark against evil, stationed along The Wall, a fortification that protects Rokugan from the horrors of the Shadowlands. Members of the Crab Clan are known for their strong sense of duty and loyalty, often prioritizing the safety of the Empire above all else. Their territory lies to the southwest of the Unicorn and Crane lands, bordering the Shadowlands, a constant source of danger.

Operating from the Castle of the Swift Sword, the Crab Clan is represented by notable figures such as Hida Kasudo and his brothers, Hida Nozomu and Hida Kenji. The Hida family embodies the clan's no-nonsense approach to warfare, characterized by their formidable physical presence, with Hida Kasudo's towering stature and the battle scars of his brothers serving as testament to their experience in combat. The clan's membership includes both warriors and scholars, such as Hida, a military scholar noted for observing court proceedings, reflecting the clan's commitment to understanding and documenting military history. The Crab Clan's influence extends into the political realm, where they are viewed as a stalwart force within the Emerald Magistrate system, committed to maintaining order and justice.

The clan's familial bonds and the burdens of duty embraced by their members are evident in significant events, such as Bayushi Monban's betrothal to a Crab, which brings both benefits and misfortunes. Tensions within the political landscape are highlighted during court proceedings surrounding the Scorpion Clan's claims and the legitimacy of various documents, including those related to the Battle of the Snow Plain. Hida Kasudo and his brothers actively participated in these discussions, witnessing Bayushi Monban's objections regarding the treatment of Scorpion evidence.

The Crab Clan's strategic positioning is further emphasized by Hida Kasudo's proposal regarding ownership of Diamond Mines, which offered an opportunity to align with the Scorpion Clan, contingent upon their pledge of non-interference in future Crane treaties. This maneuver reflects the Crab Clan's adeptness at navigating the complex interplay of alliances and rivalries while reinforcing their commitment to the Empire's stability and their clan's interests. Discussions among the Crab and other factions underscore the importance of justice and the challenges of balancing it, reflecting the weight of expectations that come with their lineage and commitments.

Recently, the Crab Clan has intensified their military preparations in response to increasing incursions from the Shadowlands, further solidifying their role as the Empire's first line of defense. Their involvement in political negotiations has also grown, as they seek to secure alliances that will bolster their strength against the encroaching darkness. In recent events, the Three Hida Brothers acted as recruiters, informing Bayushi Monban that a letter he received regarding his betrothed was likely a forgery, marked only with a Crab chop. They confirmed that his betrothed had been at The Wall, highlighting the clan's commitment to vigilance. Additionally, the party chose to travel west with the Hida brothers for protection and to potentially recruit Lion stragglers for The Wall. Doji Setsuna advised the Crab to treat unknown parties as adversaries, emphasizing their strategic focus on security and the protection of allies amid Rokugan's political complexities.

In a recent incident, the Emerald Magistrates, including Shiba Midori, a member of the Crab, faced an attack in the guest quarters at the City Of The Rich Frog. Following the incident, Shiba Midori reported that attackers had used her as a "weapon" and had tied up others, including the Miya’s Niece. The Emerald Magistrates agreed to investigate the assault as a courtesy to Miya Tetsuya, the Imperial Miya Tetsuya. The investigation revealed that the attackers had ransacked Midori's quarters and that a guard had been slain outside her door. As the inquiry progressed, the Emerald Magistrates discovered evidence of infiltration methods, including Bamboo Breathing Tubes hidden among reeds, suggesting a waterborne approach to the castle. The investigation continued with discussions among the magistrates and guests at a tea ceremony, where they sought to gather information discreetly while maintaining the appearance of normalcy.
