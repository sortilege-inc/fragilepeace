Ikoma Library

Lion

The Ikoma Library is a notable structure within the Kitsu Archives, known for its extensive collection of scrolls and documents significant to the Lion Clan's history. It serves as a center for scholarly pursuits, housing writings that include secret Scorpion Clan texts and records of treaties. The library is frequented by historians, scribes, and clan representatives who consult its resources for matters of political and historical importance. The environment is conducive to research, filled with the scent of parchment and ink, and provides spaces for private discussions and document crafting.

Recently, the library became a focal point for planning a judgment regarding the Snow Plain dispute between the Unicorn Clan and the Scorpion Clan. Notable figures such as Doji Setsuna, Bayushi Monban, and Kakita Kazumi utilized the library to review Ikoma Akuyaku’s journal, filled with secret writings. Meetings were arranged within its secluded corners for confidential discussions, including a collaboration between Kakita Kazumi and Soshi Yamako, a Scorpion Clan forger, to craft documents that could influence the dispute. The library facilitated these activities, allowing for the transcription of critical confessions and the preparation of persuasive arguments for the upcoming clan presentations.
