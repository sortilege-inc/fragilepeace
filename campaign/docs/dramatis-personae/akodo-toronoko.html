Dramatis Persona

Akodo Toronoko

Lion

What is known

S10Took a rival's argument apart with his own tactics at the first council, and later stopped Akuyaku saying something to the commander he could not take back. One of the three field commands taken forward across the bog.
S11Laid out the Lion order of battle — thin elite centre as bait, both flanks reinforced to close on the charge. Held the centre through both rounds and could not find a way to respond when the Unicorn refused the bait.
S12Broke the pincer on Somalia's cohort with Righteous Example and the centre's yari samurai. Talked Matsu Sakura out of pursuing the Unicorn withdrawal. Used Preserve Strength on the army that night, and dictated her account of the first day to Ikoma Hideri.
S13Ikoma Akuyaku's wife, and commander of the Lion left. Her own frostbite treatment failed; her triage direction is what made the medics' work possible. Took open ground for her archers, pinned the enemy leader on the fortified hill with a volley that otherwise bounced, and broke through with Righteous Example on the second attempt. Gave Akodo Kagekatsu nothing when he came for her.
S14Held open snow against a spearman charge by freezing the enemy leader in place and laying a curtain of arrows loosed high and flat together, so the Unicorn would not walk into it. Later took a bow herself under Righteous Example for five attrition and five panic.
S15Sent away from the council to cool off, and so absent from the decision she would have opposed. Killed Shinjō Gansari, one of the Unicorn's Five Pillars, in a duel through the blizzard — Flowing Water Strike, then a finishing blow at twice deadliness — and took his head.
S16Collapsed after the night's fighting with an enemy commander's head, a prisoner, and a third Pillar crippled to her name. Ordered out of the fighting for her own sake. Akodo Kagekatsu is her cousin.
S17Fought Shinjō Kamo to a standstill and was walking back at him when the heralds arrived. Somewhere in the same fighting she came within an inch of eviscerating her own husband.
S18Her account is proud, brief, and spends part of its length defending Ikoma Akuyaku to her dying day against the charge that he caused the supply disaster. Her only regret is that she did not kill all five Pillars herself. She is the only person in the archive who mentions Akuyaku at all, and she damns him with faint praise. Everything she wrote before the Snow Plain is in her own hand; the battle and everything after it are in another.
S19The common ancestor. Toturi, Sakuon and Akihito all descend from her; her second son with Ikoma Akuyaku married into the Scorpion, from which Monban and Kazumi descend, and her third son married into the Crane. She was blind for the rest of her life after the Snow Plain, and her husband wrote her records for her.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
