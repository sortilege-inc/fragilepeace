Hanto River

The Hanto River is a significant waterway in the lands of the Lion Clan, known for its role in military engagements, especially during the conflict with the Unicorn Clan. The river's banks have witnessed the capture of prisoners during battles, including Doji Setsuna's husband at the Battle of the Hanto River. It serves as a geographical marker within the ongoing Lion Clan-Unicorn Clan conflict, with the surrounding territories holding strategic importance for both clans.

The Hanto River is also referenced in a legal matter that Doji Setsuna, the newly appointed Emerald Magistrate, was tasked to adjudicate. This matter carries historical implications in the context of the war. The river's proximity to key locations such as the Drowned Merchant River and Loyalty Castle further emphasizes its importance in the strategic landscape of the Lion Clan's military operations.
