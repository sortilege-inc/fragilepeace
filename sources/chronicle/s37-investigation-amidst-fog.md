## Narrative

!lede The decoy plan worked exactly as designed, and four men died of it. [[Shiba Midori]] slept in the Imperial Envoy's room dressed as the Imperial Envoy. Men came through the door in the night, killed the guards outside it, and took her — and then one of them got a proper look at her face and said she was not the target. He said it less politely than that. They let her go. [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]] had moved his niece somewhere else and told his own men to guard a location without telling them why or that the move had happened, and the only people who might have worked it out were the guards who were killed.

[[Shiba Midori]] got back to her own room and found [[Miya Misato]] and [[Kitsu Ayoko]] tied up in it, and her things gone through.

[[Doji Setsuna]] slept through all of it. [[Shiba Midori]]'s sleeping draught worked; she was the only person in the castle who got a night's rest, and she came down to breakfast knowing nothing. Everyone else had been woken by alarm bells and guards opening every door in the guest wing and saying only *stay here, do not attract attention, the lord will inform you in the morning*.

At the table was somebody new. [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] has been the governor's guest for about a week, back from touring [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] lands studying music, and [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]] is a patron of the arts who has been enjoying his compositions. He has a reputation as a harbinger: things go wrong where he goes. He has trouble with women, which may or may not trace to promises he made as a child to certain shape-shifting spirits and did not keep. In the night he had seen the magistrates moving about — and thought, briefly, that he saw two of the governor's niece.

[[Bayushi Monban]] ate an enormous breakfast. [[Doji Setsuna]] gave him the whole of the olive branch she had in her: *I do not forbid you to travel with me.* He gave her a look back that said he was still angry. And he looked at the marks on [[Shiba Midori]] and asked why the pattern of bruises on her body was familiar to him — because he has the same ones. She said she had been sparring. *It was not my idea.*

Then [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]] came in with a dark look and asked [[Shiba Midori]] to explain how his niece had ended up where she did. *Considering I was used as a personal melee weapon, I am not sure what more you can ask of me, my lord.*

[[Doji Setsuna]] tried to keep her face out of it and failed. What she thinks is that this is the city magistrate's failure, and with nothing to show on the roll it was plain on her face, and the governor saw it. He gave it back later, in front of everyone: somebody has made a joke of his castle's security, and somebody seems to find it funny that men died protecting them while they slept in their sick bed. She went to full neutral and filed it.

The island is closed. No boats in, no boats out except those already under his orders, and the culprits are therefore still on it. Every member of staff is accounted for and being interviewed; he has suspects of his own to hand over; and if he has to lodge a formal request he will, though he had assumed this was simply the magistrates doing their duty. [[Doji Setsuna]] took it and drew the line carefully: this is a courtesy, not an elevated inquiry. Nobody of their status could have refused him anyway.

What [[Shiba Midori]] could give them was thin and useful. The attackers knew where to find the envoy's room. They did not know the envoy by sight. They were unwilling to kill an Emerald Magistrate, or an Imperial emissary, while being perfectly willing to kill four guards. Which means somebody is feeding information off this island — or the attackers are already employed on it.

To question a dozen guests without questioning them, [[Doji Setsuna]] proposed a high tea that afternoon. [[Shiba Midori]] would host it. [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] was asked to play, was embarrassed, and was talked into it on the grounds that an unfamiliar [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] melody would be beautiful to an audience unable to hear his mistakes. [[Doji Setsuna]] told [[Bayushi Monban]] to stay away from the guests. He suggested the alternative himself: search rooms while everyone is downstairs.

He found both of them on the roof of the [[Main Tower]]. On the west side of the tower, above the second-level room — an interior room with one door and no windows — one or two tiles picked off and broken, scuff marks, rope fibres wedged between two tiles, scratches from climbing claws too small to be his own, and the attic panel over the space removed and shoved aside. That is the way in. On the east side, tiles lifted and carefully put back to hide that they had been touched at all, with no claw marks anywhere near them. From there a person has overwatch of the guest quarters.

He told the guards about the west side. He said nothing about the east side, because he thinks it is [[Kakita Kazumi]] — who has been behaving strangely, who turned up at the docks with his hair dyed black and ran off, and who said this morning that he had been sparring. He went and found him and, in a conversation so elaborately about the weather that nobody nearby understood a word of it, asked him to come up on the roof.

On the roof he pointed at the east side and asked outright whether [[Kakita Kazumi]] had been up there in the night watching both rooms. [[Kakita Kazumi]] said no. He had been watching one.

They put the rest together between them. The fog was thick enough that nobody could have seen anything, and yet the attackers found the door they wanted. Two entry points, coordinated. The guards on the door were killed, so the door was the way in — and the roof was the way out, which means the entry and the exit were planned separately by people who had been up there before. [[Shiba Midori]] thinks one or several of the governor's guards are in it. [[Bayushi Monban]] thinks the attackers are not on the island at all, and could have swum.

So they went to the shore, [[Bayushi Monban]] on Riding the Clouds and giggling — *look, Kakita, I can fly* — and [[Kakita Kazumi]] on foot, putting himself in the mind of a man infiltrating from the water.

They found it. Buried under the reeds at the waterline, scraped by the rocks and hidden so carefully that only knowing what to look for turned them up: bamboo tubes about two feet long, watertight enough to breathe through under water. [[Kakita Kazumi]]'s reconstruction: come across at a busy hour, slip off a boat, get into the reeds, and lie there until well after midnight. Two groups. And the tubes are still here, uncollected, which means the men who used them have not left.

[[Kakita Kazumi]] asked [[Bayushi Monban]] to stand between him and the guards on the wall and look as though they were talking. Then he coated the insides of the tubes with a numbing poison, replaced everything exactly as it had been, and [[Bayushi Monban]] put the dirt back over them. Afterwards they wandered the shoreline looking foolish, in case anyone was watching from the water, so that it would seem they had come close and missed.

Somewhere in this [[Bayushi Monban]] said the thing neither of them usually says. *Something tells me you are no longer a duellist.* Both of them have died and come back. Neither remembers anything between the two, and both have noticed changes in themselves since.

At the tea there were a dozen guests. [[Daidoji Shin]] with [[Doji Kasami]]. [[Asahina Nao]]. [[Shinjo Higuchi]], still recovering in the castle. One [[Lion Clan|Lion]], two [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] — an [[Ide Family|Ide]] with an [[Utaku Family|Utaku]] — three [[Dragon Clan|Dragon]], another [[Phoenix Clan|Phoenix]], a [[Crab Clan|Crab]] pair including a Yasuki, and two [[Crane Clan|Crane]], both [[Doji Family|Doji]] and both cousins of [[Doji Setsuna]]'s. And not one [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] anywhere in the room.

[[Doji Setsuna]] played it well. She took [[Daidoji Shin]] into her confidence about the dishonourable attack on a fellow magistrate and invited his help — which makes him the obvious and visible investigator and leaves the magistrates free to work underneath him. [[Shiba Midori]] failed to get the room's attention to begin the ceremony at all, which turned out to be worth more than succeeding: nobody realised the ritual had started and everybody kept talking.

[[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] played a piece from the furthest edges of the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] lands. [[Doji Setsuna]] is fairly sure she has heard her husband hum it.

The Yasuki talked about travel with his eyes lit up, and then about his grievance: he is part of the far-ranging effort to keep [[The Wall]] supplied, and the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] are not letting his cargo down the river because they are pulling supplies toward the war. They are pressing him to sell to them instead. His ships cannot move and he came here hoping the governor would intervene.

And then the [[Ide Family|Ide]]. [[Daidoji Shin]] was already watching him, and nobody could say what had triggered it. He has proposed to [[Doji Setsuna]]'s cousin — a hatamoto who advises the family's investments and would make a superb merchant if being one were not beneath a samurai — that he export goods from the furthest reaches of [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] lands through [[Crane Clan|Crane]] trade networks, to the capital and beyond. He promised samples. His people have not produced them, which the hatamoto finds curious. He claims his networks are undisrupted by the conflict and that he has means of getting goods across the rivers that others do not have, which the hatamoto finds a dubious assertion.

Asked directly, he gave a different account: he is delayed because his associates must route caravans around a war zone that keeps expanding, and he is relying on unconventional means of moving material. Terms would be very favourable if peace were secured.

[[Doji Setsuna]] remarked she had never met a [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] so interested in other cultures' antiquities. He corrected her. The person he had in mind was a [[Crane Clan|Crane]] — one with an interest in curiosities from beyond the border, whose interest he could not account for, and whom he had met not long ago.

[[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] read him too, and what came back was that he carries himself with a self-possession beyond his years and possibly beyond his station. He may be of greater standing in his clan than he is letting on. Or he may simply be arrogant.

Then [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] played the Tea Seller's Song, which [[Shiba Midori]] has loved since childhood and even [[Doji Setsuna]] knows, and the room stopped talking. He spent the rest of the afternoon asking people about the night and watching for a reaction, and got nothing: everyone woke to bells and screaming, nobody was told anything, nobody slept, and not one mask slipped.

[[Kakita Kazumi]] and [[Bayushi Monban]] did not go back to wait for anyone to return for the tubes. They went to the rooms where the [[Crane Clan|Crane]] guests are staying, on a clue [[Bayushi Monban]] gave [[Kakita Kazumi]] several sessions ago. [[Bayushi Monban]] let Riding the Clouds burn off and walked behind him on his right, where a drawn blade would not reach him.

## Setsuna

**They came for the envoy and got the decoy, and the decoy worked.** [[Shiba Midori]] slept in [[Miya Misato]]'s place dressed as her, and the men who came through the door killed the guards, took the wrong woman, looked at her face and let her go. That is the plan functioning exactly as designed. Four men are dead of it, and [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]] said so in front of the room while looking at her.

He was not wrong and she should stop being surprised that he said it. The insult she could not keep off her face was that this is the city magistrate's failure. His answer was that four of his men died protecting a guest who slept through it. Both things are true. Only one of them was said out loud by someone with the standing to say it.

**What they did not know is the useful part.** They knew which room. They did not know the face. They were willing to kill four guards and unwilling to kill an Emerald Magistrate — which is a brief, not a temper. Somebody on this island told them where to look and could not tell them who to look for. The governor moved his niece and told nobody the reason; the only men positioned to notice the move are the ones who died.

It is also the second time a disciplined crew has taken [[Shiba Midori]] in the dark and then let her go once they had looked at her properly — the men at the docks in session thirty-one did the same, and two Emerald Magistrates have now been attacked and released by people who would rather withdraw than take the wrong body. Combined with the brief they were working to here, that is a method rather than a coincidence.

**And the tubes are still in the reeds.** Two feet of watertight bamboo, buried carefully enough that they were nearly missed, uncollected. Men who plan an entrance and a separate exit do not abandon their equipment. They are still here, and they intend to leave the way they came — which is why [[Kakita Kazumi]] put numbing poison inside the tubes and [[Bayushi Monban]] put the dirt back. Somebody is going to put one of those in his mouth.

That is a good trap and it is also a decision nobody brought to her. She is the one who told [[Kakita Kazumi]] to return a shuriken to its owner yesterday, so she is in no position to be shocked. But she should notice that her party now habitually decides who dies on the walk between one scene and the next.

**The [[Ide Family|Ide]] is the thread.** He wants [[Crane Clan|Crane]] trade networks to move goods out of the furthest [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] lands toward the capital and beyond. He promised samples and produced none. He told her cousin his routes are untouched by the war; he told her he is delayed and relying on unconventional means. Both cannot be true. A man with routes a war does not interrupt is either lying or smuggling, and [[Daidoji Shin]] was watching him before she was.

Then he handed her something without being asked. The person he knows with an appetite for curiosities from beyond the border is not a [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]. It is a [[Crane Clan|Crane]], and he does not know where the interest came from. There were four [[Crane Clan|Crane]] in that garden, two of them her own cousins, and [[Kakita Kazumi]] and [[Bayushi Monban]] walked off to search the [[Crane Clan|Crane]] guests' rooms on a hunch neither of them explained to her.

And the Yasuki said the quiet strategic thing while complaining about freight. The [[Lion Clan|Lion]] are stripping the river of supplies bound for [[The Wall]]. Whatever else this war is doing, it is being paid for out of the defence of the Empire's southern border, and a [[Crab Clan|Crab]] merchant sitting in a garden in the [[City Of The Rich Frog]] is the only person who has said so.

One last thing, and it is hers alone. The melody [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] played from the far edge of the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] lands is one she has heard [[Shinjo Harunobu]] hum. She was in a garden building a case against a [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] trader when she heard her husband's music played by a stranger, and she made a note to mention it politely afterwards, and then went back to work.
