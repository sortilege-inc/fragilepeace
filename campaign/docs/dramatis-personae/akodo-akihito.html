Dramatis Persona

Akodo Akihito

Lion

What is known

S2Eldest son of Akodo Sakura. Named to her for the first time here, as a lever she already half-possesses through her companions.
S19Akodo Sakuon's son, a hero of the recent battle, and holder of a Shinjō prisoner of his own. Kin to Toturi and Sakuon, connected to Monban and Kazumi, and — through Toronoko — kin to all of them.
S24Dreamed another man's memory of the battle, the Lion historian and the burial, asked for leave, and dug up a sealed jar in a hidden cave exactly where the dream put it. Sent the whole cache to Shiba Midori with a letter explaining he trusted neither Monban nor Kazumi nor a Crane with it. Refused the request to have Shinjō Harunobu released into Setsuna's household.
S44Akodo Toturi's nephew — which puts him close enough to the Lion succession to answer the insult by marriage. Holds a Unicorn prisoner. His castle is the column's destination.
—Took the duel because he could not refuse it in that room, and lost his left eye to it, permanently. Freshly titled Renowned Warrior, beaten in front of his uncle, his sensei and every Lion officer of consequence. Midori has been treating him.
S48One-eyed, doing paperwork under an embroidered patch, and gracious about it. Expects not to be chosen as the Lion delegate. Conceded that the Phoenix are owed and that the wronged woman is owed a debt of honour in her own right. Arranged for the women to have Miharu alone.
S50Speaks for the Lion, and is the lowest in standing of his own delegation, with Kage seated above and behind him. Refused the request to free Miharu in immaculately polite calligraphy of his own hand. Presented half an hour of Lion demands and meant them to be seen as reasonable — he is not trying to slip anything past the bench. His giri is to serve Toturi however told, but what he believes he is for is field command. He twitched when Ikari's treatment was enumerated.
S51Confirmed the Lion position that the broken engagement justified the incursion, and was asked in the same breath what redress the Phoenix and the Unicorn are owed for a groom who was not free to be offered. Afterwards accused Midori of arranging a second humiliation for him deliberately.
S52Came back after the recess in an entirely different register — the Lion razed nothing, answered no insult with insult, killed only warriors defending their homes, and he praised the Unicorn for their courage. He calls the war a duel writ large between clans rather than champions, presently undecided, and says this is the moment to step back before someone takes a blow they cannot answer. The Lion are prepared to settle. Someone has given him new orders.
S53Reframed the war as a duel writ large using a witticism he was misapplying. Dropped the whole posture the moment he was offered a peer to negotiate with instead of an arbiter to plead before — fresh quill, fresh linen, brisk. Conceded the borders, then the prisoners, then the horses. Withdrew the Lion's demand for redress on the marriage rather than have the arbiter add context to it. Staked ten honour on the agreement.
S54Broke the Unicorn attempt to turn the Lion flank at Ponto Pass and took their commander in single combat; she is his prisoner. Present at the signing with a fresh bandage over the eye he lost to Kakita Kazumi, and a repaired kabuto.
S55Quoted Lies to a Scorpion — unmask, change clothes, drop the name, sail the River of Gold through the enemy's front gate — and it is the plan Monban took away. Wants to know who set magistrate on magistrate and was warned off it. Read his own defeat honestly: he answered speed with power and was not given the time to make it work, and intends to fix it rather than resent it. He carries his lord's orders to release Shinjō Harunobu and deliver him to Setsuna at the City of the Rich Frog.
S58Carried the parole himself. Serves Akodo Toturi. Lost his left eye permanently to Kakita Kazumi and has resisted treatment for days; Shiba Midori got him to lightly wounded and then to the mend. Holds Shinjō Miharu to be a magnificent woman, wishes she had been his colleague at the Topaz Championship, and said the word duty with regret in it. Shares the title Renowned Warrior with Shinjō Harunobu.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
