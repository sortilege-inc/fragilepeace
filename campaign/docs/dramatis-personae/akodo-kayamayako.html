S12The Lion physic. Caught someone prowling at the general's tent in the night and could not hold them or see their face. Said plainly that both armies have spies in each other's camps and that the Unicorn would kill Lion commanders and shugenja if they could.
S13The chief medic. Called for a shugenja who could make gentle fire rather than destructive fire, warning that the full power of a fire kami would cost Akuyaku the foot. Confined him to camp on the grounds that a man who cannot feel a freezing limb will not notice it freezing.
S15Held a healer's tent that had stopped being a tent, taking in every spare pair of hands in the camp.
S18The medic. Kazumi searched the right era and the right section and found no testimony by that name at all, with Toronoko's and Kagekatsu's records sitting right there. Either she was removed from the archive or the name was never a person.
S19Thanked by name in the quartermaster's rosters for the lives she saved, and absent from three centuries of Akodo genealogy. Ikoma Ichigo could not locate her. Setsuna's suggestion is that she may be findable under a Scorpion name.
