Ikoma Hideri

Lion

What is known

S12The Lion historian on the field, in a scholar's robe and out of paper. Named the Unicorn withdrawal as a documented feint out of Kuni Kadokatsu's account of the Ridge of Bones, and said out loud that the pit traps were unlike anything the Unicorn do in open field and might not have been their commander's idea. Matsu Sakura told her to be brief.
S16Saw what nobody else saw — the calm on the Unicorn troops — and remembered that Iuchi Gero had worn talismans at the parley. That observation is what set the whole day's objective.
S18A well-regarded Lion historian with one failure on her name — that the Snow Plain went unrecorded. Everyone assumed she died in the battle. She survived it and was killed before she could finish writing it. Neither Matsu Sakura's account nor Akodo Toronoko's mentions her.
S25Her own record of the Battle of the Snow Plain was in the jar — chop, hand and phrasing all matched against library samples. Ikoma Ichigo intends to compose it into a form that can enter the Ikoma histories, which he says lifts a stain from his family's honour.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
