Unicorn Encampment

The Unicorn Encampment is located in a mountainous region with rugged terrain, recently covered by heavy snowfall that conceals hazardous ground features. This encampment serves as the operational base for the Unicorn Clan, who possess familiarity with the local terrain, although their usual markers are obscured by the snow. The encampment is fortified, and the Unicorn Clan exhibits strong morale and readiness for battle while awaiting the approach of the Lion Clan. The presence of their cavalry forces may be affected by the weather, resulting in a lower-than-expected deployment.

Following a significant snowstorm, the Lion Clan General planned a flanking maneuver through the mountains to engage the Unicorn Clan from the rear. Lion Clan scout Ikoma Ikoma Akuyaku returned from reconnaissance, reporting on the conditions within the Unicorn Encampment. He noted the Unicorn Clan's high spirits and combat readiness, with their numbers roughly equal to, or slightly larger than, those of the Lion Clan Army. The report highlighted the concealed terrain advantages known to the Unicorn Clan, now hidden by snow, and included a warning about severe cold impacting operations. As the Lion Clan forces prepared for engagement, Ikoma Akuyaku's observations informed their strategic deployment.
