Governor’s Palace

The Governor’s Palace is an ornate structure within the realm, featuring various rooms, including a kitchen and a Central Garden. It is located in a region experiencing late summer heat, which contrasts with the unusual behaviors of its inhabitants.

In the kitchen, a woman in a purple dress exhibits erratic behavior, such as feeding logs into the furnace while expressing discomfort from the cold, which is inappropriate for her noble status. During a critical incident, Miya Tetsuya leads a group to the kitchen to investigate her condition. The situation escalates when the woman, believed to be possessed by a spirit, wanders to the Central Garden's fish pond, undresses, and consumes a goldfish raw.

The group establishes a Threshold Barrier around the pond area in an attempt to contain the spirit. Despite their efforts to expel the spirit, it remains within the woman, leading to confrontations and rituals aimed at extracting and sealing the spirit. The events culminate in the successful containment of the spirit within a new vessel, while the palace's environment remains charged with supernatural energy.
