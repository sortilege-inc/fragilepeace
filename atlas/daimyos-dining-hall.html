Daimyo’s Dining Hall

The Daimyo’s Dining Hall is a spacious area within The Governor's mansion, designed for the daimyo and his guests. It features traditional decor, including sliding paper walls and tatami mat flooring, creating an inviting atmosphere for dining and social gatherings. The hall serves as a central hub for the daimyo's entertainments and feasts.

Recently, the hall was bustling with activity as guests gathered for breakfast. Shiba Midori was present, having left her meal to respond to a disturbance in the guest quarters. The Governor expressed concern for the safety of his niece, Miya Misato, and pledged to send guards to protect her after a break-in had left her room in disarray. The atmosphere shifted to urgent discussions about security and the implications of the recent theft, as attendees, including Doji Setsuna and Kakita Kazumi, strategized on ensuring Miya Misato's safety amidst rising tensions.
