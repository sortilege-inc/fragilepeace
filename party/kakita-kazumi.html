The Party

Kakita Kazumi

Crane

Kakita Kazumi, a distinguished samurai of the Crane Clan, is renowned for his exceptional dueling skills and striking appearance, characterized by his white hair and the blue demon mask he dons in battle. His martial prowess was notably showcased at the Topaz Championship, where he decisively defeated Riku in a single stroke, earning the prestigious title of Topaz Champion. As the yōjimbō for Doji Setsuna, the newly appointed Emerald Magistrate, Kazumi is unwaveringly dedicated to her protection, a vow he reaffirmed during his stay at Loyalty Castle. While at the castle, Kazumi engaged in light banter with Kakita Sugimasa, who warned him of a Lion Clan relative seeking revenge for a past duel, hinting at an imminent formal challenge. Despite his nonchalant demeanor, Kazumi expressed readiness to defend himself if provoked. As preparations for departure to the Castle of the Swift Sword commenced, it was revealed that he would face a formal duel against Matsu Koda, a student of the Lion Clan, under contentious terms that allowed all personally owned weapons and armor while prohibiting fatal blows. Kazumi made a deliberate entrance to the duel, appearing disheveled to provoke Koda. He initiated the confrontation with a swift strike from his katana, successfully hitting Koda, and prepared to use his yoroi-dōshi, an armor-piercing knife gifted by his grandfather. However, Koda countered with a technique that diminished Kazumi's physical resistance, allowing him to land a hit. The duel escalated, and despite Kazumi's attempts to feint, he ultimately faced defeat as Koda delivered a finishing blow, incapacitating him. Following the duel, Kazumi publicly referenced a prior insult made by a Lion Clan member against Shiba Midori's ancestors, prompting Koda to acknowledge his mistake and apologize. Later, during dinner at Loyalty Castle, Kazumi entertained courtiers with captivating tales of his exploits in the Phoenix Clan Lands. As travel preparations continued, he foraged for toxic herbs to prepare a noxious poison for later use. Throughout the journey, Kazumi attempted to assist in treating Bayushi Monban's injuries, though he was unsuccessful, reflecting his involvement in both the courtly and martial realms. Known for his quiet demeanor, Kazumi often meditates by a lake during gatherings of the Emerald Magistrates. He tends to remain behind the other samurai, indicating a reserved nature, and engages primarily in conversation with the Shinjo Family, preferring intimate discussions over larger social interactions. During a tea ceremony, he observes rather than contributes significantly to the dialogue, taking in the interactions among the other magistrates and Kitsu Family hosts, which include discussions on justice, beauty in Rokugan, and personal duties. His contemplative approach suggests a connection to the spiritual and philosophical aspects of samurai life. Kazumi's commitment to Doji Setsuna was further demonstrated when he defended her during an ambush while traveling to the Castle of the Swift Sword. He engaged effectively with attackers while maintaining a defensive posture, invoking the authority of the Emerald Magistrates to restore order. His actions balanced combat readiness with a sense of honor, highlighting his vigilance as a guardian prepared to defend his allies. In addition to his martial prowess, Kazumi is skilled in stealth techniques, capable of activating Skulk to vanish within obscuring terrain. During a chaotic melee near a way station, he regrouped with his allies upon recognizing the smoke bomb technique employed by the attackers. Activating Skulk, he disappeared into the smoke to approach a target unseen, striking a restrained opponent with his yoroi-dōshi and executing a Crossing Blade jutsu to inflict significant fatigue. After incapacitating the target with repeated stabs, he delivered a fatal blow before slipping back into the smoke, showcasing his proficiency in stealth and assassination as he maneuvered through chaos with calculated strikes. Kazumi is also known for his discreet and careful approach to his duties, possessing a keen sense of observation and handling sensitive situations with honor. During an investigation of an ambush site, he used a long stick to probe the bodies of slain attackers, maintaining respect for the dead. He discovered and pocketed a strip of cloth with the Doji Family pattern hidden beneath the slain leader’s sword sash, indicating a concealed affiliation. Although he recognized the leader's face as familiar, he could not place him at the time. He participated in discussions regarding the nature of the attackers, believing they were trained shinobi rather than mere bandits. After confirming the attackers had fled, Kazumi and his companions left the corpses for proper heimin rites before rejoining Doji Setsuna at the Castle of the Swift Sword. Kazumi is also adept in courtly manners and navigating complex political situations. He possesses a calm demeanor and is recognized for his loyalty to the Imperial Houses, often prioritizing it above his duty to his clan. His reputation for impartiality and integrity has occasionally placed him at odds with members of the Scorpion Clan. During a formal audience with Lady Kitsu Takeko, the daimyo of the Kitsu Family, Kazumi discussed a dispute over Diamond Mines between the Scorpion Clan and Unicorn Clan, demonstrating his understanding of the historical context of the conflict. He was actively engaged in court proceedings, contributing to discussions regarding upcoming negotiations with envoys from both clans and expressing a desire to prepare a report for the daimyo ahead of the arrival of Kitsuki Kaage, indicating his commitment to resolving the issues at hand. Kazumi interacted with other members of the court, including the Scorpion Clan courtier Bayushi Monban, acknowledging the political maneuvering involved in their situation. He understood the stakes of the conflict and recognized the potential benefits of securing documentary evidence to support the Scorpion Clan's claims, reflecting a strategic mindset as he considered how to leverage alliances and maintain the Scorpion Clan's influence within the context of the Emerald Magistracy. In his role as an Emerald Magistrate, Kazumi became involved in court proceedings at the Castle of the Swift Sword regarding the Battle of the Snow Plain. He presented evidence from his collection of journals belonging to battle participants and conveyed information from Kitsuko Ayoko concerning missing details impacting the honor of the Kitsu Family and the Scorpion's suppression of an ancestor's abilities. Kazumi stated during the proceedings that the Scorpion suppressed information about certain abilities of an ancestor, recognizing the challenges of presenting his findings without direct textual evidence. He proposed that the mines in question should be consigned to Imperial custody due to unclear ownership. When Bayushi Monban became agitated, Kazumi attempted to de-escalate the situation by offering him tea and counsel. Throughout the proceedings, he acted as a mediator and contributed to the legal discourse, advocating for his clan's interests while navigating the court's complexities. In addition to his martial and political endeavors, Kazumi is dedicated to researching ancestral histories related to the Battle of the Snow Plain. He is actively copying key passages from various records into a cohesive draft, demonstrating a meticulous approach to his work. Kazumi has access to the Kitsu Archives, where he investigates records of notable figures, including Akodo Toronoko and Matsu Sakura. Kazumi began compiling the party’s accounts and found Akodo Toronoko’s recollection of the battle, which emphasized valor and absolved Ikoma Akuyaku of responsibility for supply issues. He noted a change in Akodo Toronoko's handwriting with the entry about the Snow Plain, indicating a significant alteration in the records. In addition, he discovered Matsu Sakura’s account, which mentioned the battle but highlighted external factors like weather over personal valor. Kazumi expressed concern over the identity of Akodo Kayamayako, a medic suspected of espionage, and mentioned feeling attacked during the battle, suggesting the medic's betrayal. His research involved cross-referencing various accounts and noting omissions, such as the absence of Ikoma Hideri and Kitsu Yui in multiple records. Kazumi engaged in discussions with Doji Setsuna and Bayushi Monban about the nature of victory in battle and the importance of corroborating evidence regarding Scorpion Clan claims. He participated in a group meal in the garden, where strategic discussions about the historical claims surrounding the Snow Plain Diamond Mines continued. Kazumi is associated with exhibits featuring documents related to the Battle of the Snow Plain. These include various versions of the diaries of Ikoma Akuyaku, a Lion historian and officer, which detail his involvement and observations regarding the conflict. Kazumi's role involves presenting these documents in a courtroom setting, contributing to the legal and historical analysis of the case. There are three conflicting versions of Ikoma Akuyaku's diary. Version A presents a less incriminating account, where Akuyaku denies any personal assistance to the Scorpion in their conspiracies against the Lion. This version is part of Kazumi's exhibits and is presented by one of the Cosmics. In contrast, Version B contains explicit confessions of criminal acts, including poisoning wells and sabotage, and is referenced by other characters in the ongoing case. Kazumi's exhibits provide an overview of testimonies from individuals involved in the conflict, highlighting the complexity of the evidence discussed. The courtroom dialogue emphasizes the significance of these documents in establishing the historical context and legal implications of the claims made by the Scorpion and Lion clans regarding the battle and its aftermath. Kazumi's contributions are pivotal in navigating the intricate web of testimonies and allegations presented during the proceedings. Dressed in a blue-green kimono with a darker blue sash around his waist, Kazumi carries a katana and a wakizashi at his left hip, standing poised and prepared to act when necessary. At the Ancestral Shrine, he is introduced by Doji Setsuna alongside his fellow magistrates, assisting in resolving a dispute between the Unicorn Clan and Lion Clan. Kazumi listens attentively as evidence is presented, including journals from notable Lion Clan members implicating the Scorpion Clan in sabotage during the Battle of the Snow Plain. Shosuro Aishi privately suggests to Kazumi that Doji Setsuna might undermine him; Kazumi dismisses the insinuation but considers it, participating in discussions about the conflicting versions of Ikoma Akuyaku's journals and contributing to collective decision-making as the arbitration unfolds. Recently, Kazumi has solidified his status as a Crane duelist of high rank, possessing imperial blood and a status value of 55, making him one of the highest-ranking individuals in court proceedings. He has taken a commanding presence in discussions, particularly regarding evidence presented by the Scorpion Clan, which he claims to be forgeries. Kazumi urges a comparison of the Scorpion's evidence against his own notes and library journals, suggesting that discrepancies exist which unfairly favor the Scorpion's narrative. He questions the authenticity of the evidence brought forth by Bayushi Monban, emphasizing the need for careful examination to determine its legitimacy. While not directly accusing Monban of forgery, he implies that the evidence is misleading and intended to benefit the Scorpion. Kazumi moves to dismiss the evidence and proposes that the Diamond Mines in question be placed under Imperial custody due to the lack of trustworthy proof from either clan, delivering his arguments with conviction to influence the court's decision towards Imperial oversight. Kazumi is also an agile duelist and herbalist, recognized by his Emerald Magistrate Badge, which grants him authority in matters concerning the Empire. During a tea ceremony at Miya Tetsuya’s Tea House, he expressed concern over the absence of Bayushi Monban, a masked Scorpion operative. He praised Doji Setsuna's brushwork and highlighted his own blade skill during formal introductions. Kazumi agreed to accompany Miya Misato, an imperial envoy, toward the front lines, fulfilling his duty as an Emerald Magistrate. He advised Doji Setsuna on the use of diluted nightshade tea for certain conditions and delivered a tightly wrapped packet of nightshade leaves to Shiba Midori with strict handling instructions. He visited Hideyoshi Aki’s counting house in the Unicorn District, presented his badge, and explained the imperial mission, receiving information about recent shipments that suggested army movements to the northeast. Kazumi coordinated with Shiba Midori regarding the procurement of restricted medicinal herbs and assisted in brewing a Terminating Tea for Doji Setsuna, maintaining discretion throughout these interactions to ensure sensitive matters were handled carefully. However, Kazumi is currently missing during an investigation into a smuggling operation at The Docks, causing urgency and concern among his fellow magistrates. He is noted to be part of a group escorted by Kaeru Haya, which includes Bayushi Monban and Doji Setsuna, as they investigate suspicious activities at The Docks. His absence becomes significant when his colleague, Shiba Midori, is found incapacitated and robbed. Doji Setsuna underscores the seriousness of locating Kazumi, and the search intensifies upon confirmation that the suspected smuggling ship is departing, heightening the urgency among his colleagues. Kazumi's fate remains uncertain amidst the unfolding events. Kazumi is also involved in a confrontation in an alley, where he extracted his katana from the foot of a Peasant Thief, compelling the man to return a stolen coin purse belonging to Shiba Midori. He has an assertive demeanor and pressed the thief into his service as restitution for the offense against a member of the samurai caste. At dawn, he arrived at The Docks, where he noted the presence of Kaeru Ronin searching the area and observed that a large ship had already departed. He encountered Doji Setsuna and Shiba Midori, who showed signs of a rough night with a visible bruise and lacking her weapons. Kazumi presented the thief to Shiba Midori to return her coin purse, which was found to be light by one koku. After the thief pleaded for forgiveness while bleeding from his foot, Shiba Midori used her magic to heal him, which he witnessed with gratitude. Kazumi later tasked the former thief with purchasing supplies, emphasizing the importance of not absconding with the money, demonstrating a proactive approach to addressing issues such as theft and the potential smuggling operation. In a recent encounter at Miya Tetsuya’s Palace, Kazumi confronted a possessed woman, identifying himself and questioning whether he was speaking to her or the spirit within her. He observed her unnatural behavior, noting her boorish manners and discomfort in the heat. Spotting a foreign gold pendant with red gems around her neck, he identified it as a stylized emblem of a roaring flame. Kazumi deftly removed the pendant without her noticing and placed it in water, causing it to sizzle and burn his fingers. He participated in the group's efforts to contain the spirit, grabbing a nearby bucket of water and rushing towards the manifested Ifrit. During the confrontation, he executed an iaijutsu cut that inflicted a critical wound on The Ifrit, knocking smoking coals from its mass. The Ifrit retaliated with a powerful blow, knocking Kazumi unconscious and leaving him bleeding and burning. After the battle, Bayushi Monban performed a cleansing to remove Kazumi's bleeding condition. Recently, Kazumi has retreated to his room and is unavailable to answer questions regarding prior box preparations. Shiba Midori notes Kazumi's absence, stating that he cannot be consulted about the necessary boxes for spirit containment. His retreat is for unknown reasons, indicating a lack of engagement in the ongoing discussions and events surrounding the spirit issue. Kazumi has also taken on the role of bodyguard for Daidoji Shin, further emphasizing his commitment to his duties. He has been involved in an ongoing magistrate investigation, during which he discovered a black hemp bag coated in Firebiter Poison in the Crane couple's quarters. Demonstrating caution, he avoided direct contact with the poison and requested thick leather gloves for cleaning his wakizashi's sheath. Kazumi addressed gathered guests about the recent intrusions and the poisoning incident, revealing the potential threat posed by the poison and contributing to the ongoing investigation. His keen observational skills and strategic mindset continue to guide his actions as he navigates the complexities of his role amidst the unfolding events.
