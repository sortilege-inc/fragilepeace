Akodo Guards

Lion

The Akodo Guards are a specialized unit within the Scorpion Clan, responsible for protecting key figures and maintaining order among magistrates. Their members are trained to handle various situations in the realm's tumultuous political landscape. The guards enforce the law and uphold the interests of the Scorpion Clan, known for their loyalty and combat skills.

Recently, the Akodo Guards were mentioned in a warning from a Soshi Aisha to Bayushi Monban, a magistrate of the Scorpion Clan. Soshi Aisha cautioned Monban against visiting her quarters with a quartet of Akodo Guards, indicating that such an action could be perceived as a potential double-cross. This incident underscores the guards' reputation and the consequences of perceived betrayal in the political intrigue surrounding the Scorpion Clan.
