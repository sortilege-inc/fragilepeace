A scar that goes all the way around [[Bayushi Monban]]'s neck, like a collar. [[Matsu Tomoe]]
reached toward it in session 5 without touching it and it lit red and burned him anyway; she
recoiled and told him he bore the touch of [[Jigoku]]. He agreed, and said he had walked that
place and returned whole, judged worthy by the one who makes those choices.

**Three characters are set into the ring.** One is [[Onahime]]'s. One is the
[[Lady of Decay]]'s. There is a third, at the back, and what it is has not been said — he
began to say and did not finish. Held open; nothing should be built on a guess about it.

**What it does.** It itches near spiritual working and then burns outright: laying a
Threshold Barrier, calling on the [[Lady of Decay]], standing over water that has taken a
great infusion of negative energy. When it acts up his hand is unsteady and good rolls come
apart. Calling the [[Lady of Decay]] sets it burning and starts him coughing the way he
coughed when he was dying; using more kihō afterwards brings her out properly and the
coughing stops.

**What it looks like to anyone who does not know.** Possession by an evil spirit — which is
his own reading of the risk, not somebody else's accusation. It glows. He has shown it
deliberately to very few: to [[Matsu Tomoe]], and to the [[Crab Clan|Crab]] he called family
and first enemy blood when he came back out of the west.

No one in the Empire knows what he signed. That is the position the record has held since
session 33 and nothing has changed it.
