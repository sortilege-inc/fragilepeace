## Narrative

!lede They left the bodies where they lay. [[Bayushi Monban]] would not take the heads and would not defile them — handling the dead is an unclean act — and the practical conclusion followed, that they leave them on the road until heimin could be fetched to deal with them. Before they went, [[Kakita Kazumi]] worked the corpses over with a long stick so as not to touch them, a trick he had watched [[Kitsuki Wataru]] do with his jitte, looking for tattoos and marks, and pulled the masks off.

The leader's face was familiar and he could not place it. *I am not sure where I remember him from, but he seems familiar* — and then, out loud: *his features seem too refined for a ruffian.* Tucked under the man's armour, as part of his sword sash and folded so it would not show, was a strip of cloth carrying the [[Doji Family]] crest. [[Kakita Kazumi]] put it away in his sleeve, carefully and discreetly, and said nothing about it.

Asked directly whether the attackers had said anything, [[Bayushi Monban]] answered *nothing worth repeating*. What they had said was *Lord Scorpion, stay out of this, it is not your business* — and because no one had called him brother or cousin, he was certain they were not [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]]. He kept that to himself for the moment. Four had run. Two were dead.

[[Doji Setsuna]] rode flat out and reached the [[Castle of the Swift Sword]] after dark, filthy, dust to the elbows and looking like a fishwife who had stolen someone's very good clothes. The badge opened the gate anyway. The majordomo took one look and decided it could not wait, and brought her out to a tea house at the back where [[Kitsu Takeko]] — the [[Kitsu Family|Kitsu]] daimyō, a small slight woman with the red hair the Kitsu have more of than anyone in Rokugan, and only a year or two older than [[Doji Setsuna]] — was mid-ceremony with two advisors.

She was offered the fourth cushion and declined it until she had delivered her message. She bore Imperial writ, one under the hand of [[Kitsuki Kaage]] and one under the seal of the [[Akodo Family|Akodo]], and a man had put hands on her and pulled her from her horse as though she were a common traveller. *This is not a private grievance. This is an affront to the Empire, and as much a slap in your face as in mine.* Then she asked for strength to find and punish them, and for help with her Imperial business.

*I am absolutely inclined to do both of those things.* A snap of the fan, a bell, a servant, and [[Akodo Atsushi]] was summoned — a giant of a man who had to stoop to get into the tea house and sat awkwardly beside her. He was given the roads southeast and west and four samurai in clan garb without mon, and the daimyō's own condition, which was that they be taken alive. *We cannot question them as to what they were doing in our lands if they are dead.* His answer was a snarl worthy of a Lion: *they think they are doing well, having taken what they came for and trying to escape. We will show them otherwise.* [[Doji Setsuna]] asked to ride with him and was given leave and then talked out of it — *I believe you have another mission which takes precedence.*

Asked what she had seen, she rolled badly and knew it, and answered only what she could stand behind: smoke, no mon, nothing she could name. She offered her own read — shinobi rather than bandits — and the daimyō agreed, because banditry this soon on these roads would be close to suicide. *Then whose shinobi, and what do they want?* [[Doji Setsuna]] said she believed they had been looking for someone who was not her, and that she feared it might be one of her companions. She did not mention [[Asako Taishi]] at all. She was also told something worth keeping: the castle had been told to expect her, and only her.

Then she offered to go and make herself presentable, and was refused. *If you take the time to clean yourself, the ceremony will be over. Please, as you are, sit and join us.* So she sat down at her own strife limit, in borrowed composure and none to spare, and drank tea with a Lion daimyō and two of her advisors while they asked pleasant questions and worked out what had happened.

They asked after her companions and whether she had fled before the matter was settled. She explained that her yōjimbō had urged her to go so that he could resolve it. *Then your yōjimbō is to be commended for having performed his duties with honour.*

They asked after [[Otosan Uchi]], and after her family.

She tried to lie. It was the correct instinct and she could not keep a single die of it, because she had been compromised since she came through the gate. What came out instead was the truth, halfway through a boilerplate sentence: *I was not able to protect my retinue and those that were with me. I am alone in this place with this huge burden of responsibilities.* It was not a graceful break. It was the ugly kind, in front of a daimyō, in a foreign castle, with her husband a prisoner of the clan whose tea she was drinking.

[[Kitsu Takeko]] got up, crossed the tea house, put her arms around her and wiped the tear off her face. *It is all right. I understand your husband is on the other side of this conflict, and I know he is likely in danger. You are doing your best to be strong. This is a tea house, and you are inclined. This will not be spoken of.* And then: *I will inquire about your husband. See what can be done. Everything else can wait.* [[Doji Setsuna]] did not trust herself to answer and nodded into her shoulder.

The ceremony resumed. She walked out of it with no strife and no honour lost at all.

[[Akodo Atsushi]] met the rest of the party on the road well after midnight with two dozen Lion cavalry, announced himself, took the account — *we were assaulted on the road, but showed them the Emperor's mercy and the Emperor's justice*, and, of the man on the tree, *it will be easy to find; just look for the body* — and split his force, half to bring them in and half to hold the ground. [[Kakita Kazumi]] told him he would have to ask the others, because he had been scouting when it happened.

At the castle [[Bayushi Monban]] demanded to see [[Doji Setsuna]] and was told a lady's rest is not disturbed. He kept demanding. [[Shiba Midori]] told him not to make a scene in a Lion castle. [[Doji Setsuna]], who had failed entirely to sleep through it, made herself decent and put her head out of the door with her hair in a bun and borrowed yellow robes on. *My lady. You live.* — *I live. Did you encounter [[Akodo Atsushi]] on the road?* — *We did. You are safe.* — *I am safe. There are rooms prepared for you. You should rest.* He asked whether she had further need of him tonight, was told no, and said he was taking [[Ryu]] to the baths so that he would not hurt himself and would be close by after. His room turns out to be the next one along, which is how it should be, with enough distance between for dignity. On her way back in she left him with a line to sleep on: *I believe we will have some things to discuss on our way to breakfast.*

[[Kitsu Takeko]] had sent for healers the moment she heard he was wounded. The one waiting at the bath was a young [[Kitsu Family|Kitsu]] man in Lion maroon and yellow with a kit already laid out, who took the bandages off without doing further damage, bathed him, dressed the wounds and took five fatigue off him. [[Bayushi Monban]] asked him to say nothing about the tattoos. *I did not see anything.* [[Shiba Midori]] got her own, and was told her injuries were superficial and she would recover well — which she found surprising, given how good the man who gave them to her had been, and did not mention to anybody that the blow would have cut her in half if she had been slower.

[[Kakita Kazumi]] did not go to bed. He wrote a letter to his lord.

In the morning [[Shiba Midori]] told [[Doji Setsuna]] the part she was willing to tell: that Emerald Magistrates had been assaulted, and that she had duelled one of them and killed him. She kept back exactly why, and what she thinks about who. [[Bayushi Monban]] was delighted with her. [[Kakita Kazumi]] apologised for having been too far away gathering herbs to prevent any of it, and promised not to be so far off next time.

[[Shiba Midori]] also put a thought on the table that nobody wanted: that [[Kitsuki Kaage]] himself might have been played, and the assignment used to put them on that road. [[Doji Setsuna]] tried to reason it through and got nowhere, and kept the unease rather than shed it. She does not believe it of him. She cannot think who could arrange such a thing. She also cannot rule it out.

At breakfast the advisor [[Kitsu Yayoi]] — an amiable man with an extremely fine beard — gave them the war. Talks freely, that one. The [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] had ambushed the Lion in open field at the Battle of the [[Kanto Plains]], expecting to ride around them and harry them down, and had underestimated Lion swiftness. The general was [[Akodo Sakura]]. His son [[Akodo Akihito]] held the right flank, personally put down close to a dozen riders, rallied his troops and took one of the enemy commanders in single combat. Several high-ranking prisoners were taken and the Lion are now besieging a fortress in [[Kanto Plains|Kanto]].

[[Bayushi Monban]] began pointing at the horizon every time [[Akodo Akihito]]'s name was said, so [[Kitsu Yayoi]] began saying it more often. *He is our brother in first-enemy blood. He is my declared rival in skill at arms. One day we shall settle once and for all who is better with the blade. He is also a fellow Emerald Magistrate, which is not common among Lion generals.*

[[Doji Setsuna]] placed the father rather than the son. [[Akodo Sakura]] sits fourth or fifth in the line of succession of the [[Akodo Family|Akodo]]: [[Akodo Toturi]] has no heir, his younger brother is heir presumptive, and then a short list, and [[Akodo Sakura]] is on it. The man holding her husband is close enough to the Lion Clan Championship to count.

They also learned when their actual work arrives. [[Kitsuki Kaage]] is coming up the [[Drowned Merchant River]] and has been delayed by unseasonable rains; he will be here within days, bringing [[Ide Subane]] and another representative with him, and he will render the final judgement himself. What he wants from [[Doji Setsuna]] is the investigation prepared and ready to sign when he walks in.

[[Doji Setsuna]] asked to be taken to the [[Kitsu Archives]] — to wherever treaties are kept — and asked for an assistant researcher to go with her. She was told the daimyō is the one who gives that permission, and that it could be raised after the meeting. So they were given the tour instead.

The [[Akodo War College]] is a castle inside a castle, staffed all year, with spring for proving and summer for heavy training, and halls that alternate between silence and the clack of bokken. The [[Military Library]] there is the finest in Rokugan; the [[Ikoma Family|Ikoma]] archives are second only to their own main holding; the [[Kitsu Family|Kitsu]] keep theirs beside them.

At the [[Ancestral Shrine]] a chill wind came in behind them and put out a row of candles. [[Bayushi Monban]] saw the candles go. [[Shiba Midori]] got all of it — leather and sandals, the creak of armour, boots coming up behind, and a voice saying *Doji Setsuna!* The priests looked up and one of them said what he had seen without being asked: *the presence of an angry ancestor near the young lady.* He was not describing something wrong with their shrine. He was describing her grandmother, who told her in a waystation five days ago that she would walk with her and be watching. [[Doji Setsuna]] noticed nothing whatsoever. [[Bayushi Monban]] put himself between her and the priests before he understood they were not the threat.

The priestesses took [[Bayushi Monban]]'s wounds first and worked properly on them in a side room — cleaning it, stitching it shut, a mint-smelling balm, clean linen — and told him it would have gone through his lung had the blow been a little higher.

In the [[Military Library]] they were met by [[Kitsuko Ayoko]] in scholar's robes, who had been told to expect them and offered her help unasked. [[Shiba Midori]] saw a shimmer of a spirit hovering at her shoulder that faded when she looked at it, and heard armour creaking, and said so; [[Kakita Kazumi]] put it down to an old castle. Her father [[Kitsu Taigen]] was in the printing room adjoining, watching leaf presses go onto scrolls, and was pleased to have Emerald Magistrates in the halls on business, and suggested they make offerings at the [[Ancestral Shrine]] so their journey out would be as uneventful as their journey in had not been.

[[Doji Setsuna]] asked [[Kitsuko Ayoko]] for her namesake, and had to be asked which battle before the search could go anywhere, and had to reach for it, because she has never once looked into her own ancestors. What she found, she found with more success than she wanted.

Her namesake was her grandmother's sister. She was a renowned warrior, named in accounts of several battles and a force to be reckoned with even in the ones the Lion lost to the Crane, and she died gloriously holding off a unit of [[Daidoji Family|Daidoji]] who were pursuing an organised withdrawal, so that a great many people lived who otherwise would not have.

And attached to the record, in a general's own remarks, was a line about her grandmother: that she is to be given in marriage to the Crane, because he does not want to face her on the other side of the field.

That is the answer to the question [[Matsu Tomoe]] shouted at her in a waystation shrine. She was not traded because she was a daughter and available. She was traded because a Lion general was afraid of her. The marriage came after the sister's death.

On the way out of the library the halls were strangely cold for midsummer, and a banner lifted in the draught as they passed. The [[Akodo War College]] hangs the banners it takes off beaten armies, and this one carries the [[Shinjo Family]] crest of the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] — and [[Doji Setsuna]], who is married into that family, recognised the writing on the pole. It is from the [[Snow Plain]], which the Lion lost. [[Kitsu Taigen]] had noticed it himself years ago. *It is the only one in this entire hallway that is from a battle lost, not a battle won. And I have never been able to find any reason why it should be here. I have examined the official records.* He supposes it may have been taken at some moment in the battle when the odds ran the Lion's way, brought back as booty and hung by mistake. He does not believe that either.

Then they made offerings — rice, sake, a candle, and the name of whichever ancestor is wanted — and the candle [[Kitsuko Ayoko]] lit guttered, and a woman in heavy armour stood behind the altar with a katana in one hand and a fresh wound in her side under the other, looking very nearly real.

She was trying to say something and could not. [[Doji Setsuna]] could see that much plainly. [[Shiba Midori]] got the rest of it: a ghost that wants to speak and cannot find the way, and something secret that needs to come out. It went as fast as it came, banished before anyone could hold it.

[[Kitsuko Ayoko]]'s eyes shimmered gold for a moment. She pulled at [[Shiba Midori]]'s sleeve. *You are shugenja, yes? I am only a scholar. I have never spoken to the spirits. But I know that woman. I do not know how. Her name was [[Katsuki Yui]].*

*Let us talk to her together.* They agreed to try it properly, later, and were called away to take afternoon tea with the daimyō.

## Setsuna

She got what she came into that tea house for, and she got it by framing. The ambush went to [[Kitsu Takeko]] not as a Crane's grievance or a magistrate's inconvenience but as an insult to the Empire committed on [[Lion Clan]] soil, which made the Lion co-injured and gave the daimyō a reason of her own to spend cavalry on it. It worked on a daimyō in the middle of a war, and it cost nothing.

**Except that she did all of it compromised, and then she broke.** She arrived filthy and at her limit, was refused the chance to withdraw and clean up, and sat through an interrogation dressed as tea with none of her guard left. When they reached her family she tried to lie and could not hold a single die of it. What the daimyō got was the truth, badly. What [[Doji Setsuna]] got back was an embrace, a promise of enquiries after [[Shinjo Harunobu]], an assurance it would not leave the tea house — and a clean slate, no strife, no honour lost. It is the best outcome available and she did not choose it; she was too far gone to choose anything.

She should still notice what it taught [[Kitsu Takeko]]. Somebody now knows exactly where to press.

**And she found the thing she has been owed an answer about since the waystation.** In a Lion military library, in a general's own marginal remark: [[Matsu Tomoe]] was to be given to the Crane because he did not want to face her on the other side of the field. Her grandmother was not spare. She was a problem to be removed, and the marriage was the removal, and it happened after her sister had already died holding off [[Daidoji Family|Daidoji]] so that other people could get away. That is the whole of it, written down, kept, and never once looked at by anybody in her own family — including her.

It is also the first time she has gone looking. She had to be asked which battle before the search could start, because she did not know.

**Her grandmother was in the room and she could not tell.** A [[Kitsu Family|Kitsu]] priest said aloud that there was an angry ancestor standing near her. [[Shiba Midori]] heard the armour and the boots and her own name being shouted. [[Bayushi Monban]] watched the candles go out. [[Doji Setsuna]] felt a draught. Whatever she decides to do about the debt, she is going to have to do it through other people's senses.

The banner is the one to hold on to. A [[Shinjo Family|Shinjo]] banner from the [[Snow Plain]] hanging in a hall of trophies taken from beaten armies, in a war college that lost that battle, with no record anywhere explaining how it got there — and [[Kitsu Taigen]] has been looking for years. She is the only person in that castle married into the family whose crest is on it.

Smaller things, all of them live. The man holding her husband is fourth or fifth in line to lead the [[Akodo Family|Akodo]]. [[Kitsuki Kaage]] arrives within days and wants the investigation finished and ready to sign. [[Shiba Midori]] thinks he may have been played, and [[Doji Setsuna]] cannot rule it out. The castle was told to expect her and nobody else. She kept [[Asako Taishi]] out of the account entirely and offered her own companions as the likelier target instead, which was true, and is not the same as honest, and is a live omission in front of the woman now hunting his attackers.

And [[Kakita Kazumi]] took a strip of cloth bearing her own family's crest off a dead assassin, folded it away without a word, said the man's face was familiar, and then sat up writing a letter to his lord.
