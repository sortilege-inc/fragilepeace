Unicorn District

The Unicorn District is a notable area within the City of the Rich Frog, situated on the west bank of the river and featuring the largest docks in the city. It is governed by the Unicorn Clan, recognized for their unique shugenja magic and spiritual practices. The district is characterized by its connection to spirit magic, with locals holding a strong reverence for spiritual beings and their associated artifacts. The climate of the district reflects the bustling activity and diversity of the broader city, creating a lively atmosphere where traders and locals conduct commerce.

The district features various structures, including shops that sell "Meishodo" curios and talismans, some of which are rumored to be traded illicitly. Among these shops, there is a Curio Shop located away from the main thoroughfare of the wharf, known for its dim lighting and a clutter of strange antiques. An elderly shopkeeper with a long white beard maintains this shop, which has become a focal point for discussions regarding illicit magical items. The presence of Unicorn individuals, such as Ide Iwena, indicates the connection to local shops that offer items of spiritual significance, although trading such important relics is generally disapproved by the community.

The district is inhabited by a mix of merchants, samurai, and locals who navigate the vibrant yet sometimes hazardous environment. Notably, this Curio Shop has connections to a Fox Clan seller, as indicated by the documentation and receipts the shopkeeper possesses. The Unicorn District is a bustling merchant quarter characterized by a diverse population of samurai and rōnin engaged in various business activities. The area is not walled, fostering a vibrant atmosphere where people from all walks of life interact despite the ongoing war.

In addition to its commercial significance, the district includes administrative buildings and a counting house combined with a warehouse, highlighting its role as a center for trade and commerce. The Kaeru Ronin serve as the city constabulary for The Governor Miya Tetsuya, maintaining order within the district. The Unicorn District has also become a focal point for gathering intelligence on military movements, with merchants and information brokers providing insights into the locations of the Lion and Unicorn armies. Recent shipments have been directed northeast, indicating possible army locations. Meanwhile, the ruins of the Burnt Theater, site of a supernatural disturbance, are undergoing efforts for purification and restoration. Merchants and traders in the district offer leads on potential suppliers and contacts that could assist in ongoing conflicts.

The Unicorn District is also known for its vibrant atmosphere and diverse inhabitants. It features houses of pleasure where patrons can engage in games and socialize with entertainers, including a trio of triplets who serve sake and dice. The district is recognized for its lively gambling tables and the presence of samurai from various clans, contributing to its dynamic social scene. The architecture showcases a blend of cultural influences, with structures designed for both leisure and intrigue.

Recently, Bayushi Monban was seen in the Unicorn District after a night of revelry. He interacted with the triplets at a gambling table before securing a private room. Following a chaotic morning, he was approached by Ryu, who invoked Monban's status as an Emerald Magistrate to arrange transport back to The Governor's mansion. During this time, a market-wrapped bundle was delivered to Monban by three figures on a riverboat, indicating ongoing dealings and connections within the district.
