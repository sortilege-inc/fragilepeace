## Narrative

!lede *That's a woman. Our target is not a woman. Let her go.* They had hold of [[Doji Setsuna]] on her Utaku warsteed and they let her go the moment they looked at her, because they had been given a description and she did not match it. Everything worth knowing about the ambush on the [[Emperor’s Road]] is in that sentence: it was not a robbery, it was an errand, and the errand was [[Asako Taishi]].

He shouted his protection again and it bought him nothing again. [[Kakita Kazumi]] took one look at the smoke and said he knew the tactic. [[Bayushi Monban]] came down out of the air onto [[Doji Setsuna]]'s warsteed with steel out, put a sheath-strike into one of the men holding [[Asako Taishi]], and did not shift him; the horse did better, rearing and putting another man in the dust. The archers, blind in their own smoke, dropped their bows and came in on foot.

[[Kakita Kazumi]] went into the fog and stopped being anywhere. He came out of it long enough to put a *yoroi-dōshi* into a held man, and [[Asako Taishi]] started screaming that there were assassins on him, which by then was not news. A second bomb went in and the road disappeared entirely. [[Shiba Midori]] swung her jitte at a man's hands and lost him in the haze. [[Doji Setsuna]] took the reins of [[Asako Taishi]]'s pony and walked it around to put a horse between herself and whoever was coming — which is the correct use of a pony whose rider has already been taken.

They dragged him off the road and stabbed him in the chest and in the kidneys and left him bleeding out with minutes on him. [[Bayushi Monban]] jumped at a man, missed, and was on the ground with rope on his arms before he was up; he announced from inside the ropes that he was an Emerald Magistrate and would not be bound, and then headbutted one of the men holding him, which was the more effective of the two arguments.

Their leader came forward in black armour with a long-chained nunchaku, held a threat over [[Asako Taishi]], and turned to [[Shiba Midori]]. She read him for whether he wanted this to stop. Then the two of them walked into a clearing where the smoke was thinning, bowed to each other, and kept every form of a formal duel. He cut first and cut deep. She answered and broke him, and finished it two-handed, and he was dead on the grass with his men still holding a dying scholar.

After that it came apart. [[Bayushi Monban]] threatened what was left of them. [[Doji Setsuna]] turned her horse for the [[Castle of the Swift Sword]]. [[Kakita Kazumi]] went sideways into the shadows and nobody saw him leave. The surviving attackers regrouped around [[Asako Taishi]] and carried him off, gravely wounded, which is what they had come for and not what they had planned. What the party had to look at afterward was the clothing: dark blue, uniform, and no clan crest on any of it. *I did not expect it to go this way at all*, [[Bayushi Monban]] said, and thanked [[Doji Setsuna]], and they agreed to reassemble at the castle.

## Learned

- Masked Assailants: A disciplined body in matching dark blue with no clan crest on any of them. They fought with smoke bombs, fouling arrows and numbers, took Monban with rope rather than steel, released Setsuna the instant they saw she was a woman — they had a description and were working to it. They wanted Asako Taishi alive enough to carry, and stabbed him nearly to death getting him.
- Black-Armored Leader: Their commander, in black armour with a long-chained nunchaku. He accepted a formal duel from Midori in the middle of an assassination, bowed, kept the forms, struck first and wounded her badly. She killed him. A man who observes duelling ritual is a samurai, whatever he was wearing.
- Asako Taishi: Stabbed in the chest and the kidneys, bleeding out, and carried away alive by the men who did it. Still under the party's promised protection and now in their hands.
- Kakita Kazumi: Recognised the smoke-bomb pattern as something he had seen before. Fights unseen — Skulk into cover, yoroi-dōshi into held men, Crossing Blade to put them down — and left the field without anyone noticing him go.
- Shiba Midori: Carries a jitte and will use it to disarm rather than to kill. She reads an opponent for whether he wants the bloodshed to stop, and she will take a formal duel against an armoured man twice her business and win it two-handed.

## Setsuna

The description is the thing to keep. They had one, it specified a man, and it was specific enough that they let go of a Doji on an Utaku warsteed rather than risk taking the wrong person. Someone briefed them on [[Asako Taishi]] and did not brief them on the party — they did not know an Emerald Magistrate's escort would be on that road.

Dark blue, matched, no crests. That is a body with a paymaster who can outfit it and a reason to keep the outfit anonymous. [[Kakita Kazumi]] has seen the smoke tactic before and should be asked where.

Two Emerald Magistrates asserted Imperial authority on the [[Emperor’s Road]] four times between them and it stopped nothing at all. The one thing that moved these men was a sword. She should not plan on the badge working in open country again.

And [[Asako Taishi]] is alive, taken, and still owed the protection she gave him in as many words. Whatever he was carrying, someone wanted it enough to spend a trained company and their commander on it — and the commander died without anyone learning his name.
