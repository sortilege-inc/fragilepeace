A dealer in fine art and antiquities in the [[City Of The Rich Frog]], handling goods from
all over Rokugan and, so some say, from beyond it. Named to [[Kakita Kazumi]] as a source
worth knowing when he went looking for news of the fighting, and not approached — the party
went to [[Hideyoshi Aki]] instead, on the grounds that a trader in supplies would know more
about armies than a trader in antiquities.

Nobody has yet asked him about the foreign talisman.
