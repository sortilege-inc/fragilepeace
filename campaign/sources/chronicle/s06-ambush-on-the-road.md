## Narrative

!lede [[Asako Taishi]] joined the road out of the waystation on a pony too small for him, a tall [[Phoenix Clan|Phoenix]] scholar failing to keep pace. [[Shiba Midori]] read his papers and read his accent — a northerner, she guessed, [[Asako Family|Isawa]] perhaps. Isawa by blood, he said, raised among the [[Kaito]]. She told him she was the same shape in reverse: [[Shiba Family|Shiba]] by blood, raised among the [[Kaito]], and studying the spirits long enough that they sometimes answer. *Sometimes they even listen to me.* His papers put his origin due east of [[Morning Glory Castle]], with a route that ran up the [[Imperial Roads]] as far as the [[Shrine of Durogin]] and across the river into [[Dragon Lands|Dragon lands]] — a travelogue that is not boring and does not describe a man going anywhere in particular. She judged he was drifting toward the [[Ikoma Lands|Ikoma]] libraries, which is where a man goes when the thing he wants confirmed is old.

He was carrying something and he would not say what. What he would say was that everyone he had told about it had come to harm — *every person I've told about this has had unfortunate things happen to them* — and that he needed it authenticated and made public before he would talk. [[Bayushi Monban]] and [[Doji Setsuna]] agreed to take him to the [[Castle of the Swift Sword]] and see it done under the Emerald Magistrates' authority: a major family's castle, full of arcane knowledge, and the best place outside the [[Phoenix Lands]] to have such a thing read. He accepted, and from that moment he was a man under Imperial protection, which obliges everyone who hears of it and shields him from nothing.

The road to that castle took most of a day, and he talked the whole of it.

He asked what satisfaction they had found in the calling, and [[Bayushi Monban]] answered that it is a righteous call to fight the [[Emperor]]'s enemies — and that justice is a dangerous profession, and that they are mourning missing comrades. *Surely you will see them again in Yomi? Or perhaps Meidō?* [[Shiba Midori]] let her dead eye show him for a moment. She has been where he was talking about and was not impressed by it: so much of the same, so many eternities. *Do you not believe in the wheel of reincarnation?* — *Belief. More like.*

[[Doji Setsuna]] tried to read the two of them and failed, and got one thing anyway: those two are leaving something unsaid on purpose. She let it go without asking.

He asked what enlightenment they had brought back. [[Bayushi Monban]]: *that there is more to this life than words and papers. And that one mistake can cost you everything.* [[Shiba Midori]] added that the great enemy, the Lying One, is closer than anyone thinks. Asked how a person keeps going knowing that: *a place must be earned. What is earned is owed, and what is owed is paid.*

He asked [[Shiba Midori]] about the spirits, and got the only account of her childhood anyone has heard. She was four. Her imaginary friends were real and nobody believed her, and the little whirlwind in the courtyard was a spirit complaining that people kept leaving dirt all over it. It threw a tantrum, three windows had to be replaced, and after that they stopped calling it an overactive imagination. *Terrifying.* She gave him the temperaments as she finds them — earth spirits chill and never in a hurry, water spirits changing their minds sixty times an hour, fire spirits all burn and consume and easily distracted — and the practical warning: do not follow a wind spirit off a roof, because a wind spirit forgets that people cannot fly. She summons the dead only sparingly, because human spirits keep human wants.

Then he asked which ancestor each of them would most like to meet. [[Doji Setsuna]] had answered that question once already, five days ago, and it had brought her grandmother down on her. She removed herself from the conversation. [[Bayushi Monban]] took her place in it deliberately, to keep the man talking to somebody else, and gave him a version of what happened at the waystation shrine — *we needed one thing and got an answer from another. Spirits are fickle. They are not of this world. They are to be appeased the best way we know how.*

Asked where her own talents lay, [[Doji Setsuna]] said: *I read carefully.* He asked for a case that turned on a missing word, and she gave him one from her years as a very minor clan magistrate — a merchant who filed too many forms. The paperwork was too clean. That is how she knew they were smugglers.

Two of them checked him while this went on. [[Kitsuki Wataru]] ran Traces of Passage, which needs a bowl and water and cannot be done from the saddle, and reported that the man had cast nothing in the last day but that there is more to him than there appears. [[Bayushi Monban]] looked with his own methods and saw the shape of it: a presence overshadowing him, a shadow not thrown by the man or by anything standing near him, and not malignant — nothing like the weather a demon makes.

So he rode up alongside and held the man's eye and called a cleansing kihō, a torrent of emerald snowflakes and blue-grey wind out of his own neck that wrapped [[Asako Taishi]] and shattered and settled. *That was amazing. What was that?* — *A new trick I learned along the way.* — *How do you feel?* — *Cold. That was cold.*

It did nothing, because there was nothing of that kind to remove. Whatever is on him is not the sort of affliction a cleansing lifts. But it made the offer legible, which was the point. *What is your problem? Do you believe yourself cursed or haunted?* — *I certainly have the luck of it, to be sure.* — *Go with the Shiba now. Tell the Shiba everything.*

He was about to.

The road went into a small copse of trees by a bridge, and the copse filled with smoke — not poison, just enough to take the daylight out of it, so that everyone could make out silhouettes and nothing else.

[[Asako Taishi]] shouted that he was under the protection of the [[Emerald Magistrates]] and that they could not take him. It was the correct thing to shout and it did nothing. [[Bayushi Monban]] called the retreat, rode the clouds two range bands straight up into the canopy, took hold of a branch — and took his mask *off*, so that what looked down out of the tree was the white-painted face of a [[Kuni Wardens|Kuni Warden]] Hunter: [[Onahime]] on one side, the [[Lady of Decay]] on the other, and a grin between them. The archers below concluded he was a demon and shot at him accordingly, which is how he ended the evening with four fatigue in his face.

The arrows had fouling heads on them — blunt, the kind used for birds, so ineffective against armour that armour counts for more than usual against them. They put wounds in people and killed nobody, because killing was never the instruction.

Then hands came out of the smoke for the riders. Somebody leapt up behind [[Shiba Midori]] and got a jitte under her throat and held her there. Somebody did the same to [[Doji Setsuna]], while another took [[Kogarashi]]'s reins. [[Kakita Kazumi]] put himself on guard over her, which is his job and which he had briefly forgotten.

[[Doji Setsuna]] had composed something before the knife arrived and delivered it with the knife where it was. *I beg your pardon. I had not expected such a hot reception. It would shame me deeply to arrive at the [[Castle of the Swift Sword]] under guard rather than under my own power — and it would grieve me more should your actions lead to misunderstandings that are difficult to retract. Permit us passage, and I will note your discretion.*

The man let her go.

He did not let her go because of the speech. He let her go because he had got a look at what he was holding and she was a woman, and their man is not. The same thing happened to [[Shiba Midori]] a moment later — grabbed, examined, released, and the grabber off the horse and away. In the smoke they could not tell one rider from another, so they took hold of whoever was mounted and checked. That is also why it took them three attempts to lay hands on the man they had come for.

From where [[Doji Setsuna]] was sitting, she said a thing and a knife came away from her neck.

[[Bayushi Monban]] dropped out of the tree onto the back of her horse, then off it — keeping his feet — restating the authority that had now been ignored three times. [[Shiba Midori]] took a calming breath, put herself on guard and said the same words he did: *cease this madness. We are magistrates.* [[Kogarashi]] wheeled and kicked and hit nothing but frightened somebody. [[Kakita Kazumi]] walloped whoever came nearest.

What could be seen of the attackers, in the smoke, was that they are not bandits. They are armed and equipped as samurai. No crest was visible on any of them and nobody had white hair — [[Bayushi Monban]] looked for both, and specifically for the [[Daidoji Family|Daidoji]]. On the third attempt they took hold of [[Asako Taishi]] and held him, and the evening ended with the scholar still calling for the protection he had been promised and the smoke still in the trees.

## Learned

- Asako Taishi: A Phoenix scholar, Isawa by blood and raised among the Kaito, travelling the Imperial Roads from east of Morning Glory Castle by way of the Shrine of Durogin and Dragon lands, with no destination written anywhere — Midori reads him as drifting toward the Ikoma libraries. He carries something he will not name until it is authenticated and public, and says everyone he has told has come to harm. Wataru finds he has cast no magic in a day and that there is more to him than shows. Monban sees a presence overshadowing him that is not malignant and is not the kind of affliction a cleansing kihō can lift — the kihō did nothing. He was one sentence from telling Midori everything when the smoke came, and he was taken while under the party's promised protection.
- Kitsuki Wataru: Works by Traces of Passage — he can tell whether a person has cast recently and whether magic has been worked on them. It needs a bowl and water and cannot be done from the saddle. Precise about the distinction, and about the window.
- Shiba Midori: Shiba by blood, raised among the Kaito, a long student of the spirits. Her gift announced itself when she was four, when a slighted courtyard spirit broke three windows on her behalf and settled the question of whether she was imagining things. She summons the dead only sparingly, because a human spirit keeps human wants. She was pulled off her horse with a jitte at her throat and released when they found she was a woman.
- Bayushi Monban: Under the mask his face is painted as a Kuni Warden Hunter — Onahime on one side, the Lady of Decay on the other — and he took the mask off in front of everyone rather than lose the scholar. The archers took him for a demon and shot him for it. He also took Setsuna's place in a conversation about ancestors when he saw she did not want to be in it.
- The attackers (unpaged): Not bandits. Armed and equipped as samurai, with no crest showing and no white hair on any of them; Monban looked for the Daidoji in particular and could not tell. They laid smoke to take the light rather than poison, used fouling arrowheads that wound and do not kill, and grabbed riders at random to check them — releasing two women before getting the man they came for on the third attempt. Everything about it was built to take Asako Taishi alive and leave everyone else standing.

## Setsuna

**They came for the scholar and nobody else, and they took him anyway.** She and [[Bayushi Monban]] had given [[Asako Taishi]] the protection of the [[Emerald Magistrates]] in as many words, on the road, in front of witnesses, hours before it failed. They took a man under an Emerald Magistrate's protection out of the middle of four of them and left all four alive to report it. Recovering him is a matter of the Emerald Magistrates' own authority now rather than a favour, and every clan on this road will read what happens next as the measure of what an Imperial promise is worth in [[Lion Lands|Lion lands]] during a war.

**She should be careful about what she thinks her words did.** She spoke, and the knife came off her throat, and it is going to be very tempting to file that as courtesy working under a blade. It was not. The man holding her was checking faces in smoke, found a woman, and let go — and did exactly the same thing to [[Shiba Midori]] a moment later, and jumped down and went back to work. She has no way to know that. It is the kind of thing that gets remembered wrongly and then relied on.

What the smoke actually proves is simpler and worse. They could not tell an Emerald Magistrate from a servant in it, which means the Imperial badge never entered into their planning at all. They did not weigh whether they dared touch her. They were never looking at her.

**These were samurai.** Bandits do not lay smoke to kill the daylight, carry blunts meant for birds, and check the face of every rider before deciding whether to keep hold. Equipped as samurai, moving as a unit, using arrowheads chosen so that nobody would die and a smoke that takes the daylight without poisoning anyone. Somebody fielded a disciplined party to take one unarmed scholar alive off a road in [[Lion Lands|Lion lands]], and was careful enough to leave four Imperial magistrates unharmed while doing it. No crest, no white hair, nothing to name them with.

And there is the other thing, which she noticed and did not raise. [[Bayushi Monban]] and [[Shiba Midori]] talk about the hells and the wheel and the great enemy the way other people talk about places they have been, because that is what they are. She tried to read it and missed, and came away certain of the shape of it anyway. They are keeping something from her deliberately, and [[Bayushi Monban]] looked at her afterwards as though he had lied to her and had reasons. She let it stand. That is twice now.
