Shinomen Forest

Shinomen Forest is a vast and ancient woodland known for its dense foliage and diverse wildlife. It features towering trees, thick underbrush, and is often shrouded in mist. Recent reports indicate increased activity of Shadowlands creatures within the forest, raising concerns among local inhabitants. The forest serves as a significant natural boundary in the region and is often mentioned in discussions regarding territorial disputes and spiritual phenomena.

The forest's proximity to Unicorn Lands and the Lion Clan's potential hostilities adds to its significance, positioning it as a site of potential conflict and spiritual importance.
