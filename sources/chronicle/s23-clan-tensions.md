## Narrative

!lede [[Bayushi Monban]] put a secret treaty into evidence and told the court where he got it: among [[Shinjo Kamo]]'s letters, in the possession of the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] military library, hidden within the shelves. The document was written in [[Shinjo Kamo]]'s own hand and sealed with his chop. It set out an agreement between [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] and [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] — assistance against the Lion at the [[Snow Plain]], the diamond mines as payment — and it duplicated almost perfectly the terms [[Shosuro Aishi]] had spent weeks alleging existed.

This is not the Treaty of [[White Shores Lake]], which is the public instrument everyone has been arguing over and which the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] still hold. On that one [[Shosuro Amane]]'s chop and signature appear as a witness and not as a participant — she was in the room when it was signed, which is a fact that helps the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] rather more than it helps anybody else. The document [[Bayushi Monban]] produced is a second and secret one, and it is the one that hands over the mines.

It was also too good. [[Doji Setsuna]] read it and the objection assembled itself: far too wordy for [[Shinjo Kamo]] as history describes him, too complete, saying exactly what the man producing it needed it to say. A forgery, with nothing to prove it with.

And then the clause that gave the mines away. It did not name a recipient. It bequeathed them to *the lowest of the Bayushi* — no person, no office, nobody a three-hundred-year-old document could have identified. Reading like a prophecy, in an instrument that should read like a deed.

*The lowest of the Bayushi* is a thing people say now, about [[Bayushi Monban]]. It is spoken, never written — the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] who draws the most attention and makes the worst mistakes, whose antics on the road to [[Otosan Uchi]] are too salacious not to be repeated until even a [[Crane Clan|Crane]] has heard them. The phrasing had walked out of a present-day rumour and into a document from before the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] came home.

[[Kitsuki Kaage]] raised an eyebrow at it too. He knows what it means in the modern world.

[[Ide Tsubame]] objected before it could be entered, and objected on procedure: nothing had been examined for authentication, and there are procedures for a reason. It was the narrowest of wins and it cost her — she came off it rattled and stayed rattled. But [[Kitsuki Kaage]] ruled with her. The treaty would be authenticated before he allowed it into evidence, and he wanted the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] military library's own records of which of [[Shinjo Kamo]]'s letters it had ever held. An assistant was dispatched to fetch them. [[Bayushi Monban]] said he had returned the letters to the library — all of them, except this one.

His evidence off the table, [[Bayushi Monban]] argued anyway: three hundred years of waiting is nothing against evidence produced now, the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] have nothing to answer with and want only to buy time, and the documents were in [[Lion Clan|Lion]] hands because [[Ikoma Akuyaku]] was a Lion and hid them there. He is a magistrate, he said, and he is supposed to be irreproachable, and every time something moves the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] forward the room finds a clan prejudice to pull it back, and he is tired of it. [[Shosuro Aishi]] backed every word.

He also went at [[Ide Tsubame]] deliberately, to break her. *Your clan knows you owe us. There is a Unicorn banner hanging in this castle. You have nothing. You could not find anything. You did not keep track of your own agreement. We know what happened — we were there.*

It worked. What came back was not a speech a small, unremarkable, carefully polite advocate is supposed to be able to make. She accused him of forging the letter from [[Shinjo Kamo]] and then itemised it: conveniently perfect, conveniently in [[Lion Clan|Lion]] territory, conveniently in no [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] archive, unheard of by any Unicorn or any Lion, known only to the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] who alone knew where to look. Verbose where he was plain. *That is my ancestor. I know how he talks. I have his writings. This is not him.* And that is why no one had tried to verify it.

Then the diaries — three copies of one document, contradicting each other except where two of them conveniently agree, and both of those support the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]]. The cipher could settle it in an afternoon: run the key and see whether it produces a perfect copy. Nobody has run it. Interesting, she said, that nobody has run it. And interesting that the third version is already off the record, because the magistrate who submitted it withdrew it herself rather than sell a flat lie to an Imperial Magistrate.

[[Kitsuki Kaage]] let her get all the way to the end before he found the words. *That is quite enough.* Everything she had built that day went with it. Then, before [[Bayushi Monban]] could enjoy it: *I have heard from you, Monban. That is quite enough as well.*

[[Doji Setsuna]] was called next, and presented a motion for summary dismissal with prejudice, with a request for sanctions and compensatory relief. She built it out of definitions — what constitutes a strategic resource of the Empire, what constitutes abandonment, what competition of claims means in law, the criteria under which Imperial eminent domain may be exerted — and then closed it by conceding everything. Presume the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] are telling the truth. Presume every word of the claim is good. Then for multiple centuries they held evidence that would have established it and did not press it, and by Imperial law the claim is forfeit and the mines are the Emperor's. And damages, so that belated claims stop costing the Empire its time.

[[Bayushi Monban]] objected and was told to save it for his turn. [[Kitsuki Kaage]] asked for case law, and [[Doji Setsuna]] gave him the honest answer: case law for some of the components, none for this combination of facts. [[Ide Tsubame]] could have assisted and chose not to — assisting meant arguing the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] out of the mines in exchange for punitive damages, and she had just made a spectacle of herself besides.

The ruling was not a refusal and not a win. *I have not heard sufficient reason to believe these claims are either unfounded, false, or frivolous yet. I am tabling your motion to dismiss on the merits until I have more evidence to that effect. I expect you to present such evidence if you wish to convince me of the same.*

[[Kitsuki Kaage]] then asked whether [[Shiba Midori]] wished to speak. She asked to postpone. He called the recess himself — well past the hour of the owl, nothing productive left in the night, everyone to argue in the morning and the matter resolved tomorrow if it can be. *I expect more evidence and less accusations all around.* [[Shiba Midori]] went straight to her rooms and spoke to nobody.

Earlier that day the arbitration had been interrupted by a [[Dragon Clan|Dragon]] arriving sideways. Four [[Lion Clan|Lion]] bushi walked [[Mirumoto Isshin]] in on ropes — bound without a single knot, because a knot would imply guilt before judgment — and he fought them the whole way, treading on their feet, hauling at the cords, curling his lip, and finally spitting on one bushi's menpō. He taunted the man about his brother. He offered [[Bayushi Monban]]'s escort his cheek and invited the Lion to show him how hard they can really swing.

The Lion took the rope out of [[Bayushi Monban]]'s hands, turned on his heel, put his face in [[Mirumoto Isshin]]'s, and told him this is what he had spat on, and that if he lived through the judgment in front of him he would be cut down afterward. Then he took the rope back and thanked his lord.

*The Lion are far too disciplined for such an ending.* [[Bayushi Monban]] gave his name and his office and gave four instructions: stop this; take him to the magistrate's own rooms and let him wash; answer questions without lying; and the judgment would be final.

That was a jurisdiction grab, and it was legal. [[Mirumoto Isshin]] stands accused of murdering a [[Lion Clan|Lion]] samurai, which would have gone to a Lion magistrate in a Lion castle. An Emerald Magistrate's authority extends to asserting Imperial jurisdiction over it, and with this many Emerald Magistrates under one roof, a tribunal is the likely shape of it. He also has a duel already scheduled, should he live that far.

He was left alone in a Scorpion's quarters for several hours, which was a mistake. He drank what sake there was and searched the room. More than one family mon. Emerald snowflakes on everything. Sanctified brushes, which raised a question of their own. A bottle of invisible ink, which he swapped for ordinary ink out of pure spite. A letter naming [[Bayushi Monban]] heir to a property. And, crumpled and thrown against the wall, a letter from his lord: no stipend, because he had pocketed money meant for the temples; a thief as well as a pathetic liar and a disgrace to his clan; and assigned to the protection of an annoying bird, whom he would keep alive if he ever hoped to return to favour.

[[Bayushi Monban]] came back to find his guest sitting on the tatami, waving the letter between two fingers. He drew the family blade, put it to [[Mirumoto Isshin]]'s neck, and asked twice whether he had been through his possessions. He was told it had been on the floor by the door. He called the Lion in and had the Dragon taken to the lowest, deepest, darkest, foulest cell in the castle, with nobody to speak to him until his lord came down. And not to let him sleep.

They did exactly that — stood him in a corner, tightened the ropes each time he started to go under, and rotated a fresh quartet through every few hours.

[[Doji Setsuna]] watched the procession pass her door and used the distraction to go to work. The weak joint in her motion is the sanctions, and she remembered a precedent for it: a [[Crane Clan|Crane]] business with the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] over official communications, in which the Imperial magistrates came down hard, made the Crane pay damages, and told them never to bring the like before Emerald Magistrates again. A Lion library would keep that. It did. It keeps it on a pedestal, under a carved scene of a hunter shooting a crane from behind a duck blind — which for Lion passes as subtlety. She found a second and more modest case on the timeliness of unpressed claims, and pulled her legal assistant out of bed to do it, and did not sleep.

The flaw is visible from here and the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] will find it: her precedents all concern known claims left unpressed, not unknown ones. Her answer is that assembling this much evidence this fast implies they knew all along.

## Learned

- Ide Tsubame: Descended from Shinjo Kamo's niece Shinjo Kamuko, and she calls him her ancestor. She has his writings and knows how he wrote — which is how she knew the treaty was not his. She blocked its entry on procedure, won the authentication ruling, then was goaded into accusing Monban of forgery in open court and itemising the case. Kaage silenced her and she lost the day's ground for it. She declined to assist Setsuna's motion, because winning it costs the Unicorn the mines.
- Kitsuki Kaage: Ruled the treaty must be authenticated before entering evidence, and sent for the Lion military library's records of which of Shinjo Kamo's letters it ever held. Noticed the phrase *the lowest of the Bayushi* and what it means today. Silenced Tsubame and Monban both. Tabled Setsuna's dismissal motion on the merits — not for want of authentication, but because he has not yet heard reason to think the Scorpion claim unfounded — and told her to bring evidence if she wants to convince him.
- Bayushi Monban: Produced the secret treaty and told the court he found it among Shinjo Kamo's letters in the Lion military library, having returned all the others. Asserted Imperial jurisdiction over Mirumoto Isshin's murder case, pulling it out of a Lion court. Carries more than one family mon, a bottle of invisible ink, and a letter from his lord calling him a thief, a liar and a disgrace, and assigning him to guard an annoying bird as a punishment posting.
- Mirumoto Isshin: A Dragon bushi brought into the castle on knotless ropes, accused of murdering a Lion samurai, with a duel already scheduled. Spat on a bushi's menpō and was promised he would be cut down if he survived judgment. Searched Monban's quarters, found the punishment letter, lied about it at swordpoint, and was put in a cell and kept awake all night for it.
- Shinjo Kamo: The secret treaty is written in his hand and sealed with his chop — and his descendant says it is not his writing. His letters were held by the Lion military library, and what it held is now being checked.
- Shosuro Amane: Her chop and signature appear on the official treaty as a witness, not a participant. She was in the room when it was signed, which helps the Scorpion more than the Unicorn would like.
- Ikoma Akuyaku: Born Scorpion, married into the Lion — he wrote that he refused to help defeat *the family I am married into*. Akuyaku's diary exists in three versions: Kazumi's, the least damning; Monban's, a full confession in cipher; and Midori's, a plain-language commentary on an original found in a secret pocket sewn inside the binding, which she withdrew rather than submit.
- Shosuro Aishi: Backed every word of Monban's argument and assisted it directly. The treaty he produced matches the terms she had been alleging almost exactly.

## Setsuna

**Three people in that room knew the treaty was forged, and the only one who said so was punished for saying it.** [[Doji Setsuna]] worked it out from the wordiness and the impossible phrase. [[Kitsuki Kaage]] caught the same phrase and raised an eyebrow and said nothing. [[Ide Tsubame]] said it aloud, in order, with every supporting reason — and lost a day's ground and her dignity for it. The thing she said was true and the room agreed with it. That is not a lesson about being right.

So the practical question is what the [[Crane Clan|Crane]] does with a certainty she cannot prove. The answer for now is nothing out loud. [[Kitsuki Kaage]] has already ordered the authentication and sent for the library's own records of what letters it held — which is a better weapon than any accusation, because it does not depend on anybody believing her. If the library never held that letter, [[Bayushi Monban]] told an Imperial Magistrate a lie about provenance in open court, and the ruin is entirely his own work.

**Her motion is the best piece of law she has done and its structure is why.** Conceding the whole of the opponent's case and winning anyway is the move that cannot be answered by producing better evidence — the more the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] prove, the longer they prove they sat on it. [[Kitsuki Kaage]] did not reject it. He tabled it on the merits and told her exactly what would move him. That is an instruction, and it came from a man who had already told her during the recess that the Imperial-compensation framing was the argument he wanted made in public.

The weak joint is the one she identified herself and went to the library at midnight to shore up. Her precedents concern claims that were known and left unpressed; the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] will say theirs was unknown until the evidence surfaced. Her counter is decent — assembling this much this quickly implies knowing where to reach — and it would be a great deal stronger if the library records come back and show [[Bayushi Monban]] reached into a shelf that never held the thing.

One thing worth settling before the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] raise it, because they will. What [[Doji Setsuna]] put to [[Shosuro Imako]] was conditional — support for the Scorpion claim *in exchange for* a pledge of non-intervention in the [[Crane Clan|Crane]]–[[Lion Clan|Lion]] treaty — and the Scorpion deferred rather than close. An offer dies with its condition. She is free, and the answer should be short and ready: they were offered terms, they declined to take them, and the offer went with them.

There is a debt to note and she may not want to pay it. [[Ide Tsubame]], in the middle of losing her temper at a [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]], stopped to credit [[Shiba Midori]] in open court for withdrawing her own evidence rather than sell a lie to an Imperial Magistrate. A [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] advocate went out of her way to say that about a magistrate on the other side of the table, while losing. That is worth more than it cost her, and it was not a tactic.

[[Shiba Midori]] was invited to speak, asked to postpone, and went to her rooms without a word to anyone. No one has said why, and guessing is premature. But she has now sat silent through two sessions of a matter she withdrew her own evidence from, and whatever she is carrying she is carrying it alone.

And there is a letter in a [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]]'s quarters that [[Doji Setsuna]] has not seen, found by a [[Dragon Clan|Dragon]] in a cell who has no reason to keep it quiet and every reason not to. Her yojimbo was assigned to her as a punishment by a lord who called him a thief and a disgrace, and told him to keep the annoying bird alive if he ever wants to come back into favour. Everything he has done at her side reads one way before that sentence and another way after it. She will find out. It would be better if she found out from him.
