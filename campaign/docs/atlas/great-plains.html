Great Plains

The Great Plains is a vast expanse of open land characterized by rolling grasslands and occasional clusters of trees. This region features unique flora and fauna, with a climate that ranges from warm summers to cold winters. The inhabitants include the Shinjō family and other nomadic tribes, who maintain a deep connection to the land and its spirits. Their culture is rich in traditions, particularly in horsemanship and spiritual practices, reflecting a lifestyle that often involves traveling with herds.

The Great Plains hold significant spiritual importance, as evidenced by the sensitivity shown by a shopkeeper in the City Of The Rich Frog when discussing the region. The Shopkeeper offered steppe-themed curios, including saddle charms and trinkets, in response to inquiries about spiritually active items, indicating the cultural relevance of such items to the Shinjō and their heritage.
