For the Player

Player Notes

Working papers kept from Setsuna's side of the table — what she knows, what she owes, and what is still open.

✎
This is the player's document, not the Game Master's. Nothing here is authoritative about the world — it records what Doji Setsuna has been told, what has been inferred, and what the player intends. The GM's plans are not in it, and no claim here should be treated as confirmed unless it is tagged as such.
❖
Every claim is tagged: TABLE established in play · SOURCE verified in the books or the sheet · HEARD told to Setsuna, truth unverified · OPEN unanswered · PLAN the player's intention · SUPERSEDED no longer true · NOTE a caution.

What Setsuna knows

Standing facts worth having in front of you. The full session-by-session record is in the Chronicle and on each person's page; this holds only what keeps mattering. Started at session 39 — earlier sessions are in the chronicle, not here.

  • TABLE The escort rides west with the envoy. Miya Misato carries the Emperor's writ ordering the Lion to stand down, and the road runs toward the front where Shinjo Harunobu is held. Duty and want run through the same gate.
  • TABLE Karahaya is the governor's officer and is now the envoy's yojimbo. She beat Bayushi Monban in a Warrior's Duel without taking a hit. Competent, gave him two chances to withdraw, and reports to Governor Miya Tetsuya.
  • TABLE Ikoma Tadayoshi has a standing instruction to watch the magistrates — given by the governor, in case their methods endanger the envoy more than the assassins do.
  • HEARD A fresh containment for the Ifrit needs new Meishōdō boxes and a Crane shugenja. The old vessels are spent. From Shiba Midori.
  • TABLE The war is about honour, not the marriage. Matsu Maro said it outright: not the match, not Lord Ikoma's feelings. The refusal was a direct insult, and in Rokugan a direct insult is answered by a duel — which, between clans, is this war. Restore the honour another way and the Lion can stand down.
  • TABLE The Lion have garrisoned nothing. Monban established it on the march: no outposts behind them, no ground held — only burning and desecration of Unicorn land and Unicorn dead. A punitive expedition, not a conquest. SUPERSEDED this closes the open thread from session 42; she has it now, and from her own yojimbo.
  • TABLE Akodo Akihito is Akodo Toturi's nephew. Close enough to the Lion succession that a marriage into him answers the insult. His castle is the column's destination and he outranks Setsuna — there is no leverage, only the pitch.
  • TABLE No clan-level match existed above what Altansarnai was offered. Every other Champion married, the wrong sex, or unknown. The only step up is an Imperial marriage — which is why the question of a hidden suitor is live.
  • NOTE Three separate crews have now checked a face in the dark and let the wrong person go — the road ambush, the men at the docks, and the night raid on Midori's room. Not the same people; the same method.
  • TABLE Matsu Maro was sent to die by her own side. Read off her by Midori at the tea: die on the field or die holding a castle that cannot be held, either suits her superiors. She has her peace now, and she still cannot say why she needed it.
  • TABLE Moto Gaheris's real war is with the Shinjo, not the Lion. Primacy inside the Unicorn, frozen while the whole clan musters against an outside enemy. Peace here gives him back the war he wants — which is why he took it.
  • TABLE Her office does not win rooms; it builds the moment when the Emperor's voice can. Gaheris could take terms from neither a magistrate nor a Lion general. Two hours of groundwork bought Misato one sentence. Deploy Imperial authority last, when it lands, not first, when it gets tested.
  • NOTE She has been on the Snow Plain twice. Once as Matsu Morozane's memory three centuries back, once in her own body. Both times Lion against Unicorn on that ground, both decided by single combat between commanders, both leaving a field of Lion dead. She is the only person in the column who knows how the first one ended.
  • NOTE Session 40 is a hole, and so is the road north. No record survives of the night Monban was ejected and challenged, and none at all of the sessions between 2 March and 6 April. Both are flagged where they sit in the chronicle.

Open threads

Questions the player is carrying, and who is expected to answer them.

  • OPEN The Lion escort turned out to be the relief force. The Matsu contingent crossing at the Rich Frog became the column Setsuna now rides with, under Matsu Maro — who was sent by her own side to lose it. The price the governor warned about was never named and still has not been paid.
  • OPEN Did the ronin take the last shinobi alive? Two were dead or dying; one reached the water under fire. Unanswered, and the column has since left the island.
  • OPEN Battlemaiden Castle is the next stop, and it is the two Champions themselves. Shinjo Altansarnai — Unicorn Champion, and the woman whose broken betrothal is the whole war — is investing it against the Lion Champion Akodo Toturi. The party rides there under a Moto Khan whose ambition is to displace the Shinjo. Decide before arrival whether that is a problem or the opportunity.
  • PLAN Keep working the Shinjo lever. Gaheris's real war is inside his own clan. Everything offered to him should be priced against what it does to that, not against the Lion.
  • OPEN Misato is dying. Four-round medical timer running into the next session. Whether she lives sets the shape of everything after it.
  • PLAN The ultimatum to both commanders. Help clear their names and back the investigation, or answer for having let an Imperial Envoy be taken in their custody. Not a threat — a description of where bushidō has already put them, delivered with absolute formal correctness. Paragon of Courtesy used as the weapon.
  • OPEN It was an extraction, not an assassination. Nobody in that tent was killed who could have been. That means whoever ordered it wanted Misato in their hands, not in the ground — a different order, from different people, for different reasons.
  • OPEN Whose armour, whose perimeter, whose watch? Brown armour on the man holding Tadayoshi. At least one attacker away into the dark. Bodies in the tent to identify.
  • OPEN The Onahime witness problem. Midori and Kazumi saw it; possibly Tadayoshi; possibly Misato if she lives. Setsuna does not know yet — she was on the floor before it showed. She will have to address it once she does.
  • PLAN Use the Altansarnai back channel, and quickly. A Champion may be able to nullify the betrothal on her own authority, which would end the war without anyone conceding anything. The channel is open and unused, and she is two tents away.
  • OPEN Nobody has asked Shinjo Altansarnai anything. Every plan on the table turns on her or her daughter, and all of it is second-hand from a Lion general who admits she never understood the woman. The daughter's willingness is a rumour repeated by an enemy. Altansarnai is at Battlemaiden Castle, which is where the road goes.
  • OPEN Why did the Unicorn cede the river bank at all? The concession was extraordinary and Lord Ikoma was not the one who negotiated it. Rich, river-watered breadbasket the Lion administered for the eight centuries the Unicorn were away.
  • OPEN The Scorpion architect. Bayushi Kachiko opposed the betrothal, per Soshi Aisha; the Scorpion gain from both clans bleeding; Monban agreed with the reading. Deliberately shelved until the fighting stops — shelved, not dismissed.
  • PLAN Pitch the double marriage to Akodo Akihito. Headline: Altansarnai's daughter to Akihito — Shinjo blood into the Lion succession, the insult discharged, and the Champion not forced into a match she has already refused. Bonus: the Unicorn prisoner Akihito holds, married to him as well, folding a hostage release into the treaty.
  • PLAN Fallbacks, in order. The poison-pill reparations (betrothal upheld, reparations payable only if the marriage completes, priced past what the Lion can bear, so both sides waive); then Misato as substitute bride. The second is a bargaining position, not a preference.
  • NOTE The Imperial-suitor claim is a lie with the Emperor's name on it. Nobody could easily disprove it, which is exactly what makes using it dangerous in a document Setsuna signs. She noticed the undisprovability before she noticed anything else about it.
  • NOTE No wear-down strategy will work. The Lion will not tire before the Unicorn do. Anything that waits for exhaustion is off the table.
  • NOTE The spirit is tabled, not spent. Still inside Monban, and Setsuna has explicitly reserved it as leverage against a recalcitrant party. Let's not do it now.
  • OPEN Has anyone interviewed Midori's attendant? She saw someone going through Shiba Midori's belongings and is the only eyewitness in the case. Raised at session 38, not yet acted on.
  • OPEN The Firebiter bag above the Crane quarters. Doji Masato and Doji Miho deny it and say a shinobi planted it. The evidence does not distinguish between that and guilt.
  • OPEN No answer on Shinjo Harunobu's custody. Unanswered since session 27. The badge has not moved it; obligation once did.
  • OPEN How far has the song travelled? Daidoji Shin is composing on the duel and the governor's ronin are already saying the unmasked Scorpion.

Obligations and debts

Favours owed and owing, in both directions, with what was given for them.

  • TABLE Setsuna → Miya Misato. Protector/Ward Bond. Two nights of teaching: rhetoric, then how a government moves and how to fight with her hands. She has since walked her to a gate in the dark and out between two armies.
  • TABLE Monban → Karahaya, paid. Apology to her and her men, and a public withdrawal of what he said about their lord. Given on the dock in front of the town.
  • TABLE Midori → the Ifrit. Undertook to carry it west and release it in the Burning Sands. It is riding inside Monban until then.
  • HEARD The Lion escort comes with a price. The governor warned Tadayoshi about it privately rather than in company — which reads as political rather than material.
  • TABLE Moto Gaheris holds Monban, and Setsuna has claimed him aloud. Ransom floated, not settled. His Scorpion lord will owe for the recovery — and she has publicly reattached herself to a yojimbo she dismissed at session 34.
  • TABLE Maro → Setsuna. She opened a castle gate at night on nothing but a courteous letter, a well-chosen gift, and a recognisable horse. That is a debt in the other direction and it has not been called.
  • NOTE What actually moved Harunobu before was Kitsu Takeko yielding on the strength of Akodo Akihito's service. Obligation, not authority. Keep working that channel.

The long game

The document Setsuna has been drafting in her head since the beginning, and what it is really for. Player intent, not established fact — none of this has been proposed to anyone at the table.

  • PLAN The Toshi Ranbo treaty. Crane formally cede a city they no longer hold in practice — generous-looking, cheap. The Lion take the city, a stipend, and the duty of securing the Drowned Merchant River: honoured in martial terms, employed in a defined role. Crane and Unicorn split the uncapped trade upside on the river the Lion are now obliged to police.
  • NOTE It gives every clan what they say they value and locks the Lion into a permanent economic ceiling. Territory and martial purpose for the Lion, economic position for the Crane, trade access for the Unicorn. A polite knife. A Crane built it — a Crane trained by a Scorpion.
  • PLAN Doji Emika closes it. A Doji–Matsu marriage at the end, folding the personal restoration into the political settlement. This is why Setsuna declined Misato's request — Emika is the one chip she will not spend on the immediate crisis.
  • NOTE The treaty is a peace offering to Matsu Tomoe. Her grandmother: a Matsu who married into the Doji and never stopped being a Lion, furious about Toshi Ranbo lost and furious about the granddaughter she got — one who fears death, refused the sword, and built a life out of courtesy and procedure. The treaty answers both of her needs while refusing her surface demands. I will return your city. I will send Emika back to your family. You will have to accept the granddaughter you got.
  • NOTE The cabinet, and it is leaking. Monban hosts the spirit and wants to go west to release it. Setsuna will not let him, and has filed — without saying so to anyone, least of all to him — that she holds a catastrophic-violence option she could authorise. She is negotiating in good faith and keeping the weapon at the same time without letting either touch the other. Onahime showing herself this session is the deadline moving.
  • NOTE Gaheris is closer to it than he knows. He suspects the Crane of engineering this war. He is wrong about this war and right about the shape: a Crane is quietly rearranging the north to Crane–Unicorn benefit at Lion expense, and that Crane was sitting in his tent saying nothing.
