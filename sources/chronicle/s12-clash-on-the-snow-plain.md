## Narrative

!lede The [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] had [[Kitsu Somalia]] in a pincer — one cohort pressing her front toward the water, another round on her flank throwing fire — and it was [[Akodo Toronoko]] who broke it. She set her jaw, drew her katana and took the centre's yari samurai into the flank of the cohort that had turned its back on her to do it: Righteous Example, three successes and four more off the strife she was carrying, and elite troops on top of that. Ten measures of attrition and panic into an army that had been certain the Lion centre was pinned in the mud. It also dropped the difficulty of every other Lion leader's next attack, which was the point of doing it that way.

[[Kitsu Somalia]] took the opening and answered the fire with fire. She called a Wall of Fire down the length of the Unicorn line, anchored on their own standards, spent Void to make it land, and got three rounds of it — dazed men, burning men, six attrition and six panic, and a line the Unicorn could not come through. *They put us on fire first*, she said. The Unicorn offensive halted where the flames went up.

The one thing the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] did not win was the clash.

[[Matsu Morozane]] went into the second round of it behind on initiative and bought first move anyway, and it did not save him. The [[Unicorn Officer]] came at him with the speed of a thunderbolt and both scimitars turning like water, cleared his guard, and put a blade at his throat. *Yield, and you will be spared.* [[Matsu Morozane]] was one strife off his limit and had no yielding in him. What he had was a whistle he had spent an opportunity on a round earlier, and a lion who had heard it.

[[Shiguro Chinmoku]] came over the officer's back after the clash was already lost — the distinction mattered to [[Matsu Morozane]] and he said so — and went for the critical rather than the kill. The man will carry that injury for the rest of his life. The field around them broke down into pandemonium, both sides scrambling to drag their own commanders clear, and that is the only reason [[Matsu Morozane]] left the [[Snow Plain]] on a Lion stretcher instead of a Unicorn one.

The clash went to the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]], and with it the objective. Both armies had spent the day trying to make the other show what they were made of, and it was the Unicorn who finished it.

Then the Forest of Spears withdrew across the marsh — not broken, not hurried, an orderly walk backwards as though answering a signal nobody on the Lion side could see. [[Matsu Sakura]] was up on the hilltop with the historian [[Ikoma Hideri]], whose muddy scholar's robe made an odd shape in front of all that lacquer, and [[Ikoma Hideri]] had already found the answer in a book. *This tactic is attested in Kuni Kadokatsu's account of the skirmish of the Ridge of Bones. If the Unicorn appear to be in retreat, then what they desire is that we pursue.* [[Matsu Sakura]] was furious and ordering the advance anyway. [[Akodo Toronoko]] talked her out of it — reminding her, with a courtesy she had to spend a point of Void to find, that her own cohort had spent the morning stuck in the mud of their traps.

The scouts went out and found the rest of it: spiked pit traps seeded across the field and covered over with foliage. *You were right*, [[Matsu Sakura]] said. She had taken it for a version of the famous feigned retreat on horseback. [[Ikoma Hideri]] said the part that was worth writing down. Pit traps are close-in defensive work, quite at odds with how the Unicorn fight in open field, and the ruse took near-perfect coordination; perhaps they learned it from the [[Crab Clan|Crab]] at the [[The Wall|Wall]] — *but perhaps it was not their commander's idea at all.* She trailed off there. *Brevity, Madam Historian*, said [[Matsu Sakura]], *is more needed on the battlefield.*

The [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] reformed on a hilltop above the paddies with their general's battle standard moved from the rear of the army to the front, and [[Shinjo Kamo]] rode out between the two forces with a walking honour guard and a [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] on a small Rokugani pony. He did not dismount for [[Matsu Sakura]]. She ignored it and demanded he quit the plain; he refused flatly; and the Scorpion introduced herself as [[Shoshuro Amane]], brought along to make peace. Neither general would have it.

[[Matsu Morozane]] was not on his feet for the start of it. He was flat with his lion licking him and one of his own men working on the wound, and he took what the first aid gave him and put Warrior's Resolve on the rest, which got him up and into the honour guard before the two generals met.

[[Akodo Toronoko]] read the general instead of speaking. Martial skill of five, possibly better than her own commander's, and — from the way his troops carried themselves after the day they had — an inspiring one, which is why the Unicorn had not panicked nearly as much as they should have. [[Kitsu Somalia]] tried to read the Scorpion and got nothing at all back. Inscrutable, and a person she had never heard of.

[[Matsu Morozane]] did something worse than useless. He paid the ten honour and ten glory it costs to speak over a general at a parley, told [[Matsu Sakura]] he would be her champion and that there should be no peace with these people, and put Call the Wild on [[Shinjo Kamo]]'s Utaku steed. The technique failed and the horse was enraged anyway. It began stamping and champing; he squared off with it and it squared off back. *I would have your name, disrespectful pup*, said [[Shinjo Kamo]], looking down. [[Matsu Morozane]] bowed to his own general, turned to the Unicorn, and whinnied at him. *You take advice from a horse? I thought you were a Lion.* [[Matsu Sakura]], scarlet: *Their name is Matsu Morozane, and they gave your left flank quite a beating.* And [[Shinjo Kamo]], placing him at last — the one whose lion savaged his cousin's son-in-law — said it would be a pleasure to collect his head.

The [[Lion Clan|Lion]] withdrew for the night with less than a fifth of the advance force lost and the worst of it in [[Kitsu Somalia]]'s cohort. [[Akodo Toronoko]] used Preserve Strength on the army with [[Matsu Morozane]] assisting, the only two commanders with any medicine between them. [[Kitsu Somalia]] called Inari's Blessing twice — a fortifying meal, once for her own cohort and once for the army, six casualties taken back off the ledger. Then [[Akodo Toronoko]] went to the [[Ikoma Family|Ikoma]] archivist and dictated her account of the first day, and [[Ikoma Hideri]] gave her paper and ink out of a supply she was already rationing and wrote it down. On her way back [[Akodo Kagekatsu]] found her. *I heard your grand lion-jaw strategy got stuck in the mud. Are we going to maul them with mud-covered paws now?* He allowed that she and the scouts had been right about the trap. *Still, we almost lost using your tactics. Maybe tomorrow she will choose mine.*

[[Ikoma Akuyaku]] waited for full dark, which dropped the cost of crossing the open plain to almost nothing, and went. Plain, then rocks, then the belt of forest, then a way into the camp. The rope broke on the rocks. He had two water opportunities in hand as he went and spent both of them on not being seen and not screaming, and he fell four range bands onto the back side of the mountain. It broke his foot badly enough that it might as well have been taken off, and the cold that was going to kill him was the same cold that stopped him bleeding to death. Before he wrapped the foot and passed out he put one arrow into the [[Western Watchtower]] — from ground the tower could not see, because he had fallen behind the mountain rather than in front of it — knowing exactly what an arrow out of the dark would set moving.

In the [[Lion Camp|Lion camp]] [[Kitsu Somalia]] sleeps light. Urgent voices outside her tent woke her and were not her own guard; she came out to see a shadow move and stop being there. [[Akodo Kayamayako]] was standing by, out walking to clear her head after working on the wounded of [[Kitsu Somalia]]'s own cohort, and she had found someone prowling around the general's tent and tried to take hold of them, and they were gone before she got a look at the face. *I wouldn't put it past the Unicorn to assassinate some of our finest generals and shugenja to cripple our units.* [[Kitsu Somalia]] asked why the guard had not been doubled. Because the men have to sleep, and there are only as many as can be spared with this many wounded, and they are stretched thin as it is.

By morning an unseasonably early snowfall had half-buried the camp and remade the whole field. [[Kitsu Somalia]] wondered aloud whether there was something behind it and did not have time to find out. [[Matsu Morozane]] went to the general early and prostrated himself over the business with the horse; [[Matsu Sakura]] said only that it should not happen again, and that she would give him the chance at those generals another time — it should have been his fight.

The council was short and cold. No more supplies can be assumed; garrisons must be established before full winter; the snow is cover for a surprise attack. [[Matsu Hisoka]] said the risk was too high. [[Kitsu Yue]] said they would be waiting forever. The snow had reburied every pit trap on the field, which is a problem for whoever has to walk on it and for whoever dug it, unless the Unicorn kept the men who dug them or a good enough map. The marsh had gone from ensnaring to something closer to quicksand, and the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] still had the Lion penned in the worst of it. Someone raised taking the village for supplies, which means splitting the army in front of an enemy who can reach it downhill first.

So [[Matsu Morozane]] proposed the mountains. Take a force up through the [[Crystal Pass]], around, and down by the lake, and hit them from behind while the rest of the army walks up the middle. He staked five honour and five glory on being allowed to say it, because he was in no position to say anything, and [[Kitsu Somalia]] backed him. *It is bold. I like it*, said [[Matsu Sakura]]. *Take your forces and do that. We will march to pin them down.*

And then a runner came through the gate. [[Ikoma Akuyaku]] was carried in on a stretcher — blue, frozen, bedraggled, one foot in a crude splint and an arrow in his backside put there by a Unicorn counter-scout who was a very good shot. [[Matsu Sakura]] had written him off when he missed first light. *By the kami, what did you do to yourself?* said [[Matsu Hisoka]]. He smiled and handed over a map of the [[Unicorn Clan War Camp]]. A quick one.

[[Kitsu Somalia]] put Path to Inner Peace through him while he talked, and what he had was thin and good. He had not learned as much as he hoped. The scouting on that side was tight and the watchtower never slept. He had not seen anything like the cavalry he expected, and no sign of horses moved out either. No traffic of requests for reinforcement; after the snow began, one messenger, probably carrying an update. Nothing fresh will reach that camp now unless it is already on the road. They want the fight. And they had been pleased about a confrontation with one of the units — a commander made a fool of — though he never caught which side's commander it was. He had been pinched against the mountains and had to pull out before he was found. What he could add about the mountain was that the snow up there had collected at the top and come down, so anyone crossing that slope would be watched the whole way.

## Learned

- Akodo Toronoko: Broke the pincer on Somalia's cohort with Righteous Example and the centre's yari samurai. Talked Matsu Sakura out of pursuing the Unicorn withdrawal. Used Preserve Strength on the army that night, and dictated her account of the first day to Ikoma Hideri.
- Kitsu Somalia: Answered the Unicorn fire with a Wall of Fire down their own line, spending Void on it, and stopped their offensive for three rounds. Cast Inari's Blessing twice in camp. Could read nothing at all off Shoshuro Amane. Woke to an intruder in the Lion camp, and treated Akuyaku the next morning.
- Matsu Morozane: Lost the clash, refused to yield with a blade at his throat, and only escaped capture because both armies broke ranks to pull their commanders out. Forfeited ten honour and ten glory to speak over his general at the parley, enraged the Unicorn general's horse and whinnied at the man. Apologised the next morning, and then proposed and was granted the flanking march through the Crystal Pass.
- Unicorn Officer: Won the clash — first move, both scimitars, a blade at Morozane's throat and an offer of quarter. Took a crippling injury from Shiguro Chinmoku after the clash was decided, and was dragged off the field by his own troops.
- Shiguro Chinmoku: Went over the winning officer's back on a whistle given a round earlier, and left him a permanent injury.
- Ikoma Akuyaku: Crossed the plain unseen after dark, broke his rope on the rocks and fell four range bands, spending everything he had left on not being seen and not screaming. Broke his foot, shot the Western Watchtower from where it could not see him, and was carried in the next morning with a map of the Unicorn camp, an arrow in him, and one fact: still no cavalry.
- Ikoma Hideri: The Lion historian on the field, in a scholar's robe and out of paper. Named the Unicorn withdrawal as a documented feint out of Kuni Kadokatsu's account of the Ridge of Bones, and said out loud that the pit traps were unlike anything the Unicorn do in open field and might not have been their commander's idea. Matsu Sakura told her to be brief.
- Matsu Sakura: Demanded the Unicorn quit the plain and was refused to her face by a general who would not dismount. Wanted to pursue the withdrawal and was talked down. Approved the mountain flank.
- Shinjo Kamo: The Unicorn general. Martial skill of five, possibly better than Matsu Sakura, and an inspiring enough commander that his troops had not panicked as they should have. Stayed mounted through the parley, refused both the demand and the peace, and promised to collect Morozane's head.
- Shoshuro Amane: The Scorpion who rode to the parley on a Rokugani pony with the Unicorn honour guard, announced herself as there to make peace, and was refused by both generals. Kitsu Somalia could not read her at all and had never heard of her.
- Akodo Kayamayako: The Lion physic. Caught someone prowling at the general's tent in the night and could not hold them or see their face. Said plainly that both armies have spies in each other's camps and that the Unicorn would kill Lion commanders and shugenja if they could.
- Akodo Kagekatsu: Toronoko's rival. Conceded she had been right about the trap and used the day's losses against her anyway.
- Matsu Hisoka: Argued the snow-cover attack carried too much risk.
- Kitsu Yue: Argued against waiting, and for acting soon.
- Crystal Pass: The route through the mountains above the Snow Plain, wide enough to move a unit through. Descends by the lakeside behind the Unicorn position. Nobody could say how long it would take.

## Setsuna

The first thing to hold on to is that the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] lost the day. Not the battle — the objective. Two armies spent a day trying to force the other to show its commanders, and the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] completed it and the Lion did not, on a clash her own ancestor lost with a blade at his throat. Whatever the traditional account of the [[Snow Plain]] is, it is not describing the opening she has now watched.

The second is [[Ikoma Hideri]], and Setsuna should take her seriously, because nobody on that hilltop did. **She was there, she was writing, and she said the useful thing out loud before anybody else had thought it.** The pit traps were wrong for the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] — close-in defensive work from a clan that fights in the open, executed with a coordination that is not how they fight either — and she got as far as *perhaps it was not their commander's idea at all* before her general told her to be brief. That is the earliest anyone at the [[Snow Plain]] reaches for a third hand on the field, and it comes from the one person there whose job was to write it down, and whose account of this battle did not survive her.

[[Akodo Toronoko]] dictated her impressions of the first day into that same account, on the historian's own rationed paper. So at least one honest record of this day existed, in [[Ikoma Hideri]]'s hand, and something happened to it.

Then the night. Somebody was at [[Matsu Sakura]]'s tent, [[Akodo Kayamayako]] put a hand out and missed them, and they were gone before she saw a face — and the following morning, in a council held by an army that knew it had been infiltrated, [[Matsu Morozane]] stood up and described the [[Crystal Pass]] route out loud. She should mark the order of those two things and not lean on them yet.

What she cannot take from this session is the mines. Nobody mentions them. Not the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] council, not the scouts, not the historian, and not [[Ikoma Akuyaku]], who climbed the hill they are supposed to be on and fell off it. The one substantive thing he brought back is the same thing [[Matsu Morozane]] sold as a joke two days earlier and nobody has been able to explain since — there are not enough horses. No cavalry in the camp, no sign of cavalry moved out of it, and now no reinforcement traffic either.

And [[Shoshuro Amane]] was at that parley, on a pony, with the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] honour guard rather than beside either army, offering a peace both generals refused within a minute. [[Kitsu Somalia]] looked straight at her and read nothing. That is a fact worth more than an interpretation of it. The [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] case says they rendered assistance at the [[Snow Plain]]; Setsuna has now seen a Scorpion standing on that field in the company of the side that won it, and still cannot say what she was doing there.
