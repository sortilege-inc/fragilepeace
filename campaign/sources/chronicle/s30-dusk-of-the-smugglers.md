## Narrative

!lede Over dumplings they finished the ritual papers and [[Shiba Midori]] put the objection in its sharpest form yet. It is not that the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] use spirits — anyone may petition one. It is the difference between temporarily asking a servant of heaven to serve, and telling a servant of heaven to get in the box. They talked about the [[Yobanjin]] too, the northern tribes on the [[Phoenix Clan|Phoenix]] border whom Rokugan has been trying to absorb for a thousand years and who keep declining, and about [[Lady Tsubasa]]'s history with them and the peace she brokered.

Then they went to work on a reasonable chain of inference. If the artifacts are moving out, they are moving through [[The Docks|the Unicorn District docks]], because that is where they come from. The goods are small, so the tell is not cargo but people meeting people and handing things over — which is unremarkable in Rokugan and especially among the Unicorn, who are serious about gifts. Which leaves the hour as the only thing that is strange. And the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] have been having no luck, which probably means the ring has identified the Lion's agents as law and gone quiet.

So they went in as buyers. [[Shiba Midori]] is hunting a twin of the tea set from [[Ikoma Kotone]]'s teahouse — possibly as a present for [[Akodo Akihito]] — and since the original read as spiritually active, the twin would be meishōdō or meishōdō-compatible. She is in her good shugenja robes and would not spill sake on them, so she dabbed it on her face, where it evaporates and leaves the smell. Two samurai women out in a rough part of town looking for exactly the right amount of excitement, with bodyguards so it does not become too much.

[[Doji Setsuna]] made no attempt at stealth whatsoever. She was on a walk with her friend. [[Bayushi Monban]] hung two range bands back in his folding armour, giving appropriate social distance to his betters and failing to look casual, because folding armour marks a man as [[Crab Clan|Crab]]. [[Kakita Kazumi]] took the alleys and then the rooftops and was not seen again by anyone all night.

[[Bayushi Monban]] could have called on an informant here — there is always a [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] to deal with in a city this size — and decided not to, on the grounds that the question put to an informant tells the informant what the questioner is doing. He would rather know less than be known.

A few feet down the alley toward the water they both heard someone moving parallel to them at speed, and a man in a low straw hat stepped out ahead. *Ladies, unless you fancy a midnight swim, you are well away from where you ought to be.* He had come through the back alleys to get in front of them.

[[Doji Setsuna]] invited him to escort them. He accepted the compliment and refused the errand — the shops she wanted would not be open at this hour, but he could walk them back and point out the best ones in the morning. *It is not safe, because there are no lights down there and no rails. One could quickly find oneself in the water and, weighted down by those lovely robes, have quite a difficult time swimming back to shore. I would spare you the humiliation, the embarrassment, and the danger.*

He then walked them parallel to the docks rather than onto them, hugging the shoreline and the parts with railings, putting himself between them and where they wanted to go — and never once laying a hand on anyone. [[Bayushi Monban]] cut it short. *The ladies may go where they wish. Do not worry about them falling in the water.* And, leaning close: *I can swim.* The man looked at the mask, decided this was above his pay grade, and changed direction.

Then he lit a lantern, put it on a pole, and carried it ahead of them — swinging it, and finding every board in the dock that could creak. In a part of the city that had been dark and quiet, his light became the most prominent thing in it. He was not guiding them. He was making them impossible to miss.

The disguise did not hold either. Well-worn travelling clothes, straw sandals, a cracked rice hat — and a daishō in perfect condition, which is what gave him away to [[Kakita Kazumi]]. He claimed to take tea at the great teahouse now and then when he had a couple of zeni spare, which is what a tourist would say and not what it costs. And when [[Doji Setsuna]] asked, very courteously, for his family and his lord so that she could send thanks afterwards: *with respect, my lady, I am not so fortunate as to have either. I must make ends meet as I am able.*

Pressed on why he was down here at all, his story came apart — he happened to be in the area, coming from a bar, going to where he was staying, and saw two noble ladies about to do something unthinkable. [[Bayushi Monban]] put his arms out to halt the women and dismissed him: his duty was discharged, he could go.

He went, just as loudly, slung the lantern over his shoulder, and pulled a handheld gong out of his bag.

[[Bayushi Monban]] got there between the first strike and the second — a drawn short sword, the sheath jammed between hammer and gong, and the blade at the man's throat. *Make another sound and it will be your last, dog.*

*Kill me, and the family that I found will never stop hunting. Back off, Scorpion. This is not your domain.*

[[Doji Setsuna]] stopped it — *your point has been made* — and turned on the ronin: he would not threaten a [[Crane Clan|Crane]]. And the drunk noblewoman was gone out of her voice entirely.

What came out of him then was not what anyone had built the evening around. The [[Kaeru Ronin|Kaeru]] have been tailing the smugglers for weeks. He had been told about the magistrates already, by name, by [[Kaeru Akiara]] — who had said they would inevitably be poking their noses into matters that did not concern them. And the gong was not a signal to accomplices. It was the sound of a man aborting his own side's operation. *I would rather scare off the fish than have you bungle the catch.*

That reframed the whole street. They had not walked into a smuggler's lookout. They had walked into a stakeout and been steered out of it by someone whose orders were to keep samurai away from the water.

[[Doji Setsuna]] offered to be useful instead — *would you not like us to draw fish to you?* — and was told they already have half the men they are looking for; the other half will either do something desperate or be caught. But since the magistrates were going to press on regardless, collaboration would serve them both. He gave it up: there is a ship in the harbour already carrying some of the goods, and what they want is proof it is taking on more. A delivery was expected tonight and had not come, and the party walking in may well have prevented it. If they would lie low for a few hours, there might be an answer by morning — *but staying and waiting, according to Akiara, did not exactly seem to be your style.*

The way to his superior is the phrase *red shoes*.

He needled them while he led. *Is this how you normally get your information? I am concerned for the state of the Empire's greatest bringers of justice.* [[Bayushi Monban]] answered that it is better to take them alive so they can confess, and was told he would have expected a very different view from a man in his position. [[Doji Setsuna]] said, with a shudder, that they have learned death does not prevent a man becoming a witness — and the green showed in [[Bayushi Monban]]'s eyes.

They split three ways. [[Doji Setsuna]] and [[Bayushi Monban]] went to meet the superior. [[Shiba Midori]] stayed on [[The Docks|the docks]] — *she is far more capable than the two of us put together* — and [[Kakita Kazumi]], whom nobody had mentioned and the ronin had not counted, stayed on the roofs.

From up there he heard it first: a pole working against the current, twenty minutes or half an hour later, a small craft coming up the river the hard way toward a large dark shape. He went across the rooftops after it and settled on a warehouse roof above the ship's deck. The boat slid into the one wedge of shadow between the deck lanterns, the dock, and the moon — invisible to anyone not already following it — and tapped three times on the hull.

A sailor dropped a line over a low section of rail where a gangplank would go. The boat found it and tugged. A crane swung out; a second man lowered a net on a hook; there was shuffling in the dark, two tugs from below, and a few bags or bushels came up and onto the deck. The whole operation took about five times longer than it needed to, because every gear and winch and pulley was being worked slowly enough not to squeak. From anywhere but that roof, nothing was happening at all.

He read the ground while he watched. Poor light everywhere, so the terrain is obscuring; the cargo net would entangle anyone who fell in it. Nothing is dangerous. Nothing feels defiled. None of the hallmarks. Whatever this is, it is not the first enemy's work. He also noted the conspicuous absence of any security at all.

Then a hand went over [[Shiba Midori]]'s mouth and a small knife went into her back. *You're supposed to be on the docks. Drop your coin purse.* She complied. They stood her up, walked her along, and put her in the river, in heavy robes not meant for swimming, at three or four in the morning.

[[Kakita Kazumi]] threw a knife at the fleeing figure at four range bands and heard a soft thud and a yelp; he could not tell whether he had hit or merely terrified. He dropped to the dock and threw [[Shiba Midori]] a rope rather than diving in — she could get herself out from there — picked up his knife on the run, and found it had blood on it, which made the trail easy. He would rather nobody in the party looked closely at that knife.

He ran the thief through the [[Warehouse District]], closing steadily because her martial skill was nothing, until she stumbled turning a corner. His katana cleaved through the makeshift club she raised, through her straw hat, and very nearly through her head; two pieces of hat fell in the road. She scrambled up and ran anyway, so he threw the katana — using the memory of [[Akodo Akihito]], who has never thrown a blade in his life but would find it ridiculously good — and put it through her foot. She went down.

He knelt on her, took the hilt, turned it slightly, and smiled. *Now, friend. Why did you have to push that nice young lady into the river? She didn't smell that bad, did she?* — *Please. I only wanted her body. You can have it. Please let me live.* — *I will let you live. But you owe me your life.*

Back at the water, [[Shiba Midori]] dragged herself out and heard heavy footsteps coming along the boards behind her, from the direction [[Kakita Kazumi]] had gone. She got a hand to her daishō. And she heard one of them say it: *boss says we need one more to get out of here tomorrow.* And then, almost kindly: *it is only bad luck, but we will have to make an exception in your case.* Something silent moved through the air.

The [[Ronin Headquarters]] is a large house made over into something between a barracks and a dojo. There was no room fit for lords, so they were put at a low table with ratty cushions and told the sake was probably half drunk already but they were welcome to what was left. The man already in the room looked up at their guide and said: *Naito, you were supposed to be watching the docks. The smugglers could move tonight.* — *These fine samurai were insistent on a moonlight tour of the docks. I had to sound the alarm to make sure they would not scare away the fish.*

[[Doji Setsuna]] established that [[Kaeru Akiara]] is the one minding the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] samurai, and named her: [[Matsu Ren]]. The operation has taken weeks to set up and months to prepare, to catch a notorious ring red-handed with the goods and quite possibly others besides. The smugglers, once taken, face the justice of [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]]. And the offence is both halves at once — the unpaid docking fees and customs duties, and the nature of what is being moved.

She read the room while they talked: these smugglers have been a thorn in these particular ronin's side for a long time, and what they want is the arrest itself. Which told her what to offer. Someone of significance they could make an example of, plus the back revenue, would go a long way.

[[Bayushi Monban]] had a different approach. Why the runaround, why the deceit, why treat them as enemies — his clan gets dragged through the mud for the things it does and here everyone is treated the same; if the ronin are the law of the city and the magistrates are the law of the Empire, why not simply talk, when they could all be at the water right now instead of *in this filth waiting to talk to somebody who is going to be in a pissy mood because they have just been woken in the middle of the night.*

*I am not pissy because I have been woken in the middle of the night. I expect to be woken with the results of the investigation. I am pissy because you threatened two of my men and would not take a clear hint.*

[[Kaeru Haya]] runs the watch on the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] side. She wanted satisfaction from [[Bayushi Monban]] for drawing steel on one of hers — Naito is ronin but he is still samurai, and that is a duel she is entitled to ask for. *I am spoken for*, he said.

[[Doji Setsuna]] took it apart from both ends. To [[Bayushi Monban]], first, and using him against himself: the reason they did not immediately cede the ground is the same reason he had not stepped aside in their own last disagreement when he knew she was making a different point — and she does not begrudge them it. Then to [[Kaeru Haya]]: she had not been briefed, the arrival was a surprise and a distressing one, and yes, they are Emerald Magistrates, and they are working something that overlaps hers, and they would be glad to collaborate — *but that will require your working with us.* — *Now, that I am willing to do.*

[[Bayushi Monban]] then did something nobody at that table expected. He made his own argument, conceded once [[Doji Setsuna]] had the negotiation, and closed with a formal apology: her operation should not have been disrupted in such a way, and she would find them dependable allies. It cost him nothing he valued and earned him six honour, because talking a justified duel down is a real sacrifice — and it lifted him clear of the threshold at which his disdain for courtesy counts against him. [[Doji Setsuna]] worked at not looking surprised.

[[Kaeru Haya]] explained herself in return. She had not had [[Kaeru Akiara]]'s report until after the site was already under surveillance; Naito was new, knew nothing, and had been ordered to observe only — not to engage, not to report. *I said that by way of explanation to your associate. These are three independent people operating with three different goals.* And on the blade: *that I do want satisfaction for. We can discuss it later. It is more important that we catch these men in the act.*

Then the arithmetic went wrong in front of her. [[Doji Setsuna]] mentioned that two other magistrates were at the docks. *Naito said there were three of you.* He had left one at the water — surely her men had an eye on her. They did not. Those were not her men. And Naito, cornered: *but you told me to lead any samurai away from the docks.* — *You did not lead him away from the docks, did you.*

What she has is this. An underground organisation is moving questionable goods, mostly [[Opium]] — which is not illegal, and which still has to pay duty like everything else, and [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]] is losing revenue to it steadily. *But the quantities in this latest shipment are something else.*

There is a large [[Mantis Clan|Mantis]] ship in the harbour that came up the [[Drowned Merchant River]] and has been lingering for days. She arrested about half its crew in a drunken brawl and persuaded her lord not to release them to the captain — deliberately, to ground the ship long enough to catch whoever is supplying it. *But it seems they have taken to alternate methods of recruiting their ship, so to speak. A couple of sailors have gone missing.*

The captain is [[Yoritomo Kuroba]]. [[Bayushi Monban]] hissed. [[Doji Setsuna]] has no idea who that is.

They have not connected the disappearances to the [[Mantis Clan|Mantis]] yet, and had hoped to catch them either taking someone or being supplied. Tonight was to be the night — the captain having made an agitated appearance before the daimyō that same day.

It is close to dawn. [[Kaeru Haya]] will take them down herself, observing that they are hardly inconspicuous, and [[Bayushi Monban]] offered to dress however she thought necessary. By now the business is either done or leaving. *So we will find out whether our net caught anything after all.*

## Setsuna

**[[Kaeru Haya]] stranded that ship on purpose, and the people going missing are the consequence.** She jailed half a [[Mantis Clan|Mantis]] crew and had them held so the vessel could not sail until she found its supplier. A ship that cannot crew itself cannot leave. And somebody on that ship has started making up the difference off the docks at night — which is why a man with a knife told [[Shiba Midori]] to drop her purse and then put her in the river rather than simply taking it, and why the one who came along the boards afterwards said *boss says we need one more to get out of here tomorrow.*

They are not robbing people. They are collecting them. [[Kaeru Haya]] has not connected the disappearances to the [[Mantis Clan|Mantis]] and the connection is her own operation working exactly as designed.

That is the thing to say to her at first light, and it should be said carefully, because it is also the thing that will make her hurry. A captain short of hands with a hold half loaded and a daimyō he has already been shouting at is a man on a clock. If he gets his numbers tonight he sails, and everyone he has taken goes with him.

**They wrecked a stakeout tonight and got something better than they broke.** A delivery was expected and did not arrive, and the ronin believe the party is why. That is a real cost and it is worth being honest about. But it also bought an alliance with the only people in this city who already know the ground, and [[Doji Setsuna]] got it by conceding the point rather than pressing the office — [[Kaeru Haya]] had not been briefed, the arrival was a genuine surprise, and saying so out loud cost nothing and bought everything.

The thing she did best was turn [[Bayushi Monban]] into the argument. He would not step aside for her when he thought she was wrong, so he of all people cannot demand that strangers cede ground on sight. Using a man's own conduct to excuse the person he is angry at is a better instrument than rank, and it left her rank unspent for when she needs it.

**And then he apologised.** Formally, unprompted, to a ronin, after putting a blade to one of her men. He has spent this whole journey being the party's edge, and tonight he chose to be its diplomat and gained honour doing it. That is worth noticing, and it is worth noticing that it happened in the same week he refused an informant because the question would have given him away. Somebody is learning, and she should not make him self-conscious about it.

Two things that are hers to carry. [[Kaeru Haya]] has deferred a duel, not forgiven one, and *we can discuss that later* is a debt with her name on it as much as his. And [[Bayushi Monban]] hissed at [[Yoritomo Kuroba]]'s name, knows the man from somewhere, and did not say where. She will want that before they board anything.

**And the rope runs further than this harbour.** A [[Mantis Clan|Mantis]] ship, lingering, with a captain short of hands and a hold half full. Set that beside [[Daidoji Shin]]'s own hunt — the shugenja who roused the spirits that drowned part of the [[Crane Lands|Crane lands]], whom he believes to be [[Mantis Clan|Mantis]]. And beside a talisman holding a wild spirit out of the [[Burning Sands]], bought by a novelist who left the instructions behind. Three separate threads, one clan, one river. She should not assume they are the same rope, and she should stop being surprised each time they touch.

**[[Kakita Kazumi]] was never counted, and that is now twice.** Kaeru Naito reported three magistrates. [[Kaeru Haya]] believed there were three. Every arrangement made tonight was made by people working from a number that was wrong, and the only reason the night did not end far worse is that the uncounted one was on a roof with a knife. It is an enormous advantage and it belongs to him rather than to the party, and he has a blade he does not want anyone looking at closely.

One last thing, small and not small. [[Kakita Kazumi]] chose the chase over the rescue, threw a rope, and let [[Shiba Midori]] haul herself out of a river in shugenja robes at four in the morning — and she told him to, and was right, and was attacked again while she did it. The disguise worked perfectly. Two noblewomen wandered into a dark district looking wealthy and slightly drunk, and the district did precisely what they invited it to do.
