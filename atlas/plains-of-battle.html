Plains of Battle

The Plains of Battle are located north of Loyalty Castle and are significant for the Lion Clan's military operations. This region serves as a critical route for troop movements and logistical support, essential for managing military campaigns. The climate features open fields that facilitate swift travel along the well-maintained Lion Clan roads. Historically, the plains have been the site of numerous conflicts and are currently active in the ongoing Lion Clan–Unicorn Clan conflict, which has increased military presence in the area.

The Lion Clan roads provide efficient navigation to the Castle of the Swift Sword, connecting Loyalty Castle, the Lion Clan's primary staging ground, to key military installations. The plains have been the backdrop for major battles, including recent skirmishes involving the Lion Clan.
