Central Island

Central Island is located at the center of the City Of The Rich Frog and is home to the Miya Governor’s Palace, where Governor Miya Amaya presides. The island lies among territories controlled by the Lion on the south bank, the Dragon on the north bank, and the Unicorn on the west bank, highlighting its importance in regional governance and political dynamics. Its distinct separation from surrounding districts underscores its role as a seat of authority and imperial influence.

The significance of Central Island is recognized among various groups interested in engaging with Governor Miya Amaya. These groups are considering their approach, weighing the options of presenting themselves as Emerald Magistrates or adopting a more discreet strategy while investigating a controversial author and illicit Unicorn magic charms. The delicate political situation and the presence of rival factions are noted as potential challenges. Discussions regarding the governor's palace indicate plans to formally present themselves to Governor Amaya following their investigative efforts.
