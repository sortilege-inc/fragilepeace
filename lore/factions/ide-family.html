Ide Family

Unicorn

The Ide delegation operates within the ongoing conflict between the Unicorn Clan and Lion Clan clans. Their primary representative is Shinjo Mono, recently appointed as an Emerald Magistrate. The delegation's base of operations is Otosan Uchi, where they communicate with the Emperor regarding military matters. Their influence is underscored by Shinjo Mono's authority to deliver messages and seek resolutions amidst hostilities, focusing on navigating the political landscape to maintain peace and stability, particularly in light of the Matsu incursion into Unicorn Clan lands.

Shinjo Mono informs the Ide delegation of the successful defeat of the initial Matsu incursion and learns that the Emperor is aware of the conflict but has not made a public declaration. Shinjo Mono then travels to Loyalty Castle, where he is intercepted by Lion Clan due to his new position. At Loyalty Castle, he encounters Doji Setsuna and witnesses a duel challenge between Kakita Kazumi and Matsu Koda. The duel culminates in Kakita Kazumi's defeat and subsequent yielding to Koda. The Ide delegation's presence is significant as they navigate courtly intrigue and interactions with other clans, while addressing the ongoing conflict.
