The [[Togashi Family|Togashi]] monk who taught [[Bayushi Monban]], found tending a rock garden
in the [[Dragon Clan|Dragon]] district of the [[City Of The Rich Frog]]. He calls him *the
young lost one*, and claims to have done nothing but help him find his own way.

Asked for a disguise to travel [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] lands as a Dragon monk, he did not
simply hand one over. He asked whether the companions were samurai and whether they were
afraid of danger, and then set the terms himself: the swords put aside, which some monks never
manage, and the head shaved, which does grow back. He shaved it, gave a spare set of clothes
that fit badly, and said the choice about the swords was not his to make.

He is the one who looked at the [[The Demon Wound|demon wound]] when the shaving exposed it,
called it an impressive scar, and asked how it was today.
