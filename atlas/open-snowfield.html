Open Snowfield

The Open Snowfield is a vast, icy expanse with treacherous terrain, posing significant challenges for combat. The snowfield's surface is entangling, disadvantaging archers and those engaged in ranged combat. It is the site of a fierce battle between the Lion Clan and the Unicorn Clan, with the Lion Clan having secured a strategic Central Hill along with fortifications on this open ground. The area bears the remnants of conflict, with visible signs of panic among the Unicorn Clan forces and heavy casualties on both sides.

The Lion Clan aimed to inflict significant casualties on the Unicorn Clan during the engagement. The Lion Clan archers, led by Akodo Toronoko, executed a coordinated volley fire that halted the Unicorn Clan's advance. Kitsu Somalia, a Lion Clan Shugenja commander, invoked fire to incite panic in the Unicorn Clan ranks, further solidifying the Lion Clan's control of Central Central Hill. As daylight waned, an avalanche cascaded down the Central Central Hill, dramatically altering the battlefield dynamics and resulting in substantial losses for the Unicorn Clan forces, including the deaths of their commanding leaders. The harsh environment of the snowfield and the chaos of battle culminated in a tactical victory for the Lion Clan, who retained control of Central Central Hill as darkness fell.
