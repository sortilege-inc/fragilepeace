For Midori, honoring the kami transcends mere duty; it is a celebration of the stories of victorious ancestors. Her commitment to fostering connections within the realm is evident in her active promotion of cultural exchanges. Throughout her travels, Midori has encountered various spirits, favoring elemental spirits over ancestor spirits due to the complexities of human desires. Her childhood was marked by a significant communion with a wind spirit, and she later confronted a ghost at Float Island Harbor. During a journey with companions Bayushi Monban, Doji Setsuna, Kakita Kazumi, and Asako Taishi, she examined Taishi's travel documents and suggested the Castle of the Swift Sword as a suitable location for authenticating his potentially dangerous discovery due to its arcane knowledge.
