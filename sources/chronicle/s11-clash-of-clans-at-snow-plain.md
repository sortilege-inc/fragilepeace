## Narrative

!lede The [[Lion Clan]] went across the bog at noon, in the shape [[Akodo Toronoko]] had drawn the night before — a thin centre of elite troops held back as bait for a frontal charge, both flanks thickened to close on whatever that charge opened. [[Akodo Toronoko]] held the centre. [[Matsu Morozane]] had the left, between the rice paddies and the hill the centre stood on. [[Kitsu Somalia]] had the right, along the eastern river, which put a [[Kitsu Family|Kitsu]] shugenja inside reach of the water kami and was the entire reason for putting her there.

[[Matsu Sakura]] did not commit her best troops. This was a probe. The front ranks were yari ashigaru and yari samurai — spears, because the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] were expecting horses — with the bows held back as reserve. The [[Snow Plain]] itself was ensnaring ground, fresh snow lying over thick mud, and bad footing is the one thing a Lion army is known to handle poorly.

The order for the day was to test their mettle, and that means one thing: make the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] show who was giving their orders. Separately, [[Ikoma Akuyaku]] had gone out at sundown the night before and spent the dark closing on the [[Unicorn Clan War Camp]]. He was still short of it when the line went forward, and the noise of the attack was what he had to finish the approach under, with the next darkness as the earliest he could hope to be inside the perimeter.

The [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] did not take the bait. They formed up in front of the hill and held, content to be come at.

So the Lion came at them. [[Kitsu Somalia]] moved her cohort into contact with the Unicorn right and struck, and the strike landed — thinly, because she is a shugenja commanding troops and her command is what it is, but it landed. The Unicorn leader opposite her took what she gave him and put panic back through her ranks. On the left [[Matsu Morozane]] called a Lightning Raid into the cohort in front of him and put three measures of panic through it, and then wished he had kept something back to strike twice with. The Unicorn answered him too. At the end of the round both flanks were locked and bleeding, five casualties apiece, both centres still unspent, and discipline still ahead of panic on the Lion side — which is what a bait centre and a probing attack are supposed to look like.

In the second round [[Matsu Sakura]]'s war fan flicked toward [[Kitsu Somalia]] again. She weighed her invocations and set them aside — Biting Steel sharpens one weapon and she was commanding several hundred; Courage of Sand and Thunder would have bought her cohort one point of composure — and struck again instead, spending Void on it. Four bonus successes. The Unicorn leader facing her took it badly, five casualties and enough strife to shake his own troops, and his men still put seven casualties into hers. Void stance meant she took no panic from any of it.

[[Akodo Toronoko]] could not find a response. The centre was still holding ground it had not been asked to leave, the enemy was refusing the frontal charge the whole plan had been built to invite, and the read he needed did not come.

Then the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] made a double move and hit [[Kitsu Somalia]]'s cohort with a Lightning Raid — the same fire technique [[Matsu Morozane]] had used a round earlier, turned around and delivered with far more behind it. Six panic and an immediate assault on top of it. The Lion army was twenty-seven casualties down.

What it looked like from inside the cohort was this. Lightly armoured men in red robes came forward out of the Unicorn line, pulled what looked like torches from their bags, and threw them, and the torches burst into fire among the Lion troops. Behind them the Unicorn commander was turning through a whirling, stamping dance and throwing fire every way he faced. Men were running in the ranks screaming that they were burning. [[Kitsu Somalia]] could finally see him, which meant she could call him out — and she asked the question that matters instead. *He is not a fellow shugenja, is he. He is throwing firebombs and making magic incantations.* Nobody answered her.

[[Matsu Morozane]] heard the screaming on the far flank and steadied his own men rather than exploit anything. He set the Touchstone of Courage on his cohort, raised their discipline for the rest of the fight, and took off the one point of panic they were carrying. They were five casualties down and they believed they were winning.

[[Ikoma Akuyaku]] spent the round crossing. One range band is free to a man moving quietly; more than that is a Skulduggery check against the vigilance of Unicorn scouts, and the Unicorn scouts were the problem — open ground to the Lion right would have shown him to anyone who turned his head. Skulk is for attacking. Like a Ghost makes a man quiet, not invisible. So he went one band, hugging the ridge line and the lip of a rice paddy, and put a second arrow into something he told the general and nobody else. He did not reach the camp. He was not close to reaching it.

The last thing to happen was a challenge, and it did not come from the Lion. Horns went up on the Unicorn side and a single rider came out to the edge of their line, a scimitar turning in his hand and the man standing on his own saddle to dance, and he called for the cowardly dog commanding this force to come out and face his doom. He meant [[Matsu Morozane]].

[[Shiguro Chinmoku]] went out ahead of her master. The lion leapt for the horse, the horse reared and kicked her in the side, and the rider had to come down off the saddle to keep his seat from throwing him — which is how a mounted challenge became an answerable one. [[Matsu Morozane]] accepted, and the stake was ten honour and five glory.

He fought it in his head first. Battle of the Mind took Fire and Earth away from the [[Unicorn Officer]]'s stances before a blade moved; the man went to Water. Then [[Matsu Morozane]] bid three more strife than he needed to and bought the first move with it, standing in Void where the roll could not cost him anything. He wanted Striking as Fire and could not have it in Void. He wanted Shattering Tide Style and the ground was merely ensnaring, not confining. So he put the yari in and the [[Unicorn Officer]] came off the line of it — two points of fatigue and not much else. Then the man answered with both scimitars, took four out of [[Matsu Morozane]] and knocked chain links out of his armour, and the clash carried over unfinished.

It ended there for the day. Both flanks engaged, both centres unspent, neither side's objective achieved, [[Matsu Morozane]] locked with an opponent the Lion could not name, and [[Kitsu Somalia]] standing in the smoke with an answer forming. *I have a few choice words for them. Words I do not think they will like.*

## Learned

- Akodo Toronoko: Laid out the Lion order of battle — thin elite centre as bait, both flanks reinforced to close on the charge. Held the centre through both rounds and could not find a way to respond when the Unicorn refused the bait.
- Matsu Sakura: Ordered a probing attack under the objective of testing the Unicorn's mettle, held her heaviest troops out of it, and gave the lead to Kitsu Somalia in both rounds.
- Kitsu Somalia: Placed on the river flank so a Kitsu could reach the water kami. Struck the Unicorn right twice, the second time on a spent Void point for four bonus successes, and took firebombs and a fire technique in return without taking panic.
- Matsu Morozane: Opened with a Lightning Raid for three panic, then spent the second round on the Touchstone of Courage rather than a second strike — raising his cohort's discipline and clearing its panic while the other flank burned.
- Ikoma Akuyaku: Moved one range band toward the Unicorn camp under cover of the battle, hugging the ridge and a paddy lip to stay off open ground, and loosed a second arrow at a target he reported only to the general. He did not reach the camp.
- Unicorn Officer: Rode out under horns, dancing on his saddle with a scimitar turning, and called Matsu Morozane a cowardly dog. Dismounted when Shiguro Chinmoku went for his horse, fought the clash in Water with two scimitars, gave up two fatigue and took four back. The Lion never learned his name.
- Shiguro Chinmoku: Morozane's lion, and a lioness. Leapt at a mounted officer's horse, took a kick in the side for it, and brought the challenge to the ground where it could be answered.
- Snow Plain: Ensnaring ground — fresh snow over thick mud — and the Lion army is poor at bad footing. Not confining and not dangerous, which cost Morozane a technique in the clash.

## Setsuna

She is inside [[Matsu Morozane]] for this, as she was for the council, and what she gets is a first day of battle rather than a battle. Nothing is decided in it. That is worth saying plainly, because the shape of a several-day fight is exactly what an account written afterward flattens.

Three things the memory gives her that no document does.

**The Unicorn refused to charge.** They stood on prepared ground in front of a hill and made the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] come to them, twice, and declined the frontal attack the entire Lion plan had been built to invite. Whatever [[Ikoma Ichigo]]'s compilation says about a Unicorn victory, the first day of it was fought by an army that would not move.

**The Unicorn fought with fire.** Men in red robes throwing incendiaries out of their line, and a commander turning through a dance and throwing more. The technique that hit [[Kitsu Somalia]]'s cohort was the same Lightning Raid [[Matsu Morozane]] had used a round before — so the honest statement is that the Unicorn had a leader who could do what a Lion war-caller can do, and hangers-on with prepared incendiaries, and used both on ground of their own choosing. [[Kitsu Somalia]] asked out loud whether the man was a shugenja at all and got no answer, and Setsuna does not have one either. She should not let the question be tidied into one on her behalf. But a deed that speaks of cavalry is describing a battle nobody on that field fought on the first day.

**The Lion never identified them.** That was the whole objective and it went unmet. By nightfall the man [[Matsu Morozane]] was trading wounds with was a stranger, and the Lion knew where the Unicorn commanders were standing and nothing else about them. A record composed afterward will name them all confidently. This memory is evidence that at least one day of the battle was fought blind.

And the hole in it. [[Ikoma Akuyaku]] loosed two arrows that day at something he reported to [[Matsu Sakura]] alone. [[Matsu Morozane]] did not see the targets and so neither did she. Whatever the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] were doing on the edge of that field while the line was busy, this memory does not contain it — and she is standing three centuries away from the only man who could tell her.
