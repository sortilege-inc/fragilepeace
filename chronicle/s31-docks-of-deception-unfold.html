Docks of Deception Unfold

Session 31·2025-12-09

"Always stolen! Remember the boss needs another!" They were not robbing her. They were collecting her.

Kaeru Haya had the magistrates in position before dawn, and the operation went wrong before it started. In an alley, two men came out at Shiba Midori with weapons pulled from their backs. She drew and told them what it would cost — I will sell my life at a high price — and one of them blocked her cut with his forearm and laughed. The weapons were wooden. Always stolen! Remember the boss needs another!

They fought like water, fluid and unhurried, and adapted faster than she could change plan. They beat her down and she went out.

Down at The Docks, Bayushi Monban and Doji Setsuna arrived to find no Kakita Kazumi, and no small boats where small boats should have been. Kaeru Haya had word of a large blue-water ship at anchor in the harbour. Bayushi Monban went up to look.

From the height he saw movement on the ship and, snagged on a bridge pylon, a scrap of navy blue cloth with an emerald green border. He came off the roof unmasked shouting my big sister! and put Ride the Clouds into a flat run at the ship as it prepared to leave. Then a thin cry turned him around, and he found her in a fishing skiff tied at the dock, curled between the benches, stripped of everything.

Doji Setsuna examined her on the boards: bruising, sharp pain through the right side, no bleeding. Stabilised. And Kakita Kazumi was still gone — nothing among the moorings, unaccounted for, and in danger.

Then the ship began to move. Bayushi Monban flew to it and came down on the deck in front of Captain Kubota — six foot six, black Tortoise headdress, full-face mask — and told him to drop anchor.

Kubota would not. He said anchoring there would tear his hull, and then gave the reason underneath it: a young man on his deck is his nephew, whose daughter has been taken by local bosses, and the ransom for her is in the hold, and he is going to deliver it. He talked about the politics of these docks, and about what the magistrates' last few days had already cost people who have to live here.

Bayushi Monban slashed a sail. Kubota cut his anchor lines to keep the ship off the rocks. They threatened each other and parted, and Bayushi Monban came back to the dock, and he and Doji Setsuna carried Shiba Midori home to recover — with Kakita Kazumi still missing.

Setsuna

They were not muggers. The boss needs another is a quota. Wooden weapons are for taking people without damaging them. They stripped Shiba Midori and left her breathing in a skiff at the dockside rather than in the river. Put that against Kaeru Haya's report that sailors have been going missing, and the answer is that this city has people being collected, and last night the party put two women alone on a dark dock in a district where that is happening.

Kakita Kazumi has been taken. She should proceed on that rather than hope he is skulking. He was placed at The Docks as bait on Kaeru Akiara's suggestion, by a man who had already told the watch that magistrates would be interfering. The bait was taken.

And Captain Kubota may have been telling the truth. A ransom in his hold for his nephew's kidnapped daughter, taken by the same local bosses who are collecting people off these docks — that is consistent with everything else they learned last night. Bayushi Monban cut the sail of a man who was trying to buy a child back. If Kubota was lying, it was a good lie. If he was not, the party has just delayed a ransom and made an enemy of the one person in the harbour with a reason to talk about the bosses.

She should find out which, quickly, and send somebody other than Bayushi Monban to do it.

The through-line is unmistakable now. Bodies taken and not killed. Opium and occult goods on the same boats. A Mantis ship sitting too long. A talisman with a wild spirit in it, and a rite that needs a new vessel every time a spirit is moved.

The boss needs another. What a person is being collected for is the question, and it will not wait.
