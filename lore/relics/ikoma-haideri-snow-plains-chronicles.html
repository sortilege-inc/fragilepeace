Ikoma Haideri Snow Plains Chronicles

Lion

Ikoma Haideri is the author of a historical record detailing the Battle of the Snow Plains, which had been lost for 300 years. This record is verified by its distinctive personal seal. The associated scrolls are large, lion-headed, and trimmed with gold, signifying their importance and the stature of their creator.

Ikoma Ichigo, a Lion scribe, identifies these scrolls as the Ikoma Haideri Snow Plains chronicles and expresses gratitude for their recovery. He confirms that the scrolls appear genuine and match the style and seal of his ancestor, Ikoma Haideri. Ichigo seeks to further authenticate the documents by comparing them with other known writings of Haideri from the library. Katsuki Kage, the senior Emerald Magistrate, permits Ichigo to retrieve additional samples for comparison. The authentication of these chronicles is a pivotal moment in the court proceedings, as they are recognized as credible evidence regarding the events at the Snow Plains.
