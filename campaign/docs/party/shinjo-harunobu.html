Shinjō Harunobu

Unicorn

Shinjō Harunobu, a towering figure at 6'6", stands as a prominent lord of the Unicorn Clan, embodying the noble samurai aesthetic with his curly hair often tied back by a purple ribbon and a well-trimmed beard. Renowned for his prowess as a military strategist, he commands a specialized force within the Unicorn Clan army, serving as the personal advisor to Utaku Kamoko. In this capacity, Shinjō Harunobu frequently engages in military planning, challenging Kamoko's ideas and proposing alternative strategies that reflect his deep understanding of logistics and warfare. His upbringing is marked by a strong bond with horses and the vast landscapes of the Great Plains, reflecting his connection to nature and the spirit world.

Recently, Shinjō Harunobu found himself embroiled in a conflict with the Lion Clan, led by General Akodo Sakawan. This clash, which transpired in the lead-up to the Emerald Championship, resulted in a notable defeat for the Unicorn Clan, with several of their warriors captured. Despite this setback, General Shinju, Shinjō Harunobu's superior, managed to survive the encounter, highlighting the ongoing tensions and rivalries that define the political landscape of the realm.

Currently, Shinjō Harunobu is a prisoner of war held by the Lion Clan, treated honorably by his captors. He has communicated his well-being, noting that the soldier who captured him has tended to his injuries with care, comparable to the treatment of their own men. In a letter, he mentioned overhearing discussions about an Emerald Magistrate potentially advocating for his release, which has sparked hope amidst the turmoil.

In his personal life, Shinjō Harunobu's unwavering commitment to his military duties has strained his relationship with his spouse, Doji Setsuna. During a conversation with Shinjō Mono, Setsuna voiced her concerns for Shinjō Harunobu's well-being, emphasizing his need for open horizons. She revealed that her horse, Kogarashi, was a wedding gift from Shinjō Harunobu, a detail that surprised Mono and underscored the complexities of their marriage. Setsuna has been actively involved in ongoing court proceedings, where she has mentioned Harunobu's status and requested Bayushi Monban to cease interference regarding his captivity. Monban agreed to withdraw his request related to Harunobu, and he delivered the letter detailing Harunobu's condition to Setsuna, who expressed her desire for him to leave her husband’s situation alone.

Determined to secure her husband's release, Doji Setsuna sought the assistance of a courtier at Loyalty Castle, requesting a letter advocating for Shinjō Harunobu's freedom. The courtier advised her that such a request would be more appropriate after the cessation of hostilities, yet Setsuna remained steadfast in her desire for Shinjō Harunobu's release specifically, rather than a general hostage exchange. Her unwavering dedication to him amidst the political implications of their union was evident, and the courtier reassured her of Shinjō Harunobu's safety while in Lion Clan custody, providing a glimmer of hope in their tumultuous circumstances.

Additionally, Shinjō Harunobu's situation has garnered the attention of Lady Takeko, a Clan Daimyo, who sent a letter advocating for his transfer to a new location. This letter, sent the previous day, suggests that his successful transfer is likely pending time, with Lady Takeko's support indicating her significant influence in the matter. Shinjō Harunobu's movement is crucial to the ongoing conflicts and resolutions, underscoring its importance in the intricate dynamics of clan politics. The implications of his captivity continue to be pivotal in discussions among the Emerald Magistrates, illustrating the interconnectedness of personal and political matters within the court.

As part of the ongoing custody request initiated by Doji Setsuna, Shinjō Harunobu is currently awaiting a response regarding his situation. For his safety, he has been relocated with plans to seek peace at the Castle of the Centipede. If unsuccessful, he will be sent to the The Wall to fight against the Shadowlands. Although he holds a position of honor as a samurai, he is functionally literate and not particularly fond of reading. Nevertheless, he demonstrates sentimentality through actions such as sending a gift to his wife. Recently, a messenger delivered a package from Shinjō Harunobu to Setsuna, containing a book by the popular author Hana Noame, appreciated for its salacious content. This gesture reflects Harunobu's awareness of Setsuna's interests and adds a personal touch to their relationship, further illustrating the complexities of their bond amidst the challenges they face.

In light of his current circumstances, Doji Setsuna is also seeking a spiritually active item to help alleviate Shinjō Harunobu's spiritual burdens, emphasizing his mental and emotional well-being, particularly due to his experiences in conflict. She intends to acquire an object that resonates with his upbringing, aiming to connect him to his roots and the spiritual essence of his past. The proprietor of a Curio Shop has exhibited caution upon learning of Setsuna's request, indicating that spiritually active items may carry significant implications.
