River Near The Palace

The River Near The Palace is a significant waterway flowing through the city, surrounded by urban structures. It is strategically important, leading upriver into Unicorn Lands and downriver toward Scorpion or Lion Lands, raising political and safety concerns regarding the release of a contained Ifrit.

The river's open ground is considered suitable for the release of powerful entities, despite the associated risks, particularly the potential for destruction if not handled properly.
