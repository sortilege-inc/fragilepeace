## Narrative

!lede [[Bayushi Monban]] spent the night in a gambling house working a [[Scorpion Cousin]] for an apothecary across the water who deals in poisons. He wanted something for a woman, and he traded future favours for help getting a servant to carry the gift. He then woke at the [[Geisha House]] half-dressed with a bad head, was told the young lord he had been drinking with had left in a hurry, and went to pray before going anywhere near the governor's mansion.

While he was out, [[Kakita Kazumi]] was inside [[Shiba Midori]]'s quarters looking for [[The Box]] — the empty vessel — and was still there when [[Doji Setsuna]] woke screaming, convulsing, the medicine still in her. The air in the building was wrong.

He found [[Miya Misato]]'s room ransacked. The envoy was in it. And a shuriken was driven into the wall holding a message: **Turn back. [[Aoi]].**

He read the room rather than the note. The window was open, the dust was scuffed with footprints, and the search had been fast and specific — they were not there to hurt her. They were there for a document, and when he pressed her about it she indicated it was on her person: **a message under the [[Imperial Seal]]**.

[[Shiba Midori]] left her breakfast and went to [[Doji Setsuna]]. Exhaustion, the medicine, and something else — because caught on a rafter nail above the bed was a scrap of **dark blue fabric**.

Two rooms entered in one night, two intrusions, and the same colour of cloth the party has seen before.

[[Shiba Midori]] passed the fabric to [[Kakita Kazumi]] to work on. He sent [[Ryu]] to find [[Bayushi Monban]], and [[Ryu]] brought him back. Meanwhile [[Kakita Kazumi]] was planning to search [[Bayushi Monban]]'s own quarters while he was out of them. [[Doji Setsuna]] got up, unwell, and came down to be present for it.

[[Governor Miya Tetsuya]] promised guards for [[Miya Misato]]. [[Kakita Kazumi]] proposed a decoy for the road — put [[Shiba Midori]] in the envoy's place — and the argument bogged down in transport, because a covered wagon is not a conveyance a samurai can be seen in. From the evidence in both rooms they concluded there was more than one shinobi in the building.

## Learned

- Aoi: The name signed under a shuriken driven into the Imperial Envoy's wall, over the words "Turn back." Whoever they are, they wanted the party to know who was warning them.
- Miya Misato: Her room was searched fast and specifically by people who did not touch her. What they were after is a message under the Imperial Seal, and she is carrying it on her person.
- Scorpion Cousin: An informant Monban worked in a gambling house. Knows an apothecary across the water who deals in poisons, and has been promised future favours for arranging a servant to deliver a poisoned gift to a woman.
- Governor Miya Tetsuya: Promised guards for the envoy once the danger was laid in front of them.
- Ryu: Kazumi's subordinate. Sent to find Monban, and found him.
- Imperial Seal: A sealed Imperial message is what the intruders came for. Which makes forgery of that seal the thing to fear next.

## Setsuna

**Somebody was in her room while she was convulsing.** A scrap of dark blue caught on a rafter nail above her bed. Not the envoy's room — hers. Two rooms entered on the same night by more than one person, and only one of those rooms held anything an outsider should want.

She should take the dark blue seriously and personally. **Dark blue with no clan mon** is what the men who ambushed the road wore in session six, the ones who released two Emerald Magistrates on sight of rank and took [[Asako Taishi]] anyway. They are back, they are in the governor's house, and this time one of them was standing over her.

**The target is the seal, not the woman.** [[Miya Misato]] was not touched. Her room was searched fast and precisely for **a message under the [[Imperial Seal]]**, which she is carrying on her body. Whoever wants it does not want her dead — they want the writ, or they want a genuine impression of the seal to make one with. She has already seen this campaign turn on forged documents twice; an authentic Imperial seal in the wrong hands ends the peace before the envoy reaches the front.

**Turn back. [[Aoi]].** Somebody signed a warning. That is not how you threaten an Imperial envoy — that is how you warn off people you would rather not have to kill. Whoever [[Aoi]] is, they chose to be identifiable.

**And there is a thing in her own party she now has to look at.** [[Bayushi Monban]] spent last night buying poison for a woman and arranging a servant to deliver it as a gift. [[Kakita Kazumi]] broke into [[Shiba Midori]]'s room hunting the empty vessel, and intends to search [[Bayushi Monban]]'s next. Her yojimbo is procuring murder and her duellist is burgling the party. She is unwell, the envoy is under threat, and the two men sworn around her are running private operations against each other.

The decoy plan is sound and the objection is real: a samurai cannot be carried in a covered wagon without it being remarked on. But **she has a palanquin already requisitioned**, on medical grounds nobody will question, and nobody can see inside it.
