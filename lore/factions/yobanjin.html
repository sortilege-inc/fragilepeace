Faction

Yobanjin

The Yobanjin are northern tribes neighboring the Phoenix Clan, known for their complex relationship with the local powers in the City Of The Rich Frog. Lady Tsubasa, a notable figure, has established a peace with them, indicating a level of diplomatic engagement.

The Yobanjin are referenced in discussions among samurai investigating smuggling activities, suggesting they may play a role in regional conflicts or trade routes. Their presence and influence are acknowledged in relation to The Docks, which are considered a potential smuggling route for occult items possibly connected to the Yobanjin. The samurai's inquiry into The Docks aims to understand and disrupt smuggling operations that could involve the Yobanjin or their allies.

Interactions among various characters highlight ongoing tension surrounding smuggling in the area, implicating the Yobanjin indirectly. However, the specific details of their involvement or motivations remain unelaborated.
