## Narrative

!lede In the [[Kitsu Archives]] the four of them sat down to decide what they were going to tell [[Kitsuki Kaage]], and the argument went further than anyone expected.

[[Bayushi Monban]] put it plainly. If the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]'s bargain was that the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] would earn them the victory, the Scorpion earned it — three agents inside a Lion camp, the stores poisoned, the plans leaking — and the Unicorn owe them the [[Old Diamond Mines|mines]]. If parties make an agreement and one side performs, the other pays. That is what a deal is.

[[Doji Setsuna]] took that apart, and did it from her own training. Nobody in that room has ever seen the agreement. The only evidence it exists is in [[Bayushi Monban]]'s head. And Imperial law is porous where the facts are: magistrates are made to decide on inadequate information, and what settles it then is what serves the Empire. She had the case to cite for it — an exercise from their own instruction where the class was handed one set of facts and told to rule it both ways, and [[Bayushi Monban]] had sat in that class beside her. *If it were as open and shut as "we have an agreement, here are the signatures", your opinion would not be needed at all.*

*The law is what the Emperor says it is at the time*, somebody said, and nobody disagreed.

[[Bayushi Monban]] would not have it. *I am not going to interpret to help the clans, or my own clan, or any clan. If Emerald Magistrates start interpreting binding agreements out of necessity, the rule of law deteriorates.* [[Doji Setsuna]]'s answer was that interpreting is literally the job.

[[Shiba Midori]] named the other thing in the room. The [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] are allied with the [[Crane Clan|Crane]], half this party is Crane, and a ruling for the Unicorn strengthens them against the [[Lion Clan|Lion]]. Nobody pretended that was not a consideration.

[[Doji Setsuna]] had already set her price before any of this. Through a [[Shosuro Contact|Shosuro contact]] she had put it to the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] that her default position is that they have no claim — and that she would reconsider, and support them, if they kept out of the coming [[Crane Clan|Crane]]–[[Lion Clan|Lion]] negotiation. The Scorpion answered that they were open to it and asked what she had to offer. She still needs an affirmative, and said so out loud.

With the delegations on the road and the [[Snow Plain]] ten days away through a war, they settled on building the case here: Lion historians for expert testimony, the archive for documents — and, since one of the castle's residents is a Scorpion rumoured to be an extraordinary forger whom nobody has ever caught, a forger for the rest.

[[Doji Setsuna]] booked physicians and [[Ikoma Ichigo]], and then found the thing nobody had gone looking for. A recently filed treaty between the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] and the [[Crane Clan|Crane]]: the Lion give up a great many old claims and receive two-thirds of the Asari Plains, which puts Lion territory practically against [[Suma]], where the Topaz Championship is held, and takes a great deal of fertile ground off the Crane. Her own clan was, in the short term, badly served.

The rest of it is worse. The negotiation was held at the Crane embassy in [[Otosan Uchi]]. The Lion sent a young and untried negotiator — they were, by the look of it, expecting to lose and hoping for a reason to go to war — and he won it outright, and the Lion got what they wanted without fighting for it. The mediator was [[Otomo Genshin]], an Otomo with a habit of ruling against the Crane. And [[Doji Setsuna]] is nine parts in ten certain that the Otomo was appointed to that mediation by the Imperial Advisor.

[[Kakita Kazumi]] went looking for the forger. He knew [[Soshi Yamako]] walks the [[Path of Splinters]], so he paid a servant to mention where he was sitting and waited in the gazebo. *Well, well. I had heard the Topaz Champion was here, but I had not had the opportunity to meet you. I am grateful your kind servants brought me an excuse.* She arrived with plum wine and two glasses. They talked about how peripheral the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] are to a dispute over a battle the Lion lost — *and lost the records of too* — and *wartime does have a tendency to create many casualties, does it not.* By evening they were in a secluded corner of the [[Ikoma Library]] with wine and food from his own stores, and he recited the [[Snow Plain]] to her as he had seen it through [[Akodo Toronoko]]'s eyes. *I am curious. Your ancestor Toronoko did not confess all of this in her private writings?* — *If only I had some samples of your ancestor's handwriting, I would create such a thing for you.* She can write in Toronoko's hand with her other hand, she said, though the effort of being deliberately sloppy, as though writing blindfold, is the difficult part. It might be more believable, she added, if the confession included suspicion of the husband. And she gave him a shopping list: the right parchments, the right inks.

[[Bayushi Monban]] went to the [[Ancestral Shrine]], where [[Kitsuko Ayoko]] was praying, and found an old priest. He made an offering of rice and wine to [[Ikoma Akuyaku]] and then asked the question he had come for: whether the Kitsu gift reaches ancestors who are not [[Lion Clan|Lion]]. *It may. Whether they will answer is another question entirely.* The working depends on reverence — the Lion ancestors are always watching because the Lion never stop making offerings, and *other clans who are not so pious might have more difficulty contacting ancestors who have not received offerings in a long time.* So he commissioned a sumptuous feast for [[Shosuro Amane]] — the Scorpion who stood beside [[Shinjo Kamo]] at the [[Snow Plain]], and who is now three centuries dead — and told the priest what he was praying for. *Clarity, and a demonstration of truth, to silence critics and those who would dishonour the acts and deeds of the Scorpion.*

Then he went to [[Shosuro Imako]] and asked her to make the agreement exist.

She was ready for it. *If you gave me a sample of [[Shinjo Kamo]]'s writing, and perhaps his chop, I could arrange for such an agreement to appear.* His chop alone is on the treaty in this castle and that is not enough — she needs his hand. And she wanted to know what she was getting, because she would be the one risking exposure as a forger, and [[Bayushi Monban]] would only be risking his magistracy and his neck. *Risk is part of the game. Do you want to play or not?*

He pushed at the shape of it — whether the forged deed should grant the mines to the Scorpion outright, in which case they are their champion's to distribute, or to him to hand up — and she took that apart as currying favour without paying for it. He asked whether the beneficiary should be his own daimyō or her mistress the Imperial Advisor, and got the most useful answer of the day.

*If my lady the Imperial Advisor had need of these mines, she would put a word in the Emperor's ear and there would be no dispute. Why do you think she is allowing others to resolve it?*

She also asked, twice, why *the least of the [[Bayushi Family|Bayushi]]* should be the one to inherit a diamond mine.

[[Shiba Midori]] performed a [[Phoenix Clan|Phoenix]] tale in the [[Ikoma Hall of Scribes]] and picked a courtier of her own clan, [[Shiba Masaharu]], out of the audience.

And over tea [[Doji Setsuna]] asked her what she made of [[Otomo Genshin]] — a man, a courtier, by the look of how he was employed — and then, almost as an aside at the end of it, whether she had ever had need to learn the preparation of the various maternity teas. [[Shiba Midori]] knows everything about tea. It was the other part of the question she was being asked.

## Setsuna

**She is inside a forgery now.** Not adjacent to one. The plan was made in front of her in the [[Kitsu Archives]] and she raised no objection to it, and by nightfall there were two of them — [[Kakita Kazumi]] shopping for parchment with one Scorpion, [[Bayushi Monban]] negotiating terms with another, neither aware of the other's. If it is discovered, that she forged nothing herself will not save her, or her rulings, or [[Shinjo Harunobu]]'s transfer.

And it is not necessary. The party already has the true version — the quartermaster's ledgers, [[Ikoma Akuyaku]]'s concealed confession, a medic thanked in the rosters and struck from the genealogies. They are forging because the true evidence proves the Scorpion presence was treason against the clan whose hospitality they are enjoying, and nobody wants to say that here. The forgery is not there to invent a fact. It is there to make a true fact sayable, and that distinction will not survive being found out.

The argument she won is worth keeping. [[Bayushi Monban]] wants the law to be a machine that pays out on performance. It is not, and the training they both sat through says so: the same facts, ruled both ways, on instruction. *The law is what the Emperor says it is at the time.* She is right about that and she should be careful about how much she enjoys being right about it, because the same doctrine is what lets her sell a recommendation for a patron's notice.

The treaty is the more important document and it has nothing to do with the mines. A [[Lion Clan|Lion]]–[[Crane Clan|Crane]] agreement, newly filed, that hands the Lion two-thirds of the Asari Plains and brings their border up against [[Suma]] — negotiated at her own clan's embassy, against her own clan's interest, by a Lion boy who was sent to lose. The mediator has a record of ruling against the Crane and was, near enough certainly, appointed by the Imperial Advisor.

That is [[Bayushi Kachiko]]. The same woman [[Doji Setsuna]] asked to have her name carried to, one day ago, over tea. And [[Shosuro Imako]] has now said out loud that her mistress is *allowing* this dispute to be resolved by others, which is not a thing one says about a matter one does not control.

She should stop treating the mines as the case. The mines are the room where she was put so that somebody could see how she behaves in it.

And she asked [[Shiba Midori]] about maternity teas, both kinds. That is hers, and it is now known to one other person.
