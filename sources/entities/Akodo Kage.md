Sensei to both [[Akodo Toturi]] and [[Akodo Akihito]], and present in the
[[Lion Clan]] camp. He called [[Kakita Kazumi]] a liar to his face over the
reconstructed treaty, which is what the duel answered — and did not answer, since the
duel was fought against [[Akodo Akihito]] instead. Both his students care deeply what he
thinks.
