The Wall

The Wall, a notable post road station, stands at the border between the Kitsu Family and Akodo provinces, nestled around a serene lake. This waystation serves as a vital point of rest for travelers and dignitaries alike, featuring well-maintained accommodations and a revered Shrine of the Lake Kami. The lake, particularly enchanting at night, draws visitors to the nearby Dignitaries’ Tea House, where social gatherings flourish amidst the tranquil surroundings.

As the evening unfolds, the Emerald Magistrates, rare visitors to this location, find themselves welcomed by the Kitsu Family samurai hosts. They partake in a tea ceremony, sharing stories and discussing their duties, while the hosts express surprise at the presence of these esteemed guests. The atmosphere is one of camaraderie and reflection, enriched by the lake's calming presence.

However, the tranquility is soon disrupted when Shiba Midori senses a supernatural disturbance surrounding Doji Setsuna. This prompts a visit to the shrine, where preparations for a ritual commence. Offerings of rice, sake, and origami animals are made, and Shiba Midori leads an invocation that culminates in the manifestation of Matsu Tomoe, an ancestor who confronts Doji Setsuna regarding her lineage's responsibilities. This encounter underscores The Wall's significance as a spiritual waypoint, linking the living with their ancestral duties.

In contrast, the The Wall, a formidable defensive structure, looms as a testament to the ongoing conflict between the Unicorn Clan and the Crab Clan. Its impressive height and robust construction serve to repel invaders and protect the surrounding territories, while the harsh winters create a treacherous landscape. The Wall has become a pivotal element in military tactics, where feigned retreats and hidden traps are employed to fortify defenses.

The legacy of The Wall resonates through military discussions, particularly among the Lion Clan, who recognize the tactical advantages it offers. General Matsu Sakura notes the resemblance of the Unicorn Clan's strategies to the lessons learned at The Wall, leading to a heightened awareness of concealed dangers such as spike-filled pits camouflaged by foliage. The ongoing implications of The Wall's history continue to shape warfare in the region, influencing decisions made by both the Unicorn Clan and Lion Clan forces as they navigate the complexities of battle.

Recently, the structure has been identified as part of the broader The Wall, which serves as a formidable defense against the encroaching Shadowlands. Massive stone fortifications stand as a bulwark against the dark creatures that inhabit the lands beyond, with the climate often reflecting the continuous threat posed by these malevolent forces. The Wall is manned by the Crab Clan, whose samurai are dedicated to guarding against these dangers, playing a crucial role in the clan's military efforts.

There has been a noted increase in Shadowlands creatures in the Shinomen Forest, indicating a growing threat to The Wall. Additionally, the Sword of Hiruma, a significant ancestral blade, was stolen from the Hiruma's new castle, located beyond The Wall, and this theft is believed to be connected to the schemes of the First Enemy. In anticipation of further conflict, there is ongoing recruitment for additional warriors to bolster the defenses of the The Wall.
