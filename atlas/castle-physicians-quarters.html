Castle Physicians’ Quarters

The Castle Physicians’ Quarters is an area within the castle designated for medical care and consultation for inhabitants. It features private consultation spaces and storage for medical supplies. The climate is maintained for comfort and healing, creating a conducive environment for patients. Physicians in this location are responsible for diagnosing ailments and prescribing remedies.

The quarters play a significant role amidst ongoing political tensions surrounding the Unicorn Clan and Scorpion Clan clans, serving as a critical point for those seeking medical advice.
