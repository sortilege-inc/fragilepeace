## Narrative

!lede The year is 1123. [[Doji Satsume]], lately Emerald Champion, is dead. Natural causes is the finding entered on the record, and no account of the death has been produced to go with it. The office stands vacant, the Championship that fills it is being arranged, and every clan with a duellist is doing arithmetic. In the north the [[Lion Clan]] and the [[Unicorn Clan]] are one incident from open war. At [[Toshi Ranbo]] a treaty of nonaggression holds, on terms that favour the Lion. There is also talk of an epidemic, which nobody has confirmed and nobody has denied.

Into that, orders. Emerald Magistrate [[Kitsuki Kaage]] sent [[Doji Setsuna]] to the [[Castle of the Swift Sword]] to evaluate a dispute between the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] and the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]. The dispute turns on the Lion's own records, which means the Lion must be asked to produce the evidence by which their neighbours will be judged. A second letter arrived from her [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] mentor. It assigned her a yojimbo, [[Bayushi Monban]], and it forbade him seppuku. A bodyguard who may not answer a failure with his life answers it some other way, and so does the person he guards.

She left the castle with her household: [[Ishika]], her lady's maid; [[Otoya]], her scribe; the porters [[Ryu]] and [[So]]; [[Jiro]] for the horses; and [[Kogarashi]], the white courser her husband's family gave her. A stranger came out of the dark, asked for her by name, said *the kami favour you — these are for your companions*, and left packages bearing the crests of [[Kakita Family|Kakita]], [[Bayushi Family|Scorpion]], [[Shiba Family|Shiba]] and [[Togashi Family|Togashi]], and some bearing nothing. Then he was gone. Nobody has explained him since.

Elsewhere, at a shrine with [[Lion Clan]] marks worked small into it, several people woke up who had been dead. They remembered dying — cultists in the [[Phoenix Lands]], black scrolls opened — and they woke beside a [[Togashi Nun]] who was there and did not explain herself either. A paved road ran away from the shrine, laid to a standard that means a clan built it, if not the Empire. Whether the kami had favoured them or merely declined to finish with them, the survivors did not settle on the walk out.

On the road to [[Loyalty Castle]], [[Doji Setsuna]] recognised an old man with an untidy bun from a painting she had once seen: [[Kitsuki Wataru]], travelling with other magistrates. [[Ryu]] and [[So]] would not go near [[Bayushi Monban]] and gave no reason, and were not made to. She handed over the [[Phoenix Clan|Phoenix]]-sealed missive she had accepted in [[Otosan Uchi]] from a rider of the [[Miya Family|Miya]] who had been looking for him. It was from an [[Asako Family|Asako]] daimyō. [[Bayushi Monban]] read it and laughed: he had inherited a castle. Among the packages was a [[Togashi Family|Togashi]] box addressed to *[[Chousen]]*, a name nobody at the roadside would claim out loud, though more than one of them looked at it as though it might be theirs.

[[Kitsuki Wataru]] had the news from the north. Above the [[Aelu River]] the Lion under General [[Akodo Sakawan]] had broken the Unicorn and taken prisoners, the opposing general among them; the lord [[Shinjo Harunobu]] serves got off the field alive. *The Lion only drink after a victory*, Wataru said. The castles ahead would be loud, then, before anyone reached them. Seven years now since the [[Crane Embassy|Crane embassy]] burned in [[Otosan Uchi]], and seven years of peace between [[Lion Clan|Lion]] and [[Crane Clan|Crane]] built on top of the ashes. Nobody on the road called that peace anything but fragile.

## Learned

- Kitsuki Kaage: The Emerald Magistrate who holds her orders. He sent her to the Castle of the Swift Sword to weigh a Scorpion–Unicorn dispute that turns on Lion records — which is to say he has sent her somewhere she cannot succeed without Lion cooperation.
- Bayushi Monban: Assigned to her as yojimbo by her own Scorpion mentor, and forbidden seppuku by the same letter. He learned this session that an Asako daimyō has left him a castle. Her porters Ryu and So will not go near him and will not say why.
- Kitsuki Wataru: Old, untidy, recognisable from a painting — a magistrate of standing travelling with others of his office. He carries current military news and gives it plainly. He knows what the Lion do after a victory.
- Doji Satsume: The former Emerald Champion, dead this year. The cause of death entered on the record is natural causes.
- Akodo Sakawan: The Lion general who broke the Unicorn north of the Aelu River and took the opposing general prisoner.
- Shinjo Harunobu: His lord escaped the field north of the Aelu River alive.

## Setsuna

Her charge is narrower than it looks. She is to evaluate a Scorpion–Unicorn dispute whose evidence sits in Lion hands, at a Lion castle, during a Lion war. Every document she needs will be produced by a party with an interest in what it shows.

The letter about [[Bayushi Monban]] sits alongside that. A yojimbo denied seppuku cannot discharge a failure in the ordinary way, so any failure of his stays open, and stays hers. Her mentor knew that when the letter was written.

She has not asked who the stranger with the packages was, and no one has told her.
