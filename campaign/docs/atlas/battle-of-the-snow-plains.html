Battle of the Snow Plains

The Battle of the Snow Plains is a historical site located southwest of the City Of The Rich Frog. It features rugged terrain and is known for its harsh climate, often blanketed in snow, which has posed challenges for the armies that have clashed there, particularly between the Lion and Unicorn clans.

Recent letters indicate that items were found near this battlefield, suggesting its ongoing relevance in the conflict. Discussions at the Governor’s Tea House revealed that the Lion armies, likely commanded by Lady Matsu, were in the vicinity of the Snow Plains. The Imperial envoy Miya Misato was tasked with locating these armies and negotiating a ceasefire, underscoring the area's strategic importance. Reports of supplies being sent northeast indicate the dynamic nature of the conflict surrounding the Snow Plains.
