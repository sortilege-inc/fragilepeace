## Narrative

!lede [[Doji Setsuna]] spent the day of the duel in the governor's tower and did not
see a moment of it. Everything reached her secondhand — in a letter, in a play-by-play
shouted across a drawing room, and finally in the victor's own report, delivered in
front of her.

She was still living up there, with [[Miya Misato]] and under [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]]'s
roof, and [[Ryu]] and [[So]] had been seen carrying boxes of her clothes up the stairs.
The court had drawn the cheap conclusion — that the Crane magistrate was the governor's
private guest in the other sense — and she had not troubled to correct it. She had gone
up there to be inside one wall with the person she is sworn to keep alive.

[[Shiba Midori]] was still badly ill. The poisoning had been deliberate, and it had left
her dazed and unable to keep anything down. The physicians the castle produced disagreed
with each other and none of them cleared it; a shugenja ran Pathway to Inner Peace over
her and got her stable, and no further. The ring wounds she has been carrying for weeks
made everything slower. She dictated a message to [[Bayushi Monban]] through
[[Kitsuko Ayoko]], to be handed over only after the duel was decided: if he died in it,
she would haunt him for the rest of eternity.

[[Daidoji Shin]] set up on the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] docks across the water with a
gaijin spyglass. [[Matsu Tsuki]] and [[Ikoma Ukyo]] were on the wrong bank and said so
at length. [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] found a vantage inside the castle, nobody being permitted
off the island.

[[Karahaya]] came in full ashigaru armour and walked [[Bayushi Monban]] out along a dock
that put the whole town in view of it. She offered him one last chance to apologise for
what he had said about her lord and her men. He lifted his mask, spat on the boards in
front of her, and did not bow. She told him she meant to leave him alive with his shame,
and that she would kill him if restoring her lord's honour turned out to cost that.

He opened his robes on a tamami gazoku — foldable armour — and drew.

He moved first and his opening cut landed. Then he went for the follow-up and found that
the technique he wanted needed a snaring weapon and that a sheath is not one. That was
the whole of his advantage, and it went there.

She blocked, countered out of earth, and pushed him past his limit. Compromised, he was
open to a finishing blow and she took it at once. He got the scabbard up in a shattering
parry; it broke in half and the cut went in anyway, opening him from the side of the jaw
across the eye and into the hairline.

He unmasked. **How dare you disfigure a member of a great Clan.** He called her worthless
and lucky and spat blood at her, on a dock, in front of the town and the children who had
run down to watch. She shifted her stance a little to invite him in, feinted him, and hit
him again. He did not touch her once for the length of it.

He conceded — *it seems you have won this contest*. She lowered the blade and named her
terms: apologise to her and to her men, and withdraw what he had said about their lord.
He agreed to both. She gave him a curt bow, wiped his blood off her sword, sheathed it,
and said she was satisfied. Across the river [[Daidoji Shin]] called the whole thing as
it happened to everyone in the drawing room, down to the concession.

[[Bayushi Monban]] walked to the [[Geisha House]] with his face open. The madam met him
with alarm; he asked her for thread, a needle and a candle, sat down in front of a mirror,
and sewed it shut himself. Then he asked for a replacement scabbard, charged to the
Emerald Magistrate account. The [[Ifrit]] spoke up in his head while he worked — *I could
have burned her alive for you* — and he turned it aside without arguing, back to the
business of getting the thing home to its desert. It did not press. He told it to let him
sleep, and left word that he would ride as soon as the two horses and the guide arranged
through his [[Ide Family|Ide]] contact were ready.

Up in the tower, [[Doji Setsuna]] went on teaching. The night before had been rhetoric.
Now she gave [[Miya Misato]] the two things she has in depth — how a government actually
moves, and how to hurt someone with her hands and with whatever is lying on the table.
Go for the eyes. Everything in a room is a weapon.

[[Karahaya]] made her report in the audience chamber with [[Doji Setsuna]] and
[[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] standing in it. She had an apology out of *the unmasked Scorpion*; he
had not landed a blow; he had shamed himself several times over; and she had left him a
permanent reminder. She laid a piece of his broken scabbard down as a trophy.
[[Governor Miya Tetsuya]] smiled broadly and said something warm about the honour of those
who serve them. [[Daidoji Shin]] began composing a song. The ronin had already settled on
the name.

The letter came up from [[Shiba Midori]] afterwards — the poisoning, the ejection, the
duel and the loss, the state of the island. [[Kitsuko Ayoko]] had begun it and
[[Shiba Midori]] had taken over and finished it herself, and [[Doji Setsuna]] read the
seam: the hand changes, and so does the voice behind it, and each of them had chosen what
to put on her side of the join. Reading it properly was the best hour of her week.
[[Shiba Midori]], for her part, noted from [[Kitsuko Ayoko]]'s reply that Setsuna was being
described as *a private guest of the governor*, and approved.

Then [[Doji Setsuna]] turned to [[Miya Misato]] and told her what to do about it. *Tell
your uncle that you intend to leave with us, and anyone [[Shiba Midori]] needs to travel
safely — and at your earliest convenience, feel free to command us all to leave with you.*
The order would be the envoy's. The timing, the conditions and the baggage were Setsuna's.

[[Governor Miya Tetsuya]] briefed [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]], [[Daidoji Shin]] and [[Karahaya]] on
the shinobi. [[Central Island]] has four shores. The docks are already covered; guards would
go on the north; [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] and others would take the west and the east, with the
rooftop archers able to answer either way. And there was a second thing: a large body of
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] samurai — [[Matsu Family|Matsu]], a whole team of them — was trying to
cross to the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] side. The governor had asked them not to land at the
[[City Of The Rich Frog]], and expected to have their exact position by midday tomorrow, at
which point a meeting could be arranged.

[[Miya Misato]] asked to leave in the morning. The governor agreed, and attached
[[Karahaya]] to her as yojimbo, along with whatever [[Shiba Midori]] needed to travel.

Late that night [[Doji Setsuna]] could not sleep. She was reading [[Shiba Midori]]'s letter
by the western window when she saw them — figures crossing the rooftops, cut clean against
the dark red tiles by the moon. She called two guards over, pointed, and they ran. Then she
went down to [[Miya Misato]], who was half awake over documents with ink on her fingers,
made her tea, and mentioned while she poured that the guards seemed to have spotted the
evil-doers, and that she hoped they would catch them.

[[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] had the west shore, walking the beach with a lantern in air stance, and
the full moon gave him the whole length of it. [[Shiba Midori]] was found sleepwalking in a
corridor by a guard, slurring something about *your brother* and hoping he was alive. She
woke, dressed properly, looked in on [[Kitsuko Ayoko]] — asleep over a half-written letter —
and went down to join him, coming up on him quietly enough not to be noticed and then setting
herself facing the castle while he faced the river, so that between them they had all of it.

Watching the castle is how she saw them: four men running the rooftops, shooting fire arrows
and shouting. Those were [[Karahaya]]'s ronin. Three shinobi came off the roof ahead of them
and ran for the water.

[[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] stepped out from behind a tree and took the first with a rising cut the
man never saw, and the leg came off below the knee. *Jumped up, peasant-born scum.*
[[Shiba Midori]] went at another with her jitte, open-handed, and got a shuriken in the
shoulder for it — thrown off-hand, which told her the man was ambidextrous — and the wound
went deep enough to tear her water ring. She caught a spinning blade with a shattering parry
at the last moment and the jitte broke doing it.

Then a shinobi put a noxious red smoke bomb at [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]]'s feet. It went through
armour as though there were none, and left them both dazed and exhausted. A second one came
at him and missed; he went under it, and it struck a tree, bounced into the river, and boiled
the water where it landed, sending green steam up off the surface.

[[Shiba Midori]] took stock — wounded in more rings than she could afford, dazed, exhausted,
her weapon gone — steadied her breathing enough to move, and ran for the castle shouting for
help. She passed [[Karahaya]]'s ronin coming the other way at speed.

[[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] stayed on them to the waterline. Compromised and half blind in the smoke,
he could not land anything, and spent what he had making the last one's flight worse. The
archers dropped another off the roof with three arrows in him. It ended there: two shinobi
dead or dying, one still going for the water under fire, and the ronin closing.

## Setsuna

**Her yojimbo is beaten, scarred, named, and gone.** [[Bayushi Monban]] was defeated in
public without landing a blow, apologised on the dock, withdrew what he had said about the
governor, and rode out of the city. The governor's own ronin are calling him *the unmasked
Scorpion* and [[Daidoji Shin]] is putting it to music. That is not a private humiliation
she can manage — by the end of the week it will be a song, and the song will name the
Emerald Magistrates.

**[[Karahaya]] is now inside the escort.** The woman who took her yojimbo apart is attached
to [[Miya Misato]] as yojimbo for the road. She is plainly the most competent fighter
anyone has seen this month and she gave [[Bayushi Monban]] two chances to walk away before
she cut him, which is a fair record. She is also [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]]'s officer,
travelling with the governor's niece, alongside a magistrate the governor has already had
reason to doubt. Setsuna should treat her as an asset and assume she reports.

**The assessment from last week is now confirmed on the record.** [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] was
given a standing instruction to watch whether the magistrates' own methods endangered the
envoy. Since then their yojimbo has insulted the governor, been ejected, drawn steel on the
guard, lost a duel over it, and left the city. The governor's caution has been vindicated
in front of witnesses, and every request Setsuna makes from here is made against that.

**The Matsu are the thing to move on.** A whole team of [[Matsu Family|Matsu]] warriors is
crossing to the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] side, has been asked to keep clear of the city, and
will be located by midday tomorrow — the morning the embassy rides west with an Imperial
writ ordering the Lion to stand down. Last week she was warned the Lion escort would come
with a price. Whose troops these are, and who ordered them across, is a question she should
have answered **before** the envoy's column is anywhere near them.

**She traded a rumour for a room and it is working.** She is not correcting the talk about
the governor because correcting it would cost more than letting it stand, and it has put her
inside the tower with the envoy at the hour when people are being taken out of guarded rooms.
It has also put her in the mouths of a court that includes a [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]
delegation, in a city she entered to get her husband out of a Lion prison. That bill arrives
later and she should know it is coming.

**What she actually did this week was teach.** [[Miya Misato]] can now argue, has some idea
how a government moves, and knows to go for the eyes. She is sixteen, she carries the
Emperor's writ, and she is riding to a war. Of everything on this island, that is the piece
most likely to still matter in a year.

And she saw the roofs herself, from a window, and had guards on them inside a minute. Two
shinobi are dead and one went into the water alive. **If the ronin take that one, it is the
first person in this whole affair anyone can question.**
