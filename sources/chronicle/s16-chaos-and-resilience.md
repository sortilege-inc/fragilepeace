## Narrative

!lede The Lion had been driven off the [[Central Hill]] in the night. A Unicorn cavalry strike had come in on the rear lines where [[Matsu Sakura]] stood, killed a great many of her attendants, and been fought off her by her shugenja and her bodyguards without anybody managing to kill the officer who led it — though [[Akodo Toronoko]] laid her open badly enough on the way out that she would not be riding again that day. The Unicorn had reversed the previous day's gains and routed the Lion at a second point besides, and [[Akodo Kagekatsu]] had crawled back from the village holding his armour plates together as though to keep his own guts inside them.

Morning came bitter, with the snow finally stopped and the whole country glittering white — which favoured the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]], who knew what was under it. Rations had been quartered. There was not enough firewood. And the soldiers had begun saying that some evil spirit wanted them to fail.

[[Kitsu Somalia]] answered that by pulling [[Tengoku]] down over the camp — a place of power under hallowed fire, warmth of a banquet hall in the middle of a snowfield — and every man inside it stopped carrying what he had been carrying for three days.

Then [[Ikoma Akuyaku]] came in near dawn on a frozen foot with a fresh wound in his side, and the scouts who had written him off were amazed to see him. *Tis only a flesh wound.* He had gone into the [[Unicorn Clan War Camp]] and tried to kill [[Shinjo Kamo]]. *Killing the opposing general is more difficult than sneaking into the camp.* [[Matsu Sakura]] stopped him there. *I gave you no such orders. I don't want him assassinated. I want him defeated* — and now she would have to apologise for a thing she had not ordered, because nobody on the other side would believe that. What he brought back instead was worth having: the Unicorn reinforcements were better prepared than anyone had reason to expect, *like they knew there were going to be deeper snows.*

The tally in the general's tent was not as bad as the camp felt. [[Akodo Kayamayako]] reported most of the poisoned men recovering and advised against taking the army out at all. [[Akodo Toronoko]] had collapsed at the shrine with an enemy commander's head still in her hands and was not going back on the field if [[Matsu Sakura]] could help it, which cost the Lion one of the two commanders she still rated. [[Akodo Kagekatsu]] — [[Akodo Toronoko]]'s own cousin, and [[Ikoma Akuyaku]]'s wife's cousin with him — had a blade through one lung and had somehow lived the night. *Then at least one of them will not be challenging the other*, said [[Matsu Hisoka]]. *That alone was terrible for discipline. To see two of the finest in the army scrapping like tigers.*

Against that, the count [[Matsu Sakura]] made out loud: [[Matsu Morozane]] had brought her the head of one of the [[Five Pillars]], [[Akodo Toronoko]] another, and she had watched [[Akodo Toronoko]] flay open the leg of a third. *We have killed or incapacitated three of his five best commanders.*

[[Matsu Morozane]] wanted to use that. Every fight so far had been on ground the Unicorn chose; the one piece of ground the Lion held was the camp. So put a token force out, make it look like a boy in his father's armour, let it collapse and run, and close on whoever chases it — and he quoted *Akodo’s Leadership* on the strength of false weakness to say it was not dishonourable. [[Matsu Hisoka]] would not have it. *Every time we split our forces, we lose.* The argument never got resolved, because the Unicorn came first.

They came, and they did not come at the camp. [[Ikoma Hideri]] — the historian, again — walked over to [[Kitsu Somalia]] and gave her the thing nobody else had seen. There was a calm on the Unicorn troops that was not on the Lion. *I have noticed that the enemy general's associate, [[Iuchi Gero]], one of the Five Pillars, wore a great many talismans the day they met with the general. I wonder if she is not a shugenja herself, or is protected by one.* [[Kitsu Somalia]] asked the kami and the kami confirmed it: talismans on that woman, throwing an aura over her troops, warding them against ground and cold alike. The [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] are said to bind kami and take their power over the long term, and [[Kitsu Somalia]] wondered whether this was that.

Then [[Matsu Hisoka]] read the rest of it off the enemy's line of march. *They are making for [[Crystal Pass]]! They mean to cut off our supplies!* The Unicorn had split — most of them to hold the Lion in place, and the unit [[Matsu Morozane]] had fought at the village going the long way round the camp to take the pass.

[[Matsu Sakura]] made her order narrow, and then shouted it across the field for anyone who had missed it. *I want [[Iuchi Gero]]'s head. Will no one rid me of this troublesome general?* Take her off the field and the protection goes with her. To [[Ikoma Akuyaku]], pointedly: *I expect you to destroy them in battle, not by subterfuge.*

It was fought on dangerous ground that took men out of both armies every round, except that the Unicorn were not feeling it. [[Kitsu Somalia]] worked the flank. [[Ikoma Akuyaku]] kept his archers three range bands back and shot, and made no pretence about the kind of fighting it was. *I want them moving around. I want them burying themselves in the snow. I am done with this honourable standing here and getting wiped out.* [[Matsu Morozane]] tried Call the Wild to set the lion on her and failed it — his burned fire ring makes everything through fire three degrees harder — and then feinted instead, and let [[Shiguro Chinmoku]] go at the horse. She opened its flank so it could not carry anyone further, took a warhorse's hooves and went across the field with a wound in her air ring, and [[Iuchi Gero]] came off the saddle hard and furious.

That was the opening. [[Matsu Morozane]] could let her get to her feet and fight her honourably, or finish her where she lay. He finished her where she lay. The katana came down the front of her, took the talisman cord off her neck, and then her head; and the general who had ordered her destroyed in battle took ten honour off him for the manner of it, and gave him eight glory, and he went down to twenty honour. The Unicorn army took ten measures of panic and, for the first time that day, began to feel the cold.

And around [[Kitsu Yue]], on the other side of the field, the snow was rising in a whirlwind that removing [[Iuchi Gero]]'s talismans did nothing to touch.

She had not meant to do it. Bloodied and surrounded by Unicorn spears, holding ground she had broken off from [[Kitsu Somalia]] to take, [[Kitsu Yue]] had prayed — and armoured [[Lion Clan|Lion]] ancestors came across the [[Snow Plain]] and cut into everything in their path. She could not hold them, or herself. A spear went into her and she went down. The Unicorn broke and fell back to their fortified hill, and [[Matsu Sakura]] gathered her own troops and pulled them out, and by the time anyone was back in camp the whisper going round the Lion army was that [[Kitsu Yue]] was some terrible foreign sorcerer.

[[Matsu Morozane]] wanted no part of it — *that is the weird shugenja business I do not touch* — and took a contingent with [[Matsu Hisoka]] after the column making for the pass instead. They were too late. The Unicorn were dug in at [[Crystal Pass]] with their spears turned around, and the Lion could have had it at a price they no longer had to pay. *A valiant effort*, said [[Matsu Hisoka]], *but we cannot dislodge them today.*

[[Kitsu Somalia]] and [[Ikoma Akuyaku]] went to the shrine of the ancestors instead, where [[Kitsu Yue]] was praying with fear all over her face. *I prayed to the ancestors to live to a good death. I did not pray that they would come and fight to the death. What happened?*

What happened is that only a handful of spiritually-minded Lion have ever been able to call their ancestors into a fight, that it is the greatest honour there is, and that the ancestors do not answer anyone they do not judge worthy. [[Kitsu Somalia]] told her so, and thought [[Tengoku]] being pulled close that morning may have opened the way. [[Ikoma Akuyaku]] wrapped her in a blanket and told her she had saved everyone who made it back and that anybody with a problem could take it up with him and his wife. Then he sang to her until she stopped shaking.

Then [[Kitsu Somalia]] communed, and the ancestors were there — hers, Yue's, the long dead and the newly dead, standing and watching. *We do not know. We have never been called like this before. This one*, they said, pointing at [[Kitsu Yue]], *something about her. We heard the call, we took up arms, and we found we could stand in the field with our descendants. You cannot know how glorious it is. Show her, Spirit Talker, how she can do it again.*

[[Kitsu Somalia]] asked the question that mattered: if the ancestors win the battle, whose honour is it? *A Lion's honour is the honour of his ancestors and his own. We earned this together. When you call them for aid and they answer, it is because you are righteous, because you are walking the path of honour.* [[Kitsu Yue]] — who is not a shugenja, and was never found to be one, and is apparently something nobody had a test for — nodded slowly, and the dead beamed at her. *You understand what it is to be Lion. Call us again. We long for battle.* They stamped their naginatas and danced and faded, not far.

[[Kitsu Somalia]] drew [[Tengoku]] down again over the whole camp, and a great deal of the day's panic went out of the army with it.

And [[Matsu Morozane]] handed her the black talisman he had cut off [[Iuchi Gero]]'s neck. She knew what was in it the moment she touched it. A spirit, bound into a physical object, which is not a thing Rokugan permits anyone to do. She wanted to study how it had been done so she could let the thing out — and on terms, because a freed spirit that hates her is worse than a bound one. It might also know a good deal about the Unicorn camp, which is more than anyone had got out of the prisoner.

The pass was held, the supply line was cut, and there was about one more day of food.

## Setsuna

The flashback ends here, and it hands her three things.

**The dead fight, and [[Kitsu Yue]] did not know she could call them.** She prayed to live to a good death. What came were armoured [[Lion Clan|Lion]] ancestors who cut into a living army, and she could not hold them, and they put her on the ground. That is the working described in Kitsu Mitsuru's scroll — the one that came off a shelf in the [[Kitsu Archives]] at [[Kitsu Taigen]]'s daughter — and the warning on that scroll is the same thing the ancestors said out loud on the field: they do not answer a summons from an honourless samurai. They answered [[Kitsu Yue]]. That is a finding about her character that no document will ever carry, and the army's own response to it was to start calling her a foreign sorcerer.

She should hold that beside what [[Kitsu Ayoko]] was told at the shrine: that the kami did not speak to her, or not loudly enough, and she was turned away. The ancestors say of [[Kitsu Yue]] that they have never been called like this before, and that it is *something about her*. Whatever that is, it is not the thing the shrine maidens knew how to test for, and it has come down the line.

**The Lion won the day and lost the campaign in it.** Three of the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]'s [[Five Pillars]] dead, a fourth crippled, the enemy driven back to their own hill — and at the end of it the [[Crystal Pass]] is in Unicorn hands, the supply line is cut, and there is one day of food in the camp. Whatever finally decided the [[Snow Plain]], it was logistics and it was betrayal, and the account in the [[Ikoma Hall of Scribes|Hall of Scribes]] describes neither.

And there is the matter of her own ancestor. [[Matsu Morozane]] was given a choice between letting a disarmed woman stand up and fight, and cutting her throat where she lay, and he took the second and paid ten honour for it, which leaves him at twenty. Two days ago he pretended not to hear a challenge. She is inside a man who is winning by doing things the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] do not write down — and she is going into a courtroom to argue that the Lion record of this battle is incomplete. It is. That is not the same as it being unfair.

The last thing is a black talisman with something alive bound inside it, cut off a Unicorn shugenja's neck by [[Matsu Morozane]] and put into a [[Kitsu Family|Kitsu]]'s hands. Binding a spirit into an object is not a thing Rokugan allows. That thread is worth following, and the memory chose to stop exactly there.
