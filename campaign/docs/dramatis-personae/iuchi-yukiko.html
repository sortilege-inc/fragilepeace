Iuchi Yukiko

Unicorn — Iuchi

What is known

S52A middle-aged woman in a shugenja's robes and talismans who gave the morning blessing herself because Midori is a party to the case. Kage's opposite number. Her warning: a lion in a trap is more dangerous, because it believes it has nothing left to lose, and the trick is to let them lose without feeling they have lost. Her advice on Ikari: find her quickly, and do not put her in front of this table. Her priorities, in order: land, horses, hostages — give those three and she can likely bring Altansarnai to the rest.
S53The Unicorn's advisor, named at last — one of the grandmothers of the Iuchi, here for Shinjō Altansarnai exactly as Akodo Kage is here for Akodo Toturi: high standing, no power to bind, present so a daimyō's weight is felt without a daimyō's word. Meishōdō and a shugenja. She may be addressed as grandmother without offence. She holds that the Crane are plucking at Lion fur, that the arbiter would rather be right than have peace, and that being right will not bring back her dead grandchildren. Her counsel to Midori: give the Lion the acknowledgement of victory, which costs least, and keep marriages off the table or be here until Winter Court.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
