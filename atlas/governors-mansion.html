Governor’s Mansion

The Governor’s Manor is located on an island in the center of a river, surrounded by the city known as the Rich Frog. This prominent structure features resplendent architecture that reflects the authority of Governor Miya Tetsuya. Visitors are greeted by guards upon arrival and escorted to a great meeting room designated for significant discussions. The formal interior includes a large table where the governor and his associates convene, indicating its role in high-level decision-making.

Recently, the Emerald Magistrates were summoned to the manor to address an unusual issue faced by the governor. Upon entering, they found Governor Miya Tetsuya and Daidoji Shin already present. The discussions focused on a theater fire linked to a released spirit, with the governor describing the strange behavior of a guest, suggesting potential possession. The meeting underscored the need for discretion and the importance of the magistrates' expertise in these unusual situations, reinforcing the manor's role as a central hub for addressing critical issues within the city.

The Governor's Mansion is a prominent structure in the city, serving as the residence of the local daimyo. It includes guest quarters for visitors such as samurai and courtiers, with individual rooms available for guests.

Recently, a series of alarming events occurred within the mansion, starting with a scream from the Guest Wing. Miya Misato, The Governor's niece, was discovered in her ransacked room, visibly terrified and pointing to a shuriken embedded in the wall. This shuriken was later identified as a potential weapon used in a theft rather than an assassination attempt. The Governor and his guards responded swiftly, retrieving the shuriken along with a warning message written in dark blue ink.

As the situation escalated, it became evident that multiple shinobi had infiltrated the mansion. A plan was proposed to switch Miya Misato with another individual for her protection, and The Governor pledged to send men to safeguard her, acknowledging the risks she faced. Evidence suggested that intruders had searched hastily for a specific item, leaving signs of their presence and raising concerns about the mansion's security.

The Governor’s Palace is an ornate structure within the realm, featuring various rooms, including a kitchen and a Central Garden. It is located in a region experiencing late summer heat, which contrasts with the unusual behaviors of its inhabitants.

In the kitchen, a woman in a purple dress exhibits erratic behavior, such as feeding logs into the furnace while expressing discomfort from the cold, which is inappropriate for her noble status. During a critical incident, Miya Tetsuya leads a group to the kitchen to investigate her condition. The situation escalates when the woman, believed to be possessed by a spirit, wanders to the Central Garden's fish pond, undresses, and consumes a goldfish raw.

The group establishes a Threshold Barrier around the pond area in an attempt to contain the spirit. Despite their efforts to expel the spirit, it remains within the woman, leading to confrontations and rituals aimed at extracting and sealing the spirit. The events culminate in the successful containment of the spirit within a new vessel, while the palace's environment remains charged with supernatural energy.

The Governor’s residence is a fortified structure in the City Of The Rich Frog, serving as the home of Governor Miya Tetsuya. It features multiple levels with heightened security, especially on the upper residential floors. The interior includes private quarters, a courtyard, and garden areas for various activities and discussions. The residence is currently a focal point of political maneuvering, particularly regarding the safety of the governor's niece, Miya Misato, who has been relocated within the castle due to multiple shinobi attacks. Doji Setsuna gained entry to the residence by persuading the ronin guards, despite standing orders against visitors. Inside, she encountered a young woman attempting to mimic Miya Misato's voice, leading her to realize the individual was neither the governor's wife nor his niece. After addressing the deception, Setsuna continued her search for Miya Misato and accessed the guarded room where she was hidden. Meanwhile, Ikoma Tadayoshi spread rumors in the garden while playing the flute, and the governor held a private audience with Tadayoshi to discuss escorting Miya Misato to the Lion and Unicorn camps, expressing concerns about potential demands from the Lion and the implications of the ongoing political situation.

The Miya Governor’s Palace is located on a Central Island within the City Of The Rich Frog, which is divided among the territories of the Lion, Dragon, and Unicorn clans. It is governed by Miya Amaya, an Imperial, highlighting its significance in the region's political landscape.

The palace serves as a focal point for governance and authority, facilitating the administrative functions essential for managing the interactions among the clans. Its central location signifies its importance in the city, as it is a key destination for discussions and engagements with local authorities.
