Slow Tide Harbor

Slow Tide Harbor is a coastal navigable river port known for its organized structure and diverse trade, strategically positioned on the border of three clan territories: the Dragon control the north bank, the Lion control the south bank, and the Unicorn control the west bank. The harbor is equipped with docks and moorings for a variety of vessels, including a notably large blue-water ship that is unsuitable for river travel. The climate is typically mild, supporting year-round maritime activities, making it a hub of commerce and intrigue.

At the heart of Slow Tide Harbor lies the Miya Governor’s Palace, where Governor Miya Amaya resides, situated on a Central Island within the harbor. The area is a politically sensitive zone, especially given the presence of the Emperor's representatives. Various factions, including the Lion, Dragon, and Unicorn, pursue their own interests, creating a competitive atmosphere fueled by different motives. The harbor is home to multiple investigators, highlighting the complexities of local politics and smuggling, as well as the urgent nature of ongoing investigations.

Recently, local head lawman Kaeru Haya escorted Emerald Magistrates to The Docks based on suspicions of imminent smuggling activities. The situation escalated when Shiba Midori, a Phoenix Emerald Magistrate, encountered two assailants in a dark alley near The Docks, leading to a violent confrontation. The investigation revealed a significant smuggling operation, with Kaeru Haya reporting rumors of foreign relics sought by smugglers, including Mantis interests. During this investigation, the Emerald Magistrates discovered a smaller fishing skiff tied to the dock, where Midori was later found incapacitated after an attack.

The search for missing magistrates intensified, revealing fewer boats than expected, necessitating further action from the magistrates. Tensions rose as the large blue-water ship prepared to depart the harbor, leading to a confrontation between Bayushi Monban and Captain Kubota regarding the ship's activities and a ransom situation involving a kidnapped girl. Reports of disappearances and criminal activity suggest that Slow Tide Harbor is a focal point for both legitimate and illicit dealings, further complicating the already intricate web of intrigue that defines this vital location.
