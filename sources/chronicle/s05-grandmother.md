## Narrative

!lede The [[Border Waystation]] sits between the [[Kitsu Family|Kitsu]] and [[Akodo Family|Akodo]] provinces on a small lake with a [[Shrine of the Lake Kami]] on it. Three [[Kitsu Family|Kitsu]] samurai keep the post — two of vassal families, one a pureblood who placed himself, precisely, thirty-seven steps from the line of succession. A Lion will tell you his ancestry to that resolution and expect you to find it useful. The hosts were surprised to be handed several Emerald Magistrates at once and said the papers would be read in the morning.

Over tea the conversation ran the usual course and then stopped being usual. A magistrate remarked on [[Doji Setsuna]]'s southern upbringing and hoped the plainer chicken and beef of the waystation would suit; she answered that a narrow palate understands nothing, and told the table about a meal taken with her [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] mentor where the diplomacy happened between the courses. [[Shiba Midori]] said home is family, and she and [[Bayushi Monban]] found they were both missing the same people — a sister neither of them named lightly, and [[Lady Tsubasa]]. [[Shiba Midori]] said she caught a glimpse of the [[Moon Maiden]] in his eyes when he said it. Then she made the case that magistrates are the [[Emperor]]'s voice and owe the people as much as the people owe them, in a figure about a bonsai: trim too much and the tree dies, trim too little and it is a bush.

Then a cold wind came through a tea house at the wrong season. [[Shiba Midori]] felt something and did not like where it was standing, which was next to [[Doji Setsuna]]. The three of them went out to the lake, and from there to the station's [[Shrine to the Ancestors]] with candles, sake, and clan animals [[Bayushi Monban]] folded from paper. [[Bayushi Monban]] considered taking off his mask for the rite. [[Shiba Midori]] told him not to — *not all spirits are trustworthy* — and that unmasking would offer a spirit the trust owed to family or to a fellow magistrate. The shrine's monk shifted at hearing an ancestor spoken of as a thing that might not be reliable.

[[Shiba Midori]] made the communion. The lights went out, gold ginkgo leaves gathered themselves into a shape, and [[Matsu Tomoe]] walked into the hall smelling of jasmine and leaving blood on the floor behind her. *You are not [[Kitsu Family|Kitsu]]. Why have you called me here?*

Then she saw her granddaughter, and the audience became a reckoning. [[Doji Setsuna]] has never once come to the [[Hall of Ancestors]] to pay respect. She keeps a yojimbo. *You are so weak that you depend on another's sword to keep your breath?* [[Doji Setsuna]] bowed to the floor, named the neglect as hers, apologised for a sacrifice unbefitting the woman it was owed to, and asked to be told how to remember her properly. She was told: honour first, before everything, including when honour is inconvenient. She swore in the same breath to restore her husband's honour.

[[Bayushi Monban]] showed the scar on his neck. [[Matsu Tomoe]] brought her fingers near it and it burned him without being touched. *You bear the touch of [[Jigoku]].* She ordered him cleansed. She ordered [[Shiba Midori]] to cleanse herself as well, for having come to the spirits with a bargain in her hands. Then she said that [[Doji Setsuna]]'s companions are compromised, that they are walking through [[Lion Clan]] ancestral ground, and that she would walk with them and keep her eye on her granddaughter — and went back to ginkgo leaves and jasmine.

[[Bayushi Monban]] told the shrine monk to say nothing of what he had seen or heard. The monk agreed readily, which is what a man does when he has understood the size of the thing he has just watched.

## Learned

- Matsu Tomoe: Her grandmother. A Matsu, armoured above and in hakama below, daishō and courtier's fan both, trailing blood and the scent of jasmine. She answers a summons even from a caller who is not Kitsu, and she is angry about neglect: Setsuna has never attended the Hall of Ancestors. Her standing instruction is honour before all else, particularly when it is inconvenient. She says she will walk with the party through Lion ancestral lands and watch Setsuna.
- Bayushi Monban: He bears the touch of Jigoku, in a scar on his neck that burned when an ancestor's hand came near it without touching him. Tomoe ordered him cleansed. He has said nothing about how he got it, and he told the shrine's monk to keep the whole matter quiet.
- Shiba Midori: She can sense a spirit's presence in a room and locate it, which is how this began. She holds that not all spirits are trustworthy, including ancestors — a position the shrine monk found uncomfortable. Tomoe rebuked her for approaching the dead with a transaction in mind and ordered her to cleanse herself.
- Lady Tsubasa: Missed by both Monban and Midori, along with a sister they did not name. Midori says she sees the Moon Maiden in Monban's eyes when the subject comes up.

## Setsuna

Three things came out of that shrine and all three are hers to carry.

She has an ancestor walking with the party through [[Lion Lands|Lion ancestral lands]] and watching her specifically. That is protection and it is supervision, and [[Matsu Tomoe]] did not distinguish between them. The standing instruction is honour first, and it was given in the form of a correction rather than advice.

She has a debt at the [[Hall of Ancestors]] that she has never paid, now named aloud in front of two companions and a monk. It is the first thing to fix, and fixing it is the only thing she was told plainly enough to act on.

And her yojimbo is tainted by [[Jigoku]] — confirmed by an ancestor, not guessed at — and under an order to be cleansed. [[Bayushi Monban]] has not explained the scar. He may not answer failure with seppuku, he is forbidden that; and now he is carrying something that will keep failing him. The monk who saw it has been told to be quiet, which works until it does not.
