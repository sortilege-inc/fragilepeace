Tea and Tensions in Transit

Session 35·2026-01-06

The Imperial Envoy she is sworn to see succeed walked into a tea house carrying a writ, and turned out to be young, unsure where either army is, and entirely in earnest.

Shiba Midori hosted the tea at the Governor's Tea House. Kakita Kazumi and Doji Setsuna came; Bayushi Monban did not, and Kakita Kazumi did not like the shape of that absence.

Then Miya Misato arrived — the Emperor's envoy, young, sent to broker the peace between the Lion and the Unicorn — with an Imperial writ ordering the Lion armies to stop and to seek out the Unicorn Champion. She is inexperienced and she is not pretending otherwise, and she means it.

Shiba Midori and Kakita Kazumi committed on the spot to go with her to the front. Shiba Midori told the room why it matters to Doji Setsuna in particular: her husband is among the prisoners. They would leave at first light.

There was a problem underneath the mission. Miya Misato does not know where either army currently is. The group remembered a missive about items turning up near the Battle of the Snow Plains, which gives them somewhere to start looking.

So they went to ask. Governor Miya Tetsuya gave them a boat across to the Unicorn District and its merchant quarter, thick with samurai and rōnin. Kakita Kazumi's contact put him onto Ide Chiharu, who deals in curiosities, and Hideyoshi Aki, a trader of real weight — and from Aki's people he got the useful thing: recent shipments have shifted northeast. Goods follow armies.

Bayushi Monban tried a young Scorpion samurai, who knew nothing about the armies and knew exactly who Bayushi Monban was. Then he went to the Burnt Theater and walked the ruin, where the spiritual disturbance has not settled.

Shiba Midori confirmed the Ifrit's box was secured in her own quarters inside a long receptacle, and started thinking about guards and about a Kitsu she could trust with it.

And through the day, quietly, the other thing. Doji Setsuna asked Shiba Midori about a Terminating Tea — what it does, what it costs, whether it would be available if her condition required it. Shiba Midori said what it was and did not argue with her.

Ishika and Aika were sent for the ingredients and came back with mostly the wrong things, which is what happens when servants are sent for something nobody will name. Kakita Kazumi fixed it without being asked — advised her on diluted nightshade, and delivered the correct leaves to Shiba Midori himself with instructions on handling them. Shiba Midori used her authority as an Emerald Magistrate to make an apothecary hand over the last restricted herb.

Then she brewed the first dose, and measured it carefully.

That evening Doji Setsuna drank it, under Shiba Midori's eye. Her attendants turned Bayushi Monban away at the door and Kakita Kazumi steered him off asking further. It worked, and it took the toll Shiba Midori had told her it would: it wounded Doji Setsuna badly in the void, and she was still carrying that wound weeks later.

Doji Setsuna requisitioned a palanquin from Governor Miya Tetsuya for the journey.

Setsuna

Her giri just walked into the room. She is sworn to see Miya Misato succeed in brokering this peace, and until today that was a sentence in a letter. The envoy is young, does not know where the armies are, and is going to the front anyway with a writ in her hand. Whatever else is true this week, this is the duty, and it is now a person she is responsible for rather than an objective.

It also puts her exactly where she wants to be. The writ orders the Lion to stop fighting and treat with the Unicorn Champion. Prisoner exchanges happen when armies stop. Shinjo Harunobu is with the Lion host. Serving the envoy honestly and getting her husband back are, for the first time in thirty-five sessions, the same road — and she should be careful, because that is precisely when a person stops noticing which one they are actually serving.

Northeast. Hideyoshi Aki's shipments have moved and goods follow armies. That is the only hard fact anyone has about where the front is, and it came from a merchant's stock ledger rather than from anyone's intelligence service. Same lesson as the quartermasters' books at the Snow Plain: the people who count things know first.

And the private matter is being handled by her friends and not by her household. Ishika and Aika were sent for the ingredients without being told what they were for and came back with the wrong things. Kakita Kazumi fixed it quietly and made no remark. Shiba Midori brewed it herself and measured the dose.

That is three people who now know, plus an apothecary who was compelled by a magistrate's authority to hand over a restricted herb and will remember which magistrate it was. Doji Setsuna drank it the same evening, and it cost her a severe wound in the void — hers, not the woman who brewed it. She has a palanquin ordered for a journey she would ordinarily ride, and she is leaving at dawn for a war.

Two people know what the palanquin is actually for — Shiba Midori and Kakita Kazumi. Bayushi Monban was turned away at the door and does not. She should decide deliberately whether that stays true, rather than letting it be decided by whoever is standing nearest when she next collapses.

Nothing here needs advice. It needs noting that the timing is hers, that Shiba Midori did not argue, and that the road to the front is three days of a woman being carried in a box while she decides.
