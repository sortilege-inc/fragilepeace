Western Wall

The Western Wall is part of the City Of The Rich Frog's castle structure, located on the western side. It features a second-level tower with a single door for entry and no windows, designed for security. The area above the tower has an open ceiling, providing potential access points for intruders. The wall has carefully removed and replaced tiles on the eastern side, concealing disturbances.

An investigation revealed scratches on the roof above the Western Wall consistent with climbing claws or a grappling hook, along with rope fibers found between tiles. This suggests that intruders may have traversed the roof. The area allows for an overwatch of the guest quarters, including the room assigned to Shiba Midori, an Emerald Magistrate. Guards were informed about disturbances on the west side, while details regarding the eastern side's findings were withheld. The castle's perimeter was checked for potential infiltration tools, indicating ongoing security concerns related to a recent attack.
