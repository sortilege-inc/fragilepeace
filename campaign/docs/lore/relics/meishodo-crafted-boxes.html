Relic

Meishōdō crafted boxes

Meishōdō crafted boxes are specialized containers designed to trap spirits. They are required for the containment of a fire spirit that has manifested within Bayushi Monban. Shiba Midori confirmed that previous boxes used for this purpose are no longer effective, necessitating the creation of new Meishōdō crafted boxes. The assistance of shugenja is necessary for the successful containment of the spirit.
