Dramatis Persona

Kitsu Ayoko

Lion

What is known

S8A scholar of the Military Library who offered her help unprompted, and the daughter of Kitsu Taigen. Something invisible stands at her shoulder. Her eyes turn gold, and when the specter appeared she knew its name without being told and without ever having spoken to a spirit in her life.
S9Adopted, not of samurai birth, just short of her genpuku, golden-eyed. She asked to be a shrine maiden and was rejected — they said the kami did not speak to her. She is manifesting as a shugenja now. She dreams the Snow Plain through someone else's eyes: a pass, a field, a hill everything was fought over, purple and burgundy banners, and the Lion on the field. The archives move around her.
S18Sat with her ancestor's scroll all evening and could not say what the poem meant. Found the treaty for Midori and handed it over. Doji Setsuna insulted her birth to her face, and she left.
S21Was assigned, with a team of researchers, to gather the Snow Plain documents for the magistrates before they arrived, and Ichigo calls her diligent and thorough. In on the Imperial-possession conversation, and working with Setsuna on summoning Kitsu Yue's spirit.
S22Arrived with the documents Kaage requested and evidence of her own to present. Her ancestor is Kitsu Yue.
S25Testified from field reports and quartermaster records that the Lion army absorbed disaster after disaster and was still fighting when the Imperials arrived, so the sabotage did not decide the battle.
S26Called to testify with no warning and went white, then delivered the session's clearest finding while compromised. Named her ancestor's mistake as fear, and fear of having very nearly won. Says only the most honourable spirit speakers can call the ancestors to fight for them, and intends to become worthy of it.
S27Now Midori's yoriki, and visibly delighted about it in a room that reads faces. Drove an intruder out of Midori's quarters with a broom because a katana would not be fair, and did not look at the things she was told to guard.
S41Took Midori's dictation while she was too ill to write — the message to Monban, and the first half of the letter to Setsuna. Fell asleep over the next one.
S46Midori's attendant, and the only other person in the tent with real medical training. She came in after the first attempt failed and her hands are the reason the second one landed.
S47Found the envoy's body.
—Nursed Setsuna through a week of fever and kept her in tea.
S56Turned into a walking bonfire by Shiba Midori — dangerous to approach and impossible for anything on the river to ignore. She was the one who noticed her master fading out of the world.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
