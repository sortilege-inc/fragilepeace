Dramatis Persona

Akodo Toronoko

Lion

What is known

S10One of the three field commands taken forward across the bog.
S11Set the Lion order of battle — a thin elite centre as bait for a frontal charge, both flanks reinforced to close on what the charge opened. The centre had still not been committed by the end of the second round.
S13Ikoma Akuyaku's wife, and a commander of the Lion left. She treated his frostbite herself with sage water and paste, and when Akodo Kagekatsu blamed her for her husband's intelligence work in front of others, she gave him nothing.
S14Pinned the Unicorn with a curtain of arrows and broke a spearman advance by splitting her volleys high and low.
S15Won a duel against a Unicorn field leader in the blizzard with a Flowing Water Strike, and the Lion line lifted off it.
S16Killed one of the Unicorn's Five Pillars and brought the head back as a trophy, and took another prisoner.
S17Duelled Shinjo Kamo to a standstill and back up again. Her own husband was nearly gutted in the confusion, and the account says the hand was hers; the blame that followed settled on one person.
S18Her account of the Snow Plain boasts of her own valour, plays down the supply failures, and omits Ikoma Hideri. Her writing changes markedly after that battle.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
