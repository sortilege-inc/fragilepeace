Traitor’s Grove

Traitor’s Grove is associated with punishment and betrayal. It serves as a site for executions or public displays of retribution against those deemed traitors. The location is referenced in a warning issued by a Soshi Aisha, who threatened that any double-cross would result in execution at Traitor’s Grove.

The grove symbolizes fear and control within the social and political structures of the setting, marking the stakes of loyalty and treachery. The specific physical features or climate of Traitor’s Grove are not detailed.
