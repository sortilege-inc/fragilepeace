Confrontation with the Efreet

Session 34·2025-12-30

Setsuna offered herself as the vessel. Asahina Nao put a box in front of her instead, and then spent the rest of the night trying to get her to let go of it.

They went back over the fight while it was still on them. Shiba Midori and Bayushi Monban had held the warding circle, which protected the people inside it and hurt anyone who crossed it. Kakita Kazumi had gone down. And it was Shiba Midori who talked the Efreet into standing down — she argued against enslaving it, out loud, while it burned in front of her, and it listened.

Then the question of where to put it. Doji Setsuna offered herself. Asahina Nao put a containment box in front of her instead, and she and Shiba Midori worked the sealing with Doji Setsuna while the Efreet let it happen.

Bayushi Monban went at her the moment it was done — you will not allow me to be what I am for — and she dismissed him, which he cannot accept, because he is bound by Daimyo Shosuro's edict to keep her alive whether she wants it or not.

He carried Kakita Kazumi still smouldering to a pool and went in with him to put the fire out.

Governor Miya Tetsuya found them all somewhere to sleep while the city was put back in order. Asahina Nao warned Doji Setsuna plainly — you must get rid of it — and Doji Setsuna took the box to her own quarters anyway. Shiba Midori tried to talk her out of it and got nowhere. She left orders that she was not to be disturbed.

An hour later Bayushi Monban was at her door with his Emerald Magistrate's badge out, refusing to be turned away, Shiba Midori beside him. And inside, Doji Setsuna came apart — she is frightened of losing them, and said so. Bayushi Monban asked for the box gently, on the grounds that keeping her safe is the thing he is for. She gave it to him. He put it inside his kimono.

Kakita Kazumi went up onto the hospital roof, peeled back the tatami, and watched through the window in case the box was ever left alone.

Then the box started working on Bayushi Monban. Skin contact made him irritable and gave him an appetite for fire, and he announced he was taking it out of the city to let the Efreet go and earn its goodwill. Shiba Midori said a shugenja should be the one holding it and offered to hang it in her quarters, away from skin, until they had all slept. He would not wait — every hour it sits here is an hour someone can steal it.

They worked out the ground. Open country has no thresholds, which makes warding a release almost impossible. So: the River Near The Palace. Bayushi Monban took an unmanned boat and started rowing upstream.

Setsuna

She offered to be the vessel. That is the fact to sit with. A woman who has spent this campaign carrying her ancestor's memories inside her head volunteered to carry a fire spirit inside her body, in a garden, an hour after watching it put Kakita Kazumi on the ground. Asahina Nao stopped her with a box. Then she carried the box to her room against explicit warning and locked the door.

Bayushi Monban was right and he was right the first time, in the garden, when she was angry at him for saying it in public. The correction to make is not that he was wrong to worry.

And she let go when he asked gently. She wept, said aloud that she is afraid of losing them, and handed it over. That is the first time in thirty-four sessions the record shows her giving something up because someone she trusts asked properly rather than because she was outmanoeuvred. Note what worked, and note that she was alone in a locked room before it did.

The box is not safe with anyone. Skin contact made Bayushi Monban irritable and hungry for fire within the hour, and he immediately began arguing for taking it out of the city alone to release it and earn the Efreet's favour. That is the artifact talking as much as the man. Shiba Midori's proposal — a shugenja holds it, off the skin, until everyone has slept — is the correct one, and it lost to urgency at two in the morning, which is when every bad decision in this campaign has been made.

She should be at that river. Bayushi Monban is rowing upstream, alone, in a stolen boat, carrying a spirit whose goodwill he wants, while marked by the Lady of Decay and irritable from contact. Whatever is decided about releasing the Efreet, it should not be decided by the person the box has been touching.

One thing in the party's favour: Kakita Kazumi is on a roof with the tatami peeled back, watching the box. He does not trust it either.
