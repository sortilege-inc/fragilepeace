Dramatis Persona

Miya Misato

Imperial

What is known

S35Governor Miya Tetsuya's niece. Sixteen, with no retinue, no experience and no actual authority. She carries the Emperor's written order; her instructions from the Imperial Advisor were given to her orally, by one of the Advisor's servants, which is the whole of the suspicion against them. She does not know where either army is, and the magistrates have already told her to her face that she is a cat's paw.
S36Her room was searched exhaustively and fast, in the dark, by people who had all the time they needed to kill her and did not. They were after one specific thing and did not find it: the message under the Imperial Seal, which she carries on her person and which she indicated in front of a room full of people when asked. The window she had latched against the chill was standing open.
S39Moved secretly into the castle's fortified core by her uncle. Still being hunted. Setsuna spent the night teaching her rhetoric.
S41Still in the tower with Setsuna, working over documents at all hours. Setsuna taught her how government moves and how to fight with her hands and whatever is to hand. She asked her uncle for a morning departure and got it.
S42Held that the abandoned castle was a trap and would not be talked out of it. Dressed to meet the Emperor and walked to the gate frightened. Took Setsuna's argument that the approach is to affirm the Lion victory rather than to sue for peace.
S43Found her voice. Talked Gaheris out of the demand that the Lion apologise to villagers, then delivered the Emperor's command that the Lion would accompany her — the one intervention the entire night was built to make possible. Sealed the treaty on the Emperor's behalf.
S45The target. Taken at knifepoint in her own tent by men who wanted her alive and out, not dead. Setsuna got the knife off her throat and she kneed the man herself. She is dying. Earlier that evening she was asking the party for unmarried relatives, looking for a substitute bride.
S46Alive, and blind. Both eyes bandaged over, the prognosis blindness, and only time will say if either can be saved. The stroke was aimed at her neck. Multiple major wounds, severe damage in water and earth, and half again the fatigue she can carry. She was two rounds from dead when Midori got the dying stopped.
S47Dead. Not by anyone's hand — she lost too much blood, in her sleep, hours after the surgery. Found by Kitsu Ayoko in the small hours.
S54Could not have been dispatched. Word of the war reached Otosan Uchi less than a week ago, by the Unicorn ambassador, months after she left. The official account will be that she acted on her own authority and not in a right mind. The Herald suspects forgery, or an order sent without the Emperor's knowledge.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
