Cherry Tree

The Cherry Tree is a notable location, recognized for its aesthetic beauty and fragrant blossoms. It serves as a meeting point for clandestine encounters, often holding personal significance for those involved. The tree symbolizes delicate interactions and the complexities of relationships among the samurai class, particularly within the context of courtly affairs.

In one notable instance, Bayushi Monban approached the Cherry Tree for a rendezvous, expecting to meet an admirer. Instead, he encountered a catamite who mistakenly identified him as his brother, leading to an unexpected misunderstanding. The catamite fled upon realizing the mistake, leaving behind the lingering scent of cherry blossoms in the garden. This moment under the Cherry Tree reflects the blend of personal conflict and societal expectations that influence the lives of the characters involved.
