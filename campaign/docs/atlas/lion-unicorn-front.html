Lion–Unicorn front

The Lion–Unicorn front is a contested area of strategic significance in the war between the Lion and Unicorn clans. The region has experienced recent escalations, including multiple shinobi attacks, creating a volatile environment. Military movements are evident, with a contingent of Lion Clan expected to pass through en route to their main camp. The front is a focal point for diplomatic efforts, marked by the presence of Miya Misato, the governor's niece, who is on an imperial mission to broker peace between the factions. She is supported by figures such as Ikoma Tadayoshi, who has proposed to escort her to enhance her safety and facilitate negotiations. The area is also under scrutiny from the Emerald Magistrates, responsible for maintaining order amid the ongoing conflict.
