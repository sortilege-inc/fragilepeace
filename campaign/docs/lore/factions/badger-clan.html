Faction

Badger Clan

The Badger Clan is a minor clan known for its close ties to the Lion Clan, primarily serving as a source of iron and weaponry. Members are skilled artisans, particularly in smithing. Ichiro Kan is a notable Badger Clan ironsmith who trades iron and forges Lion Clan-issue arms at Loyalty Castle.

The clan's operations focus on crafting quality materials, including weapons, essential for the military strength of the Lion Clan. The Badger Clan supports the Lion Clan's military endeavors and plays a crucial role in supplying arms and armor. Their involvement in trade and military logistics underscores their significance as a supporting faction in the ongoing Lion Clan-Unicorn Clan conflict.
