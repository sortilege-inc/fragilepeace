One of the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]'s [[Five Pillars]], killed on the third day of the
[[Snow Plain]] by [[Akodo Toronoko]] in single combat. He dismounted to fight her because
she was on foot, matched her technique for technique through obscuring snow, and offered
her his hand and a blessing as he died of the blow. She took his head, and his army took
the panic of losing him.

The name is as the memory carries it. No account of the battle Setsuna has read names the
man [[Akodo Toronoko]] killed, or records that a Pillar died in a duel at all.
