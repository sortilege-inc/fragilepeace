Library Of The Lion

The Library Of The Lion is a significant repository of knowledge located within the Ikoma Hall Of Scribes. The hall is filled with scribes engaged in copying scrolls and attending lectures, establishing it as a center of learning and record-keeping. The atmosphere is characterized by the low murmur of conversation and the sounds of quills on parchment.

The Library is associated with political intrigues, serving as a backdrop for matters of honor, legal disputes, and historical documentation. Bayushi Monban has been suspected of improper use of the Library's facilities, highlighting its role in ongoing investigations. A Soshi Aisha, a Scorpion Clan woman, refers to Monban as potentially a petty burglar within the Library, emphasizing the site's importance as a repository of valuable records. She suggests that Monban must justify the provenance of certain documents he is expected to present. This exchange underscores the Library's function as a focal point for political maneuvering and the protection of secrets, reinforcing its critical role in the complex relationships between the clans.
