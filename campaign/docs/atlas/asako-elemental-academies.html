Asako Elemental Academies

Phoenix

The Asako Elemental Academies are located in the Phoenix Lands, characterized by a remote and serene environment conducive to the study of elemental magic and philosophy. Associated with the Phoenix Clan, known for their mastery of the elements and spiritual teachings, the academies serve as a training ground for aspiring shugenja and scholars, offering rigorous instruction under the guidance of Elemental Masters. The geographical setting features mountainous terrain and a tranquil atmosphere, ideal for meditation and learning.

Traveling by river can facilitate the journey to the academies from the Unicorn Lands, with a potential stop at Shiba Castle before continuing onward. The academies are a destination for further training and fulfilling obligations tied to roles as Emerald Magistrates.
