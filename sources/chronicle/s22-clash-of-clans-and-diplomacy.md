## Narrative

!lede The [[Ancestral Shrine]] was cleaned out, sanctified and blessed and given over to the arbitration for as long as it takes, which [[Kitsuki Kaage]] does not intend to be long. He arrived expecting one Emerald Magistrate — [[Doji Setsuna]], whom he had sent to research the matter — and found four. He had no idea the others were in the castle.

The two advocates are badly matched. [[Ide Tsubame]] is a Unicorn courtier arguing her first case of this kind. [[Shosuro Aishi]] has already prevailed in three or four claims of exactly this shape for her clan, dresses to the tens, and keeps one maimed arm folded away inside her clothes where nobody can see it. [[Kitsuki Kaage]] himself takes bushidō seriously and holds honour at about the rank [[Doji Setsuna]] does — and he is soft-hearted, which is the sort of thing that can be used against a man.

Nobody can say why the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] want the mines at all. The workings have fallen into disrepair. The [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] position is not about ore. *Our territory is not to be subdivided. It is ours.*

[[Ide Tsubame]] put the Treaty of [[White Shores Lake]] in first, and made the strongest version of it. The Unicorn won, the lands are theirs, and every party in the room's own ancestor conceded it — the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] because they signed, the Emperor because the Miya herald signed, and the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] because a Scorpion signed. As a gesture of goodwill the Unicorn granted the Lion the rights to the produce of ground that had been Lion before the Emperor gave it away, for a term of years. The term ended. The land is theirs entire. Then she produced letters about General [[Shinjo Kamo]], written by his niece Shinjo Kamuko — her own ancestor's correspondence. Her case against the Scorpion is that they showed no interest whatever for three hundred years and have suddenly discovered one.

[[Shosuro Aishi]] closed her parasol, leaned on it, and produced a letter from a Scorpion ancestor describing a private agreement that hands the mines to her clan.

She also came at [[Doji Setsuna]] sideways before any of it. *I know that Lady Midori of the Phoenix is more than likely a shugenja. You would not be happening to try to court spiritual help?* — because it was the Kitsuki themselves who barred spiritual witnesses from being relied upon in legal proceedings. [[Doji Setsuna]] said she was not, that if [[Kitsuki Kaage]] wanted it summoned [[Shiba Midori]] could provide it, and that it should not be necessary to a decision. She had already told [[Ide Tsubame]] the substance of her recommendation to her face: that the Emperor take the mines, and neither Scorpion nor Unicorn nor Lion hold them. Which told the Unicorn advocate, at the outset, that the magistrate researching the case was working against her.

Then [[Kitsuki Kaage]] summoned the magistrates to chambers, and told [[Doji Setsuna]] she was not the one being summoned. *But I want you to witness what is about to transpire.*

He had three versions of [[Ikoma Akuyaku]]'s journals in front of him. One a transcription by another scholar, claiming outrageous things Akuyaku supposedly did at the battle. One tame, admitting he knew a conspiracy was under way and did nothing. One with no mention of it at all.

*Clearly, of these three, two at least are forgeries. Which means you either knew they were forgeries and presented them as lies to my court — or, possibly worse, that you did not detect that they were forgeries, which would speak to a lack of competence in individuals I trained, and I expect better of all of you. I am saving you the embarrassment of calling this matter out. Would any of you like to confess to a document being known to you to be a lie? Or would you like me to submit them to a third party for examination?*

[[Shiba Midori]] took the other road. *Kaage-sensei, it will shock you that the truth is even stranger than that.* And she told him: the four of them had a mystical experience in common, had gone back and lived the Battle of the [[Snow Plain]] through their own ancestors. [[Doji Setsuna]] nodded assent when he looked at her, and put the limit on it herself — there are discrepancies between their memories they cannot reconcile, but the core of it, and particularly the [[Emperor Hantei Hantei Hantei Hantei|Emperor]]'s intervention stopping the fighting, is coherent.

*But none of that is allowable as evidence.*

[[Bayushi Monban]] argued his own in, and asked that [[Lady Kitsu]] be given the chance to help him call two witnesses. He had brought documents out of the Lion's own archive and shown writings only a Scorpion would have found. *A Scorpion*, said [[Kitsuki Kaage]], *or somebody I trained how to detect invisible ink.*

[[Kakita Kazumi]] staked five measures of honour on his document being found the better of the two when the examination is run, which guarantees an argument over evidence later. [[Shiba Midori]] withdrew hers. *I wanted to make sure these concerns were addressed. I did not feel that they were.*

Then a servant slid the door open. [[Kitsu Ayoko]] had brought the documents [[Kitsuki Kaage]] asked for, and she had other evidence of her own she wished to present. He looked at the four of them and asked whether they had known anything about this. Her ancestor is [[Kitsu Yue]] — the shugenja who called the dead onto that field, and whose work [[Shiba Midori]]'s own ancestor was credited with.

*I think there's been quite enough delays arguing over evidence. I will not accept any new evidence without substantial reason as to why I should. But I will hear out this Kitsu Ayoko.*

[[Doji Setsuna]] refilled his teacup, and then put her case on a footing nobody had expected. Not a plea that both clans relinquish. An Imperial edict on dormant strategic resources: where a claim is contested and the ground has gone unused for more than a century, the [[Emperor Hantei Hantei Hantei Hantei|Emperor]] may take it, and no special circumstance is required. She found the law, cited it, and applied it. The mines have been inactive far beyond a century — and by the eyewitness testimony of her own ancestor [[Matsu Morozane]], they were not even in use at the time of the battle.

The party's testimony survives the spiritual exclusion, because it is not spiritual. Four Emerald Magistrates attesting to what they themselves experienced, each able to attest to the others, under the oath they swore to the Emperor. *In that case that would not fall under the exclusion of spiritual entities. You are staking your own honour on the accounts.*

[[Bayushi Monban]] argued for the Scorpion, and argued it well. If the Emperor wanted these mines he would have taken them at any point in three centuries. If the Emperor did not think the Scorpion claim was good, he would have taken them. He knows the claim is being pressed; anyone who thinks otherwise is a fool. And — *we were there.*

[[Kitsuki Kaage]] listed what he wanted authenticated, accepted a Lion recommendation for an expert to do it, and adjourned. Cross-examinations next, and the presentation of documents.

## Learned

Ruled the party's shared memory inadmissible but their sworn first-person testimony admissible, since it stakes their own honour rather than a spirit's word. Soft-hearted, which can be used against him.

## Setsuna

**Her case is no longer a plea, it is a statute.** She stopped asking two clans to relinquish and found the Imperial edict on dormant strategic resources instead: contested claim, unused above a century, the Emperor may take it, no special circumstance needed. That is a ruling [[Kitsuki Kaage]] can make without deciding who was right three hundred years ago, which is exactly what he needs, because deciding that is the one thing the evidence in this room cannot support.

And her own ancestor is a witness to the century. [[Matsu Morozane]] crossed that hill and the workings were already abandoned. She can say so under oath, and it is not a spirit speaking — it is a magistrate staking her own honour, which [[Kitsuki Kaage]] has now ruled is a different thing. [[Shosuro Aishi]] went looking for the spiritual exclusion before the session began and found nothing to hang it on.

The forgery is dead and it died badly. Three mutually contradictory journals in front of the one school in the Empire built to detect exactly that. He did not need an hour. What should frighten her is not that he caught it but how he handled it: he put it to them in chambers, called it a lack of competence in people he trained, and said out loud that he was *saving them the embarrassment*. Nobody has been named. That mercy is the whole of what stands between this party and being defendants, and it is his to withdraw.

[[Shiba Midori]] is the reason it has not been withdrawn. Offered the choice between confessing to a forgery and letting the documents go out for examination, she told him the truth — a thing she knew would not be believed and could not be tested — and then withdrew her own evidence rather than press it. [[Kakita Kazumi]] staked five honour on his. The difference between those two responses is going to matter.

Two things to carry. [[Kitsuki Kaage]] now knows [[Bayushi Monban]] can read invisible ink because he taught him, which means every clever thing her yojimbo does in this room is legible to the man judging it. And [[Kitsu Ayoko]] has walked in with evidence nobody vetted, on behalf of an ancestor whose work was credited to somebody else — which is either the best thing that has happened to this case or the end of it, and [[Doji Setsuna]] does not know which.

She still cannot say why the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] want a set of dead mines. Until she can, every argument she makes is aimed at the wrong target.
