Iuchi Minoru

Unicorn

What is known

S28A Unicorn shugenja, older and severe, who holds that the spirits bound into her workings are allies and friends rather than servants. As a child she dropped her grandmother's jade teacup and saw something rise out of it that asked why she had been so careless with its home — which is where her whole position comes from, and the story holds up to testing. She has all but admitted the gift without stating it, and a Unicorn-trained shugenja knows how these talismans are made. Her warning: a sacred relic is the home and vessel of a being of great power, and such a thing is extremely dangerous. She has a streak of grandeur and does not care to be spoken down to.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
