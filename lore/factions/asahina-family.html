Asahina Family

Crane

The Asahina Family is a noble clan within the Crane, known for their spiritual and mystical inclinations. They are affiliated with the Crane Clan and are committed to the teachings of Shinsei and the Tao.

Asahina Mamoru is a notable member of this family, and his sister is described as a grieving pilgrim who seeks solace and guidance at various shrines after her brother's death. The family is connected to a noble lady who is on a quest across Rokugan for spiritual enlightenment following personal tragedy.

At Loyalty Castle, an Asahina Noble Lady is mentioned among the shrine pilgrims, highlighting the family's active engagement in spiritual practices. The Asahina Family has connections to the Lion Clan, as they are present during crucial events at Loyalty Castle, which serves as a staging ground for the ongoing Lion Clan-Unicorn Clan conflict. Their reputation is intertwined with the broader political and social dynamics of the region, reflecting their dedication to their faith and their navigation of challenges posed by war and personal loss.
