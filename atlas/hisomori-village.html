Hisomori Village

A border settlement, and one of the territories the Unicorn Clan agreed to cede to the Lion Clan under the broken betrothal. The cession would have settled claims the two clans have argued over for centuries, which is why the Lion counted the marriage worth what it cost and why they will not let go of the land now the marriage is gone.
