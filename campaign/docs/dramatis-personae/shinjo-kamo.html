What is known

S12The Unicorn general. Martial skill of five, possibly better than Matsu Sakura, and an inspiring enough commander that his troops had not panicked as they should have. Stayed mounted through the parley, refused both the demand and the peace, and promised to collect Morozane's head.
S16Survived an assassination attempt his enemy's general had not ordered and will have to apologise for.
S17The Unicorn general. Committed his last reserve to one all-out push, fought Akodo Toronoko through it, and was thrown off his own horse and stripped of his standard. He came off the Snow Plain alive twice over, since Matsu Sakura had already refused to have him assassinated.
S23The secret treaty is written in his hand and sealed with his chop — and his descendant says it is not his writing. His letters were held by the Lion military library, and what it held is now being checked.
