Earthen Court

Spirit

The court of the earth spirits beneath the mountains west of the Empire, ruled by The Earthen Queen. Its guards are man-shaped boulders that come out of the rock face and move through solid stone as though it were water; being carried through it takes several minutes and is not survivable by anyone the Court does not intend to arrive.

Everything in those mountains is its realm, whether or not a human knows it. A cave offered to somebody else, by somebody who never asked, is still theirs. You should have been aware, human. Everything is someone's realm.

The Archivist export was taken before this entry existed. What the page holds is drawn from the sessions themselves.
