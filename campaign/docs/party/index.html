The ancestral company

Sessions 10–17 are not the present. They run three centuries back, on the Snow Plain, and the table played its own forebears through them — Setsuna living the memories of Matsu Morozane, her Lion ancestor. What she took from that arc she remembers rather than witnessed, and the site marks it so. It is no longer only memory: the present war has come back to that same ground, and settled it the same way — Lion against Unicorn, one commander killing the other in single combat, a field of Lion dead afterwards. Setsuna has now been on the Snow Plain twice, and is the only person in the column who knows it settled nothing the first time.
