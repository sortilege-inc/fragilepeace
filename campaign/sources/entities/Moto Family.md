A family of the [[Unicorn Clan]], divided into branches distinct enough that a bard can
insult one of them specifically and be understood. [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] did exactly that
across a tea ceremony, aiming at [[Moto Gaheris]]'s yojimbo, and the Unicorn side of the
ground was the agitated one by the end of it. A clan whose internal seams a stranger can
find by ear is a clan whose politics are audible from outside.
