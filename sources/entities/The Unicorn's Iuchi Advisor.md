The record has not given her a name. A middle-aged [[Iuchi Family|Iuchi]] woman in a
shugenja's robes, hung about with talismans, seated on the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]
delegation — and [[Akodo Kage]]'s opposite number in every sense. She performed the
morning's blessing at the negotiation herself rather than let [[Shiba Midori]] do it,
[[Shiba Midori]] being a party to the case.

She is the person who told [[Doji Setsuna]] what the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] actually
need and in what order, and who warned her that a lion in a trap is more dangerous than a
lion loose. Worth a name when the record gives her one.
