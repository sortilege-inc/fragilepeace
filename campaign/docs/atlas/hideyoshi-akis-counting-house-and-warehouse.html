Hideyoshi Aki’s Counting House And Warehouse

Hideyoshi Aki’s Counting House And Warehouse is situated in the Unicorn District, a bustling business area. The structure serves as both a counting house and a warehouse, with numerous workers actively moving goods and runners frequently entering and exiting. The atmosphere reflects a vibrant merchant quarter where trade is ongoing.

Kakita Kazumi, during a visit, presented his Emerald Magistrate badge and sought information about suppliers who could assist in locating a large group of Unicorns, highlighting the significance of his inquiry for the safety of the empire. A yoriki at the counting house reported that recent shipments had been directed northeast over the past few days, having shifted from the south earlier, indicating potential army movements in that direction.
