Clash of Clans and Diplomacy

Session 22·2025-09-23

Kaage laid three versions of the same journal side by side and observed that at least two of them were forgeries. He is a Kitsuki. Detecting exactly this is what the school is for.

They sanctified the Ancestral Shrine and held the arbitration in it, which tells you how the Lion intended the proceedings to be understood. Kitsuki Kaage sat as Senior Emerald Magistrate. Doji Setsuna called Shiba Midori and Kakita Kazumi to sit beside her as magistrates. Shiba Midori wore a newly accented kimono and served tea to the room; Kakita Kazumi came plain and ready.

The claims went in. Ide Subane bowed, underdressed among all that silk, and presented the Treaty of White Shores Lake out of the Unicorn archives, which puts the Snow Plain in Unicorn hands. Shosuro Aishi closed her parasol, leaned on it, and produced a letter from a Scorpion ancestor describing a private agreement that hands the mines to the Scorpion. She has a reputation for winning and an arm she keeps out of sight.

Bayushi Monban moved to enter Ikoma Akuyaku's journals and asked for Lady Kitsu to help him call two witnesses. He showed the hidden writing inside them — the concealed reports, the sabotage. Shiba Midori had to tell the room that a journal she needed was simply missing, and that she was reconstructing it from memory, which put the word forgery into the proceedings before anyone else said it.

Then Kitsuki Kaage put three versions of Ikoma Akuyaku's journal on the table next to each other, noted that they disagreed, and said what follows from that: at least two are false.

Shiba Midori took the harder road out. She told the arbitration what she had actually experienced of the Battle of the Snow Plain — the mystical encounter, the thing the party carries and cannot document — and asked to be allowed to swear to it. Kitsuki Kaage permitted sworn testimony, subject to cross-examination. And then, having won that, she withdrew her own account.

Bayushi Monban argued hard to keep his evidence in. Kitsuko Ayoko arrived with more. And Kitsuki Kaage took Bayushi Monban aside privately and took him apart for having sat on a secret treaty document instead of producing it — then allowed the document in anyway. The rebuke stayed in the room after the ruling did.

Doji Setsuna put her draft on the table: both clans relinquish their claims, and the mines pass to the Emperor. She also spent part of the day using her own authority to keep people off Kitsuki Kaage while he worked.

He listed what he needed authenticated, and adjourned. Cross-examinations next.

Setsuna

Her proposal is on the record. Both clans relinquish, the mines pass to the Emperor. Shiba Midori devised it and Doji Setsuna drafted it, and it is now a document in front of Kitsuki Kaage rather than an idea over tea. Everything after this should be aimed at making that the path of least resistance for him.

And the forgery is already dead. Three journals, mutually contradictory, in front of a Kitsuki — the one school in the Empire built to find exactly this. He did not need a session to see it; he laid them out and said it. Nobody has yet been named, and the party should understand that this is the only reason they are still sitting as magistrates rather than standing as defendants. The forged material has bought them nothing and cost them the room's confidence in every document they touch, including the real ones.

Which is the part to feel properly. The quartermasters' ledgers are genuine. Ikoma Akuyaku's concealed reports are genuine. Akodo Kayamayako's erasure from the genealogies is genuine and checkable. All of it now sits in a case where the arbiter has publicly counted two forgeries, and a poisoned well does not care which bucket you draw from.

Shiba Midori did the right thing and it cost her. She won the concession that sworn testimony could be heard, and then withdrew her own — which is what you do when you know your account is true and cannot be tested, and you would rather lose the point than put an untestable thing in front of a magistrate. That is the standard the rest of them are now measured against, including her.

Two to watch. Shosuro Aishi wins, and conceals a maimed arm, and produced a letter from an ancestor — the same evidentiary shape as everything else in this room. And Kitsuki Kaage now knows Bayushi Monban withholds documents. Whatever Doji Setsuna puts in front of him from here will be weighed with her yojimbo's rebuke attached to it.
