Champion of the [[Phoenix Clan]], newly raised to it at the time [[Doji Setsuna]] saw her
work. She was the visiting dignitary at the finest evening of diplomacy Setsuna has
witnessed — across a table from Setsuna's own lord and her [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]]
mentor, with [[Shiba Midori]] also in the room — where every party left satisfied and
certain they had played their best hand, and nothing of consequence was ever said outright.
Most of what [[Doji Setsuna]] knows about being understood without speaking, she learned
watching that table.
