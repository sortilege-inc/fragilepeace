Shinjo Altansarnai

Unicorn

What is known

S9One half of the betrothal, under Imperial seal, that set off the trouble with the Unicorn.
S44Champion of the Unicorn, and a woman Setsuna has actually met — she was a guest at her wedding. Was to give up the Championship, the name, her clan's border territory and her place in the Unicorn succession for this marriage, and refused at the last. A free spirit; her daughter more so, and offended on her mother's behalf. Her arrangement with her lover is as good as a marriage in all but name, kept unmarried so her own value stayed available to the clan. No clan-level match exists above what she was offered.
S45In Moto Gaheris's camp. As Unicorn Champion she may be able to nullify the betrothal on her own authority — which makes her the person who can end this. A back channel to her now exists, opened without Gaheris having to concede anything.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
