Golden Yurts of the Ide

The Golden Yurts of the Ide are mobile structures associated with the Ide family of the Unicorn Clan. These yurts are notable for their vibrant golden color and serve as temporary encampments for the Ide as they traverse the rolling plains of Unicorn Lands. The region features open terrain with variable road conditions that can affect travel. The yurts embody the nomadic lifestyle of the Unicorn, reflecting their cultural heritage and adaptability to the landscape.

The Golden Yurts of the Ide are positioned along routes in the Unicorn Lands. Their transient nature is significant for logistical considerations, particularly in navigating an area marked by ongoing hostilities.
