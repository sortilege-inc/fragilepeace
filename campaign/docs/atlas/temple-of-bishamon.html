Temple of Bishamon

The Temple of Bishamon is situated in the province beyond the Border Waystation where the Emerald Magistrates have arrived. It serves as a significant religious site dedicated to Bishamon, the greatest of the fortunes, and is known for granting luck and long life in battle to its visitors, which makes it particularly important for those seeking favor in martial endeavors.

A Kitsu Family samurai host recommends that Bayushi Monban visit the temple, noting that it is slightly out of their way from the Castle of the Swift Sword. This suggestion underscores the temple's esteemed reputation and the cultural practice of seeking blessings from Bishamon, particularly among samurai and those engaged in warfare. Monban intends to visit, recognizing the temple's significance during his travels.
