A new [[Kaeru Family|Kaeru]] scout in the [[City Of The Rich Frog]], under orders to observe
the magistrates and nothing else — not to engage them, not to report on them. He sounded the
gong at the smugglers' landing deliberately, to scare the quarry off rather than let the
magistrates walk into a stakeout weeks in the building. [[Kaeru Akiara]] had already told him
who they were. He counted three of them.
