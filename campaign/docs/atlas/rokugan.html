Rokugan

Rokugan is characterized by diverse landscapes, ranging from lush forests to rugged mountains, with a climate that varies across regions. This variation influences agricultural practices and the lifestyles of its inhabitants. The society is organized into clans, each with distinct customs, values, and responsibilities, including the Lion, Unicorn, and Scorpion clans, which play pivotal roles in political and military affairs. The culture emphasizes Bushido, guiding the samurai in their conduct and interactions. Significant historical events, such as the Snow Plain, have shaped ongoing conflicts and alliances among these clans.

The Snow Plain occurred 300 years ago. Kitsu Ayoko spoke of her ancestor Kitsu Yue, who felt guilt over the battle's outcome, believing it was achieved through unfair means, leading to the loss of historical records. The discussion included various perspectives on key figures and events related to the battle. Doji Setsuna provided an account of the lion's struggles, while Ide Tsubame defended the unicorn's honor. Shiba Midori described witnessing sabotage against the Lion, implicating Scorpion involvement. Bayushi Monban presented an argument supporting the unicorn, asserting that Scorpion aid was critical to their victory. The proceedings included discussions of historical documents and the potential summoning of ancestor spirits for further testimony. A séance manifested the spirits of Shosuro Aishi and Ikoma Akuyaku, who are poised to provide their accounts.

In addition to its varied landscapes, Rokugan is also marked by its rich cultural heritage and intricate governance, exemplified by the Governor’s Palace. This palace features a Central Garden, serving as a space for various activities, including rituals and confrontations. The climate is notably hot during late summer, yet inhabitants experience unnatural sensations, such as coldness, indicating supernatural influences. The palace is populated by nobles and shugenja, who engage in spiritual practices and rituals, reflecting the society's deep connection to the mystical.

Recently, Miya Tetsuya, the governor, sought assistance to save his friend Higuchi from a possessing spirit. A disturbance in the palace prompted immediate action, leading to the discovery of a disheveled woman in the kitchen, exhibiting bizarre behavior that raised concerns about her possession. Observers noted her unnatural actions, including frantically feeding logs into a furnace despite the heat. The situation escalated as they confronted the spirit within her, leading to attempts to expel and contain the entity, culminating in a ritual that successfully sealed the spirit into a new vessel. These events highlight the palace's role as a site of conflict between the spiritual and the mundane, showcasing the complexities of Rokugan's supernatural landscape.
