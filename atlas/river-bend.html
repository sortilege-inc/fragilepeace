River Bend

River Bend is characterized by icy terrain and proximity to a strategic camp. A river flows past the eastern side of the camp, where deceased scouts were reported, indicating its significance in recent conflicts. The climate is harsh, with bitter cold causing fatigue among soldiers, affecting morale and combat readiness. The landscape presents treacherous conditions due to recent avalanches that have blocked paths and hindered movement, complicating reconnaissance efforts.

The location has become a focal point for discussions about troop movements and potential threats. The deaths of returning scouts raised concerns about enemy spies or ambushes. Observations noted a lack of tracks near the river, suggesting the enemy may have used hidden routes to infiltrate the camp. The river is tied to broader military strategy, as council members debated whether to send forces to parley with the Unicorn Clan, who had reinforcements in the area. The terrain around River Bend, particularly the snow-covered Rice Paddies, is confining and obscuring, complicating maneuvers by the Lion Clan forces.
