Meeting on the Road

Session 1·2025-03-11

At Toshi Ranbo the treaty of nonaggression holds, on terms that favour the Lion. North of the Aelu River the Lion have just taken the opposing general prisoner.

The year is 1123. Doji Satsume, lately Emerald Champion, is dead. Natural causes is the finding entered on the record, and no account of the death has been produced to go with it. The office stands vacant, the Championship that fills it is being arranged, and every clan with a duellist is doing arithmetic. In the north the Lion Clan and the Unicorn Clan are one incident from open war. At Toshi Ranbo a treaty of nonaggression holds, on terms that favour the Lion. There is also talk of an epidemic, which nobody has confirmed and nobody has denied.

Into that, orders. Emerald Magistrate Kitsuki Kaage sent Doji Setsuna to the Castle of the Swift Sword to evaluate a dispute between the Scorpion and the Unicorn. The dispute turns on the Lion's own records, which means the Lion must be asked to produce the evidence by which their neighbours will be judged. A second letter arrived from her Scorpion mentor. It assigned her a yojimbo, Bayushi Monban, and it forbade him seppuku. A bodyguard who may not answer a failure with his life answers it some other way, and so does the person he guards.

She left the castle with her household: Ishika, her lady's maid; Otoya, her scribe; the porters Ryu and So; Jiro for the horses; and Kogarashi, the white courser her husband's family gave her. A stranger came out of the dark, asked for her by name, said the kami favour you — these are for your companions, and left packages bearing the crests of Kakita, Scorpion, Shiba and Togashi, and some bearing nothing. Then he was gone. Nobody has explained him since.

Elsewhere, at a shrine with Lion Clan marks worked small into it, several people woke up who had been dead. They remembered dying — cultists in the Phoenix Lands, black scrolls opened — and they woke beside a Togashi Nun who was there and did not explain herself either. A paved road ran away from the shrine, laid to a standard that means a clan built it, if not the Empire. Whether the kami had favoured them or merely declined to finish with them, the survivors did not settle on the walk out.

On the road to Loyalty Castle, Doji Setsuna recognised an old man with an untidy bun from a painting she had once seen: Kitsuki Wataru, travelling with other magistrates. Ryu and So would not go near Bayushi Monban and gave no reason, and were not made to. She handed over the Phoenix-sealed missive she had accepted in Otosan Uchi from a rider of the Miya who had been looking for him. It was from an Asako daimyō. Bayushi Monban read it and laughed: he had inherited a castle. Among the packages was a Togashi box addressed to Chousen, a name nobody at the roadside would claim out loud, though more than one of them looked at it as though it might be theirs.

Kitsuki Wataru had the news from the north. Above the Aelu River the Lion under General Akodo Sakawan had broken the Unicorn and taken prisoners, the opposing general among them; the lord Shinjo Harunobu serves got off the field alive. The Lion only drink after a victory, Wataru said. The castles ahead would be loud, then, before anyone reached them. Seven years now since the Crane embassy burned in Otosan Uchi, and seven years of peace between Lion and Crane built on top of the ashes. Nobody on the road called that peace anything but fragile.

Setsuna

Her charge is narrower than it looks. She is to evaluate a Scorpion–Unicorn dispute whose evidence sits in Lion hands, at a Lion castle, during a Lion war. Every document she needs will be produced by a party with an interest in what it shows.

The letter about Bayushi Monban sits alongside that. A yojimbo denied seppuku cannot discharge a failure in the ordinary way, so any failure of his stays open, and stays hers. Her mentor knew that when the letter was written.

She has not asked who the stranger with the packages was, and no one has told her.
