Eda Shōgi Set

A shōgi set of Eda family make, requisitioned from the abandoned castle's stores on an Emerald Magistrate's authority and given to Matsu Matsumaro as a gift, with Well of Desire attached to it. She accepted, and never learned what refusing would have cost her. Not in the Archivist export.
