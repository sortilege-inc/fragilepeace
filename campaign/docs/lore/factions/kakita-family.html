Kakita Family

Crane

Kakita is a prominent family within the Crane Clan, recognized for its strong connections to the political and martial aspects of the realm. The family crest has been highlighted in various interactions, notably in gifts delivered to Doji Setsuna by a mysterious stranger. Members of the Kakita family are dedicated to honor and the arts, often balancing diplomacy and warfare within the region.

In 1123, following the death of former Emerald Champion Doji Satsume, tensions heightened between the Lion Clan and the Unicorn Clan. Doji Setsuna, a member of the Crane, was assigned by Emerald Magistrate Kitsuki Kaage to evaluate a dispute involving the Scorpion and Unicorn clans, which relied on the Lion's Records. In preparation for her mission, she received a letter from her Scorpion mentor appointing Bayushi Monban as her yojimbo, with specific instructions regarding his role.

During her departure, a stranger approached Setsuna and left packages marked with various family crests, including that of Kakita, intended for her companions. As Setsuna traveled toward Loyalty Castle, she offered to deliver a Phoenix-sealed missive to Monban, which later revealed news of his inheritance of a castle. This coincided with reports of a Lion Clan victory over the Unicorn, adding complexity to the political dynamics in which Kakita operates. The ongoing tensions and intricate relationships among the clans continue to influence Kakita's ambitions and allegiances.
