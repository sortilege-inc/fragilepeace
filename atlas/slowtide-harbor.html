Slowtide Harbor

Slowtide Harbor is a coastal location in Crane lands, southeast of Otosan Uchi. It is a key maritime point for trade and travel, characterized by tranquil waters and scenic vistas. The harbor is known for its bustling merchant activities and connections to various clans, particularly the Scorpion. Boats frequently navigate the calm sea, and the climate is generally mild, making it a pleasant destination. The area is vibrant with commerce and social interactions, largely unaffected by ongoing regional conflicts.

Historically, Slowtide Harbor has been the site of significant events, including encounters with magical entities. Recently, local leaders expressed concern over a Mahosukai that escaped from the harbor. Kitsu Takeko, a Lion daimyo, reacted strongly to an image of this Mahosukai during a tea ceremony, highlighting the harbor's relevance to current political dynamics. The harbor facilitates communication and movement between factions, exemplified by a recent interaction involving a young Scorpion samurai in a cat mask seeking directions to the Teahouse With No Name. This underscores Slowtide Harbor's significance as a hub for information exchange and its role as a strategic location for intelligence and covert operations. The harbor's reputation for harboring mysterious and potentially dangerous individuals adds to its significance in ongoing conflicts and investigations in the region.
