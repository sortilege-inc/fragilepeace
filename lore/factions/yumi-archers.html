Yumi Archers

The Yumi Archers are a reserve unit within the Lion Clan army, equipped with yumi, traditional Japanese bows, allowing them to provide ranged support from a distance. Their primary role is to target enemies without exposing themselves to direct confrontation, enhancing the army's overall combat effectiveness.

During the Battle of the Snow Plain, the Lion Clan, commanded by General Matsu Sakura, aimed to assess the Unicorn Clan's capabilities through probing attacks. Kitsu Somalia, a Lion Clan shugenja, led an initial assault, while Matsu Morozane executed a Lightning Raid against the enemy. Although the Yumi Archers remained unengaged in these early phases, their position in reserve was crucial for the overall strategy, enabling them to provide cover and support as the battle progressed. Their involvement became pivotal in maintaining pressure on the enemy as opportunities arose from the frontline actions.
