Gaki-dō

The realm of the hungry dead. Nothing in the record says when Bayushi Monban was close enough to it to learn the sound, only that he was: he put a hand in the Drowned Merchant River at the City Of The Rich Frog and named the howling in it without having to think about it.

The Archivist export was taken before this entry existed. What the page holds is drawn from the sessions themselves.
