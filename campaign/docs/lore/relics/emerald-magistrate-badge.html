Emerald Magistrate Badge

The Emerald Magistrate Badge is a symbol of authority and duty, associated with the role of an Emerald Magistrate. It signifies the bearer’s position and responsibilities within the realm, embodying the trust placed in them to uphold justice and investigate matters of significance.

Bayushi Monban displayed his Emerald Magistrate Badge to a servant, asserting his urgent need to speak with Doji Setsuna regarding a matter of life and death. This action highlights the badge's importance in granting access and authority in critical situations. Monban used the badge to persuade the servant to allow him entry to Doji Setsuna’s quarters, emphasizing the urgency of the situation involving the Efreet’s box. The badge facilitated navigation through the complexities of authority and responsibility, particularly in light of the dangers posed by the entity contained within the box.

In a subsequent inquiry regarding a poisoning incident and other suspicious activities, the Emerald Magistrate Badge was again wielded by Bayushi Monban. During a confrontation, he brandished the badge, declaring the Crane's behavior an embarrassment to their clan and demanding their confinement under guard until further evidence could be assessed. This incident further illustrates the badge's role as a token of trust in investigations, reflecting the power vested in the Emerald Magistrates and their responsibility to maintain order and justice within the realm.
