## Narrative

!lede [[Kakita Kazumi]] was never taken. He was in an alley with his katana through a thief's foot, setting terms. The man would return [[Shiba Midori]]'s purse, and would repay not being killed for robbing a samurai by entering [[Kakita Kazumi]]'s service — *and goodness for you, she happens to be a fairly decent medic.* He pumped him for local knowledge first.

At [[The Docks]] after dawn he presented him. [[Shiba Midori]] arrived with a fresh shiner and no weapons at all — *you look like something the wharf washed up* — and the purse came back a koku light. A smack across the shoulders with a sheathed katana produced the explanation: he had not spent it, some must have spilled during the chase, it was dark. He had not even seen she carried a daishō. *I assumed someone foolish enough to be caught alone on the docks would be an easy mark, and it was easy to cut the purse string. Avoiding being chased down by this one was the hard part.*

He grovelled, which hurt, because his foot was bleeding a trail down the walkway. And [[Shiba Midori]] knelt and did water magic on him — *I will pull back his blood into his body* so that it would clot, which also settled his ki and closed the wound under it — and bandaged it. He bowed again, having watched a miracle, and thanked her for ending his pain. The whole assembled watch saw her do it.

[[Kaeru Haya]] was less impressed with everyone else. *You left this spirit speaker alone on the docks at night? Where is her yojimbo?* She does not have one; the man who was to have been hers was sent away. *You had best protect her better. A spirit speaker is a precious treasure. If we find those rough men who ill-treated you, be assured they will be dealt with most harshly.*

Then a Kaeru came running with a fisherman who had found something in his boat and knew better than to touch it — *I know the rules and so do I.* [[Shiba Midori]]'s swords, dumped and abandoned, a good distance from where her robe had torn. [[Bayushi Monban]] went down after them and came back up. *Big sister, are these yours?* They are: her own chop is on them, they were forged by [[Kakita Kazumi]]'s father, and she won them at the Topaz Championship. He did not hand them back. He carried them himself, standing close enough that she could reach out and draw if she needed to, because of the state she was in.

The business, then. [[Kaeru Haya]] wanted the [[Mantis Clan|Mantis]] captain on the record and could not write it herself — *I cannot write. None of my ronin know how. If I must have something written I go to the lord and use his scribe*, and the scribe is never free. So she gave testimony and [[Doji Setsuna]] took it, which also means [[Doji Setsuna]]'s chop carries it where Haya has none. Haya has never met [[Captain Kubota]]; she has only heard him at her lord's house, loudly refusing to hear the word of *a masterless samurai dog* against his own.

[[Doji Setsuna]] wrote it to do two jobs at once. Two-thirds of her effort went into the [[Kaeru Ronin|Kaeru]] — what they did, how properly, how agreeably they handled going through the right channels and then a sudden pair of Emerald Magistrates; that their conduct was *infallible*, and that they should be considered for inclusion as a clan, on the strength of how well they uphold the Emperor's law. The remaining third was a scathing account of the captain: his disrespect to a magistrate and to the governor, his dishonourable conduct, and his abandoning half his own crew. Two copies — one to the capital, one to the Emerald Champion.

[[Bayushi Monban]] wrote his own letter, separately, and it was worse. Obstruction of justice. Dishonourable conduct toward an Emerald Magistrate and an Imperial governor. Dereliction of duty, for leaving half his crew in a city far from [[Mantis Clan|Mantis]] lands with no lord — *you are supposed to be their lord, you are responsible for them, and you dumped them at somebody's house and left.* He implied the business at the Centipede palace with the [[Crane Clan|Crane]] and the artifacts of dubious origin without quite alleging smuggling, which is the case the investigation was meant to establish. It was factual, only mildly inflammatory, and almost subtle, and everyone found that more unsettling than the letter. *His name is on it and mine is not, and it will stay that way.*

They took lodgings on the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] side, sent word to the island that they would like to be on the governor's calendar, and slept. [[Bayushi Monban]] tried Cleansing Spirit twice and failed both times, and then slept in the corridor outside their chambers, [[Ryu]] and [[So]] being stranded on the wrong bank.

They woke late on their second afternoon in the city. [[Shiba Midori]] healed her own rib. [[Kakita Kazumi]] sent his new henchman out shopping with money, on the understanding that vanishing with it would mean never being found again — and among the purchases, digging trowels. *Just a little gardening. Certainly for no other purpose.*

The [[Dragonfly Clan]] keep a cluster of shrines along the riverbank, ringed with lavish gardens and a small contingent of bushi, and the whole place is built around water: artificial waterfalls, fountains, water features fed by diverted channels of the [[Drowned Merchant River]]. [[Tonbo Kuma]] was on a balcony over one of the gardens with tea and a go board, playing [[Tonbo Higuchi]], who excused himself. *It is not every day that four Emerald Magistrates come into the [[City Of The Rich Frog]] and want to see me, of all people.*

[[Doji Setsuna]] said plainly that this was not official business, and asked after a novelist who might have come here to research something. *I did make some interesting acquaintances at the teahouse with no name in the last week or so. And you are not the first person to ask me about them — a young [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] and a [[Lion Clan|Lion]] samurai were here yesterday, within a few hours of each other.*

She played it as a coordination failure among young and overly enthusiastic people, and then he gestured at the board. *Do any of you play?* — *I am no go master, but I understand the rudiments. Would you educate me?* She asked for the maximum handicap and won. He proposed a second game to see whether it was beginner's luck. She changed her approach and won again. *I am convinced you did not need as much handicap as you asked for. You play a good game. You are an excellent teacher.*

What he had was this. The person asking questions was *very interested in humans' interactions with spirits — they wanted to know how people who are not shugenja contact the spirit world. I thought that a very strange question, because the spirits speak only to whom they choose.* The name he gave was Shinjo Higuchi, which none of them had heard, and it matches the description they have been chasing. [[Doji Setsuna]]'s husband is a [[Shinjo Family|Shinjo]].

She reached for what she knew of the family and came up with nothing about this particular member — the [[Shinjo Family|Shinjo]] are large and widespread — but something else surfaced instead, unbidden: another Shinjo was taken prisoner at the same battle as her husband. A cousin of his, [[Shinjo Miharu]]. It came to her out of the postscript of the letter [[Kitsuki Kaage]] was reading in court before [[Shiba Midori]] pointed out it was not in evidence. Which means [[Shiba Midori]] may know more about it than she does.

[[Tonbo Kuma]] has one of [[Hana no Ame]]'s books on his shelf and let it be known, delicately, that he is an admirer. He does not connect the two, and nobody told him. He also believes the questioner is staying with the governor — *with my friend, the Miya governor, of course* — because [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]] is the one who referred them to him in the first place, though he does not actually know where they lodge. And they meant to speak to [[Asahina Nao]] as well.

[[Asahina Nao]] is a [[Crane Clan|Crane]] shugenja, a friend of [[Daidoji Shin]]'s, here visiting the Crane's resident cultural director. She favours fire and water — and she has a specialist's interest and expertise in [[Meishodo Talismans|meishōdō]], the art the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] keep to themselves. She was taught its principles, has travelled extensively in Unicorn lands, and has studied with Unicorn shugenja on a more even footing than almost anyone outside that clan. If anyone in this city should be consulted on the question, it is her.

While [[Doji Setsuna]] played go, [[Bayushi Monban]] went to find an elderly tattooed monk he thought he had seen, on the reasoning that the offensive kihō mostly come from the [[Dragon Clan|Dragon]]. *Have you been reading much of the Tao?* He took off his mask and showed the paint on his face, and the monk averted his eyes, because for a [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] that is nakedness. He admitted he was a new initiate who had not delved deeply, and that his duties kept getting in the way.

*The answers are in the way.* He demonstrated Fist of Air, Fist of Fire, and the Body Is An Anvil, badly, and the monk watched him struggle, corrected his posture with a staff, and told him the truth of it: *the techniques allow you to do what your body can already do. You must simply remind it.* Then a tap on the head — *the Tao must live here* — and a hand to his chest — *but it must be breathed here. You are imbalanced. Very imbalanced, and prone to dream. You will find it difficult to master the way. If you cannot learn the form, seek the formless.*

*Strange, a Scorpion being taught by a Dragon. But your words ring true. I am more impulsive and self-determined than most. I have recently had a second opportunity, and old habits are dying hard.* He undertook to get a copy of the Tao and read it morning and night.

*The Little Master's teachings will guide you. Only you can choose to walk it.* Then the old man bowed, turned, made one short sharp gesture, and Fist of Air lashed out and broke the nearby waterfall across [[Bayushi Monban]], and he smiled and walked away. He could have put him in the water at any point in the conversation.

What is underneath that, and what [[Bayushi Monban]] said out loud: he had an incurable disease and never expected to outlive his gempuku, and in fact died shortly after it. His memories of his own life are marred now by a hazy sense of an existence that did not quite happen. He had been certain of everything — he was going to marry a [[Crab Clan|Crab]] and spend his life in Crab lands — and instead he is a magistrate, and a yojimbo, and working for a man he knows hates him. He came back to the others quiet and thoughtful, which from him is itself alarming.

[[Kakita Kazumi]] spent the afternoon over a map of the city, connecting what they have.

Then the theatre. [[Daidoji Shin]]'s planned Riverfront Theater is a burned-out ruin being picked over, which is a piece of gossip the [[Crane Clan|Crane]] would trade almost anything for — and the Riverfront Theater Group is the company that staged the play about [[Kakita Kazumi]]. [[Doji Setsuna]] began composing a note to ask the governor about acquiring the property, partly so that plays about her colleague could be forbidden.

[[Shiba Midori]] could see fire spirits still dancing in the ruin. She communed with them and they were entirely uninhibited about it. Shugenja magic of some kind made this fire. And when she asked whether it had been a free spirit or a caged one: *a caged spirit was unleashed here — and it called us to join in the celebration of its liberation.*

Then the part that matters most, and it came from asking one more question. The released spirit is gone. But the fire spirits mention that there was a big one in that space too, when the little one was let out — which reads as a lesser bound spirit spent to burn a building, and a far more powerful one still held in reserve.

*I bring all the good news to the yard.*

They resolved to force an early audience on the strength of magical arson, and were spared the rudeness: on their way to the water a handful of [[Kaeru Ronin|Kaeru]] met them. *Governor Tetsuya requests a meeting with you, with urgency.*

The boat carried a well-dressed woman in blue robes with white hair. [[Asahina Nao]] knows the two [[Crane Clan|Crane]] from various functions and knows [[Shiba Midori]] by reputation as the most talented young shugenja to come out of this year's Topaz Championship. She came to the city to visit [[Daidoji Shin]] and has been waiting on the governor, who wanted a favour of her and would not specify beyond saying it required an expert on possession — *and now he is calling me over to deal with it urgently. I wonder whether you are summoned for the same purpose.*

[[Doji Setsuna]] heard the word possession and looked at [[Shiba Midori]], and did not let it show. They had both read the handling instructions. They had just finished looking at what a small spirit does when it gets out.

They compared notes on the fire on the way across. *Those spirits were quite uninhibited.* — *They are fire spirits, what do you expect?* — *Some of them. But every one of them has the potential to go from a warm, banked flame to a raging inferno. All the difference is whether you feed them, and how much.* — *And these were very well fed.*

The island at the river junction is crowned with the governor's manor, and the setting sun laid the city out pink and orange behind them. Inside, two men rose: [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]], and [[Daidoji Shin]], already seated. *I regret that the circumstances of our meeting must be less than formal, but you are Emerald Magistrates with a reputation for solving unusual circumstances, and I have a very unusual problem.*

[[Daidoji Shin]] waved his fan. *Is this to do with the theatre business?*

[[Doji Setsuna]] opened the door for [[Shiba Midori]] and let her walk through it. *Midori here has solved the mystery of the burning down of the place where you hoped to build that theatre. It appears to have been a spirit let loose upon it. Oh, what a shame. I am so sorry, Shin.*

*From what I was able to ascertain, a spirit bound in the chains of meishōdō was released — explosively so — and it summoned other ambient spirits to partake in the subsequent destruction.*

Distressing was the aim, rather than insulting.

The governor's problem is a guest under his roof. He did not care for the burning look in her eyes, nor for the way she consumed her breakfast and then threw the bowl into the fire, nor for who she has been asking after. He has already sent for the [[Dragonfly Clan]] representative, understanding them to be a capable shugenja; they have not arrived. When he learned there was an Emerald Magistrate shugenja in the city he set his men to find her, and [[Kaeru Haya]] knew where to look.

*I will trust this matter to you. I will clear the island of anyone you consider inessential.* Told that an evacuation is not discreet, he offered to invent excuses to send people away instead. *I would like this dealt with quietly and as non-destructively as possible. If you can get the spirit thing out of my friend without burning down my house, that would be ideal.*

[[Kakita Kazumi]] declined to pretend he could help. *I have no affinity for the supernatural. This is all on Midori and Monban. Best I can say is good luck.*

## Setsuna

**The author has a name, and it is her husband's name.** The person who has been asking a shrine keeper how someone who is not a shugenja reaches the spirit world is a [[Shinjo Family|Shinjo]]. [[Doji Setsuna]] married into that family. She could recall nothing about this particular member, and what surfaced instead of the answer was worse and more useful: another Shinjo was taken at the same battle as [[Shinjo Harunobu]] — his cousin [[Shinjo Miharu]] — and she knows it from a postscript [[Kitsuki Kaage]] was reading aloud before [[Shiba Midori]] stopped him, because it was not in evidence. Two things follow. Her husband's captivity and this investigation are not separate matters. And [[Shiba Midori]] has been carrying something about that letter for eight sessions.

**And the woman who bought a wild spirit out of the [[Burning Sands]] is living in the governor's house.** The buyer left the handling instructions behind. The governor has a guest with a burning look in her eyes who threw her breakfast bowl into the fire. He calls her his friend, and he does not know what he has. Nobody in that room has said the two things are the same thing yet, and somebody is going to have to.

**The urgent finding is the one nobody reacted to.** The fire spirits said a caged one was let out to burn the theatre — and that a *bigger* one was in the room when it happened. A lesser spirit was spent as arson; the vessel that held it is ruined; the larger one is still bound and still held by whoever did this. Everything the party has established about the trade says a released spirit needs a new vessel prepared in advance. Somebody is working up to something, and the theatre was the small demonstration.

That it was [[Daidoji Shin]]'s theatre is not a coincidence worth ignoring either. The [[Crane Clan|Crane]] agent hunting whoever roused the spirits that drowned part of the [[Crane Lands|Crane lands]] has had his own investment burned by a released spirit here. He is not merely chasing this trade. He has been answered by it — and he has, by every account, a whole list of enemies, which is exactly the confusion a careful arsonist would rely on.

**Three parties were hunting the same author and none of them are hunting a writer.** A [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]], a [[Lion Clan|Lion]], and the magistrates, all at [[Tonbo Kuma]]'s shrine within a day of each other. [[Ide Iwena]] is protecting a friend she believes is in danger. The [[Lion Clan|Lion]] want a story rewritten. And the men on the docks were working to a quota — *the boss needs another* — which is a third kind of hunting altogether. Only the magistrates now know she is a customer — and she may well be the one thing everyone actually needs, which is a person who knows what she bought.

**[[Asahina Nao]] is the most valuable person to arrive in this city.** A [[Crane Clan|Crane]] shugenja with a real expertise in [[Meishodo Talismans|meishōdō]], trained in its principles, who has studied with [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] shugenja on nearly even terms. Everything the party has failed to understand for four sessions, she can probably read. She is also [[Daidoji Shin]]'s friend and the governor's acquaintance, which means every question put to her travels. [[Doji Setsuna]] should decide now what she is willing to have known.

Two things done well and one to watch. The report was well built: she spent two thirds of it making the [[Kaeru Ronin|Kaeru]] look excellent, which costs her nothing and buys a great deal from people who have already proved reliable, and went after the captain with the remaining third. And she used the governor's own table to set [[Daidoji Shin]] up and let [[Shiba Midori]] deliver it — distressing rather than insulting, which is the better instrument.

The thing to watch is that she still does not know whether [[Captain Kubota]]'s ransom story was true, and she has now put her chop on a document calling him dishonourable, and [[Bayushi Monban]] has sent a harsher one under his own name. If the child is real, both letters are going to look very different in a month.

And [[Bayushi Monban]] told an old monk on a balcony what he has not told her: that he expected to die before his gempuku and did die shortly after it, and that his memories of his own life now include one that did not quite happen. He came back from that conversation quiet. She should not let that pass unremarked forever.
