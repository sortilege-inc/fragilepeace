The Capital

The Capital, known as the City of the Rich Frog, features a network of districts, including the Dragonfly Clan Shrine, recognized for its lavish gardens and shrines. The Dragonfly Clan maintains several shrines along the riverbank, surrounded by beautiful landscaping and guarded by bushi. The city is situated in a temperate environment, as suggested by its interactions with water. It serves as a significant center of political activity, with Governor Miya Tetsuya residing in a grand manor located on an island in the river. The area is a hub for the Emerald Magistrates, who oversee the conduct of samurai and other citizens, addressing unusual problems.

The Emerald Magistrates engage with various characters in the city, including Kaeru Ronin and Kaeru Haya, to discuss smuggling and local governance. They visit the Dragonfly Clan Shrine to meet Tonbo Kuma, the leader of the Dragonfly Clan, and learn about inquiries from other clans regarding a notable author. This indicates the city's bustling social dynamics. They also investigate a burned-down theater site, linking it to potential magical arson, and seek an urgent audience with Governor Miya Tetsuya to address the pressing issues.
