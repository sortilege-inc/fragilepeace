I. Identity & Standing

Asahina Jūjirō is a Crane shugenja-artisan of the Asahina Artificer School, held at Rank 2. They make sacred objects — and, at the height of the craft, objects with something awake inside them. They are non-binary; they/them throughout.

Their standing is peculiar and worth reading carefully, because the three numbers disagree with each other. Status 35 is an ordinary household artisan: no office, no lands, no command. Honour 60 is the honour of someone who has never been seriously tested. Glory 78 is the fame of a Clan Champion. That gap is not decoration — it is the whole political fact of the character. Jūjirō is known far beyond anything their rank can protect.

The glory has a single cause and a date. Master Artisan carries a glory award of +30, to a maximum of 80, and the title can only be granted by “someone of status 75 or higher after the character performs a great artistic feat” — a threshold no clan daimō reaches. Four months before the campaign began, at Otosan Uchi, the Imperial consort Shiba Ayame named them, and 48 became 78 in a season.

Physical Description

Beautiful, and sickly in the wrong light. They are wracked by coughs when the body is pushed too hard, and it is neither concealed nor concealable.

Manner of Presence

Courtesy is their paramount tenet, and it is professional before it is moral: every commission is somebody's patronage, and an artisan who cannot hold a room does not eat. Courage is their least significant, and the ordering is honest — nothing has yet asked it of them, and the body gives out before the nerve is tested.

They are known for working past the point where a piece is finished, and for giving away the result.

Current Obligation & Drive

Giri

Lord: Asahina Shigehiro of Shirogome, a distant relation, to whose house the family is attached. That duty stands but is in abeyance: when the Emperor's consort asks for your artisan, you lend them, and Shigehiro did.

Current giri: to outfit Shiba Ayame's new shrine to Benten — a careful curation of awakened objects, each shintai chosen for the place it will stand and the people who will stand before it. It is the family method applied at the scale of a building.

Ninjō

To convince the family to use their own time-honoured method to find them a match that truly fits, rather than one forced to fit.

The two are not merely in tension; they are the same instrument pointed in two directions. The giri is a shrine to Benten, Fortune of Arts and Romantic Love — “patron of artists, lovers, and those seeking beauty.” Every hour Jūjirō spends discharging their duty is an hour furnishing a house for the god of the thing their duty forbids them.

Asahina Artificer School, Rank 2. The school's final mastery, at Rank 6, is Stir the Slumbering Spirit — awaken an item's spirit so that it becomes a nemuranai until the end of the session.

Master Artisan (Courts of Stone p.130), invested at 12 of 60 XP. Title ability Inspired Creations: when crafting an item, spend three opportunities to make it a singular masterwork, which must be named, and which lets a character add one ring set to any result once per session.

III. The Family Method

The first Asahina to distinguish themselves was also called Jūjirō, and what they discovered was that people have elemental temperaments and can be read the way an object is read. The finding was put to two uses: pairing a person with an awakened object so that neither is wasted on the other, and pairing people with each other. The family kept the discovery, the method, and the name — which goes in each generation to the child judged likeliest to have inherited the eye for it.

Three things follow, and they are the spine of the character.

The blessing is a technique, not a gift

Benten's Blessing reads, in the rules, as a Fortune's favour arriving unearned. Under the family method it is the instrument working on the practitioner's own behalf — which is worse to carry, because it cannot be switched off and it never returns a comfortable answer.

The name was given before the talent was known

Their parents named them for the ancestor before anyone could know whether the aptitude had carried. It had. They have spent their life being the vindication of somebody else's guess.

The method is a rival tradition, in their own clan

Matchmaking is already a prestigious Crane institution: Musume Mura, near Kyuden Doji, holds “the Doji's most prestigious nakodo school,” where samurai parents make pilgrimages for advice, and where the Emperor's intended spends a night before the wedding. The Doji nakodo read lineage, standing and the compatibility of houses. The Asahina claim to read the person. When the two disagree, the Asahina lose.

IV. Interior Architecture

Under stress

They hole themselves up and work until the body gives out, and they are stubborn in their refusals. The workshop is the one room where the reading is theirs to make. It is also exactly how the fits are brought on, so their response to pressure removes them from the situation a day or two later. Strangers read that as delicacy.

What calms them

Giving the work away. A commission satisfied is a debt discharged and nothing more; a piece put into hands that could never have paid for it settles them completely. They have done it often enough that people they have never met will grant them a reasonable favour on the strength of it.

What they fear

Enclosed spaces — kilns, storehouses, shrine interiors, the inner rooms of the Forbidden City. Anywhere the air is finite. It compounds the illness, because the first sign of a fit is that breathing stops being automatic, and a closed room leaves nothing to do but wait for it.

The illness

It began under Asahina Riku, the old woman in Shirogome who taught them the school's arts. The talent and the damage arrived together, which is the fact the character never quite gets out from under.

How this should end

The illness will kill them young. They likely have only a few years left, and they intend to spend them gloriously.

VI. The Otosan Uchi Season

Everything that matters about this character happened in one stretch of about four months before the campaign began, and the two halves of it are the same trip.

The feat. Ayame commissioned them to rework a damaged family heirloom. What they made did not merely stun; it woke. She had it recorded as a great artistic feat, named them Master Artisan, and moved them to Otosan Uchi under her patronage to begin Benten's shrine.

The aptitude. It was the same season the family gift came in fully. Since then they have known, after a scene or two in a person's company, whether that person is open to them — reliably, and without having asked to know.

The affair. And it was the same season they met Doji Setsuna at Ayame's salon and the reading came back open.

Their fame and their worst secret date from the same weeks. That is a useful thing to be able to say about someone whose distinction is knowing who wants them.

What Jūjirō does not know

That Setsuna conceived; that she carried it through the Loyalty Castle mission; that she considered carrying to term and then elected termination; that the procedure severely wounded her Void ring and has never fully healed. The decision to conceal it was hers, taken partly to spare them knowledge that would mark them, and partly because they are betrothed elsewhere and it would not have been theirs alone to carry.

VII. The Betrothal

The house that invented the science of matching people matched Jūjirō to a woman they cannot love.

The method was consulted and did not object. Shizuka is Earth where Jūjirō has none, and would steady them. It was correct. That is the bitterness of it — not that the instrument failed, but that it worked, and being accurately matched to someone you cannot love is the worse outcome. It leaves the method's authority intact, which matters, because it is the same authority that told them the truth about Setsuna.

The mechanical text of Bitter Betrothal offers three reasons a spouse undermines you — malice, negligence, or an incompatible set of goals. This is the third, which is the only one that does not make Shizuka a villain. She knows there is no chemistry. She was raised in Jūjirō's own family's courtier school for the express purpose of this marriage, by a decision made over both their heads, and she has as little say in it as they do.

Since Ayame's patronage it no longer makes political sense either — a household artisan of status 35 with the glory of a Champion and the ear of the Emperor's consort is not the match that was negotiated for. Nonetheless promises were made, and a marriage can only be deferred so long.

IX. Advancement Plan

One structural fact governs everything below: Master Artisan cannot be completed for a very long time. Its two technique prerequisites are rank-gated far above Jūjirō's School Rank 2 — All Arts Are One is a Rank 3 shūji, and Formal Tea Ceremony is a Rank 5 ritual. With 48 of 60 XP still outstanding and a Rank-5 gate on the last piece, this is an end-game title, not a near-term goal. Plan around the school, not the title.

Priority 1 · Earth 1 → 2 (6 XP)

The single most valuable purchase available, and it is not close. Earth 1 is the floor. Raising it to 2 takes Endurance from 8 to 10 and Composure from 6 to 8 — which raises the strife maximum from 6 to 8, moving them from “compromised by two bad moments” to something survivable. It also blunts the Incurable Illness, which is an Earth adversity.

Out of curriculum, so full price at 3 × the new rank. Buy it first.

Priority 2 · Finish the Rank 2 curriculum

Rank 2 wants Scholar Skills, Aesthetics, Design, Performance, Rank 1–2 Water Invocations, Grasp of the Air Dragon and Tea Ceremony. Performance and Tea Ceremony are bought. In-curriculum skills cost 2 × the new rank, so the cheap advances are Design 1→2 (4 XP) and Aesthetics 3→4 (8 XP) — the latter being their signature skill and the one every commission runs through.

Reaching Rank 3 is what unlocks the title's first prerequisite, so this is also the only road to Master Artisan.

Priority 3 · All Arts Are One, at Rank 3

The first title prerequisite, and unavailable until School Rank 3. A Support action, TN 4 Martial skill, Games or Performance check on Void, to absorb a new activity without the usual TN increase for unfamiliarity.

Deliberately not recommended

  • More Fire or Air. Both are already 3 and both are the half of them that works. Spending there widens the gap that is already the problem.
  • Martial skills. All five are at 0 and should stay there. The wakizashi and yumi are the school's starting outfit, not a plan.
  • Pushing the title before Rank 3. XP put into Culture, Medicine and Meditation counts toward the title, but the gate is the school rank, not the ledger.

Noted, not costed

The school's Rank 6 mastery, Stir the Slumbering Spirit, is what Jūjirō actually wants — and even it only holds until the end of the session. The thing they are reaching for is not on the advancement table at all.

X. Trajectory

The shrine

Finishing it pays a giri, and giri completion pays 10 or more glory. Jūjirō is at 78 of a possible 100. Complete Benten's shrine and they land near 88 — glory rank 8 against status rank 3, with no office, no lands, and a lord who has lent them out. That gap is not a bug. It is the thing that will eventually get them hurt.

The exposure

Ayame's fondness is genuine, which makes it the least predictable risk in the arrangement: a woman who finds a secret charming is a woman who may one day say so warmly to the wrong listener. She sits in the Forbidden City and outranks everyone who could protect Jūjirō from the consequences.

The marriage

It can be deferred and it cannot be avoided, and the ninjō is not to escape it but to make the family use its own instrument honestly. That is a desire Jūjirō can pursue in a conversation, at range, in any session — which is what makes it playable rather than decorative.

The end

The lungs. Young, with the work unfinished, and sooner than anyone has arranged for.
