Dramatis Persona

Ikoma Akuyaku

Lion

What is known

S10Argued for delay and for an eastern approach through the village and up into the mountains, to find the unmapped paths into the Unicorn camp. Overruled on the delay, given the infiltration, to begin at sunset on the army's muster.
S11Of the Ikoma Shadow School. Went into the Unicorn camp unseen under cover of the whole Lion line moving, and stayed unseen.
S12Crossed the plain unseen and got most of the way. The rope failed on the rock climb; he spent his advantage on staying unseen rather than on not falling, broke his foot, and blacked out in snow cold enough to keep him from bleeding out. He shot the Western Watchtower on the way down knowing it would raise the alarm. Came back on a stretcher with a sketch map of the Unicorn camp and their scouting pattern.
S13Came back on a stretcher with his feet frostbitten and a map of the conditions. Reported the Unicorn undaunted, their cavalry late or lost in the storm, their strength about equal, and the cold lethal. Confined to camp, and left his tent on those feet anyway to watch the battle.
S16Went into the Unicorn camp on his own initiative to kill Shinjo Kamo, and came back wounded and rebuked. Ordered a massed volley into Gero's unit with no pretence of honourable tactics. Sat with Kitsu Yui afterwards until she was steady.
S19His journals carry a concealed account. He was embedding Scorpion reports inside the Lion Clan's own records. The Lion officer who ran the infiltration at the Snow Plain was working for the Scorpion.
S26Manifested alongside her. The scout who erased the histories, called to answer for it.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
