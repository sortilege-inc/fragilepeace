A commander under the [[Crab Clan]] Champion, [[Hida Chizuzu]]'s father, and the officer
[[Shinjō Harunobu]] trained under as a young man in the [[Crab Clan|Crab]] lands — a
connection nobody in the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] camp knew existed until [[Doji Setsuna]] read
his name off a letter. He taught that hesitation is death, that war is not a duel, that
tactics mean nothing without supply, and that command is a burden rather than a privilege.

He wrote to [[Bayushi Monban]] after receiving a warning not to send his daughter over the
[[The Wall|Wall]] — a warning that arrived days after he had already sent her. The letter
demands to know how he knew, what else he knows, why he did not say so sooner, and why he
was not there to stop it. It is a lord's language with a frightened father inside it.
