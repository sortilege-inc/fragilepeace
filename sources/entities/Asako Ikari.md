[[Ikoma Anakazu]]'s first wife, divorced to clear the way for a match that then collapsed.
In Rokugan a divorce is social death, and nobody has offered her or the
[[Phoenix Clan]] any recompense at all. Also recorded as Ikoma Akari, the name she
carried while married. Not in the Archivist export.
