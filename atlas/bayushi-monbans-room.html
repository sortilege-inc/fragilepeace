Bayushi Monban’s Room

Scorpion

Bayushi Monban’s Room is situated within a Geisha House, featuring traditional Japanese decor such as tatami mats and sliding shoji screens. The room reflects a recent night of revelry, with empty sake bottles and disarray evident throughout.

Monban awoke surrounded by three women and attempted a cleansing prayer to alleviate his hangover. He later received information from a serving girl about a young lord who had left in haste. Kakita Kazumi planned to search Monban's quarters during his absence, indicating the room's significance to others. As Ryu opened the door to fetch clothes for Monban, Kazumi noticed a box-like object inside, suggesting the presence of items of importance.
