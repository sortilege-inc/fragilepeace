Faction

Dragonfly Clan

The Dragonfly Clan is a minor clan located in the mountainous regions, serving as a gateway to the Dragon Clan lands. Their strategic position enables them to control access to crucial paths and resources. They maintain an alliance with the Dragon Clan, providing them with a measure of autonomy and protection from direct aggression by major clans such as the Lion Clan. The Dragonfly Clan is also a faction within the city, connected to nature and spiritual practices, operating from the Dragonfly Clan Shrine, which is characterized by shrines and lavish water gardens, all protected by a contingent of bushi.

The clan is led by Tonbo Kuma, who plays a pivotal role in interacting with visitors and addressing matters related to spiritual interactions. Under his leadership, the Dragonfly Clan has developed a keen interest in the relationship between humans and spirits, engaging in discussions on spiritual research and authorship. Notably, Tonbo Kuma provided information about novelist Shinjo Higoichi to the Emerald Magistrates, having been referred to him by Governor Miya Tetsuya. Both a young Unicorn and a Lion samurai had inquired about Higoichi, further emphasizing the clan's connections and influence.

Recently, the Dragonfly Clan's significance has been underscored by a conflict regarding ownership of the Old Diamond Mines, with the Scorpion Clan making claims against the Unicorn Clan. Kitsu Takeko, the daimyo of the Kitsu Family, has indicated that resolving this dispute is connected to ancient agreements from the Battle of Snow Plain. The Emerald Magistrates, including Doji Setsuna and Shiba Midori, have been involved in discussions about the implications and historical context of these claims. The Dragonfly Clan is considered pivotal in the political maneuvering among the clans amidst tensions with the Lion Clan, Unicorn Clan, and Scorpion Clan.

Their geographical positioning further allows the Dragonfly Clan to act as a buffer and control point between more aggressive factions. Their historical ties and the involvement of individuals like Kitsuko Ayoko, an adopted member of the Kitsu Family, may enhance their influence in ongoing clan dynamics. The clan's influence was recognized when Governor Miya Tetsuya mentioned seeking a Dragonfly representative for assistance with a possession issue involving his guest, highlighting the clan's role in mediating spiritual matters and supporting the Emerald Magistrates in their investigations.
