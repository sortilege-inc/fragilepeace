Negotiations and Hidden Truths

Session 19·2025-09-02

The Scorpion's proof that they were at the Snow Plain has been sitting inside the Lion's own archive for three hundred years, put there by a Lion officer who worked for them.

Three days. Doji Setsuna spent the first of them writing — to Doji Hotaru, to Doji Masateru, to Akodo Masami — about her husband's ransom, and reading what came back. The front is under Okoto Sakuon, who has a reputation for honour and for listening to reason, and Akodo Akihito is kin to him, and also connected to Bayushi Monban and Kakita Kazumi. That is a chain of obligation running from this castle to the man holding Shinjo Harunobu.

Then the party went into the archives in three directions.

Bayushi Monban took the military records at the Akodo War College and came out with the thing they had been looking for. The arrival and return rosters put the losses at roughly two-thirds. And the quartermasters' books record what the journals do not: illness through the army, and water and food supplies contaminated. Not one personal account mentions it. He also found a physic named Akodo Kayamayako in those rosters — named, and praised, for the lives they saved.

When he put the possibility of a cover-up to Akodo Atsushi, the instructor went at him. I don't think I've ever heard of a Scorpion keeping a diary — which is a way of refusing a question rather than answering it.

Shiba Midori went after the same medic from the other end, because Akodo Kayamayako is tied to the attempted murder of her own ancestor. She took Ikoma Ichigo into the Ikoma House of Scribes and they went through the genealogies of the Lion Clan and found nothing. I'm having great difficulty locating this person, Ichigo said, of a man his own clan's rosters praise. What they did find, tracing the dynastic lines, was how many of them run forward into the current leadership of the Lion — and into the party themselves.

Kakita Kazumi sat in the Kitsu Archives with Ikoma Akuyaku's journals spread around him and found the concealed account. Ikoma Akuyaku had been embedding Scorpion reports inside the Lion Clan's own records.

Put together, they arrived at three: a known Unicorn-aligned agent, Ikoma Akuyaku, and the medic. Three people, working the same battle from inside a Lion camp, and the Lion lost it.

Around that, the politics. Doji Setsuna gave Bayushi Monban a draft treaty framework she has been building — river stability, postwar provisions, the standing between Crane, Lion and Unicorn — and went looking for Scorpion support for it. She took tea with Shosuro Imako and set out, plainly, that her recommendation would favour the Scorpion, and asked to be credited for it if it was taken. She sang Ikoma Ichigo a poem that told the traditional story of the Snow Plain and left a second story showing through it — that the Lion had been made to treat by the Emperor rather than beaten into it.

And at dawn she asked Kitsu Takeko to move her husband's custody out of Okoto Sakuon's command. Takeko agreed, on the strength of Akodo Akihito's recent service. Then Doji Setsuna asked, on her way out, whether Akodo Masami was married.

The group also discussed forging Lion documents — they have, after all, been inside the battle, and could write what the record is missing. Bayushi Monban went to the sparring ground rather than keep arguing about it. Shiba Midori went to find a Kitsu shugenja who could call her ancestor up and ask him directly what the medic did.

Setsuna

The ledgers held. She said stores are counted and they were: the quartermasters' books record illness through the army and contaminated water and food, and not one personal journal admits it. The sabotage at the Snow Plain is a documented fact now rather than an ancestral memory, and it is documented in the Lion's own hand, in the one kind of book nobody thought to edit.

The medic was praised and then erased. Akodo Kayamayako is in the rosters, thanked for saving lives, and in no genealogy at all. Common soldiers remember them; samurai do not. That is not a person who was forgotten. That is a person taken out of the family records afterward, which requires a family to do it.

And Ikoma Akuyaku hid Scorpion reports inside the Lion archive. It is the largest thing the party has found and it cuts both ways at once. It proves the Scorpion were present and active at the Snow Plain, which is their whole claim, and it proves the presence was espionage against the clan whose archive she is standing in. A claim founded on that evidence is a claim founded on treason. Shosuro Aishi cannot lead with it. Neither can she, in front of a Lion daimyō who has been generous to her four times over.

The forgery is the one thing that would destroy her. They can write what the record is missing because they have been inside it. An Emerald Magistrate who manufactures evidence in a case she is weighing is finished, and so is every ruling she has ever made, and so is her husband's transfer. If the party goes that way she wants no part in it and no knowledge of it.

Two things to track. She has opened with Shosuro Imako — her recommendation to favour the Scorpion, conditional on their pledge of non-intervention in the Crane–Lion treaty, and credit if it is acted on. Conditional is the right shape: if the Scorpion never close on the condition, the offer lapses and she is free. What she is watching for is an attempt to hold the offer without paying for it.

And she has Shinjo Harunobu's custody moving, which is the first real thing she has won, and which she now owes Kitsu Takeko for.
