White Shores Lake

White Shores Lake is a significant body of water surrounded by lush greenery, serving as a natural boundary between various clan territories. The region has a temperate climate, suitable for both reflection and conflict resolution.

The Ancestral Shrine, located near the lake, is a sacred structure repurposed for important events, such as arbitration proceedings regarding land disputes. Notable figures including Kitsuki Kaage, the Senior Emerald Magistrates, and various clan representatives gathered there for these discussions. Ide Tsubame presented the Treaty of White Shores Lake, which was signed by key leaders and asserted Unicorn Clan ownership of the Snow Plain, while outlining temporary rights to agricultural produce for the Lion Clan.

The proceedings at the shrine involved careful scrutiny of documents and testimonies, with the lake's historical significance enhancing the gravity of the arbitration. Various documents, including letters and journals, were examined to determine rightful claims to the contested lands surrounding White Shores Lake.
