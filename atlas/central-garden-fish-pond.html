Central Garden Fish Pond

The Central Garden Fish Pond is situated within the Governor’s Palace, featuring serene waters and lush surroundings. The garden serves as a controlled space for rituals and gatherings, with multiple entrances that can be closed for privacy and security. The pond is a focal point for events, including the bathing of individuals and interactions with spirits.

A significant event occurred when a woman possessed by a spirit approached the pond, undressed, and bathed, consuming a goldfish raw. A Threshold Barrier was established around the pond to contain the spirit, transforming the area into a battleground for the expulsion of the possessing spirit. This culminated in a ritual that successfully sealed the spirit within a new vessel. The tranquil setting of the pond contrasted with the chaos of the events, as spiritual confrontations and protective measures took place.
