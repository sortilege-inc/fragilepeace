## Narrative

!lede [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]] wanted his friend saved and did not care what became of the thing inside her. [[Shinjo Higuchi]] is a noble author of standing, she had been acting strangely for days, and he wanted it handled quietly. He had already put [[Asahina Nao]] at the magistrates' disposal, with a fresh vessel brought for the purpose. [[Shiba Midori]] had the handling instructions — the papers the buyer left behind in the curio shop, unpaid for. They were still arguing about where to do it when the palace produced the sound of breaking crockery.

She was in the [[Palace Kitchen]] in a fine purple dress, dishevelled, shoving logs into the furnace one after another to make the fire burn hotter, a shattered pot at her feet. It was the dog days of summer and she was complaining of the cold while sweating through her clothes, then sitting down to empty a rice bowl in five seconds with her chopsticks. Dressed like a courtier and behaving like something worse than a peasant.

[[Kakita Kazumi]] walked up to her and asked whether he was speaking to the lady or to the being inhabiting her body. She said she had no idea what he was talking about — *it is just too hot in this house* — and there was a flash of recognition in her face before the confusion closed over it again. Two things came out of that. The first was the pendant at her throat, gold with red gems in a stylised roaring flame, and not Rokugani in any particular. He took it off her neck with a twist of the wrist and she never felt it go. It blistered his fingers, and he dropped it in a pot of water, where it sizzled. The second was that she had complained of cold and then of heat inside a minute, which is what told [[Shiba Midori]] there were two of them in there.

[[Bayushi Monban]] knew the squiggles on the back of it. It is the writing used in [[Burning Sands|the Burning Sands]], east of Rokugan, across the desert the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] crossed — and he knows it for the reason that he wants to leave Rokugan and see the rest of the world, which is not a thing his companions know about him. He cannot read it.

[[Shiba Midori]] looked at it with spiritual senses rather than eyes. The magic on it is not meishōdō and not [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]: it is wholly alien, the method and the power both, and somebody able to do things she cannot conceive of took a very powerful spirit and forced it into this object. And the object is empty, and was emptied recently. Whatever had been in it had been in [[Shinjo Higuchi]] for about a day.

Meanwhile the woman had walked out to the [[Central Garden]], taken her clothes off, and got into the [[Central Garden Fish Pond|fish pond]]. [[Doji Setsuna]] excused herself; [[Kakita Kazumi]] took her by the shoulders, turned her around and walked her out. [[Governor Miya Tetsuya]] stayed and watched his friend pluck a goldfish out of the water and eat it raw.

[[Bayushi Monban]] stripped to the waist and went in after her with a handful of marks to press into the ground around the pond. She looked up at him from the water and knew exactly who he was — she wanted to know, one day, why he had felt the need to show off to that old lady, since surely the woman knew he had one without needing to see it. His reputation has reached the spirits. He explained himself at length. When [[Asahina Nao]] said she had not known [[Bayushi Monban]] was a shugenja at all, he took the mask off, showed her the paint underneath, and told her he was a little more specialised than that.

The Threshold Barrier went down around the pond, two range bands wide, with the governor's servants shutting the garden entrances behind them. It cost him. The demon wound itched the whole time he was laying it and then began to burn outright, and he spent his karmic tie with [[Akodo Akihito]] to push the bad luck onto a man who was not there.

Then he pulled her bodily out of the water and through the barrier, on the theory that it would strip the spirit off her on the way through. She made the check, came through whole, and slapped him across the face hard enough to count as a critical hit. *Brute.* She struggled free and went for her clothes. He tackled her back through the second time and this time she failed it — and instead of the spirit tearing loose, she hit the barrier like a rock and bounced off it onto the ground. The barrier works both ways. The two of them are one thing while the possession lasts, and dragging the pair of them about does not separate them.

[[Doji Setsuna]] and [[Kakita Kazumi]] met [[Tonbo Kuma]] and their bodyguard at the gate and briefed them on what they were walking into. *Despite my ojumbo's many flaws, I do not believe that he is doing anything ill-advised to that woman.* Malicious, no. Ill-advised, probably.

And [[Shiba Midori]], reading the instructions properly, found the sentence at the top of them. The sealing ritual only works on an unbound spirit. A spirit riding a person is already bound — it is simply bound in a person rather than in an object. [[Bayushi Monban]] had read the same instructions and they had not made sense to him. Nothing anyone had planned that evening could have worked in the order they had planned it. Drive it out first; seal it second.

[[Shiba Midori]] summoned the Heart of Amaterasu over the warded ground — a working past anything [[Asahina Nao]] knew of, so far past it that she could not be asked to assist. That left the Cleansing Rite, which both shugenja have, and which requires laying hands on the target. [[Bayushi Monban]] called on [[Lady of Decay|the Lady of Decay]] — *Lady of Decay, be with me* — and the tattoos began to move: the two phoenix and the crane flapping their wings, the dragon and the lion and the crab and the scorpion travelling round his body. Half his hair turned emerald green. His eyes went black as the void but for two spinning emerald snowflakes, the sickly yellow came up through his veins, the demon wound burned, and he started coughing the way he had not coughed in weeks.

He went up on Riding the Clouds, carried the vessel and the instructions across to [[Shiba Midori]], and then picked her up and threw her at a naked woman, because she is small and light and the rite needs a hand on the target. She caught [[Shinjo Higuchi]] by the temples. *Spirit, I revoke your welcome in this body. Go away.*

The body convulsed, flames came up around it, and the fire boiled her clothes off her as the thing tore loose. [[Bayushi Monban]] pulled her clear of it, closed his eyes, and said *Onahime, little sister, time to play* — and a second black shape came out of his shoulders and his head as he drew [[Family]], the blood-bound blade.

The [[Ifrit]] stood up out of her, towering, made of ash and dust and fire. [[Doji Setsuna]] and [[Tonbo Kuma]] walked in on it. [[Kakita Kazumi]] looked up from his drink and thought: this is the apocalypse.

It had manifested inside the barrier. [[Shiba Midori]] was the one who noticed, and it is the only thing that went right all evening — for all its power the creature is not spiritually inclined, and it spent a void point trying to breach the ward and could not. Everything that followed happened because the [[Ifrit]] was already in a cage when it arrived.

[[Kakita Kazumi]] took a servant's water bucket off the decking on his way past, crossed into burning ground, and put an iaijutsu cut through it — his blade smoking, flaming coals knocked out of the centre of its mass. It reduced the cut to a glancing blow and hit him back once, and once was enough: over his fatigue in a single punch, on fire and bleeding on the ground. [[Bayushi Monban]] crossed the burning ground on Riding the Clouds, dragged him out of the barrier, and stood over him with a blade drawn. *Do not worry, Topaz Champion. I will have that fixed in a minute.* Nobody outside the ward was ever in danger. [[Kakita Kazumi]] was hit because he had gone inside it.

[[Tonbo Kuma]] never entered the fight. They stood and looked at it. [[Asahina Nao]] worked the sealing rite off to one side, failed at it more than once, and wanted it destroyed — *it is dangerous, it must be destroyed if you can* — or failing that, put in the box.

[[Doji Setsuna]] went to the edge of the barrier, knelt, and bowed to it. *Spirit, you wish to be freed. I could bear you to a location where you could be freed.* And underneath the words she pushed a picture at it: a castle where the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] prisoners of war are held, the prisoners walking out of it, and the [[Ifrit]] loose to blow east across the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] armies. [[Bayushi Monban]] had spent the evening building a box to keep it in, and objected. [[Asahina Nao]] asked whether she had lost her mind.

[[Shiba Midori]] relayed every word of it. She does not agree with a syllable of it and she said so afterward, but she would not have the right to stand if she conveyed less than what was said — the [[Phoenix Clan|Phoenix]] in her — so she went full verse.

Its voice came back like thunder, smoking out of its mouth, and this time it was understood. *Must I shrink myself to fill a mortal again? Just free me.* And then it brought its face down uncomfortably close to the interpreter and said that if there was no other way, it would go with them.

[[Shiba Midori]] bared the dead eye and refused. *The laws of Rokugan are no kinder than the laws of the dead. We cannot let this monster free.* [[Doji Setsuna]] told her it is not a monster. It is a spirit, and it was put into an object against its will, and it had not asked to be allowed to keep a body — it had asked whether it had to go back into one. *Do I have to go back into a person? If there is no other choice, I will, but do I have to?* [[Shiba Midori]] heard the sentence back and admitted she had misunderstood it. Then she looked at it with the full dead eyes and told it yes, it did have to.

[[Doji Setsuna]] took the vessel and held it out. The face was hard to read, almost resigned, and the [[Ifrit]] folded down into the jar in her hands — and what came through her hands as it went was sadness, with a weight behind it. [[Shiba Midori]] closed radiant hands over the box and found the thing inside radiating more power than anything that size should be able to. [[Asahina Nao]] sealed it, and said it was almost as though it had given up.

[[Bayushi Monban]] cleared the bleeding off [[Kakita Kazumi]] and closed his injuries. And then, still unmasked, still half dressed, with the shadow circling him and feeding on his temper, he turned on [[Doji Setsuna]] in front of the entire garden. Not for talking to it. For offering her own life to it without asking him first. *Your words are your responsibility. Your life is mine.* She answered in equal heat — *that is quite enough, Monban. You don't understand* — and relieved him of the burden. He did not answer at all. He put his top on, covered [[Shinjo Higuchi]] with it, and walked away, and the shadow gave [[Doji Setsuna]] a look of flat contempt and shrank back into him as the tattoos went home. Both of them unmasked, in front of everyone. Nobody in that garden was going to be surprised by extreme emotion after what they had just watched.

[[Shinjo Higuchi]] was physically unhurt and entirely out of it.

## Learned

## Setsuna

**The record has been calling her Tonbo Higuchi. She is [[Shinjo Higuchi]]** — the name [[Tonbo Kuma]] gave at the go board, the name of the person asking how people who are not shugenja contact the spirit world, and the name of the woman in the governor's kitchen. [[Tonbo Higuchi]] is somebody else: the man [[Tonbo Kuma]] was playing go against when the magistrates arrived, who excused himself. Two Higuchis in one city, and the record merged them.

**The parley did not fail, and it was not accepted either.** It ran into [[Shiba Midori]], who heard the whole of it because she insisted on translating the whole of it, and then said no. That is the shape of the evening: [[Doji Setsuna]] made the argument that the thing in the ward is a person, and [[Shiba Midori]] agreed with the premise, corrected her own misreading of what it had said out loud, and told it to get in the box anyway. *The laws of Rokugan are no kinder than the laws of the dead.*

Which is a defensible position and is not the one [[Doji Setsuna]] took. Every technical approach had already failed in sequence — the barrier did not separate them, dragging her through it twice did nothing but earn a slap, the sealing ritual could not touch a bound spirit at all, and [[Kakita Kazumi]] put a proper cut into the thing and was carried off unconscious for it. The only reason anyone survived is that the [[Ifrit]] happened to manifest inside a ward and is bad at void. Nobody controlled that. It was luck with a barrier standing in front of it.

**And the offer she made was not a small one.** She did not propose to let it go somewhere harmless. She proposed to carry it to the castle holding the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] prisoners of war, open it there, and let it blow east into the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] armies. Her husband is among those prisoners. That is the shape of a plan she has not admitted to having, and she offered it in a garden, in front of the governor's shugenja, with [[Shiba Midori]] obliged by her own honour to say every word of it out loud. If anyone in that garden was listening properly, [[Doji Setsuna]] has told them what she would do with a weapon if one were handed to her.

So the vessel in [[Asahina Nao]]'s keeping is a prisoner rather than a trophy, and the party made it one. They have done what the smugglers do — moved a bound spirit out of a spent vessel into a fresh one — with better manners, the governor's blessing, and a promise that was offered and then withdrawn. It asked whether it had to. It was told yes.

She should find out what it wants, because it is the only witness in this city who has been inside the traffic from the cargo's side. It knows who bound it and who put it into [[Shinjo Higuchi]]. And it knows something else now, which is which of the two women who spoke to it was willing to lie to it.

**The evidence is better than the argument, at least.** A foreign pendant in the script of [[Burning Sands|the Burning Sands]], taken off the host's neck, bound by a method [[Shiba Midori]] could not begin to describe — and a receipt under [[Hana no Ame]]'s own chop, for exactly such a talisman, already in her custody. The buyer, the author, and the possessed woman in the governor's house are one person, and the thing she signed for was round her neck.

Which leaves the only question that matters. She bought it. She did not buy the instructions for it. A novelist researching how ordinary people reach the spirit world is precisely the customer to steer a bound [[Ifrit]] toward, if what someone wants is a noble author walking a governor's palace with a fire spirit inside her — and a [[Lion Clan|Lion]] and a [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] were both already hunting her before it happened.

One last thing. [[Bayushi Monban]] shouted at her in front of a garden full of people, and what he actually said was not about the spirit. *Your words are your responsibility. Your life is mine.* He was frightened, he was marked, and he was not wrong about the risk. He was wrong about the claim. She relieved him of the burden on the spot and he walked away without a word, which is not the same as accepting it.
