The fortified trade city at the western end of the Empire's road, and the gate to the
[[Burning Sands]]. Rokugani doors are set into al-Qamari columns and tiling, which is
what a border looks like when it has been a market for long enough. A
[[Moto Family|Moto]] governor holds the White Tower. The gates shut at dark and the wall
is climbable from either side, as [[Bayushi Monban]] established twice in one night.
