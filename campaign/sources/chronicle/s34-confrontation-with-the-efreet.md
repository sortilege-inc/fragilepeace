## Narrative

!lede They went back over the fight while it was still on them, and got one thing wrong doing it. The party's account was that [[Shiba Midori]] had talked the [[Efreet]] into surrendering. What actually happened is that it ran out of options: the warding circle held, it could kill inside the circle but could not get out of one, and it understood it was going back in a box whether it liked it or not. It gave up because it had no valid targets left. [[Doji Setsuna]] had offered to carry it — strongly implying inside herself — and [[Asahina Nao]] produced a containment box instead.

[[Kakita Kazumi]] had come out of it with both bleeding and burning on him from the creature's claws. [[Bayushi Monban]] had already stopped the bleeding in the garden; the fire was still on him. So he picked him up and walked into the pool with him and put them both under. [[Kakita Kazumi]] surfaced coughing and awake, and [[Bayushi Monban]] spent a void point on Warrior's Resolve to take five fatigue off him and then sat there humming a lullaby at him to keep him calm, on the grounds that the man had just been hit by the worst thing any of them had met on this plane of existence. [[Kakita Kazumi]] looked down at the ruin of his kimono and tore the top half off.

[[Governor Miya Tetsuya]] had already found them all somewhere to sleep while the city was put back in order. [[Doji Setsuna]] would not let go of the box. She was maxed out on strife and physically shaking and in no state to process anything said to her, and what [[Shiba Midori]] and [[Asahina Nao]] were saying was correct. *You must get rid of it. It will possess you as surely as it possessed her.* [[Shiba Midori]] meant to take it and hang it in her own quarters — *you do not know magic, you do not know what you are getting into, I do* — and put a courtesy working into persuading her, and could not make it land. [[Doji Setsuna]] ignored both of them and went to her quarters and left orders that she was not to be disturbed.

The thing [[Bayushi Monban]] cannot do is stop being her yojimbo. Her own lord asked for a bodyguard, because she has a habit of being in dangerous situations, and the favour was traded around a table until somebody said *Hametsu, you owe me one* and everybody else said it would not be them. [[Shosuro Hametsu]] happened to have an Emerald Magistrate in his employ who annoys him with some frequency, and could think of nothing better to do with him than attach him to a [[Crane Clan|Crane]]'s tail feathers. The annoyance is old and specific: [[Bayushi Monban]] once refused him a request any other [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] would have leapt at, and then called the guards on him for asking. He pulled strings to take him into service afterward, to torture him, because it amuses him, and because his man keeps finding interesting ways to cost him money. There is a letter coming about a [[Mantis Clan|Mantis]] ship's sails.

So the bind is asymmetric. [[Doji Setsuna]] is under no obligation to have a yojimbo at all and may treat him as dismissed as much as she likes. He is under orders until those orders are rescinded.

An hour later he was outside her door, and as angry as he was, and as close as he was to walking in and taking the box off her, he let [[Shiba Midori]] knock first. The servant said the lady was not to be disturbed and had ordered the household to take cover long before any of it started. [[Bayushi Monban]] put his Emerald Magistrate's badge in front of her and said he wished to speak to [[Doji Setsuna]] on an urgent matter of life and death, and the servant gave way and then lay face down on the floor apologising.

[[Doji Setsuna]] was asleep wrapped around the box. She woke with cold ferocity — *what are you doing in my quarters? You were released* — and then [[Bayushi Monban]] squatted down and got onto his knees with his head on his hands and his eyes level with hers, and there was a softness in him she does not think she has ever seen. *Setsuna-san, Lady Doji, please remember our deal. That box puts you in danger.* She did not get to the end of the sentence before something in her snapped and she was weeping about having been frightened they were all going to die. He moved closer and whispered that she did not understand — they already have — and held his hand out. *Please give me the box. Your words are your responsibility, but your life is mine.*

She did not hand it over. She moved so that it was no longer behind her, and let him take it. He put it inside his kimono, told her her dreams would be softer, pulled the blanket back over her and patted it.

There was a fourth person in the room and nobody saw him. [[Kakita Kazumi]] had come in unnoticed past everyone's vigilance while the rest of them were occupied with each other, and he was there for all of it. Afterward he went up onto the roof, peeled back the tatami, and watched through the gap, waiting for the room to empty so he could take the box himself.

Then the box got to work. [[Bayushi Monban]] had put it against his skin, failed the meditation that would have kept it off him, and came out of it stuck in fire for the rest of the night and distinctly more irritable. He bowed to [[Doji Setsuna]], walked past [[Shiba Midori]] and announced that he was taking it out of the city to release the spirit.

[[Shiba Midori]]'s objection was not that he was wrong about the [[Efreet]] wanting to be free. It was that the people making decisions about the thing should not be the people it is touching — which is what was wrong with [[Doji Setsuna]] sleeping on it, and is now what is wrong with him carrying it under his clothes. Hang it on a support, away from anyone's skin, with the rest of her magical trinkets; sleep; argue in the morning. She also pointed out the ground: the ward that held it depends on thresholds, and open country has none, which makes warding a release almost impossible. She has kept dangerous things safe since before [[Snow Plain|the Battle of the Snow Plain]], and she knows it can be done. He would not wait. Every hour it sits here is an hour somebody can steal it, and they have already seen how that goes.

[[Asahina Nao]] met them on the way out, leaving the kitchens with a rice cake halfway to her mouth, and asked where exactly they thought they were going — and then made the objection that ends the argument. The palace is on an island in the middle of the river. There is city in every direction. Upstream turns a fire spirit of great power loose in [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] lands; downstream, in [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] or [[Lion Clan|Lion]] lands, possibly [[Dragon Clan|Dragon]]. A forest is a forest fire. There is no ground anywhere near this city that is not somebody's country. Her own answer was a dark hole, or a well, and forgetting about it.

[[Bayushi Monban]] would not have it punished. *If we put you in a cage and left you there until we found something reasonable for you to do* — nothing deserves that, fire spirit or not. He intends to take a boat out past the city on the [[River Near The Palace]], go a little way upriver, break the box, and tell the thing that as long as it goes its own way nobody will trouble it. And to warn it off the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]], who have a tendency to trap things like this. Asked whether he had any way to make it agree, he said he was freeing it, and that should be enough. Asked whether *should* was the same as sure, he said he was sure enough that he would swim out of the city if he had to.

[[Asahina Nao]] told him to put the box down and go with her to the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] shugenja instead, to have it sealed properly, past anyone's reach. He refused.

[[Shiba Midori]] went with him and said plainly why. She was too busy fighting her own dark thoughts to make a coherent argument, everyone in the party was compromised, and nobody would wait long enough to be rational — so there were no rational decisions available to be made. She went to be there when it happened.

The boats at the landing had nobody to crew them. What [[Bayushi Monban]] did not notice is that one of them acquired a boatman: [[Kakita Kazumi]] in servant's clothes with his hair greased black, who got past him unrecognised and took the oars.

[[Doji Setsuna]] slept for a day and a half.

## Setsuna

**She offered to be the vessel.** A woman who has spent this campaign carrying her ancestor's memories inside her head volunteered to carry a fire spirit inside her body, an hour after watching it put [[Kakita Kazumi]] on the ground. [[Asahina Nao]] stopped her with a box. Then she carried the box to her room against an explicit warning, locked the door, and fell asleep with her arms round it.

She was also, by her own account, maxed out on strife and shaking too hard to think. That is the honest version and it is worth writing down, because it means the refusal was not a decision. It was a woman past the end of herself holding onto the nearest solid object, which happened to be a bound spirit.

**And she let go when he knelt.** He asked gently, and she wept, and said aloud that she is afraid of losing them, and stopped blocking the box. That is the first time in thirty-four sessions the record shows her giving something up because somebody she trusts asked properly rather than because she was outmanoeuvred. It worked. She was alone in a locked room before it did.

[[Bayushi Monban]] was right, and he was right the first time, in the garden, when she was angry at him for saying it in public. Whatever the correction to make, it is not that he was wrong to worry. What he cannot say and she cannot hear is that he is not free to stop: the order is [[Shosuro Hametsu]]'s and it stands whatever she says, and his lord gave it to him as a punishment. She fired a man who cannot be fired, for a reason that was never his to answer for.

**The box is not safe with anybody, and he proved it inside an hour.** Skin contact left him stuck in fire and irritable, and he immediately began arguing for taking it out of the city alone to release it and earn the [[Efreet]]'s goodwill. That is the artifact talking as much as the man, on top of the mark [[Lady of Decay|the Lady of Decay]] left on him in the garden. [[Shiba Midori]]'s proposal — a shugenja holds it, off the skin, until everybody has slept — is the correct one, and she lost it to urgency at two in the morning, which is when every bad decision in this campaign has been made. She said so herself, and went anyway, because she was compromised too and knew she could not argue straight.

**And there is nowhere to put it.** [[Asahina Nao]] is the only one who said the obvious thing out loud: the palace is an island, the city runs in every direction, and past the city it is [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] country upstream and [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] or [[Lion Clan|Lion]] country down. Releasing a great fire spirit anywhere within reach of this river is releasing it into somebody's province. Nobody answered her. [[Bayushi Monban]]'s plan is to go a little way upriver, break the box, and ask it politely to behave — and his one assurance is that freeing it *should* be enough.

She should be at that river and she is asleep. Whatever is decided about the [[Efreet]], it is being decided by the man the box has been touching, accompanied by a shugenja who has said she cannot think clearly, watched from the oars by a third magistrate in disguise who wants the box for himself and has told nobody.
