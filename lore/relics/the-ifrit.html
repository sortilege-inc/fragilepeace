The Ifrit

The Ifrit is a spirit contained within a sealed box, known as a spirit-in-a-box. Shiba Midori possesses this box, which is stored in her quarters in a long receptacle, out of sight. The Ifrit is considered dangerous, as touching it can lead to significant problems. Midori has expressed concern about the need for guards to protect her quarters, indicating the potential threat posed by the Ifrit.

The Ifrit is a topic of discussion among individuals aware of its existence and location. Its containment and the implications of its power are acknowledged, suggesting that the Ifrit has a significant impact on events surrounding those who are aware of it.
