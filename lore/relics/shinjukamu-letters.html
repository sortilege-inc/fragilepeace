Shinjukamu Letters

The Shinjukamu Letters are a collection of travel letters attributed to the figure Shinjukamu, detailing lands beyond Rokugan. The letters are few in number and do not reference a secret treaty. They form part of a travelogue discussing regions outside Rokugan.

After a visit from Bayushi Monban, an Emerald Magistrate, during after-hours access, the entire collection was discovered to be missing from the library. The letters were returned two days later, with no new documents added to the collection.

During court proceedings, the Ikoma librarian testified that the Shinjukamu Letters were pre-pulled for review by Emerald Magistrates, noting the unusual absence following Monban's visit. The librarian confirmed there were no records of any secret treaty associated with the letters. This situation raised suspicions of potential forgery or misconduct, particularly due to the timing of the letters' disappearance and their subsequent return. The authenticity and provenance of the collection became a focal point in court proceedings, linked to broader claims regarding ownership of the Snow Plains and related land disputes.
