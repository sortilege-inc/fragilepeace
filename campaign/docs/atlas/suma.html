Suma

Suma is a village located near the Topaz Championship grounds, characterized by its fertile lands and strategic position within the region. Its proximity to significant territories has made it a focal point for various treaties, particularly those involving the Lion Clan and the Crane Clan. A notable recent development includes a seven-year truce that has altered territorial claims, ceding two-thirds of the Asari Plains to the Lion Clan and enhancing Suma's importance in political discussions.

The Kitsu Archives witnessed a gathering of magistrates to deliberate on a judgment related to the Snow Plain dispute between the Unicorn Clan and the Scorpion Clan. This meeting highlighted the need for documented proof of agreements between the clans concerning disputed Diamond Mines. The magistrates strategized on how to gather evidence locally in anticipation of representatives from both clans. Suma's relevance in ongoing territorial negotiations and the implications of the treaties affecting the clans was a central theme of their discussions. Political maneuvering related to Suma included intricate dealings, potential forgeries, and claims of past agreements, all crucial to the dynamics regarding rights to the Diamond Mines and the balance of power among the clans.
