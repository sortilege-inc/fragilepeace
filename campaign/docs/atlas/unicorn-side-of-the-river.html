Unicorn side of the river

The Unicorn side of the river features lush landscapes and well-maintained structures, including lavish gardens and shrines that showcase the cultural heritage of the Unicorn Clan. This area has a temperate climate, ideal for outdoor gatherings and ceremonies. It is known for its serene ambiance, enhanced by water features like fountains and waterfalls. The Dragonfly Clan has established a presence here, overseeing the shrines and ensuring the safety of visitors with a small contingent of bushi.

The Emerald Magistrates have secured accommodations on the Unicorn side for rest. They noted the ease of crossing the river, facilitating movement between districts. During their visit, they met Tonbo Kuma, leader of the Dragonfly Clan, who provided insights into local matters and connections to individuals of interest. They also explored the site of a burned-down theater associated with Daidoji Shin, uncovering remnants that generated gossip about the circumstances of the fire. The Unicorn side serves as a hub for political and social engagement within the city.
