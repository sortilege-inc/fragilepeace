Faction

Emerald Magistrates

In the vast and intricate lands of Rokugan, the Emerald Magistrates serve as the unwavering enforcers of justice under the auspices of Emperor Hantei. This esteemed faction, composed of samurai from various clans, is dedicated to maintaining order and resolving disputes throughout the Empire, embodying the principles of Bushido while skillfully navigating the complex web of clan politics. Their headquarters, the Castle of the Emerald Magistrates, also known as the Castle of the Swift Sword, acts as a central hub for overseeing conflicts and mediating clan disputes. Among their notable members are the resolute Shiba Midori and the insightful Asako Taishi of the Phoenix Clan, the cunning Bayushi Monban of the Scorpion Clan, the skilled Kakita Kazumi of the Crane Clan, and Ide Tsubame from the Unicorn Clan. Additionally, Kitsuki Kaage serves as a senior Emerald Magistrate, contributing to the faction's leadership and judicial responsibilities. Together, they form a familial bond united by a shared commitment to the welfare of the Empire, acting not only as law enforcers but also as mediators in clan conflicts. Their current focus revolves around a significant ownership dispute concerning the Snow Plain Diamond Mines, which the Scorpion Clan claims were deeded to them by the Unicorn Clan. Recently summoned to Kitsu Keep by Lady Kitsu Takeko, the daimyo of the Kitsu Family, the Magistrates are tasked with resolving this contentious issue, represented by Kitsuki Kaage alongside ambassadors from both the Scorpion Clan and Unicorn Clan. As they delve into the historical claims surrounding the Battle of the Snow Plain, the Emerald Magistrates meticulously investigate records and personal accounts to uncover inconsistencies regarding the Scorpion Clan's involvement. Monban collaborates with a Scorpion Clan historian to strategize delaying proceedings for further evidence collection, while the group prioritizes reviewing quartermaster records to identify potential logistical sabotage during the battle. They remain steadfast in opposing the Scorpion Clan's claims unless substantial evidence is presented, noting the absence of key figures like Shosuro Amane in historical records. The path of the Emerald Magistrates is fraught with danger, as they frequently encounter ambushes and threats to their mission. A recent assault on the Imperial Roads by masked assailants posing as samurai without clan insignia highlighted the risks they face. Doji Setsuna reported this incident to Kitsu Takeko, characterizing the attack as an affront to both the Empire and the Lion Clan. In response, the Kitsu Family ordered a search for the attackers, tasking Akodo Atsushi with deploying forces to capture them alive for questioning. Meanwhile, fellow Magistrates, including Shiba Midori and Kakita Kazumi, investigated the ambush site, gathering discreet evidence to confirm the attackers' identities. The camaraderie and loyalty among the Emerald Magistrates shine through their actions during chaotic confrontations. Monban, injured in the attack, received care from the Lion Clan, while Midori engaged in a duel with an assailant leader to protect Taishi. Kazumi employed stealth to strike at enemies, exemplifying their readiness to safeguard one another and uphold their mission. As they continue their pursuit of justice, their commitment to their ancestral legacy and the welfare of the Empire drives them forward, embodying the spirit of the samurai in every challenge they face. The Emerald Magistrates are not only enforcers of justice but also a judicial faction responsible for overseeing legal matters and maintaining order within the realm. They assert jurisdiction over significant cases, particularly those involving inter-clan disputes and allegations of misconduct, as exemplified by the case of Mirumoto Isshin, who was presented for judgment. In a notable case, Bayushi Monban presented a secret treaty allegedly between the Unicorn and Scorpion clans, prompting various magistrates, including Ide Tsubame, to examine its authenticity and implications. Tensions between the Scorpion and Lion clans escalated during these proceedings, with the Emerald Magistrates mediating disputes and ensuring decorum in the courtroom, illustrating their pivotal role in adjudicating complex inter-clan issues and navigating the political intricacies of the realm. With the anticipated arrival of Magister Kitsuki Kaage, the Emerald Magistrates remain vigilant against the threats looming over Rokugan, prepared to navigate the complexities of their roles with a profound sense of purpose and camaraderie. Their influence extends through key figures such as Doji Setsuna, who has engaged in diplomatic efforts to secure support from notable clan members, and Shiba Midori, who investigates the disappearance of a Lion Clan medic suspected of being a Scorpion Clan infiltrator. The activities of the Emerald Magistrates culminate in a tea ceremony with Kitsu Takeko, during which Setsuna requests the transfer of her imprisoned husband, highlighting the faction's complex alliances and negotiations within the political landscape. As the court sessions at their castle continue, the Emerald Magistrates investigate cases such as the claims surrounding the missing secret treaty that Bayushi Monban asserts exists. Ide Tsubame, after discovering a concealed message on a letter from the Head Librarian, delves deeper into the matter, seeking information from the Commandant of the Academy. During court proceedings, evidence including letters from Akodo Akihito emerges, detailing a dream that resulted in the discovery of documents linked to the Battle of the Snow Plain. The court evaluates the ownership of the Diamond Mines and the validity of the Scorpion Clan's claims, with disputes arising regarding the authenticity of documents, prompting arguments for Imperial custody of the mines. Doji Setsuna suggests a non-interference pact with Shosuro Aishi, proposing support for the Scorpion claim in exchange for a commitment not to interfere with upcoming Crane negotiations. This leads to an agreement where both parties stake honor to solidify their commitments, reflecting the complex political maneuvering typical of the Emerald Magistrates. Recently, the Emerald Magistrates have expanded their operations to the City of the Rich Frog, where they are investigating local intrigues. Key members, including Kakita Kazumi, Doji Setsuna, Shiba Midori, and Bayushi Monban, are currently engaged in uncovering the truth behind a controversial author, Hana no Ame, whose work threatens the reputation of a Lion samurai, as well as the source of illicitly sold Unicorn spirit magic charms. They have met at an eatery near The Docks to develop a strategy against smuggling activities linked to occult items, preparing a cover story to approach potential smugglers. During this operation, they encountered a ronin who initially tried to mislead them but eventually agreed to help, providing critical information on a ship in the harbor associated with the smuggling operation and a potential threat from a group tailing the Emerald Magistrates. At the Ronin Headquarters, they met Kaeru Haya, the watch leader, who detailed ongoing surveillance of a smuggling ring involving Opium and other substances. Cooperation between the Emerald Magistrates and the ronin was established, with plans for a coordinated effort against the smugglers, including a dawn confrontation at The Docks. In addition to their ongoing investigations, the Emerald Magistrates are now tasked with the protection of Miya Misato, the governor's niece, who has been relocated within the castle due to multiple shinobi attacks. The Emerald Magistrates ordered the confinement of two Crane individuals accused of involvement in these attacks, with Daidoji Shin also investigating the matter. Concerns were raised about their capability to protect Miya Misato, leading Ikoma Tadayoshi, a court poet, to suggest he would personally guard her instead. The governor instructed Tadayoshi to monitor the Emerald Magistrates to ensure they did not endanger Miya Misato. Plans for her escort to the Lion and Unicorn camps include the Emerald Magistrates, with the governor emphasizing their involvement for additional protection, set to occur in a few days, coinciding with the arrival of a contingent of Lion soldiers.
