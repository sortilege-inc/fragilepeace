Togashi Family

Dragon

The Togashi are a faction associated with the Dragon Clan, recognized for their unique spiritual beliefs and practices. They have a deep connection to the kami and fulfill roles as monks, embodying a philosophy that combines martial prowess with spiritual understanding. The Togashi are distinguished by their own markings and symbols that signify their identity and heritage.

In the year 1123, a Togashi Nun and others awoke from death after being killed by cultists who opened black scrolls in the Phoenix lands. This revival took place near a shrine featuring Lion Clan markings, indicating a possible reverence towards the kami. The revived individuals observed a paved road built to clan standards, suggesting infrastructure related to ongoing clan conflicts. The Togashi's influence is also reflected in the mysterious gifts sent to Doji Setsuna, which featured the Togashi family crest, highlighting their interactions with other factions amidst the evolving political landscape marked by clan tensions and disputes.
