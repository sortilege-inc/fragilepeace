Lion District

The Lion District is located on the south bank of the City Of The Rich Frog, a strategically significant area among the three clan territories: Lion, Dragon, and Unicorn. The district features an organized structure and is overseen by the Lion Clan, which holds considerable influence over local governance and commerce.

A prominent feature within the Lion District is a Lion-run inn, the closest lodging option for travelers coming from the landward side. The area serves as a bustling hub where various families operate under the imperial governor, Miya Amaya, who resides on a Central Island. Local customs include discussions at teahouses, which are venues for strategizing and sharing information about local affairs. The district is home to Kaeru Akiara, a local bodyguard, known for providing insights about the city and warning against visiting The Docks after dark due to criminal activity.
