Owari Plains

Ground in the north. Moto Gaheris holds that the recent Crane–Lion treaty cut it off from the reach of the Unicorn Clan's allies, and counts that among his reasons for suspecting the Crane of arranging this war. Nobody has answered the charge; Kakita Kazumi turned it aside rather than deny it.

The Archivist export was taken before this entry existed. What the page holds is drawn from the sessions themselves.
