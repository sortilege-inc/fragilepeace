Ikoma Lands

Lion

The Ikoma Lands are located within the Lion Clan territories and are known for their libraries and repositories of knowledge. The landscape includes both flatlands and hills, as indicated by travel patterns toward the Castle of the Swift Sword. The Ikoma family is recognized for their significant arcane knowledge, suggesting that the region hosts scholars and practitioners of the mystical arts.

Asako Taishi, a Phoenix Clan scholar, was noted traveling toward the Ikoma Lands, which are reputed for their excellent libraries. Shiba Midori, a Phoenix Clan shugenja, assessed Taishi's travel documents and observed that he had wandered along the Imperial Roads toward the Ikoma Lands. The party discussed the need for authentication of an item Taishi possessed, which he claimed was dangerous, leading to an agreement for his escort to the Castle of the Swift Sword for safety and scrutiny. The conversation also indicated a deep connection to the region's mystical elements.
