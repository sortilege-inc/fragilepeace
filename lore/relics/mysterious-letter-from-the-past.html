Mysterious Letter From The Past

The Mysterious Letter From The Past is a significant document requested by Bayushi Monban from Katsuki Kage during a court session at Swift Sword Castle. The letter had been previously removed from official records, and Monban sought its return for reasons not detailed in the records. Kage discreetly placed the letter into Monban's hand, underscoring its importance in the context of ongoing events.

The letter is linked to various characters, including Doji Setsuna and Shinjo Harunobu, Monban's betrothed. Monban's interest in the letter relates to the tumultuous political landscape surrounding the Unicorn and Lion clans, particularly amid recent hostilities. The letter is also connected to the Sword of Hiruma, a revered ancestral blade that had been stolen, suggesting it may contain crucial information regarding ongoing conflicts and the fate of those involved.
