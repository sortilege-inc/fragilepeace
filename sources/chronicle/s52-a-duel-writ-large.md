## Narrative

!lede [[Doji Setsuna]] went to sit with the man she had humiliated the day before, and
[[Ikoma Ujiaki]] was charming for half an hour and took more out of it than she did. Then
[[Akodo Akihito]] came back into the tent speaking in a completely different voice.

Word had finally reached [[Bayushi Monban]], out west, of what had happened while he was
gone — that a [[Lion Clan|Lion]] had refused to take an Emerald Magistrate's word for the
writ, that the challenge had been answered by pointing at [[Akodo Akihito]], and that
**[[Akodo Akihito]] had said he wished he did not have to do this because he believed
[[Kakita Kazumi]], and his clan made him fight anyway.** He took that badly. Not the eye —
the fact that a magistrate's word had been settled with a blade instead of being accepted.

Up the mountain, the [[The Earthen Queen|Earthen Queen]] permitted him **two** fire spirits and not six. He
took two and the fox and left the rest below, and climbed — and found, in the last week of
summer, that **it was snowing.**

The cave was as he had left it, which is to say the ribcage was still hanging at the mouth
with fresh meat on it and the torches inside were lit. He had the spirits set the meat
alight to bring the trolls home, and slipped in past them.

Inside was a whole cow roasted on a spit and left to cool, two sleeping places, soup gone
cold, a missing pot he knows exactly what became of — and **a drawing on the wall of a
samurai in a helmet**, which is meant to be him, spattered with offal by people who wanted
him to know how they felt.

He had them burn everything except the picture. He wants whoever ends up living here to
look at it and remember who got them the place.

The weapons were gone — clubs, probably spears, and the travois they haul with — which is
how he knew the trolls were out, and how they came back: two of them, dragging poached
cattle, bellowing, running for a cave that was on fire.

He met them at the mouth, unmasked, and told them they were the lowest bottom-feeding
filth he had ever stood near and ought to be fried alive for making a person of rank clean
their midden. Then he mooned them. It cost him something in standing to begin a fight with
his clothing in that condition, which he judged worth it.

It went badly. **Troll skin shifts to meet what is hurting it**; the second burst of flame
did much less than the first, and they simply walked through the fire and set about the
spirits with clubs. One elemental was beaten into the snow until it went out. The other is
being pounded down now.

So [[Bayushi Monban]] went up — rode the air to the smoke-vent in the roof — and on the
way **he took off his mask and called [[Onahime]] up.**

Blackness came out of the left side of him and a woman's laughter filled the cave, and
what hung in the vent was half a demon and half a rather handsome man with a very wicked
smile. Which is the first time the how of it has been said plainly: **when they died, they
had to wear masks to go into [[Jigoku]], and he made a bargain with [[Onahime]] that they
would stay joined.** She is not an evil thing. She kept him alive down there and brought
him out, and what she asked in return was to take a piece of him with her when she left.
She has kept her side of it since.

He kicked one troll off its feet with a fist of air and hauled himself out onto the roof of
the mountain, between two rock teeth, with one spirit dead, one dying, two trolls entirely
unhurt, and a question he cannot answer: whether the [[The Earthen Queen|Earthen Queen]] is watching, and
whether a favour owed by him is worth more to her than a pair of trolls.

**At the shrine.** [[Doji Setsuna]] went to [[Ikoma Ujiaki]] over the recess — the man
she had pinned in front of the whole tent the day before, who had every reason to be
bristling. He turned on the charm instead.

She has been walking a rope between two buildings for weeks and can do it blindfolded, and
what she felt sitting opposite him was something close to enjoyment — the particular lift
of being pushed hard by somebody the same size as you. It was not a flawless victory. She
knew that at the time.

What she got out of it: she pressed him, without ever saying it, toward being the man who
takes the initiative on a settlement with his Champion's backing. And she declined every
premise he offered without once appearing to. When he assured her the [[Lion Clan|Lion]]
had of course been concerned for the well-being of the daimyō's former wife and would
never have left her destitute, she said only that she looked forward to the record showing
it, at the appropriate time. When he called the whole business a misunderstanding escalated
by other people's choices, she was polite and did not agree.

She got something out of him too, and it is the most useful thing anyone has learned about
a [[Lion Clan|Lion]] in this negotiation. **She found out what he actually wants.** Not
power — he does not want to be a champion of anything. **He wants to go down in history as
one of the [[Lion Clan|Lion]]'s most competent negotiators**, and he believes he is well
on his way.

And the tell had already been in front of her. It was [[Akodo Akihito]]'s place to raise
the objection about [[Shiba Midori]] representing two clans. [[Ikoma Ujiaki]] did not wait
for him. **He keeps stepping in front of the man his own clan seated in that chair**, and
now she knows why.

But he got more. He read her — her composure, the old [[Kakita Family|Kakita]] grudge she
carries, and a social objective, which was the decoy she had laid and not the real one.
Then he did something to her that will keep: **he turned her legal scholarship into a
liability.** From now on, when he opposes her, her own learning works against her —
precedents she does not know, weaknesses found in her own arguments, the strength
reversed.

And in the pleasant part of the conversation, the small talk, he found out **the one thing
she wants for herself**, which is the harder loss of the two. There is a small private gathering the [[Asahina Family|Asahina]]
hold on the [[Crane Clan|Crane]] coast in spring — poetry, a few days, by invitation —
utterly unlike everything she has spent her life on, and she would say yes to it in a
heartbeat and believes she would never be asked. She dismissed it prettily as impossible
for someone like her. *Don't undersell yourself*, he said. *You are an extraordinary young
magistrate, making a name for yourself.*

Afterwards she went to [[The Unicorn's Iuchi Advisor|the Unicorn's own Iuchi advisor]] —
a middle-aged woman in a shugenja's robes hung about with talismans, who had performed the
morning's blessing herself rather than let [[Shiba Midori]] do it, [[Shiba Midori]] being a
party. She is [[Akodo Kage]]'s opposite number and knows it.

She admired the trap. And she warned her about it. **A lion in a trap is more dangerous
than a lion loose, because it believes it has nothing left to lose. The trick is to let
them lose without letting them feel that they have lost.**

On [[Asako Ikari]] she was blunter than anyone has been: find her quickly — and **it is not
in your interest to put her on her feet in front of this table.** Better to settle it
directly with the [[Lion Clan|Lion]]. There is always Winter Court if the talks stall.

And she gave the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]'s priorities in order, which is the most useful
thing anyone said all day. **Land. Horses. Hostages.** Give her those three and she is
fairly confident she can bring [[Shinjo Altansarnai]] to the rest, and let the territorial
argument be had properly at Winter Court.

Then [[Doji Setsuna]] called them back, and [[Akodo Akihito]] was not the man who had
spoken before lunch.

The [[Lion Clan|Lion]], he said, had never sought to raze anything. They had not answered
insult with insult. They killed only warriors fighting in defence of their homes, as honour
requires — and he **praised the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] for the courage with which they
had fought.** Then the frame: this war is **a duel writ large, between clans rather than
between champions.** It hangs undecided. And this is precisely the moment at which either
party may step back, before one of them takes a blow it cannot answer. The
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] are prepared to settle accounts, if the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] are
willing.

Somebody had given him new orders.

[[Doji Setsuna]] took the metaphor and turned it over. **Is a duel one party never accepted
a duel at all?** [[Shiba Midori]] put the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] position: the
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] took a slight and inflated it beyond all proportion; this belonged
before the Emperor, or in a tent exactly like this one, and never on a field. They never
agreed that a war was the proper answer to anything.

[[Akodo Akihito]] answered that the terms of a duel are normally settled in advance — but
that by meeting the call to battle with an army of their own, the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]
had accepted the terms in fact, and had acquitted themselves well in them.

So she asked him what complete defeat would look like, since he had named it. He said that
usually one side yields, convincingly, as in a duel; that there is always the chance to
surrender, whether or not it is taken; and that in these circumstances it would not be
advisable to fight until one side can no longer stand. Which is why, he said, they are
sitting here instead.

## Setsuna

**She lost that conversation and should say so to herself plainly.** [[Ikoma Ujiaki]] took
her composure, an old wound, and a reading of what she wants — and he turned her legal
learning into a weapon he can use on her the next time they are opposed. She got him nearer
to initiating a settlement, refused his premises gracefully, and came away with the thing
he wants, which is real and which is less than he took. **She enjoyed it, which is the part to be
careful about.** Going toe to toe with someone her own size felt good, and feeling good
across a table from [[Ikoma Ujiaki]] is how a person gets handled.

**But what she took off him is a handle, and she knew it while he was still talking.** He
wants to be remembered as one of the great [[Lion Clan|Lion]] negotiators, and he is vain
enough to believe he is becoming one. That is not a man who wants a war settled over his
head by an arbiter, and it is certainly not a man who wants his name on the losing side of
an Imperial ruling. **A settlement he can be said to have authored is worth more to him
than a Lion victory he merely attended** — and he has been talking over [[Akodo Akihito]]
all week to make sure people notice him doing it.

**Which means his want and her want are the same shape.** She has been trying since the
first day to get the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] to settle rather than be ruled against, and she
has just found the man across the table who needs that outcome for reasons entirely his
own. She does not have to persuade him of anything. **She has to let him have the credit
for it**, and be seen not to mind.

**And she knows he read her — accurately, and further than she would like.** She was there
for it. She felt him take her measure, felt the decoy hold and the true objective stay
buried, and she knows he came away from the small talk holding the one private thing she
wants. That is uncomfortable and it is survivable, because she can see the shape of it and
plan around it.

**What she cannot see is the one that will actually hurt her.** She has no idea that her
own legal learning has been turned against her — that the next time she reasons from
precedent with [[Ikoma Ujiaki]] opposite, her best instrument will work backwards in her
hands. **She will walk into her strongest lane not knowing it has been mined**, and she
will find out in front of the whole tent.

**And he knows what she wants.** Not what the magistrate wants — what *she* wants, the
poetry gathering on the coast that she has never once asked for and does not believe she
would be given. **That is the only thing anyone has learned about her in this campaign
that could be offered rather than threatened**, and it is now in the hands of a man who
has just demonstrated he will use whatever he is given. She should decide, before it is
ever offered, what her answer is.

**[[The Unicorn's Iuchi Advisor|The Iuchi advisor]] gave her the settlement.** Land, horses, hostages, in
that order, and the rest can follow. That is a floor she can actually build on: the land is
the thing the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] will not move on, the horses cost the
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] nothing but face, and the hostages are the piece [[Akodo Akihito]] has
already shown he will bargain with. Everything else — reparations, ransoms, the territorial
argument — goes to Winter Court unresolved, which is where she has been steering all of it.

**And the warning under it is the most useful thing said to her since [[Matsu Maro]] told
her the war was about honour.** She has the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] in a trap. A trapped lion
does not become reasonable; it becomes willing to lose everything. **The whole art from
here is letting them lose without letting them feel it** — which means whatever she rules,
[[Akodo Kage]] must be able to carry it back to [[Akodo Toturi]] as a thing the
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] chose.

**[[Akodo Akihito]]'s new voice is the trap closing from the other side.** Calling it a
duel between clans is elegant and it is a defence: duels are lawful, duels are honourable,
duels end with a yielding and not a reckoning — and if this was a duel then nobody
committed a crime, they merely fought. He praised the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]'s courage in
the same breath, which is how you make a defeat survivable for the other party. Somebody
handed him that frame between the recess and the tent, and she should like it more than
she does, because **it is also the only shape in which the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] can leave
here without ruin.**

Her own answer is the right one and she should press it. **A duel one side never accepted
is not a duel.** The [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] never agreed that a war answered this; they
say it belonged in front of the Emperor, or in a tent like this one. If she can make that
stand, then the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] did not fight a lawful duel — they made an unlawful war
over a betrothal that may never have been lawfully available to break.

And [[Asako Ikari]] is still lost, and now the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]'s own advisor is
telling her it would be better for the case if the woman were never heard from. **She
should notice how much easier this all becomes if the person actually wronged stays
missing**, and how many people at this table would find that convenient.
