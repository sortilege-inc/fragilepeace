Faction

Kaeru Ronin

Ronin

The Kaeru Ronin is a faction in the region focused on investigations and maintaining order. Comprised of ronin operating independently, they often function as a neutral party under the Governor Miya Tetsuya's guidance. Their leader, Kaeru Haia, significantly influences their operations in matters of law and order. The Kaeru Ronin serves as the city constabulary for Governor Miya Tetsuya Miya Tetsuya, operating within the City Of The Rich Frog. They are affiliated with the Emerald Magistrates, collaborating to tackle issues such as smuggling and spirit disturbances.

Members of the Kaeru Ronin have been observed conducting searches at The Docks, indicating their proactive role in local affairs. They expressed concern for Shiba Midori, a spirit speaker, emphasizing the dangers of leaving her unprotected. Recently, the Kaeru Ronin have been involved in a poisoning investigation concerning Shiba Midori, enforcing house arrest for the Crane couple, Doji Masato and Doji Miho, after evidence implicated them in a potential crime. During this investigation, they restricted exits around the courtyard to maintain control and ensure the safety of all guests. Kaeru Haia has engaged the Emerald Magistrates to prepare a formal report regarding a Mantis Captain suspected of smuggling, further underscoring the Kaeru Ronin's commitment to law and order. The faction has shared information with the Emerald Magistrates about a smuggling vessel and discussed the dishonorable conduct of the Mantis Captain.

Their involvement also includes assisting in investigations into magical disturbances, such as a theater fire caused by a released spirit. The Kaeru Ronin has facilitated communication between the Emerald Magistrates and the Governor Miya Tetsuya to ensure that critical information is relayed and the region's safety is prioritized. Recently, the Kaeru Ronin have taken on the additional mission of locating the Lion armies and negotiating peace between the Lion and Unicorn clans. Their role as city constabulary was acknowledged during their arrival in the Unicorn District, where they interacted positively with various samurai and ronin, further solidifying their reputation, as evidenced by a favorable report from Bayushi Monban.
