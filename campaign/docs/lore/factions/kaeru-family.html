Faction

Kaeru Family

Ronin

The Kaeru Family is an insular group of ronin based in the City of the Rich Frog. They provide security services and maintain peace under the direction of the Miya governor. The Kaeru are known for employing unconventional methods to ensure order in the city and operate independently of the three major clans, highlighting their unique role in the local power structure.

The Kaeru Family functions similarly to a samurai group, emphasizing loyalty among members. Their activities blend law enforcement with community protection, establishing a notable presence and influence within the city's social fabric. Their services include security operations connected to ongoing investigations, particularly related to illicit activities involving Meishodo Talismans in the region.
