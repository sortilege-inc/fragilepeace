Dramatis Persona

Shinjō Higuchi

Unicorn — Shinjō

What is known

S32The name Tonbo Kuma gave for the person researching spirits, matching the description the party has been chasing — someone who wanted to know how people who are not shugenja contact the spirit world. Believed to be lodging with Governor Miya Tetsuya, who referred them to Kuma in the first place, and who also meant to consult Asahina Nao. Doji Setsuna's husband is a Shinjō.
S33The noble author who writes as Hana no Ame, and Governor Miya Tetsuya's friend — the buyer whose chop is on the curio shop receipt, who left the handling instructions behind unpaid for. She was the possessed guest: a foreign pendant at her throat, complaining of cold in high summer while feeding a furnace, eating like she was starving, eating a goldfish raw out of the pond. Freed, physically unhurt, exhausted and badly shaken.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
