Kitsu Family

Lion

The Kitsu Family, a distinguished lineage within the Lion Clan of Rokugan, serves as both spiritual mediators and guardians of ancestral traditions, intricately woven into the cultural fabric of their society. Under the leadership of the esteemed daimyo Kitsu Takeko, recognized for her distinctive red hair and small stature, the family operates from the Castle of the Swift Sword, also known as Kitsu Keep, where they uphold their responsibilities as Shugenja and protectors of the clan’s heritage.

The Kitsu Family is deeply committed to the well-being of the Lion Clan, providing spiritual guidance and military support. Notable members include Saemi, the court soothsayer and astrologer-adept, and Hoshiko, the family apothecary, who ensures the health of the garrison at Loyalty Castle. Their meticulous documentation of lineage allows members to recite their ancestry with precision, emphasizing the honor and familial ties that define their identity as both samurai and spiritual leaders. This dedication to their heritage is further exemplified by Kitsu Taigen, a shrine attendant, and Kitsuko Ayoko, a historian-shugenja who possesses the ability to summon spirits and is recognized for her potential despite being adopted and lacking samurai birth.

Historically, the Kitsu Family has played a significant role in key events, such as the Battle of Snow Plain, where their ancestor Kitsu Yue was instrumental in saving the Lion Clan general Matsu Sakura. Recently, Kitsu Taigen sought to meditate at the ancestral shrine to connect with Kitsu Yue's spirit, believing that Ayoko might be the focus of an ancestral vision. The family’s connection to their ancestors is profound, as illustrated by a significant spiritual event at a Shinseist shrine where an ancestor manifested, demanding respect for their lineage from outsiders.

Kitsu Takeko’s diplomatic skills are evident in her interactions with dignitaries, including the Emerald Magistrates and her engagement with Doji Setsuna following an ambush on Lion Clan roads. She demonstrated her commitment to hospitality and care for allies by providing comfort and overseeing the treatment of the injured. The Kitsu Family actively participates in the political landscape of the region, addressing disputes such as those involving diamond mines with the Scorpion Clan and Unicorn Clan, and engaging in ongoing negotiations regarding marriage alliances and historical claims.

In recent discussions surrounding the legacy of the Battle of Snow Plain, Kitsuko Ayoko has raised concerns regarding suppressed information about an ancestor's abilities, which has implications for the family's honor. Her attempts to summon ancestral spirits during legal proceedings aim to illuminate the events of the battle and uphold the Kitsu Family's reputation amidst allegations of Scorpion suppression. This focus on the Kitsu Family's historical significance and the role of ancestor spirits reflects their importance in the ongoing discourse regarding clan honor and historical records.

Despite their spiritual significance, the Kitsu Family faces challenges in asserting their political influence within the Lion Clan, often overshadowed by court intrigues. They navigate these complexities while striving for reconciliation and harmonious relationships within the court, as seen in Saemi's invitation to Doji Setsuna for tea. Their collaborative approach to governance and military training, particularly their strong ties with the Akodo War College, underscores their commitment to upholding the honor of the Lion Clan and preserving the integrity of their ancestral traditions amidst the intricate dynamics of courtly life.
