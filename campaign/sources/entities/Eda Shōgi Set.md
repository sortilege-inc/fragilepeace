A shōgi set of Eda family make, taken from the abandoned castle's stores on an Emerald
Magistrate's authority and sent to [[Matsu Maro]] as a gift, with Well of Desire worked
into it. She had been asked about beforehand: [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] knew his own clan's
officers, and knew she loved the game. She accepted, and opened the castle gate that
night on her own authority. What refusing would have cost her she was never told.
