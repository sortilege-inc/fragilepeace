Drowned Merchant River

The Drowned Merchant River flows with a sense of foreboding, its dark, murky waters serving as a significant boundary between the territories of the Lion Clan and the Unicorn Clan. Located north of the Lion Clan's Loyalty Castle, this river is not merely a geographical feature but a pivotal element in the ongoing conflict between the clans. Its strategic position influences military movements and serves as a critical point for prisoner exchanges, particularly highlighted by the capture of Doji Setsuna's husband during the tumultuous Battle of the Hanto River.

Historically, the Drowned Merchant River has been a site of trade and negotiation, its waters steeped in tales of loss and betrayal. It has witnessed the signing of treaties and the unfolding of disputes, particularly concerning the lands of Toshi Ranbo and the Asari Plains. The river's name evokes a sense of caution, reflecting the complexities of clan relationships and the political landscape that surrounds it. Recent treaties filed in Otosan Uchi have attempted to address the ongoing tensions, yet the claims of the Scorpion Clan regarding the river's significance remain unresolved, underscoring its role as a point of contention in the broader narrative of clan rivalries.

The Drowned Merchant River is a significant waterway that merges with the Three Sides River at the City Of The Rich Frog, a vital port for trade among the Unicorn, Dragon, and Lion clans. The river's wide banks facilitate the movement of goods and commerce. The City Of The Rich Frog is divided into three districts: the Unicorn district, which features a large wharf of polished black granite and stone buildings topped with violet roofs; the Dragon district, marked by shrines along the riverbank but lacking a wharf; and the Lion district, which utilizes a canal system for cargo transport, enhancing its defensive capabilities.

The city serves as a strategic crossing point for travelers heading into Unicorn Lands. Within the Lion district lies a renowned teahouse known for its exceptional tea and skilled hostess, frequented by notable patrons from the Dragon and Lion clans. This highlights the river's role as a hub of social and political interaction, with the teahouse's long history reflecting the cultural significance of the Drowned Merchant River in facilitating trade and the exchange of information and alliances among the clans.

Surrounding the river are the bustling docks of the Unicorn District, characterized by creaky wooden boards and dim lighting from lanterns. The area exhibits a mix of vibrant nightlife and shadowy corners, fostering an environment conducive to clandestine dealings. Recently, The Docks have attracted attention due to ongoing smuggling operations, particularly involving a large Mantis ship that had arrived from the Drowned Merchant River. Covert activity, including the transfer of bags or bushels onto the ship, has raised suspicions of connections to a smuggling ring. The river's presence symbolizes the crime and intrigue that define the area, further complicating the already tense relations between the clans.
