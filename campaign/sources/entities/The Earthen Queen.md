clan: Spirit — the Earthen Court
---

Sovereign of the Earthen Court beneath the mountains west of the Empire, a woman made of
uncut jewels holding a single light in a geode hall. Her guards are man-shaped boulders
who move through stone as though it were water. The cave [[Bayushi Monban]] offered to
the fire spirits is in her realm, and he offered it without asking her.
