The Midnight Treaty

The agreement drafted by Doji Setsuna at the castle between the two rivers and signed at the shōgi table. Signatories Moto Gaheris and Matsu Maro; sealed by Miya Misato on the Emperor's behalf.

Its terms: the Lion march out with arms and armour; they yield the castle as it was yielded to them; they proceed on their mission without interference; they concede that the Unicorn defeated them there; no further violence against Unicorn citizens; and the Lion and the Moto jointly escort the Imperial Envoy's party to its next destination.

Every party staked ten honour on keeping it, forfeit to whoever breaks it second. It is the first instrument in this campaign that costs anybody anything to break.
