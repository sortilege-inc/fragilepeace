Akodo Roads

Lion

The Akodo Roads are a network of travel routes located within the Lion Clan's territory, known for their strategic importance and well-maintained pathways. These roads provide access to various locations, including the Castle of the Swift Sword, recognized for its arcane knowledge and historical significance. The terrain along the Akodo Roads features open spaces and copse areas, which can offer concealment and opportunities for ambush. The climate in this region is temperate, conducive to travel.

The Castle of the Swift Sword is noted for its reputation as a safe location for authentication of potentially dangerous discoveries, further enhancing its significance for travelers.
