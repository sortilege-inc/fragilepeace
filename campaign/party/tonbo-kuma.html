Tonbo Kuma

Dragonfly

Tombokuma

Tonbo Kuma serves as the shrine keeper and leader of the Dragonfly Clan in the Dragon District, known for his calm demeanor and presence in the lavish gardens surrounding the clan's shrines. A Dragonfly shugenja, he is involved in investigating illicit Meishodo Talismans sold on the black market, having guided a young Dragon magistrate to the City of the Rich Frog for inquiries about a woman seeking these magical items.

Kuma met with the author Hana no Ame at the Dragon shrine, indicating his connection to literary pursuits and research for a novel. During a tea ceremony at an unnamed teahouse, it was revealed that the author was inquiring about potentially scandalous subjects that could implicate the Unicorn Clan's magical practices. The Dragon magistrate noted that he had been instructed by Tonbo Kuma regarding the investigation, reinforcing Kuma's role as a figure of authority and knowledge in the current events unfolding in the city.

During a recent encounter, Kuma welcomed the Emerald Magistrates with a polite bow, expressing gratitude for their visit and acknowledging the honor of having esteemed guests seek their attention. His attire and posture reflected a figure of authority and tranquility, embodying the elegance of his clan. In this meeting, he conversed with Doji Setsuna, who inquired about a novelist researching spirit interactions. Kuma mentioned meeting a young Unicorn and a Lion samurai who were also seeking the same author, indicating recent interest in this individual among multiple parties. He demonstrated an understanding of the dynamics at play, suggesting coordination among those interested in the novelist, and offered assistance in connecting with the author.

Kuma participated in a teaching game of go with Doji Setsuna, applying handicaps, and acknowledged her skill after her victory. Throughout the interaction, his gestures appeared to influence the surrounding water features, hinting at his potential shugenja abilities. He provided information on Shinjō Higoichi, the author of interest, confirming he was referred to him by Governor Miya Tetsuya, The Governor. Kuma speculated that Higoichi might be a guest of The Governor, linking the inquiries into spirit interactions with the political landscape surrounding the magistrates' visit.

Recently, Kuma arrived with a bodyguard and was briefed on the situation by Doji Setsuna, expressing familiarity with The Governor and his guest, indicating prior communication with The Governor. He was informed about a possessing spirit and the challenges the group faced in dealing with it. Encouraged to assist Bayushi Monban and Shiba Midori in expelling the spirit, Kuma was made aware of the potential dangers involved, as the situation was described as not for the faint of heart. His demeanor suggested readiness to engage with the unfolding events, although he did not immediately enter the fray.

In a recent confrontation with an Efreet, an Ifrit, Tonbo Kuma displayed confidence, stating, "I don't know what the problem is. I'm sexy." Following the battle, he participated in discussions about the containment of the Efreet, which was successfully bound within a warding circle. Kuma observed interactions among other characters, including Bayushi Monban and Doji Setsuna, as tensions arose over the risks of the Efreet's containment. He was present when the group recounted the binding of the Efreet and the decisions made regarding its containment, particularly concerning the safety and handling of the Efreet's box.

Tonbo Kuma has shown a distinctively feminine aspect and does not conform strictly to gender stereotypes. He is associated with the Emerald Magistrates and engages in strategic discussions regarding spirits and their containment. During a game of go, Kuma expressed a preference for releasing a spirit rather than confining it, arguing that keeping it imprisoned would lead to greater danger in the future. He participated in a debate about handling a Fire Spirit possessing Bayushi Monban, advocating for its release while acknowledging the risks involved. Kuma's perspective was that if the spirit was not freed, it would return with increased anger and power. Additionally, his opinions were shared with Asahina, who critiqued the plan to move the spirit into a trained warrior, emphasizing the potential dangers of such actions.
