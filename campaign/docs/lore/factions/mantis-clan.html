Faction

Mantis Clan

The Mantis is a faction engaged in maritime activities and piracy, primarily operating from the coastal regions of Rokugan. They are characterized by their willingness to engage in piracy and are viewed as a destabilizing force within the political landscape. Their actions have impacted trade routes, particularly those of the Crane Clan, and they do not discourage piracy, which affects their relationships with other clans. The Mantis seek to leverage their position to influence political dynamics through opportunistic means. They are also recognized for their involvement in both legitimate trade and questionable dealings, drawing the attention of other clans and authorities. Skilled in navigating the complexities of commerce and the political landscape of Rokugan, the Mantis interact with various clans and factions, further entrenching their influence.

In addition to their piratical endeavors, the Mantis are deeply involved in smuggling operations within the City of the Rich Frog, particularly active along The Docks. They are linked to a larger underground organization known for trafficking questionable substances, primarily Opium and other items evading customs duties. Their operations have drawn the attention of the Emerald Magistrates, who are currently investigating their smuggling activities. A notable ship within the faction is captained by Yoritomo Kuroba, whose crew has encountered legal troubles due to a drunken brawl, resulting in the detention of half the crew to maintain control over the ship's activities. The Mantis also operate blue-water ships, which, while not ideal for river navigation, are present in the area due to favorable conditions.

The faction is led by Captain Kubota, an experienced and brash captain known for acquiring foreign and illicit goods. He is confident in his ability to navigate deep within the Empire to conduct his business. The Mantis maintain connections with various clans and engage in complex political dynamics, particularly with the Unicorn and Crane clans. Rumors suggest that they are seeking relics from beyond Rokugan. However, Captain Kubota's recent behavior has drawn criticism; he has shown disrespect towards the Emerald Magistrates and the Governor Miya Tetsuya, leading to concerns about his actions. The Kaeru Ronin, an independent faction led by Kaeru Haya, have publicly opposed him, condemning his treatment of his crew and his involvement in smuggling activities.

Recently, Daidoji Shin, an agent of the Crane Clan, has expressed suspicion regarding a Mantis' potential link to a shugenja responsible for a tsunami that affected the Crane Lands. This allegation reflects the Mantis' possible involvement in illicit activities that disrupt the peace of Rokugan. Additionally, Shin's investigation into the distribution of illegal magical items in the city emphasizes the Mantis' connection to ongoing tensions surrounding magic and trade. The Emerald Magistrates, including Doji Setsuna and her companions, have decided to investigate The Docks in the Unicorn District, indicating the Mantis' significant role in the dynamics of power, commerce, and justice in the region.

In the political arena, the Mantis have participated in discussions concerning a dispute over diamond mines between the Scorpion Clan and Unicorn Clan. The Scorpion Clan claims an ancient agreement for assistance at the Battle of Snow Plain, which the Unicorn Clan contests, leading to tensions that the Mantis aim to exploit. The Scorpion Clan has provided documentary evidence to support their claims, and the Mantis have positioned themselves to facilitate this process. They are regarded as a potential ally for the Scorpion Clan, who benefit from keeping the Lion Clan engaged in conflicts to prevent them from focusing on the Scorpion Clan. The Mantis' strategic positioning illustrates their desire to maintain influence and protect their interests amid the shifting alliances and hostilities among the great clans.

Recently, Captain Kubota's ship was implicated in a smuggling operation tied to the disappearance of Shiba Midori. Tensions rose when Bayushi Monban, an Emerald Magistrate, confronted Kubota and ordered him to anchor his ship. Kubota refused, citing concerns about damaging his vessel and the urgency of delivering ransom cargo for a kidnapped child. The situation escalated when Monban cut the ship's sails in retaliation, leading to a heated exchange in which Kubota asserted his innocence regarding smuggling charges, defending his reputation as an honest merchant and fleet admiral. The confrontation ended with mutual threats and ongoing tensions, as the ship continued its journey downriver and the search for the missing Kakita Kazumi persisted. Meanwhile, the Kaeru Ronin's formal report to the Emerald Magistrates regarding Kubota's actions and the treatment of Shiba Midori has intensified scrutiny on the Mantis, signaling a growing backlash against their operations and leadership.
