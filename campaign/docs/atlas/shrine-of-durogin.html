Shrine of Durogin

The Shrine of Durogin is located along the Imperial Roads, east of Morning Glory Castle in the Phoenix Clan lands. It serves as a waypoint for travelers, especially scholars and those in search of knowledge. The shrine is notable for its spiritual significance, drawing individuals who commune with spirits or seek arcane wisdom. The climate around the shrine features lush landscapes that support diverse flora and fauna. Traditional structures for worship and meditation may be present at the shrine, though specific architectural details are not mentioned.

The shrine is recognized as a stop along the route to Dragonlands, as noted by Shiba Midori, who mentions that Asako Taishi has visited the Shrine of Durogin before crossing the river. This reinforces the shrine's importance as a point of interest for those engaged in scholarly pursuits and highlights Shiba Midori's connection with its spiritual heritage.
