Lion Camp’s Ancestor Shrine

Lion Camp’s Ancestor Shrine is a sacred space within the Lion Clan's encampment, serving as a focal point for spiritual connection and reverence to the clan's ancestors. The shrine is simple, adorned with offerings and symbols honoring the spirits of fallen warriors. It exists amid harsh conditions, where bitter cold and deep snow have affected troop morale. The camp faces recent hardships, including ambushes, tainted supplies, and dwindling food stocks, leaving many ashigaru exhausted.

After a fierce battle, Kitsu Yue retreats to the shrine for comfort, receiving reassurance from Ikoma Akuyaku, who offers spiritual support. Kitsu Somalia, another Lion Clan shugenja, communes with the ancestor spirits gathered at the shrine, seeking their guidance and understanding of their presence during the battle. The ancestors express delight in participating in the fight alongside their descendants, encouraging Somalia to teach Yue how to call upon them again. This interaction reinforces the connection between the living and the dead, embodying the Lion Clan's values of honor and duty. Somalia performs a ritual to draw the warmth of Tengoku, the Celestial Realm of Heaven, over the camp, helping to lift the spirits of the soldiers and reduce panic. The shrine serves as a critical site for spiritual healing and connection during a time of great strife for the Lion Clan.
