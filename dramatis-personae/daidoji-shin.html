Daidoji Shin

Crane

What is known

S29A Crane agent whose work is rogue shugenja. Hunting whoever roused the spirits that struck the Crane lands, with a possible Mantis connection. Warned the party off the Dragon District, pointed them at the Unicorn District for suppliers, and said he believes the real dealers are elsewhere. Owes Setsuna a written survey of the city's commerce. Flirts, and travels with a bodyguard who apologises for him.
S32His planned theatre was burned down. The fire spirits say a caged spirit was released to do it.
S41Watched the duel from the Unicorn docks with a gaijin spyglass and narrated it live to the castle's drawing room. Began composing a song about Karahaya's victory afterwards.
