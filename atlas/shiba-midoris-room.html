Shiba Midori’s Room

Phoenix

Shiba Midori’s room is located within a larger structure that accommodates various guests, including samurai and courtiers. The room features traditional Japanese architecture, with tatami mat flooring and sliding shoji doors. Midori conducts her duties as a healer here, treating ailments and injuries of those within the mansion.

During a chaotic incident, Shiba Midori responded to a scream from the Guest Wing, leaving her breakfast in the Daimyo’s Dining Hall. She assessed the condition of Doji Setsuna, who was suffering from an illness exacerbated by prior medicine. Midori conducted a thorough examination and determined that fatigue treatment might alleviate Setsuna's symptoms. During her examination, she discovered a scrap of dark blue fabric caught on a rafter nail, which was suited to vanish in deep shadow. This fabric was later passed to Kakita Kazumi for analysis.
