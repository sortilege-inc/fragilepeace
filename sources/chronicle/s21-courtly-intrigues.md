## Narrative

!lede The [[War College]] cleared [[Bayushi Monban]] outright. *At no point are you under any suspicion of wrongdoing* — everything he had done had been within his purview to do. He took the dismissal and kept the unease, and went to the [[Ikoma Hall of Scribes]] to find [[Soshi Aisha]].

She met him on the [[Path of Splinters]] with her fan up and denied everything in public, which was the polite half. The rest was a warning and a threat in the same breath. He had been *seen*, which was careless. He should not come to the meeting with a retinue, because people who break these arrangements come to harm. And she has his chop on an incriminating docket, and she will not be producing it, and she rather enjoyed telling him so.

That is the shape of the trap the party walked into when it started dealing with forgers. He went to buy a false document and discovered his own seal was already on somebody else's.

[[Doji Setsuna]] spent the day before [[Kitsuki Kaage]]'s arrival out on the plains under a small umbrella with a chair, riding and doing nothing, which was the most sensible thing anyone did that day.

Over tea afterwards, with [[Shiba Midori]] and [[Kakita Kazumi]], the case turned a corner. [[Shiba Midori]] — working with [[Kakita Kazumi]] and [[Kitsuko Ayoko]] — put up an idea that had not been on the table: **declare the mines an Imperial possession.** Neither clan gets them. The dispute stops being a dispute. And in the doing of it, the honour of [[Kitsu Yui]] and [[Ikoma Hideri]] can be restored, since an Imperial finding can say what a clan history will not.

[[Ikoma Ichigo]] backed the shape of it as a historian. Acknowledging that the Empire intervened at the [[Snow Plain]] — rather than declaring which clan won — would be a balm rather than a wound, and would not hand anyone a victory to build the next quarrel on. He also brought documents from a young [[Kitsu Family|Kitsu]] on the Lion's supply problems during the battle: early-season snow nobody expected, and shortages that look like more than weather. Third-party interference, he said, without saying whose.

[[Doji Setsuna]] began turning over something harder: calling [[Kitsu Yui]]'s spirit to give testimony directly.

Then she sat [[Bayushi Monban]] down. She has been carrying a worry about his conduct and she put it to him. He argued for Imperial control of the mines as the cleanest way to buy [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] non-intervention — *if the Emperor wanted the diamond mines, he could have them* — and she pushed back: you do not set aside a rightful owner because it is convenient, not without evidence, and evidence is the one thing this case keeps failing to produce honestly.

That evening a delivery reached [[Bayushi Monban]] — the documents he wanted, but not from the source he was expecting, with a note on top of them: *What is earned is owed, what is owed is paid.*

## Setsuna

**The imperial-possession proposal is the best thing anyone has put on this table, and it is [[Shiba Midori]]'s.** Look at what it does. Neither the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] nor the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] is declared the winner, so nobody carries a grievance out of the room. The Empire is acknowledged as the party that actually stopped the [[Snow Plain]] — which is *true*, and which [[Ikoma Ichigo]] will say out loud as a historian. The honour of [[Kitsu Yui]] and [[Ikoma Hideri]] gets restored by a finding rather than by an argument. And **it moots the forgery entirely**, because a ruling that gives the mines to the Emperor needs no proof of who was on the hill three hundred years ago.

She should push it, and she should get [[Ikoma Ichigo]] to say the historical part in front of [[Kitsuki Kaage]] before she says the legal part.

Her caution to [[Bayushi Monban]] was the right one and she should hold it: you do not set aside a rightful owner for convenience. But note that the Imperial route is not that. It is not a finding *against* an owner — it is a finding that the Empire, having stopped the battle, holds what the battle was fought below. That distinction is the whole argument and it needs to be made carefully.

**And the forgery has already turned on them.** [[Soshi Aisha]] holds a docket with [[Bayushi Monban]]'s chop on it, and told him so to his face while denying everything in public. Whoever is running her did not need the party's cooperation to implicate them; they only needed the party to start dealing. Her instruction that he come without a retinue is not courtesy. She has said plainly what happens to people who break these arrangements.

Two more things. Somebody sent [[Bayushi Monban]] the papers he wanted from a source he was not expecting, with *what is earned is owed, what is owed is paid* on top — a debt being opened, not a favour being done. And [[Ikoma Ichigo]]'s young [[Kitsu Family|Kitsu]] has supply documents pointing at third-party interference, which is the ledger evidence again, arriving unbidden from a Lion this time.
