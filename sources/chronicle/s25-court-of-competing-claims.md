## Narrative

!lede In the recess [[Shiba Midori]] presented her badge of office to [[Ikoma Ichigo]] in the [[Ikoma Hall of Scribes]] and required him, on the authority of the Emerald Magistracy, to come and examine documents. [[Kitsu Ayoko]] came with her. Two people watched them go: a Hida with military histories spread in front of him, in exactly the right place to be reading them — and a [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] in the Hall of Scribes who is, apparently, a humble historian. [[Shiba Midori]] marked the Hida for later and said nothing about either.

[[Ikoma Ichigo]] worked through the three documents in order. The [[Imperial-Sealed Official Treaty]] is a copy of an authentic instrument, signed and witnessed by [[Matsu Sakura]] and [[Shinjo Kamo]], carrying the Miya herald's signature for the Emperor, and entered in the Imperial Record. It is a valid confirmation of the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]'s claim to the [[Snow Plain]], and it was never what anyone was arguing about.

The two secret agreements are another matter, and his finding on them is the one that governs everything after. [[Bayushi Monban]]'s copy has the proper form of a land transfer and would suffice — except that it has never been entered or sealed by any member of the Imperial House. *All land transfers in Rokugan must be witnessed by a member of the Imperial House. This has no such seal upon it.* Which makes it, in his words, a worthless piece of paper until a member of that House signs off, and they have not.

The one from the ground is in a completely different hand, with terms suspiciously similar, and carries no signature, seal or mark of authentication at all — a draft, most likely, never formalised. It wills the mines to one [[Shosuro Amane]] *and or their descendants*, which is at least a person the record can identify, since Amane's own signature stands on the official treaty. [[Bayushi Monban]]'s wills them to *the least of the [[Bayushi Family|Bayushi]]*, who is nobody: a legal instrument must say to whom a thing is given, and a living person must be there to receive it.

Then the detail that will not go away. Both documents are old, and the buried one is in worse condition than the one that spent three centuries in a library — which is backwards. Looking at the ink, [[Ikoma Ichigo]] said it reads as though someone took an old piece of parchment and drafted something new upon it: the ink darker, fragmenting differently, younger than the paper under it. The other one's ink is as old as its paper. He would not go further than that. *I just do not feel confident in saying these two documents were written at the same time.*

Then [[Shiba Midori]] gave him the rest of the cache. Four large scrolls, gold-trimmed, with lion's-head scroll heads. He took them with trepidation, unrolled one, read a little, and began to cry. *Where did you find this?* — and when he was told: *Then he has my eternal gratitude, for this is my ancestor [[Ikoma Hideri]]'s record of the Battle of the Snow Plains. This has been lost for three hundred years. I knew it existed.* He pointed at the seal. *That is my ancestor's seal. That is her chop. I know it.* He asked leave to fetch comparison samples from the library, and [[Kitsuki Kaage]] — who had been watching the whole exchange from behind his fan, turning over something [[Doji Setsuna]] had written to him — let him go.

The jar stopper and the pottery went to him too. Sealed, he said, and it would have kept the moisture off. *It is as if someone was putting them away and knew they might not be accessed for a while.*

[[Ide Tsubame]] spent the recess with the [[Commandant of the Academy]], which is where the hidden ink had pointed her. What he had was an incident report. Soldiers on duty two nights earlier had seen [[Bayushi Monban]] in the library after hours, taking the [[Shinjukamu Letters]]. The library does not permit night work — candles and irreplaceable paper — and his men had standing orders not to interfere with Emerald Magistrates, so they wrote it down instead of stopping him. The letters were found missing the next morning. He returned them roughly two days later. And the Commandant's point was not the theft: *he could have walked into the library a few hours later and asked for the paperwork and been given it, but he chose to sneak in after hours instead.* He could not say what that implied about an Emerald Magistrate. He handed her the originals off his desk and offered her the librarian as a witness.

[[Bayushi Monban]] spent the recess in the [[Garden Of Virtuous Contemplation]], meditating, contemplating betrayal — the [[Dragon Clan|Dragon]] leaning and allowing certain things, and a [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] treated differently for doing them. He was disappointed in [[Shosuro Aishi]] for arriving with one piece of paper, and disappointed in the Soshi for having to be paid for her work. He steadied himself on the understanding that he probably has one more chance to get the mines for his clan.

Court resumed. [[Kitsuki Kaage]] explained why he was hearing new documents at all after ordering that there be none: the chain of provenance runs from the ground through the hands of two Emerald Magistrates, and only that. He offered both advocates leave to bring their own experts. Order of argument: [[Ide Tsubame]], then [[Shosuro Aishi]], then his magistrates. *Then I will decide if I am going to entertain this supernatural hokum that people are requesting — summoning ancestors, testimony of dead people, and so forth. The written testimony should be sufficient to decide this matter, but we will see.*

[[Ide Tsubame]] went first and went for the throat, politely. She laid the facts out as flat bullet points and let them do the accusing, apologising as she went — *I do not want to disgrace this court with something so scandalous, but the facts are forcing my hand, and I do not know how else to serve both my clan and this court's duty to the truth.* She had staked five honour to say it, that being [[Bayushi Monban]]'s status. The [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] advocate objected on relevance and she answered that it goes to the provenance of a document that ought to agree with its own other copies and does not.

It did not stick. Part of the reason is that the accusation came from the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]'s own advocate, who wants the mines; part of it is that she forgot to formally enter the library's list of [[Shinjo Kamo]] holdings alongside it. [[Kitsuki Kaage]]: *Advocate Tsubame, this is dangerously close to accusing one of my magistrates of a crime.* He took it under advisement, asked for more on provenance, and sent for the librarian.

[[Shosuro Aishi]] doubled down. The only reason the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] attack the treaty is that it is true. A draft does not disprove a final copy — one can draft a document without that draft being the thing. A different hand wrote it; so what. The signatures and the seals are there. It simply was never ratified by the appropriate authority, and she asked [[Kitsuki Kaage]] to ratify it now, from the bench.

He would not. *It is because your document's provenance is questionable that I am even entertaining this line of discussion. One of these two documents is either authentic, or neither of them is. But none of them are of a valid form of land transfer in and of themselves, for lacking the appropriate seal. You will have to do better than that, Advocate Aishi.*

[[Bayushi Monban]] had an answer to the seal, and it was a good one: the transfer of land did not occur until now, so it had no need of Imperial authorisation until now — which is what this hearing is for. He also noted, accurately, that every time matters move against the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] their advocate cries Scorpion treachery, and that he had been reluctant to bring the treaty forward in the first place. *Without proof and without evidence, the Unicorn's accusations are dishonourable at best.*

Then he entered [[Ikoma Akuyaku]]'s journals, with the confessions: poisoning the troops, relaying information and advice, procuring the lost histories and delivering them to the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]], killing the scouts and leaving them in the snow. [[Shosuro Aishi]] assisted, and it landed — [[Kitsuki Kaage]] acknowledged there is now significant evidence that the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] were given [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] help. And [[Bayushi Monban]] disposed of the sealed letter in one line: the [[Shosuro Family|Shosuro]] do not rule the Scorpion, the [[Bayushi Family|Bayushi]] do, and lands given to the Scorpion are given to the Scorpion Champion.

[[Doji Setsuna]] rose, bowed an apology to [[Kakita Kazumi]] for taking precedence, and unbuilt her own case. The precedents she brought the day before were accurate, she said — but not all precedents bear equal weight, and read strongly, hers would put every [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] holding in the Empire at risk, since the Unicorn's own claim went unpressed for centuries and was honoured on their return. Therefore the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] are owed the chance to press their claim, however belatedly, in good faith — so as not to undermine the very tenet the Unicorn hold their lands by.

[[Kitsuki Kaage]] looked at her as though she had begun speaking another language, and then took it apart. The Emperor confirmed the transfer. Unicorn ownership was never transferred at all; the land was held in keeping by the [[Lion Clan|Lion]]. By Imperial decree the Unicorn never abandoned their lands nor their claim, which the first Hantei guaranteed by his own will. *That was never in dispute. This dispute concerning when and how an unpressed claim is made bears no relevance. The cases are not similar.* And he looked at her oddly, because she was undermining the argument she had made the day before.

[[Ide Tsubame]] put the knife in the joint: pressing a claim requires that the claim be valid, which is the very thing this court is convened to determine. [[Kitsuki Kaage]] sustained the point. [[Bayushi Monban]] answered with [[Toshi Ranbo]] — the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] and the [[Crane Clan|Crane]] have contested that city for generations under more than one Emperor, and the throne has respected it, so there is no time limit on pressing a claim. [[Kitsuki Kaage]] narrowed it again and moved on: the Unicorn's title to their ancestral lands was never in question, only who holds this piece of it, and only because of what happened at the [[Snow Plain]].

Then [[Kakita Kazumi]], who has the status to say a thing like this without losing face, said it. He moved that both claims be denied and the mines pass into Imperial holding, and that [[Bayushi Monban]]'s evidence — a good document, he allowed — would be shown by careful study to be a forgery. *May I ask how you came to it?* Compare it, he said, against the notes he took from the library journals, and the differences will show a document written to put the whole battle in the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]]'s light rather than to record it. And then the careful part: *I am not saying that Monban himself wrote the forgery. I would never accuse a fellow Emerald Magistrate of such a base action.* Whoever supplied it may well have deceived him; forgeries are hard to read.

Then he turned his back to the bench and mouthed something at [[Bayushi Monban]] that the court did not hear.

It worked exactly as intended. [[Bayushi Monban]] paid an honour to interrupt and came apart in public — if not him, then who wrote it, and explain the chops, the rough draft, the letter, the history, *we were there*; and did [[Kakita Kazumi]]'s own ancestor not write journals defending [[Ikoma Akuyaku]] as loyal, when the journals now say he served the Scorpion until his death; and was this not the very document [[Kitsuki Kaage]] questioned them all about before the proceedings even opened, and would he still present it — *I understand, maybe in [[Crane Clan|Crane]] lands these things are acceptable.* [[Kakita Kazumi]] stood through all of it with his arms behind his back and a slight smirk, watching the outburst he had bought.

[[Doji Setsuna]] put a hand on [[Bayushi Monban]]'s shoulder to stop him and was ignored entirely. So she paid an honour of her own to interrupt and tried to get him out of the room — she had forgotten something in her quarters, and would send her yojimbo for it. He refused: they are in official proceedings, and she may send a servant. Then she used the authority of her office on the room instead, on everyone but the three highest present: *He may be a dumbass, but he is my yojimbo, and you will not speak ill of him. And he will leave right now.* [[Shiba Midori]], meanwhile, was working a fan at [[Bayushi Monban]] from across the chamber, entirely without words, to make it worse.

[[Kitsuki Kaage]] let [[Kakita Kazumi]] finish and refused his motion. *I warned you both about introducing these contradictory documents. You ignored my warnings, and now we have a further conflict of evidence. I am not convinced that this document has greater provenance than this one.* Bring something explicatory and he would rule. Until then he wanted the temperature in the room lowered.

The door slid open and [[Ikoma Ichigo]] came back in with an armful of scrolls, to an audible sigh from the bench. The chop matches. The writing style matches. The turn of phrase matches. The cache scrolls are [[Ikoma Hideri]]'s own history of the [[Snow Plain]] and he can prove it. *Whatever ancestor spirit led [[Akodo Akihito]] to find these documents, I am grateful to them, for they have cleared up the missing pieces in our history. A stain upon our family's honour is lifted.* [[Kitsuki Kaage]] said he was grateful to finally have something in the room whose provenance was clear.

[[Shiba Midori]] used it carefully. Because the histories are authentic and the draft treaty came out of the same jar, the draft is the better-founded of the two — and she pushed that toward the Imperial ruling rather than toward discrediting [[Bayushi Monban]], deliberately, to stay out of a fight between magistrates over forged evidence. It was the most productive argument anyone made all day, and [[Kitsuki Kaage]] praised Midori's soft-spoken but well-articulated points from the bench.

[[Kitsu Ayoko]] testified from the field reports and the quartermaster's records: disaster after disaster struck the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] army and the Lion were still in the field and still fighting when the Imperials arrived. Whatever the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] were doing, it did not work. [[Bayushi Monban]] turned it over on her: then why were the Lion not declared the winners — did what happened in that camp not delay the Lion victory long enough for the Imperials to arrive and hand it to the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]? He was trying to make her snap and did not manage it; neither of them carried the point. Her answer steadied [[Ide Tsubame]], who saw in it the thing that helps the Unicorn most: it explains why the agreement stayed a draft and was never ratified, because the Unicorn looked at the help they had been given and declined to pay for it.

The librarian arrived. He identified the list of [[Shinjo Kamo]] holdings as his own hand, his own seal, his own chop — a few letters, unsigned and undated but certainly written before the [[Snow Plain]], since they describe lands outside Rokugan from before the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] came home. Asked whether anything in it referenced a secret treaty, he was baffled: he had never seen such a document, and had it existed in his library it would plainly have been germane. He had the collection pulled and set aside in advance precisely so the Emerald Magistrates could ask for it. The empty space where it should have been is what made him raise the alarm.

[[Doji Setsuna]] paid an honour to interrupt and told the court that [[Bayushi Monban]] had been returning the documents on her behalf. She did not address the night — only that she had them that morning and he returned them when they were done with them. *I apologise, your honour. My yojimbo is a Scorpion. He does weird things when I ask sometimes.* Her own aides would back her, because they will say what she tells them to. It worked. [[Ide Tsubame]] tried to catch it, and failed, and knew she had failed. Asked directly what she had wanted the letters for, [[Doji Setsuna]] said that she and [[Bayushi Monban]] had disagreed over how to read the evidence and the letters were meant to bring them to a shared view, and had not.

So [[Ide Tsubame]] made the argument anyway, in the only form left to her. Nothing in the [[Lion Clan|Lion]]'s records mentions this treaty. It does not appear to exist until [[Bayushi Monban]] makes off with the whole collection of [[Shinjo Kamo]]'s letters in the night and then produces it himself with no corroborating copy anywhere. And those letters are exactly what a person would need to forge a convincing document in [[Shinjo Kamo]]'s hand.

[[Bayushi Monban]] dismantled the witness rather than the argument. Has the librarian read every scroll in that library — could someone hide a document there without his knowing? *I suppose it is possible.* Did he personally witness anything? *No.* Then the testimony is hearsay: he came to work, papers were missing, and shortly afterward they all came back. The librarian, pressed, confirmed the rest of it anyway — the letters came back two days later, the whole collection, and nothing new appeared with them; nobody sneaks past the night guards easily; and [[Bayushi Monban]] could have asked for the collection at any hour of any working day.

[[Ide Tsubame]] could not close it. [[Kitsuki Kaage]] ruled what the evidence permitted and no further. Whether [[Bayushi Monban]] did or did not take documents from a library at an odd hour has no bearing on whether the documents he presented are valid, and he would sustain no more argument on the treaty. *It is highly likely that one of them is a forgery. However, the evidence chain is not sufficient to prove any one at this time.* He would hear anything that might elucidate it later. For now, the testamentary phase.

Nothing was thrown out. Nothing was proved. It comes down to a third-party authenticator.

## Learned

- Ikoma Ichigo: Authenticated the Imperial-Sealed Official Treaty and confirmed it validates the Unicorn claim. Found Monban's secret treaty properly formed but never sealed or entered by the Imperial House, which by his account leaves it worthless until it is. Observed that one of the two secret documents appears to be new ink on old parchment, and would not say the two were written at the same time. Wept over Ikoma Hideri's recovered history, identified her chop on sight, and returned with library samples that proved the match beyond doubt.
- Imperial-Sealed Official Treaty: Signed and witnessed by Matsu Sakura and Shinjo Kamo, carrying the Miya herald's signature for the Emperor and entered in the Imperial Record. Genuine, and never actually in dispute.
- Ikoma Hideri: Her own record of the Battle of the Snow Plain was in the jar — chop, hand and phrasing all matched against library samples. Ikoma Ichigo intends to compose it into a form that can enter the Ikoma histories, which he says lifts a stain from his family's honour.
- Commandant of the Academy: Held an incident report placing Bayushi Monban in the library after hours, two nights before, taking the Shinjukamu Letters. Gave Ide Tsubame the originals and offered her the librarian as a witness. His men had standing orders not to interfere with Emerald Magistrates.
- Shinjukamu Letters: Taken from the library in the night and returned about two days later — the entire collection, with nothing new among them. They are travel correspondence from before the Unicorn returned to Rokugan, and they are also exactly what a forger would need to copy Shinjo Kamo's hand.
- Ide Tsubame: Staked five honour to accuse an Emerald Magistrate and could not make it stick, twice. Tried and failed to catch Setsuna's account as a lie. Established through the librarian that no secret treaty has ever been in the Lion's records.
- Kitsuki Kaage: Ruled that at most one of the two secret agreements is authentic and possibly neither, and that neither is a valid land transfer for want of an Imperial seal. Refused to ratify Monban's treaty from the bench, refused Kazumi's motion to throw it out, and held that how Monban obtained library documents has no bearing on whether his documents are valid. Sustained Tsubame's point that a claim must be valid before it can be pressed.
- Kakita Kazumi: Moved that both claims be denied and the mines pass to the Empire, on the ground that neither clan can be trusted. Called Monban's evidence a forgery in open court while explicitly declining to call Monban the forger, and offered his own library notes for comparison. Then mouthed something at Monban, unheard, that provoked exactly the outburst he wanted.
- Bayushi Monban: Answered the missing seal by arguing no Imperial authorisation was needed until the transfer itself, which is now. Answered the sealed letter by observing the Shosuro do not rule the Scorpion. Reduced the librarian to admitting he witnessed nothing. Spent the recess meditating in the Garden Of Virtuous Contemplation, disappointed in Aishi for bringing one piece of paper and in the Soshi forger for expecting to be paid.
- Kitsu Ayoko: Testified from field reports and quartermaster records that the Lion army absorbed disaster after disaster and was still fighting when the Imperials arrived, so the sabotage did not decide the battle.
- Shosuro Aishi: Argued that a draft does not disprove a final copy and asked Kaage to ratify the unratified treaty from the bench. He declined.

## Setsuna

**She lied to an Imperial court to give her yojimbo an alibi, and it worked.** The librarian had just finished establishing that [[Bayushi Monban]] went into a closed library at night, past guards, for the one collection in Rokugan that would let a forger copy [[Shinjo Kamo]]'s hand — and that the collection had been sitting in the open, available for the asking, any working hour of any day. [[Doji Setsuna]] paid an honour, stood up, and said he had been fetching them for her. [[Ide Tsubame]] reached for it and came up empty.

The lie is not the dangerous part. The dangerous part is that it is *checkable*. Disproving it needs only one thing: evidence about whether those letters were ever in [[Doji Setsuna]]'s hands and what was done with them. [[Ide Tsubame]] was told in as many words that this would take a more thorough investigation than she has time for. She will not have this little time forever, and she now has a reason to look.

And it bought nothing. [[Kitsuki Kaage]] ruled, minutes later, that how [[Bayushi Monban]] came by library documents has no bearing on whether his documents are valid. The alibi was unnecessary to the outcome and it is the one act of the day that can end her career.

**Everything honest went well, which is what makes the rest so stupid.** [[Ikoma Ichigo]] authenticated [[Ikoma Hideri]]'s history against library samples and lifted three centuries of shame off his family doing it. [[Shiba Midori]] turned that into the day's most productive argument and did it while deliberately refusing to join a knife-fight between magistrates. [[Kitsu Ayoko]] showed the sabotage did not decide the battle. And [[Ikoma Ichigo]]'s finding on the seal is the strongest thing anyone holds: *no* secret agreement in this case is a valid transfer of land, because no member of the Imperial House ever sealed one. That is not a finding about forgery. It is a finding that both instruments are void on their face, and it is the shortest road to the ruling she says she wants.

**Then she stood up and argued against her own case.** The [[Shosuro Aishi]] bargain came due today, and paying it meant telling [[Kitsuki Kaage]] that the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] are owed their chance to press. He did not merely reject it. He explained that the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] never abandoned anything, that the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] held the land in keeping, that the first Hantei guaranteed the claim by his own will — and then he looked at her, and noted aloud that she was undermining what she had argued the day before. She kept nothing from the roll. A senior Kitsuki has now watched her reverse herself in public for no reason he can see, which means he will start looking for the reason.

[[Kakita Kazumi]] is the other thing to watch, and the reading has changed. He denounced the forgery he helped commission, structured the denunciation to leave [[Bayushi Monban]] technically innocent, offered his own notes as the comparison standard, and then mouthed something across the chamber that nobody heard, and [[Bayushi Monban]] detonated on cue. That is not a man cutting a forgery loose to save the party. That is a man running a discredit against a colleague with a clean public record and a private channel. What he said is not known. It should be found out.

One small thing worth keeping. [[Bayushi Monban]] spent the recess resenting the Soshi forger for having to be paid for her work — so [[Soshi Yamako]] was not only [[Kakita Kazumi]]'s purchase. Both of them bought from the same forger, separately, and neither knows what the other commissioned. That is why there are two documents, why the terms are suspiciously similar, and why neither of them can afford for the other to be caught.
