Warehouse District

The Warehouse District is a bustling area within the City Of The Rich Frog, characterized by numerous structures used for storage and shipping activities. It is located near the The Docks, which serve as a key smuggling route for illicit goods, including small occult items and questionable substances such as Opium. The climate is humid and warm, typical of a port city, and the area has a mix of lively and shadowy environments, particularly at night when The Docks are less illuminated.

Various inhabitants populate the district, including ronin, local enforcers, and smugglers, who navigate the complexities of trade and law enforcement. The Docks are recognized as a prime location for illicit activities, and the presence of a larger Mantis ship has been noted in connection with smuggling operations. The area is fraught with potential threats, reflecting the ongoing tensions and dangers associated with the trade and enforcement landscape within the Warehouse District.
