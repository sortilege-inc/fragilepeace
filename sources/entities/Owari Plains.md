Ground in the north. [[Moto Gaheris]] holds that the recent
[[Crane Clan|Crane]]–[[Lion Clan|Lion]] treaty cut it off from the reach of the
[[Unicorn Clan]]'s allies, and counts that among his reasons for suspecting the Crane of
arranging this war. Nobody has answered the charge; [[Kakita Kazumi]] turned it aside
rather than deny it.
