Teahouse With No Name

The Teahouse With No Name is a notable establishment in the Lion district of the City Of The Rich Frog, overlooking the Drowned Merchant River. Its original name has been forgotten due to frequent changes in ownership, and it is now commonly referred to simply as "the teahouse." The teahouse is distinguished by its superb utensils, including an antique tea set believed to be centuries old, which reflects the local reverence for vessels associated with powerful spirits. The exceptional hospitality of its Tea Mistress, Ikoma Kotone, who has an outstanding memory for her patrons, enhances the experience.

The City Of The Rich Frog serves as an essential trade hub among the Unicorn, Dragon, and Lion clans, facilitated by a canal system that enhances the movement of goods. The establishment attracts a variety of patrons, including a young man in Dragon colors, two young women likely related to Ide Tsubame, and a pair of Lion samurai. Conversations often reveal information about local events and notable figures, such as the author Hana Noame, indicating ongoing intrigues in the city. The teahouse serves as a social hub where various factions gather for both casual interactions and serious discussions regarding political intrigue.

The atmosphere is charged with the presence of multiple investigating pairs from different clans, contributing to an environment ripe for gathering information, particularly on a controversial author and illicit Unicorn spirit magic charms. Local bodyguard Kaeru Akiara advises caution against venturing to The Docks after dark due to ongoing criminal activities. Patrons engage in discussions about the Lion's interest in persuading the author to change a scandalous narrative, while also exploring leads on black market dealings of sacred relics. The Tea Mistress emphasizes the importance of proper etiquette during the tea ceremony, setting the tone for the group’s departure. The teahouse plays a critical role in facilitating investigations and navigating the complex political landscape of the region.

Recently, the Teahouse With No Name has also become a venue for significant gatherings, where notable figures such as Shiba Midori, Kakita Kazumi, and Miya Misato convene for tea ceremonies. The atmosphere emphasizes formality and tradition, allowing for discussions of important matters, including imperial missions and diplomatic negotiations. Shiba Midori recently hosted Miya Misato, who was tasked with delivering a peace demand from The Emperor to the Lion and Unicorn clans. This gathering included urgent discussions about the absence of Bayushi Monban and the necessity of Miya Misato's mission, with attendees expressing their concerns and intentions regarding the ongoing conflict, and plans to depart for the front the following morning. The teahouse continues to play a crucial role as a central meeting point for influential individuals navigating the complexities of war and diplomacy.
