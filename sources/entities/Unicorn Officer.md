The mounted [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] officer who rode out at the [[Snow Plain]] on the
first day of the battle, danced on his own saddle with a scimitar turning in his hand, and
called for the cowardly dog commanding the Lion left to come out and face his doom. He
meant [[Matsu Morozane]]. [[Shiguro Chinmoku]] went for the horse and put him on the
ground, and the clash that followed ran one round and stopped unfinished — two fatigue
paid, four taken, both scimitars still up.

The Lion never learned who he was. Making the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] name their
commanders was the whole strategic objective of that day's attack and the objective went
unmet: the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] finished the day knowing where the enemy leaders stood and
nothing else about them. The export files this entry under General [[Shinjo Kamo]], which
is the identification a later account supplies and the field did not.
