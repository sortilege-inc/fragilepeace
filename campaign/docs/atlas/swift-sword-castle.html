Swift Sword Castle

Swift Sword Castle is a stronghold in the territory of the Lion Clan, serving as the court for the Lion daimyo, Kitsu Takeko. It features traditional architecture with stone walls and well-maintained gardens. The castle is central to governance and legal matters, hosting court sessions that address various issues such as custody requests and diplomatic relations. The surrounding climate is temperate, allowing for year-round accessibility.

The castle's court plays a significant role in clan dynamics, highlighted by recent events where Kitsu Takeko formalized the arrangement for Shiba Midori to have Kitsu Ayoko as her assistant. A tea ceremony led by Shiba Midori was held to ease tensions among attendees. Kitsu Takeko was noted to experience distress upon receiving a letter with a drawing of a Mahosukai.

During the proceedings, Bayushi Monban reported the theft of the Sword of Hiruma, an important ancestral blade of the Crab Clan. A messenger later delivered a package from Shinjō Harunobu to Doji Setsuna, further intertwining personal and political affairs. Swift Sword Castle continues to function as a hub for legal and diplomatic activities, reflecting the complexities of clan relations and the ongoing challenges faced by its inhabitants.
