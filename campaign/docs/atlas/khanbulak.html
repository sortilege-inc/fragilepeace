Khanbulak

The fortified trade city at the western end of the Empire's road, and the gate to the Burning Sands. Rokugani doors are set into al-Qamari columns and tiling, which is what a border looks like when it has been a market for long enough. A Moto governor holds the White Tower. The gates shut at dark and the wall is climbable from either side, as Bayushi Monban established twice in one night.

The Archivist export was taken before this entry existed. What the page holds is drawn from the sessions themselves.
