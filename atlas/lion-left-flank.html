Lion Left Flank

The Lion Left Flank is a strategic position in a snowy battlefield characterized by heavy snowfall and rugged mountainous features. This location serves as a critical point for the Lion Clan, who have planned a flanking maneuver through the mountains to engage the Unicorn Clan from the rear. The terrain includes Old Diamond Mines, frozen rivers, and forested slopes that complicate navigation and troop deployment. The harsh climate poses risks of frostbite to the soldiers.

The Lion Clan's general has authorized the deployment of mountain-born Ashigaru archers and spearmen to this flank, while a small cavalry reserve remains off the mountain approach. The engagement begins with Lion Clan forces moving through the challenging terrain, encountering obstacles and enemy units. Archers from the left flank provide covering fire toward a fortified hill held by the Unicorn Clan. The left flank becomes a focal point of combat as units execute assaults and engage in skirmishes with counter-flanking forces from the Unicorn Clan. The dynamic between the Lion Clan forces and their adversaries underscores the tactical significance of this location in the broader context of the battle.
