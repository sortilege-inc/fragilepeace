Shinjo Harunobu

Unicorn

What is known

S1His lord escaped the field north of the Aelu River alive.
S2Her husband, taken prisoner at the Battle of the Hanto River, north of the Drowned Merchant River, and alive in custody. Nobody sent word to her; she heard it in passing at a Lion castle. An Ikoma bard says a letter can reach him from the Castle of the Swift Sword.
S4Held by the Lion. A courtier of the castle judged him safest in their custody until the fighting stops, and would not write for his release before then.
S24Being treated honourably as a prisoner of war, per two letters Monban obtained. Setsuna asked him to stop interfering and he agreed.
S27Sent her a gift, wrapped in purple, by a Unicorn messenger. No answer has come on the custody request.
S48Alive, well, and at the Castle of the Swift Sword. Fought valiantly, did not fall, was recovered and treated, and was released under safe conduct two weeks ago at the Lady Kitsu's request.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
