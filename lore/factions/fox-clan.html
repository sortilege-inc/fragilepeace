Fox Clan

The Fox Clan is a minor clan in Rokugan, recognized for its connections to the spirit world and its expertise in meishodo, a form of magic involving spirits. Clan members often act as intermediaries between the physical and spiritual realms, engaging in practices that may evoke suspicion from other clans. The Fox Clan is known for trading magical goods, which can lead to conflicts with more traditional clans. Their connection to the occult and the binding of spirits to objects is seen as blasphemous by some, further complicating their relationships with other factions.

Recently, the clan was linked to a talisman containing a wild spirit, sold to a buyer using the pen name "Hanano Ame." This transaction was facilitated by a shopkeeper who obtained the item from an unnamed Fox Clan Samurai. The shopkeeper provided the buyer with the seller's address, highlighting the clan's active involvement in the trade of spiritual artifacts. This activity has attracted the attention of the Emerald Magistrates, who are investigating illicit magical items in the City Of The Rich Frog. The Emerald Magistrates have identified The Docks as a likely route for smuggling small occult items and have devised a cover story to approach potential buyers, with members acting in disguises.

Their investigation has led them to a straw-hatted ronin who warned them of dangers in the area and revealed that a ship in the harbor was connected to the smuggling operation, citing a source named Akiara. Further inquiries led them to the Ronin Headquarters, where they discovered an ongoing surveillance operation targeting a smuggling ring involving questionable substances such as Opium. The ronin leader, Kaeru Haia, expressed concerns about the interference of the Emerald Magistrates in their long-term stakeout. Despite initial tensions, cooperation was agreed upon, with plans to return to The Docks at dawn to potentially catch the smugglers in the act. The Fox Clan's influence regarding smuggling and occult practices remains a critical focus for the Emerald Magistrates as they continue their investigation, raising concerns about the potential impact on the spiritual balance in the region and contributing to the political tensions surrounding magic in Rokugan.
