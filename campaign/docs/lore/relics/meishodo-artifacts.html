Meishodo Artifacts

Meishodo artifacts are items of significant interest due to their presence in the Dragon lands. These artifacts are associated with spiritual magic and have drawn the attention of various investigators because of their controversial nature. A Dragon clan magistrate, the son of Katsuki Wataru, is actively looking into the unauthorized appearances of these artifacts.

The artifacts are connected to Unicorn spirit magic charms and talismans, which are rumored to be sold on the black market. Their presence has caused concern among local authorities, particularly the Dragon clan, who seek to halt the trade of what they deem dangerous magical items. This situation has attracted the attention of other parties, including Unicorn representatives, who are aware of the spiritual significance of the artifacts and the potential repercussions of trafficking them. The Meishodo artifacts act as a focal point for intrigue and investigation, influencing the political landscape of the City of the Rich Frog.
