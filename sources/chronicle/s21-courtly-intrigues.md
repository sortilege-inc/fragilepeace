## Narrative

!lede The sensei of the [[War College]] let [[Bayushi Monban]] go. He had been summoned over the improper use of the facilities at an improper hour, and the old man gave him a line he was not expecting. *At no point are you under any suspicion of wrongdoing, because as I observe, everything you did was within your purview to do at the appropriate times.* Then a slight inclination of the head, and dismissal. [[Bayushi Monban]] walked straight out and went to find the Soshi, because he was fairly sure she had put him there.

She was in the [[Ikoma Hall of Scribes]], where she always is at that hour. She raised her fan and suggested a stroll on the [[Path of Splinters]], which is where the students go to reflect and where there were still enough people about to keep it public. *My lord Monban. We have too many of these rendezvous. People will suspect you are courting me.*

He asked for his paperwork back and got a performance. *I am sure I have no idea what you are talking about* — fan up, eyes moving over everyone in earshot — *I am so sorry that you were seen. I am concerned for your skills, that you were seen.*

Then the rest of it, which was not a performance. If he were thinking of visiting her quarters later with a quartet of Akodo guards and his fellow magistrates and intentions to do something stupid, he should know that anyone who double-crosses her hangs at the traitor's grove long before she does. *You forget. I have your chop on a very incriminating docket, and I have your word as a Bayushi to back it up.* She would not be producing it herself. *Why do you think I am not the one producing it? You are the one who is going to present it, and you are going to stake yourself on by what providence you came by this miraculous record.*

*Do not pick fights where you do not need to. Do not make deals without backup. I will deliver you what you need. It is up to you to make good use of it.*

That is the shape of it. He went looking for a forger and found out he had already been forged, by a woman who will hand him the document and let him carry every consequence of using it.

[[Doji Setsuna]] spent the last free day out on the plains with an umbrella, a chair, an attendant and a tethered horse, reading a pillow book, which she put away rather quickly when company arrived.

The company was [[Shiba Midori]], with tea, and news. [[Bayushi Monban]] had been called in by the sensei over documents, something to do with [[Shinjo Kamo]], so it touched the case. And she and [[Kakita Kazumi]] had spent a long conversation in a courtyard with [[Kitsu Ayoko]] and come out of it with two things.

The first was restoring the honour of [[Kitsu Yue]] — and, once somebody said it out loud, of [[Ikoma Hideri]] as well.

The second was [[Kakita Kazumi]]'s, and it was the better idea. Declare the mines an Imperial possession. Neither clan has a strong suit; hand them to the higher authority and the quarrel has nothing left to be about. [[Doji Setsuna]] said she was intrigued, and then said she was in favour.

She had one of her own, worked out with [[Kitsu Ayoko]]: summon [[Kitsu Yue]]'s spirit and have her attest to or repudiate an account she may have made after the battle. [[Shiba Midori]] advised keeping that in reserve, for use only if the documents are challenged. [[Doji Setsuna]] put it further back than that — a plan F they should not need.

[[Ikoma Ichigo]], asked whether any of it would matter to a Lion, gave the historian's answer and then the useful one. It is a distinction that is not without a difference, and it would be something of a balm — *not the same as if one could conjure my ancestor's account of the battle to prove what did or did not happen*, but something. Because the treaty is right there, and the treaty is silent. The Emperor awarded the [[Snow Plain]] to the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]], and the income from it to the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] for a term, and said nothing whatever about who had won. It acknowledged only that there was a dispute.

He also had a raft of documents, gathered for him by a young [[Kitsu Family|Kitsu]] — the Lion's supply failures during the battle, unforeseen early-season snow, [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] traps that made the field impossible, and incidents that raise the question of interference from a third party. *Not enough to actually strongly suggest anything. It is possible there was a third party intervening in this battle in some way or another. There is just no evidence to prove who, or how.* And the thing that sits under all of it: *there is a glaring hole in our histories where there should have been a document, and I know my ancestor would have done better than to leave an empty gap.*

[[Doji Setsuna]] asked whether he would testify. He would, and set his own limits on it. *I am happy to testify to what is. I do not want to speculate on what is not. I am a historian. I deal in truths. I can speak to what the documents attest to; I will not speculate on what they do not have in them.* She wants [[Kitsu Ayoko]] on the stand beside him, because [[Kitsu Ayoko]] was the one assigned — with a team of researchers, as soon as the castle knew the magistrates were coming — to pull the documents for them, and [[Ikoma Ichigo]] calls her diligent and thorough.

In the evening [[Doji Setsuna]] called [[Bayushi Monban]] in on a procedural pretext and asked. *I hear you had a visitor today.*

*Nothing to concern yourself with. A misunderstanding.*

*How you comport yourself is of the utmost concern to me. Is this something where I need to intervene on your behalf?* — No. Everything is fine. — *I believe you. Should it become the case that what you told me was incorrect, it will make things harder rather than easier.* And she left it there, having been told the same thing he told the Lion.

Then they fought about the judgement, and it was the real business of the day.

She laid out the Imperial route: no solid documentation, eyewitness testimony that cannot be produced, the Emperor's own intervention, and three magistrates of four in agreement. [[Bayushi Monban]] said he was a no.

*You are treating this as a case of rightful ownership. It is not. It is a case of what is best for the Empire. It is a case of balance.*

*No, it is not. It is a case of rightful ownership. The Scorpion held the Unicorn. The Unicorn got what they wanted. Now the Unicorn have to pay for the Scorpion's assistance. What is earned is owed. What is owed is paid.*

And he put the Imperial route itself in doubt. *If the Emperor wanted the diamond mines, he could have just taken them. If there were no merit to the claim, and this were causing problems like [[Toshi Ranbo]] or the Battle of the [[Snow Plain]], the Emperor would say he was tired of listening to it and take them.* Nobody has. Dressing the award as the Empire's will is therefore a thing they are doing, not a thing the Empire has asked for.

He told her there would be recompense for awarding away a legal claim, and backlash, and no discussion afterward — *you have taken what they believe is theirs and given it to someone else. It has been three hundred years. It will be a few years more.* She answered that she was seeking the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]]'s non-intervention rather than their backing, and that a decision like this would make them understand how serious she is. He told her clan prejudice was getting in the way of a rightful judgement, and that a woman treated as well by the Scorpion as she has been might at least wait to see what they bring.

*We were there. I know the Scorpion are responsible for the Unicorn winning that battle. If it were not for certain party members, the Lion would have wiped them out before the Imperials even showed up.*

She ended it. *I believe we are repeating ourselves. I am aware of your position. I do not forbid you anything* — and she would not prevent him offering a dissent — *I am grateful and appreciative of you serving your role and protecting my life, just as I do my best and utmost to protect your honour. After all, it reflects upon me.* Dismissed, courteously.

That night the delivery reached [[Bayushi Monban]]. All of it, with every ancillary document, carried by hands he was not expecting. And on the bottom of it, a note in his own words.

*What is earned is owed, what is owed is paid. Your words. Remember them well. Earn that piece of paper.*

## Setsuna

**The Imperial-possession proposal is the best thing anyone has put on this table, and it is [[Kakita Kazumi]]'s.** Neither the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] nor the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] is declared the winner, so nobody carries a grievance out of the room. The Empire is acknowledged as the party that actually stopped the [[Snow Plain]] — which is true, and which [[Ikoma Ichigo]] will say out loud as a historian. The honour of [[Kitsu Yue]] and [[Ikoma Hideri]] gets restored by a finding rather than an argument. And it moots the forgery entirely, because a ruling that gives the mines to the Emperor needs no proof of who stood on that hill three hundred years ago.

She should get [[Ikoma Ichigo]] to say the historical part in front of [[Kitsuki Kaage]] before she says the legal part, and she should have [[Kitsu Ayoko]] beside him.

But [[Bayushi Monban]]'s objection is not nothing and she should write it down properly rather than win it and forget it. His case is that this is a matter of rightful ownership: a bargain was struck, one party performed, the other owes. Hers is that it is a matter of what serves the Empire. He is not wrong that awarding away a claim that may be good makes an enemy, and he is not wrong that she cannot see what the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] are about to put on the table. *It has been three hundred years. It will be a few years more* is the most dangerous sentence anyone said to her today.

And he lied to her face about the summons and she let him. She asked, she was told it was a misunderstanding, and she chose to accept it with a warning attached. That was a decision, not an oversight. [[Soshi Aisha]] holds a docket with his chop on it and intends him to present a document she will not stand behind. Whoever is running her never needed the party's cooperation to implicate them — only for the party to start dealing.

The thing she should worry about most is the thing she cannot see. She put terms to the Scorpion over tea: non-intervention in the [[Crane Clan|Crane]]–[[Lion Clan|Lion]] negotiation, in exchange for her support. She came away believing she had no answer and has been building a ruling on the assumption that she still needs one. [[Bayushi Monban]] believes the answer was given. If he is right, she has spent the last two days negotiating against a door that was already open, and the reason she missed it is that the Scorpion who answered her is subtle enough never to have been caught at anything.

She should find out. Before [[Kitsuki Kaage]] arrives, and before she rules in a way that takes from the Scorpion something they may already have paid her for.
