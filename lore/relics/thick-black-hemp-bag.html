Thick Black Hemp Bag

The Thick Black Hemp Bag is a substantial bag located in the ceiling space of the Crane couple's guest quarters. It is coated in a mild tincture of Firebiter poison, a forbidden contact poison known for its bitter odor and yellowish tint.

Kakita Kazumi discovered the bag while investigating the Crane quarters, using a wakizashi's sheath to avoid direct contact with the poison. Examination revealed that the bag's coating matched the symptoms experienced by Shiba Midori, who had suffered from poison exposure. The bag was placed into a thick leather carry bag for safety. During a public address, Kakita Kazumi revealed the bag's existence and its dangerous contents, linking it to the ongoing investigation into recent nocturnal intrusions and a poisoning incident.
