House Of Pleasure

The House Of Pleasure is a vibrant establishment known for its lively atmosphere, where patrons engage in games and revelry. Guests are attended by a trio of triplets, each embodying beauty and grace, serving sake and facilitating entertainment. The air is filled with laughter and the clinking of dice, as samurai and other visitors partake in gambling and socializing. The decor is ornate, reflecting the opulence associated with such venues, creating an inviting yet discreet environment for clandestine meetings.

Bayushi Monban, a Scorpion samurai, arranged a rendezvous at the House Of Pleasure, where he met with a Scorpion Cousin at a gambling table. They secured a private room using hand-signs in Cadence to discuss matters away from prying eyes. The cousin, acting as an informant, warned Monban about the dangers of family intrigue and offered information about an apothecary across the water that could provide noxious supplies. Monban expressed a need for a quick method to poison a target, negotiating terms for assistance in exchange for future favors.

After Monban's night of indulgence, he awoke in the House Of Pleasure, hungover and disoriented, before departing for The Governor's mansion. Kakita Kazumi, a Crane shinobi, planned to search Monban's quarters during his absence, suspecting him of involvement in a recent theft.
