Curio Shop

The Curio Shop is a small, cluttered establishment located off the wharf in the City Of The Rich Frog. Thick, dark drapes cover the windows, allowing only a narrow sliver of light to filter through. The shop is filled with an eclectic assortment of antiques and curiosities, including Rokugani artifacts and Gaijin goods, reflecting a blend of cultural influences.

The elderly proprietor, distinguished by his long white beard, greets visitors warmly, creating a welcoming atmosphere despite the shop's shadowy nature. Inside, the shop is packed with strange antiques, including a prominently displayed older tea set. Doji Setsuna and Shiba Midori inquire about items that could ease the spiritual burdens of Shinjō Harunobu. The Shopkeeper, cautious in his response, shows steppe-themed curios while avoiding overtly spiritual items.

A silver necklace with an unusual locket design is offered, and the group negotiates for information regarding a Fox Clan seller linked to a talisman containing a wild spirit. The Shopkeeper provides receipts and hints at local police activity outside, revealing a tailing figure as a local officer. The group secures the necessary paperwork and a decorative saddle tassel at an inflated price before exiting the shop, which the proprietor promptly shutters behind them.
