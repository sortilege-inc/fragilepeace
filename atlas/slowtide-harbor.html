Slowtide Harbor

Slowtide Harbor is a coastal navigable river port known for its organized structure and diverse trade, strategically positioned on the border of three clan territories: the Dragon control the north bank, the Lion control the south bank, and the Unicorn control the west bank. The harbor is equipped with docks and moorings for a variety of vessels, including a notably large blue-water ship that is unsuitable for river travel. The climate is typically mild, supporting year-round maritime activities, making it a hub of commerce and intrigue.

At the heart of Slowtide Harbor lies the Miya Governor’s Palace, where Governor Miya Amaya resides, situated on a Central Island within the harbor. The area is a politically sensitive zone, especially given the presence of the Emperor's representatives. Various factions, including the Lion, Dragon, and Unicorn, pursue their own interests, creating a competitive atmosphere fueled by different motives. The harbor is home to multiple investigators, highlighting the complexities of local politics and smuggling, as well as the urgent nature of ongoing investigations.

Recently, local head lawman Kaeru Haya escorted Emerald Magistrates to The Docks based on suspicions of imminent smuggling activities. The situation escalated when Shiba Midori, a Phoenix Emerald Magistrate, encountered two assailants in a dark alley near The Docks, leading to a violent confrontation. The investigation revealed a significant smuggling operation, with Kaeru Haya reporting rumors of foreign relics sought by smugglers, including Mantis interests. During this investigation, the Emerald Magistrates discovered a smaller fishing skiff tied to the dock, where Midori was later found incapacitated after an attack.

The search for missing magistrates intensified, revealing fewer boats than expected, necessitating further action from the magistrates. Tensions rose as the large blue-water ship prepared to depart the harbor, leading to a confrontation between Bayushi Monban and Captain Kubota regarding the ship's activities and a ransom situation involving a kidnapped girl. Reports of disappearances and criminal activity suggest that Slowtide Harbor is a focal point for both legitimate and illicit dealings, further complicating the already intricate web of intrigue that defines this vital location.

Slowtide Harbor is a coastal location in Crane lands, southeast of Otosan Uchi. It is a key maritime point for trade and travel, characterized by tranquil waters and scenic vistas. The harbor is known for its bustling merchant activities and connections to various clans, particularly the Scorpion. Boats frequently navigate the calm sea, and the climate is generally mild, making it a pleasant destination. The area is vibrant with commerce and social interactions, largely unaffected by ongoing regional conflicts.

Historically, Slowtide Harbor has been the site of significant events, including encounters with magical entities. Recently, local leaders expressed concern over a Mahosukai that escaped from the harbor. Kitsu Takeko, a Lion daimyo, reacted strongly to an image of this Mahosukai during a tea ceremony, highlighting the harbor's relevance to current political dynamics. The harbor facilitates communication and movement between factions, exemplified by a recent interaction involving a young Scorpion samurai in a cat mask seeking directions to the Teahouse With No Name. This underscores Slowtide Harbor's significance as a hub for information exchange and its role as a strategic location for intelligence and covert operations. The harbor's reputation for harboring mysterious and potentially dangerous individuals adds to its significance in ongoing conflicts and investigations in the region.
