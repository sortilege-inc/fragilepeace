Doji Setsuna, a distinguished courtier of the Crane Clan and newly appointed Emerald Magistrate, embarks on a critical mission assigned by Kitsu Kaage to mediate a dispute between the Scorpion Clan and the Unicorn Clan at the Castle of the Swift Sword. A member of the Doji Family, her noble lineage provides her with protection and a significant political advantage in the intricate landscape of Rokugan. Known for her elegant demeanor and strategic acumen, Setsuna wears traditional samurai gear, including a katana, reflecting the grace expected of her heritage. Accompanied by her yojimbo, Bayushi Monban, known for the red scarf that conceals the lower part of his face, and her quiet, middle-aged lady’s maid, Ishika, Setsuna travels with a retinue that includes porters Ryu and So, a knowledgeable scribe named Otoya, a horse attendant named Jiro, and her striking white courser, Kogarashi, distinguished by its silver mane and blue-gray eyes. As they journey, Setsuna receives a letter from her Scorpion Clan mentor, which not only assigns Monban to her service but also prohibits him from the rite of seppuku. During their travels, a stranger approaches her, addressing her by name and delivering packages bearing the crests of various families, including Kakita and Togashi. On the way to Loyalty Castle, a weary Miya rider inquires about Monban, prompting Setsuna to offer to deliver a Phoenix Clan-sealed missive to him. Upon reaching Loyalty Castle, Setsuna is instructed to remain in her quarters for her safety. There, she learns from an Ikoma Bard that her husband, Shinjo Harunobu, captured during the Battle of the Hanto River in Unicorn Clan lands, is alive but held hostage. Concerned for his well-being, she suspects that his horse may be among the spoils of war. Despite the tension surrounding her situation, she engages in light gossip with Lion Clan courtiers and discusses her assignment with Akodo Masami, demonstrating her political acumen as she navigates the complex social dynamics of the castle. In her quest for self-improvement, Setsuna practices unarmed combat with Monban to learn self-defense, balancing her responsibilities as a magistrate with her personal concerns. She later recognizes Kitsu Wataru among a group of magistrates and is surprised to see familiar faces. Wataru shares news of a recent battle where the Lion Clan defeated the Unicorn Clan, capturing several individuals, and discusses the tradition of drinking only after victory. As her party prepares to depart for the Castle of the Swift Sword, Setsuna remains resolute, focused on her duties and the fate of her husband, determined to fulfill her mission amidst the ongoing conflict. Setsuna approaches Shinjo Family Mono, acknowledging him as a provisional magistrate and inquiring about his lord's role in border security. She reveals her marriage to Shinjo Harunobu, advising him to exercise discretion in his inquiries, reflecting her understanding of the delicate nature of relationships and the information she chooses to share. At Loyalty Castle, she witnesses a duel between Kakita Kazumi and Matsu Koda, acknowledging the outcome and its implications for her clan. Following the duel, she invokes dueling customs to address grievances on behalf of the offended Crane Clan, showcasing her commitment to courtly traditions and her clan's honor. Later, she appeals to a well-connected courtier for a letter supporting her husband's release, emphasizing the necessity of her husband's well-being and the idea of open horizons typical of the Unicorn Clan. Throughout her interactions, she maintains a calm and composed presence, adeptly balancing her responsibilities within the court. Setsuna is also recognized for her radiant attire and refined appreciation for art, particularly pillow books. She recalls a coastal bluff near her childhood home as one of the most beautiful places in Rokugan, where she enjoyed sunsets with her husband. During a tea ceremony at a waystation, she converses with other samurai about culinary preferences and the beauty of various locations in Rokugan, emphasizing the importance of a magistrate's duty to the people and expressing a desire to maintain honor, even when faced with difficult choices. In a significant moment, Setsuna participates in a ritual to invoke her grandmother, Matsu Tomoe, alongside Shiba Midori and Bayushi Monban. During the invocation, Matsu Tomoe manifests, confronting Setsuna about her neglect and the need to honor her ancestors. Setsuna apologizes for her past neglect and expresses a desire to make amends. Matsu Tomoe's rebuke highlights the expectations of honor and duty placed upon her, emphasizing the importance of integrity in her role as a magistrate. Setsuna vows to rectify her previous shortcomings and honor her ancestors as instructed, reinforcing her commitment to her clan and her duties. Throughout her journey, Setsuna demonstrates awareness of her surroundings and engages in conversations that show her commitment to her mission and concern for proper conduct in the Lion Clan Lands. She observes the nervous behavior of Asako Taishi, showcasing her perceptiveness. During an ambush, she is briefly seized by an assailant but asserts her status and mission, leading to her release. She delivers a formal speech to the attackers, invoking the blessing of the Akodo Roads and her duty, which prompts them to reconsider their actions. Kakita Kazumi, her yojimbo, moves protectively to guard her during the skirmish. Setsuna's efforts to maintain dignity in danger reflect her adherence to the values of her clan and her role as a magistrate. As a skilled rider, Setsuna commands her Utaku Warsteed with authority, using firm commands to guide it effectively in chaotic situations. During a chaotic melee on The Emperor Hantei Hantei’s Road, she maneuvered her steed to block an attacker from seizing control of Taishi's pony. With skilled handling, she seized the bridle and guided the pony beside her, effectively protecting Taishi. As the situation escalated, she spurred her steed toward the Castle of the Swift Sword to rally with her attendants, demonstrating her strategic thinking and commitment to her allies amidst the chaos. Upon arriving at the Castle of the Swift Sword, she displayed her Emerald Magistrates' badge to the guards, securing her entry. Setsuna participated in a tea ceremony with Kitsu Takeko, a Lion Clan daimyo, and her advisors, where she reported the ambush she experienced on the Lion Clan roads while carrying an imperial writ from Magister Kitsuki Kaage. She described the attackers as trained, masked assailants with no visible clan symbols, framing the incident as an affront to both the Imperial Houses and the Lion Clan. During her audience with Kitsu Takeko, Setsuna requested assistance in punishing the attackers and sought strength for her ongoing imperial mission. Although she asked to accompany Akodo Atsushi on a search for the assailants, Kitsu Takeko emphasized that her other mission took precedence. Setsuna maintained decorum during the tea ceremony and omitted mention of Asako Taishi, a fellow magistrate present during the ambush. When asked about her Family, Setsuna expressed distress over her husband fighting on the opposing side of the Lion Clan-Unicorn Clan conflict, prompting Kitsu Takeko to offer her comfort. Afterward, the party reviewed the ambush site, confirming that the attackers had fled, leaving behind dead bodies. Setsuna sent a letter to Akodo Masami via one of the castle's fastest riders. The following day, she received her full wardrobe and other effects and was provided with suitable accommodations by the castle staff. As the party engaged with various individuals in the castle, Setsuna participated in discussions regarding the ongoing conflict while maintaining her focus on her duties as a magistrate. In a significant battle against the forces led by Shinjukamu, Setsuna demonstrated her strategic prowess by proposing a plan to seize Shinjukamu’s horse to secure the banner attached to it. Coordinating an assault, she instructed soldiers to throw heavy log-like objects while directing Shiguro Chinmoku to distract Shinjukamu's horse. Setsuna successfully grabbed the reins of the horse, threw Shinjukamu to the snow, and hoisted the enemy's banner, marking a significant moment in the conflict as the enemy's morale was expected to falter due to the seizure of their banner. Following this, Torunako engaged Shinjukamu in a duel as riders bearing the chrysanthemum banner arrived, signaling the achievement of a strategic objective. In her role as an Emerald Magistrate, Setsuna is known for her legal acumen and advocacy within the court system. She actively engages in legal discourse during court sessions, often citing relevant case law to support her arguments. Recently, she received two letters from Bayushi Monban, one detailing the status of her husband, Harunobu, who was reported as a prisoner of war treated honorably by the Lion Clan. She requested Monban to cease his interference regarding Harunobu's captivity, to which he agreed. Setsuna presented arguments for Imperial custody of disputed Diamond Mines, referencing legal precedents such as Shinjo v. Asawa and Daidoji v. Akodo, which were noted and entered into the record by the presiding magistrate, Kitsuki Kaage. During court proceedings, Setsuna formally addresses Kitsuki Kaage to signal her intent to contribute constructively to the discussions. She argues that allowing the Scorpion a belated claim could preserve significant legal principles for the Unicorn's restored lands. As the proceedings continue, she asserts her authority in the chamber, discouraging slander against Bayushi Monban and urging restraint among the advocates. Setsuna deflects implications of wrongdoing regarding certain documents, asserting that Monban returned them to her on request for review. Despite facing challenges from other advocates, she maintains a strategic approach in the courtroom, navigating the complexities of the legal arguments presented. Setsuna is also known for her calm demeanor and is skilled at managing discussions in a courtroom setting. She provides succinct and factual testimony, particularly regarding the Battle of Snow Plain, where she recounts events from the perspective of her ancestor, Matsu Morozane. She details the challenges faced by the Lion forces, including poisonings and adverse weather, asserting that the Lion had a slight advantage until Imperial intervention halted the battle. During the proceedings, she supports the testimony of others, including Kitsuko Ayoko, subtly encouraging her to maintain composure while addressing the court. She expresses skepticism about the effectiveness of summoning ancestor spirits, opposing the idea and requesting to be excused from the séance, which Kitsuki Kaage denies. Despite her reservations, she remains engaged in the courtroom dynamics, focusing on the testimonies presented and upholding her clan's interests amid the legal dispute. Recently, Doji Setsuna is recognized as a Crane author known for her brushwork. She arrived at Miya Tetsuya’s Tea House during a tea ceremony hosted by Shiba Midori, accompanied by Kakita Kazumi and Miya Misato. Properly dressed and roadworthy, she was praised by Kakita Kazumi for her skill with the brush, comparing it to his own prowess with the blade. Setsuna later sought a private moment with Shiba Midori to discuss a previous counsel regarding a Terminating Tea, indicating her personal stake in the matter. Shiba Midori advised her that the treatment would be unpleasant and would require a slow recovery. Setsuna considered taking the tea before their departure if necessary. Setsuna's servants, Ishika and Aika, were sent to procure specific abortifacient ingredients but returned with mostly incorrect items. Kakita Kazumi inferred that the herbs were for a delicate treatment and discreetly advised Setsuna about the use of diluted nightshade tea. Eventually, Setsuna consumed the Terminating Tea under Shiba Midori's care, resulting in severe wounding of her Void aspect, consistent with the anticipated effects of the treatment. As the group prepared to depart, they aimed to leave the following morning, pending Setsuna's recovery. Currently, Setsuna is suffering from a severe illness, described as the worst she has ever experienced. She is attended by rotating servants, reflecting her high status and the care she receives. Despite her condition, she manages to present herself upright, though she appears exhausted and disoriented. Her face is pale and ghostly, and she struggles with the effects of the medicine taken the previous night, which may have exacerbated her illness. During a disturbance in the guest quarters, Setsuna hears a scream and discovers a ransacked room. She delicately removes a shuriken embedded in the wall and wraps it in cloth, demonstrating her awareness of the potential danger. Setsuna engages with Miya Misato, Miya Tetsuya's niece, inquiring about the safety of their travel plans and expressing her determination to proceed despite her illness. She participates in discussions regarding the security situation and the implications of recent events, showing her active involvement in the crisis. Additionally, Setsuna's cousin, a high-status Crane financier known as a hatamoto, is involved in trade discussions concerning the export of goods from distant lands via Crane networks. He has expressed interest in utilizing these networks to reach the capital but has yet to receive any samples from Ide Subane he is negotiating with. His dealings are complicated by the ongoing Lion-Unicorn war, which has made overland routes uncertain. During a tea gathering, he discussed trade opportunities and challenges with Doji Setsuna, noting that Ide Subane had promised examples of goods but had not yet delivered.
