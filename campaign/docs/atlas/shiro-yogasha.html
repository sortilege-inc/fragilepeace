Shiro Yogasha

Shiro Yogasha is a castle located in the region of Rokugan, serving as a stronghold for the Dragon Clan. It features imposing architecture and a strategic position that offers defensive capabilities and a vantage point over the surrounding lands. The climate is temperate, suitable for cultivating rice and other essential crops for its inhabitants.

The castle is home to various samurai and courtiers, including Kitsuki Kaage, a Dragon magistrate who oversees legal matters and conducts official business there. Recently, Kage planned to report on a legal case involving Doji Setsuna, a Crane courtier. The castle acts as a hub for administrative activities, where magistrates exercise discretion in law and governance. It is recognized as a key location for Kage's responsibilities and for coordinating the magistrates' efforts in addressing hostilities between the Lion and Unicorn clans.
