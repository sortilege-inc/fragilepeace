Geisha House

The Geisha House is an establishment in the city of the Rich Frog, known for its lively atmosphere filled with samurai and patrons engaged in games and conversation. Inside, triplets serve guests, offering sake and entertainment. The house features private rooms for discreet meetings and discussions, with decor that blends elegance and comfort.

Bayushi Monban, a Scorpion samurai, arranged a meeting at the Geisha House with a Scorpion Cousin to discuss matters of intrigue. The cousin, acting as an informant, warned Monban about family exploitation and provided leads on acquiring noxious supplies from an apothecary across the water. Monban sought assistance in poisoning a target through a servant, negotiating terms for future favors in exchange for the cousin's help. Tensions rose as events unfolded, including a theft attempt by Kakita Kazumi, a Crane shinobi, and a disturbance in the Guest Wing, creating a sense of urgency among the patrons.
