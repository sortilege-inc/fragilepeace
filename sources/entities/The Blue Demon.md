The masked figure who moved through the smoke on the [[Emperor’s Road]] and put a poisoned
blade into [[Asako Taishi]] while the rest of the party was trying to keep him alive.

It is [[Kakita Kazumi]]. Nobody else on that road knows it. Nobody in the party has ever
seen the mask, which is blue-black and demon-faced; [[Doji Setsuna]] had already turned for
the [[Castle of the Swift Sword]], [[Bayushi Monban]] was roped and up a tree, and
[[Shiba Midori]] was in a formal duel twenty paces away. He took the *yoroi-dōshi* he
carries — his grandfather's, an armour-piercer — dressed the blade with the poison he
gathered on the road out of [[Loyalty Castle]], put it into a man who was held and could
not resist, and went back into the smoke.

What the record has of his reason is one line of his own and one of the record's: that his
job was done and the target was no longer an issue, and that the wounds were not hatred —
an order had been given about this man by somebody very motivated to see him gone.
