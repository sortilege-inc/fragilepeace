## Narrative

!lede The game was still going when it started, and [[Moto Gaheris]] was ahead on the
board. Five hundred [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] cavalry outside; a hundred and fifty
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] ashigaru inside, fifty of them wounded. Everyone at the table knew
the arithmetic and nobody said it out loud.

[[Moto Gaheris]] opened with his terms, in the flattest voice he had. The
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] would surrender their arms and armour, samurai keeping swords alone.
They would march home. At every village on the way they would stop and beg forgiveness
for having been rude house guests. They would swear on their ancestors never to draw on
the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] again. And if they would not, their heads would go up on
the castle wall.

[[Matsu Maro]] countered with what she called civilised terms. March out armed. Yield
the castle in the state it was yielded to them. Continue on their mission unhindered.
Concede that the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] had beaten them here — and promise to beat
them next time.

[[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] went first at the frame rather than the terms. The
[[Otaku Family|Otaku]], he observed, had already disgraced themselves by withdrawing
from the last battle, which does the [[Moto Family|Moto]] nothing but good. Breaking
the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] here would only be tidying up after them. Then he offered
[[Moto Gaheris]] something better than a massacre: let his daughter [[Kojin]] ride up
afterwards and take formal possession of the castle. A rested garrison could yield to
her with very little fighting, she would have a moment of her own, and the
[[Moto Family|Moto]] would still hold the ground.

[[Moto Gaheris]] found it interesting and did not move. Five hundred horse against a
hundred foot; he saw little reason not to make a sport of it. Later, when the room got
hot between him and [[Matsu Maro]], [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] played instead of arguing — the
high plains and the empty steppe of the [[Moto Family|Moto]] country — and the
[[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] side of the table cooled.

[[Doji Setsuna]] spent her turn reading the man rather than answering him, and found the
thing worth having. His war is not with the [[Lion Clan|Lion]]. It is with the
[[Shinjo Family|Shinjo]], for primacy inside his own clan — and it is on hold, because
the whole [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] is mustering against some enemy from outside and
internal rivalry has been put away for the duration. A peace here does not cost him a
war. It gives him back the one he actually wants.

She put it to him plainly. *I do not believe that you will get the satisfaction that you
need from a conflict here. I do think that there is an opportunity for greatness in
collaboration with us.*

He did not deny it. *The Imperial Envoy, after all, has to choose their protectors. She
has chosen you for their advisor, so that she sees some value in you. I await a reason
not to declaw this cat before me.* He was still weighing which course served him better.
But his eye lit when the [[Shinjo Family|Shinjo]] were named, and that told her she had
her hand on the right lever.

[[Shiba Midori]] recited poetry — enlightened verse, ostensibly for the room, actually
built to hand [[Matsu Maro]] the weaknesses in [[Moto Gaheris]]'s position on the board.
It went in cleanly, and it told on the next move.

The game ran alongside all of it. [[Moto Gaheris]] took the opening in earth and
[[Matsu Maro]] began badly. Then she shifted to water, found a seam, and took several of
his pieces — *a lion is most dangerous when it is cornered*. In the third round he made
a reckless-looking, aggressive move that gave away how much better he knew the game than
he had been letting on, and she did not take the bait; she set one of her own instead and
went ahead. *Shōgi is a game for the patient.* Eleven to seven.

Halfway through, they brought [[Bayushi Monban]] in. He was dragged through the crowd
with the bag pulled off his head and the gag still in, and put on his knees. He chose to
bow gracefully and wink at the Khan. [[Moto Gaheris]] enjoyed it: *see — even the Scorpion
understand their place in the world, sometimes better than the Lion.* Gagged and kneeling,
he watched the whole negotiation and missed nothing; he knows Cadence himself, and he
would have seen exactly what [[Shiba Midori]] was doing with it.

[[Miya Misato]] spoke for the first time not long after. *My Lord Gaheris, I believe you
asked much of the Lion. They are willing to concede already their defeat. Is that not
shameful enough?* He gave up the demand about the villages on the spot — *I suppose asking
them to apologise to the peasants was a bit much. They weren't that rude.* The ask had
never been serious. She had given him a door out of it.

And then the moment the whole night had been built for. [[Shiba Midori]] used Cadence on
the envoy — arranged between her and [[Doji Setsuna]] before the session began — and
goaded her into speaking. [[Miya Misato]] stepped forward and stated that the
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] would accompany her. Not asked. Not proposed. Ordered. *(The
recording of this exchange was lost; what it contained is reconstructed from the people
who were in the room.)*

[[Moto Gaheris]]: *Finally, the envoy speaks. I am an obedient servant of the Emperor.*

He had been waiting for that all night. He could not take terms from Emerald Magistrates,
and he could not take them from a [[Lion Clan|Lion]] general. The Emperor's voice, used
properly, let him stand down without conceding anything to anyone in that room.

Later [[Miya Misato]] turned to [[Matsu Maro]] herself: *My lady, perhaps the mission you
should proceed with is escorting us alongside the Moto as Lady Setsuna suggests.*
[[Matsu Maro]] allowed that she could hardly escort an Imperial Envoy unarmed.
[[Moto Gaheris]] smiled and said she could always come along as a prisoner. Nobody took
it seriously by then.

[[Doji Setsuna]] turned to the ransom. She checked first whether he was dealing straight,
with [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] helping, and he was: a hundred koku, which is what a samurai of
[[Bayushi Monban]]'s standing is worth and not a koku of extortion in it. Then she divided
the bill where she thought it belonged — five koku from her own purse on the spot as good
faith, twenty from her father's house, twenty-five from the Emperor because ransoming an
Emerald Magistrate is Imperial business, and fifty from [[Bayushi Monban]]'s own lord, who
assigned him this duty in the first place.

[[Moto Gaheris]] accepted, had him cut loose, gave him back his swords, and handed him to
her. On the ropes he offered: *I know it isn't proper Shibari, and I'm sorry about the
implication.* [[Bayushi Monban]], free, asked the Great Khan for a private word during the
march. [[Moto Gaheris]] agreed, visibly curious. On his way back to his own people
[[Bayushi Monban]] moved one of the shōgi pieces and winked at him.

[[Matsu Maro]] won the game. *She played well and fairly*, [[Moto Gaheris]] said, and told
her to rest — they were marching in the morning.

Then [[Doji Setsuna]] wrote the treaty, with [[Otoya]] at her elbow, and read it to the
room. It was clean enough that everyone in it was satisfied there were no mistakes in it
and no loopholes worth objecting to, and by putting his name to it [[Moto Gaheris]] ended
up somewhere he could not refuse the final terms without losing face. Every party staked
ten honour on keeping it. Whoever breaks it forfeits that, unless somebody broke it first.

[[The Midnight Treaty]] was signed by [[Moto Gaheris]] and [[Matsu Maro]], sealed by
[[Miya Misato]] for the Emperor, and written by [[Doji Setsuna]]. The
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] march out armed; they yield the castle as it was yielded to them; they
go on unhindered; they concede the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] beat them there; there is no
further violence against Unicorn citizens; and the Lion and the [[Moto Family|Moto]]
escort the Imperial Envoy's party onward together.

Afterwards, [[Doji Setsuna]] wrote to [[Bayushi Monban]]'s lord. The letter is courteous
to the last syllable. It regrets that a hundred koku might read as a modest valuation of
so fine a vassal, explains that the circumstances required expediency, reports that half
the sum has been arranged, and directs the remaining fifty to his household on the grounds
that the debt arose from the duty his lordship assigned. It encloses the particulars for
his records. [[Bayushi Monban]] has not seen it.

## Learned

- Moto Gaheris: A one-eyed Moto Khan with five hundred cavalry. Opened with terms nobody could accept and gave ground the moment the envoy handed him a way to. His real contest is with the Shinjo for primacy inside the Unicorn, suspended while the whole clan musters against an enemy from outside — which is why peace here suits him. Dealt fairly on the ransom, freed Monban, returned his swords, and signed.
- Matsu Maro: The Lion general — a hundred and fifty ashigaru, fifty wounded. Countered Gaheris's terms with her own and would not be baited into an unwinnable board or an unwinnable field. Won the shōgi game on her own play, with Midori's help. Signed.
- Miya Misato: Found her voice. Talked Gaheris out of the demand that the Lion apologise to villagers, then delivered the Emperor's command that the Lion would accompany her — the one intervention the entire night was built to make possible. Sealed the treaty on the Emperor's behalf.
- Kojin: Moto Gaheris's daughter. Tadayoshi proposed she ride up afterwards and take formal possession of the castle, so that she would have the moment and the Lion could yield without being slaughtered for it.
- Otaku Family: Withdrew from the battle on the Snow Plain and are held to have shamed themselves by it, which strengthens the Moto against them inside the Unicorn.
- Shinjo Family: Moto Gaheris's actual rivals for primacy in the Unicorn clan. The rivalry is on hold only because the clan is mustering against something outside it.
- Otoya: Her scribe, at her elbow while she drafted the treaty.
- The Midnight Treaty: Six terms, two signatories, the envoy's seal, and ten honour staked by every party to it against breaking it first.
- Bayushi Monban: Brought in gagged and made to kneel, and chose to bow gracefully and wink rather than resist. Ransomed at a hundred koku, cut loose, and given his swords back. Asked the Khan for a private conversation on the march, and has not said what about.

## Setsuna

**Two hours of work bought one sentence, and the sentence was not hers.** Everything she
and the others did — the reading, the music, the poetry, the shōgi coaching, the
atmosphere — was scaffolding for [[Miya Misato]] to give one order at the moment
[[Moto Gaheris]] was ready to obey it. He could not take terms from an Emerald Magistrate
and he could not take them from a [[Lion Clan|Lion]] general. He could take them from the
Emperor. Her office does not win rooms; it builds the moment when someone whose office
does can speak. Deployed last, it lands. Deployed first, it gets tested.

The lever is the [[Shinjo Family|Shinjo]], and it is still there. [[Moto Gaheris]]'s war
is inside his own clan and it is currently frozen by a general muster against something
from outside. He wants this settled so he can get back to it. That is the single most
useful thing anyone learned tonight, and it does not expire when the column moves.

And nobody has said what the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] are mustering against. An entire
Great Clan has suspended its internal politics for an external enemy and the Empire's own
envoy is riding into their country without knowing what it is. That is worth asking
[[Moto Gaheris]] directly, on the march, while he is pleased with himself.

[[Miya Misato]] has given a command now and been obeyed. She talked a Khan out of a demand
and then ordered a clan's general to accompany her, in front of five hundred horse. She
needed [[Shiba Midori]] to goad her into it and she may need that again — but an envoy who
has done it once is a different instrument from an envoy who never has, and the whole
embassy is built on her.

The treaty has teeth, which is new. Ten honour staked by every party, forfeit to whoever
breaks it second. Nothing else in this campaign has bound anybody. This does, it is in her
handwriting, and it will be read.

[[Bayushi Monban]] wants a private word with the man who held him. He was gagged through
the entire negotiation with nothing to do but watch, he reads Cadence, and he therefore saw
exactly how [[Miya Misato]] was steered. Then he moved a piece on the board and winked at
the Khan. She should find out what that conversation is for before it happens, and she
probably cannot.

She has also just billed a [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] lord fifty koku and framed it as his
own obligation, in a letter of impeccable courtesy that her yojimbo has not read. It is
correct — the duty was his lordship's assignment — and it puts a Scorpion daimyō in debt
to her arithmetic. Worth being clear-eyed about which of those she was doing.

And the road runs to [[Battlemaiden Castle]], where [[Shinjo Altensari]] is said to be
investing against [[Kodo Totori]]. She is travelling there under the escort of a
[[Moto Family|Moto]] Khan whose ambition is to displace the [[Shinjo Family|Shinjo]].
Whatever happens at that siege, [[Moto Gaheris]] will not be a neutral party to it, and
she should decide now whether that is a problem or the whole opportunity.
