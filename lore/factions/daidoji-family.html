Daidoji Family

Crane

The Daidoji clan is a prominent family within the Lion Clan, recognized for their martial prowess and adherence to the principles of honor and duty. They excel in defense and military tactics, serving as the backbone of the Lion's military forces. Operating out of the Castle of the Swift Sword, a strategic location critical to their military endeavors and also significant for Emerald Magistrates' court proceedings, the Daidoji maintain a strong presence in both military and political spheres.

Members of the Daidoji are known for their disciplined nature and commitment to the Lion Clan's ideals. They engage in diplomacy and conflict with other clans, particularly the Crane Clan and Unicorn Clan. The Daidoji demonstrate their commitment to protecting their lands and allies through strategic military actions. Notably, Doji Setsuna, an Emerald Magistrate representing the Crane Clan, plays a significant role in legal and political matters at the court, particularly in disputes involving the Scorpion Clan and the ownership of the Snow Plain. Setsuna engages in legal arguments during court proceedings, referencing the Daidoji v. Akodo case to argue against vexatious litigation and advocate for Imperial custody of contested diamond mines.

In a recent incident, an ambush by unidentified assailants occurred near the Castle of the Swift Sword. Doji Setsuna reported the attack to Kitsu Takeko, the Lion daimyo, leading to the deployment of Akodo Atsushi, a former instructor from the Akodo War College, to investigate. Among the slain attackers, a strip of cloth with a Doji Family pattern was found, indicating potential involvement of the Daidoji in the events. This incident underscores the complexities of their relationships with other clans, as the Daidoji also seek to negotiate a written pact with Shosuro Aishi, offering support for the Scorpion claim in exchange for a pledge of non-interference regarding Crane treaties.

The Daidoji clan's influence is evident in both military actions and interactions with other clans, highlighting their integral role in the conflicts within Rokugan. They are known for providing care and hospitality to travelers at their castle, reflecting their reputation for honoring guests and maintaining strong ties within the clan. Their strategic approach to diplomacy and legal matters, balancing clan interests with broader political considerations, reinforces their position within clan politics and the ongoing narrative of Rokugan.
