Gatherer of Wind’s Castle

Gatherer of Wind’s Castle is a fortification located north of the City of the Rich Frog, currently under siege by the Lion Clan. It serves as a strategic stronghold within Unicorn Lands and plays a critical role in the ongoing conflicts in the region. The castle is situated in an area characterized by rolling plains, which have not been well-maintained, leading to uncertain road conditions for travel. The surrounding geography is marked by rivers, including the Three Sides River and the Drowned Merchant River, which facilitate trade and movement.

The Lion Clan's active siege of Gatherer of Wind’s Castle raises concerns about the legitimacy of their hostilities against the Unicorn. The location is significant in the power struggle between the Unicorn and Lion Clans, with the potential for further military engagements in the area.
