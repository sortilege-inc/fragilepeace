Doji Family

Crane

The Doji Family are a distinguished faction within the Phoenix Clan, celebrated for their dedication to beauty, art, and diplomacy. As members of the Crane Clan family, they embody the ideals of honor and the responsibilities inherent to their samurai status, engaging in delicate negotiations that reflect their clan's values. Their influence extends significantly into the realm of the Emerald Magistrates, where they act as enforcers of the Emperor's will and advocates for justice.

Notable among them is Doji Setsuna, an esteemed Emerald Magistrate recognized for her exceptional diplomatic skills and artistic sensibilities. Setsuna's commitment to justice is evident in her investigations, including a recent ambush involving an imperial writ from Magister Kitsuki Kaage. During her journey to the Castle of the Swift Sword, she encountered masked assailants, prompting her to seek an audience with Kitsu Takeko, the Lion daimyo, to report the incident and request assistance. Although Kitsu Takeko sent Akodo Atsushi to investigate the attackers, she forbade Setsuna from accompanying him due to her other responsibilities.

The Doji Family clan's influence is further illustrated through their participation in social and political events, such as a tea ceremony hosted by the Kitsu Family at a Border Waystation. Here, Setsuna showcased her diplomatic acumen while discussing cuisine and the beauty of Rokugan. However, the gathering was overshadowed by the sensing of a supernatural presence, leading to private discussions among the Emerald Magistrates regarding its implications. In a moment of personal reflection, Setsuna honored her ancestors, particularly invoking her grandmother, Matsu Family Tomoe. This invocation brought forth Matsu Family Tomoe, who challenged Setsuna about her ancestral duties and the honor of her lineage, underscoring the importance of upholding her family's honor as a magistrate.

The Doji Family clan maintains strong connections to the Imperial court and the Lion clan, navigating the complex political landscape of Rokugan with a focus on justice and order. Setsuna's actions and relationships exemplify the clan's dedication to these principles, as they strive to uphold their honor amidst the challenges they face.
