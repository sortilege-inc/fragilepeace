Faction

Scorpion Clan

In the year 1123, the Scorpion Clan has risen to prominence within the intricate political landscape of Rokugan, known for their cunning, strategic prowess, and adeptness at manipulation. They navigate the ongoing tensions between the Lion Clan and the Unicorn Clan, employing a blend of diplomacy and subterfuge to secure their position amidst shifting allegiances. Their influence is particularly pronounced in the contested ownership of the Snow Plain Diamond Mines, which they assert were historically deeded to them in recognition of their services to the Unicorn Clan—a claim that the Unicorn Clan vehemently disputes. At the helm of the Scorpion Clan's endeavors is Bayushi Monban, a distinguished bushi and yojimbo to Doji Setsuna of the Crane Clan. Monban exemplifies the clan's commitment to duty, often prioritizing his responsibilities over personal desires, especially as he grapples with the urgent matter of his missing betrothed, Hida Chizuzu, a Crab samurai. His dedication to justice and honor is underscored by his role as an evaluator in disputes involving the Scorpion Clan and the Unicorn Clan, showcasing their dual role as both a political power and a mediator. Key members, including Shosuro Aishi, a seasoned negotiator for Lord Shosuro Hametsu, enhance the clan's political clout. Aishi's skills are highlighted during arbitration proceedings, where she presents a letter from a Scorpion Clan ancestor asserting an agreement between the Unicorn Clan and Scorpion Clan that favors the latter's ownership of the mines. The Scorpion Clan's intricate web of alliances with clans such as Kakita, Shiba, and Togashi amplifies their influence, allowing them to engage in discussions on justice and honor that transcend clan affiliations. However, their tactics often draw criticism for being duplicitous, particularly from the Lion Clan and Crane Clans. The Scorpion Clan's claim to the Diamond Mines is rooted in a supposed ancient agreement linked to their assistance at the Battle of Snow Plain, which the Unicorn Clan contests, citing a lack of official records. Recent incidents, such as the investigation into a Doji family lost in Slowtide Harbor and the burning of the Crane Clan embassy in Otosan Uchi, illustrate the Scorpion Clan's involvement in critical events that shape the political landscape. Monban's insistence on immediate meetings concerning clan business, even after enduring injuries from an ambush involving masked assailants on the Lion Clan roads, further emphasizes the clan's focus on safeguarding its own interests. As the Scorpion Clan continues to weave their influence through alliances and intelligence, they remain ever vigilant, prepared to exploit opportunities that arise amidst the chaos of clan rivalries. Monban has taken proactive steps to arrange meetings with the Unicorn Clan ambassador to improve relations and reduce tensions, advising Soshi Aisha, a Scorpion Clan courtier, to cultivate a relationship with Kitsu Ayoko, an emerging Shugenja, potentially utilizing her as an insider within the Lion Clan. This strategic focus on maintaining a balance of power that favors their interests underscores the Scorpion Clan's commitment to secrecy and manipulation of social dynamics. In the midst of ongoing conflict, Monban has emerged as a blue-skinned infiltrator aligned with the Lion Clan, furthering the Scorpion Clan's interests through stealth and intelligence-gathering efforts against the Unicorn Clan forces. During a pivotal battle, he exploits the chaos to infiltrate the Unicorn Clan War Camp, returning with critical intelligence that enhances the Lion Clan's understanding of the Unicorn Clan's defensive tactics. This reliance on subtlety and intelligence over direct confrontation exemplifies the Scorpion Clan's unique approach to warfare within the broader conflict among the clans. As a conference at the Castle of the Emerald Magistrates approaches, where Kitsuki Kaage will adjudicate the dispute over the Diamond Mines, the Scorpion Clan's historical influence is under examination. The lack of records from the Lion Clan generals acknowledging their involvement in the Battle of Snow Plain suggests a coordinated effort to obscure their actions, setting the stage for a tense confrontation at the upcoming conference. Monban's consultations with historians to strategize about delaying proceedings to gather further evidence underscore the clan's determination to solidify their claims, even as the opposing party prepares arguments against them, emphasizing the need for stronger documentation before recognizing their ownership of the contested resources. The Scorpion is a prominent faction within the realm, represented by members such as Bayushi Monban and Shosuro Aishi. They are deeply involved in the Emerald Magistrates, with Monban asserting jurisdiction over cases involving various clans, including the Lion. The Scorpion employs a strategic approach to political maneuvering and evidence presentation, often utilizing secrecy and subterfuge to advance their interests. They engage in complex legal disputes, particularly regarding historical treaties and the legitimacy of their claims, including those related to the Diamond Mines. Recently, the Scorpion Clan's influence has been further highlighted by their involvement in the containment of an Efreet, a powerful Ifrit. Monban's role as bodyguard to Doji Setsuna has been complicated by her refusal to acknowledge his authority, leading to tensions in their dynamic. After a confrontation where Shiba Midori successfully persuaded the Efreet to surrender, Monban expressed concern for Setsuna's safety when she insisted on taking the Efreet's containment box to her quarters. Ultimately, Monban took custody of The Box after she relinquished it, declaring his intention to take it outside the city to release the Efreet, believing this would secure its goodwill. This decision sparked further debate among the group regarding the best course of action, highlighting the Scorpion Clan's strategic considerations in dealing with powerful entities and the potential consequences of their choices. Additionally, Monban's absence during a tea ceremony hosted by Shiba Midori raised concerns among attendees, while his interaction with a young Scorpion samurai in a cat mask from Slowtide Harbor further emphasizes the clan's ongoing covert activities and intelligence gathering within the political landscape of the Emerald Empire. The Scorpion Clan is comprised of samurai and informants, known for their involvement in espionage and manipulation. They operate from various locations, including houses of pleasure, where discreet conversations and negotiations take place. Members of the Scorpion often navigate complex family intrigues, with some, like Bayushi Monban, pursuing personal objectives while managing these relationships. Recently, Monban coordinated with Shiba Midori to manage the spirit situation, intending to set up a barrier by a courtyard pond to draw the spirit out and trap it in boxes, requiring new Meishōdō crafted boxes and assistance from shugenja, as the previous boxes are no longer effective. Monban expressed a strong desire to expel the spirit from his body and prepared to depart swiftly after informing allies of the situation, reflecting the ongoing challenges the Scorpion Clan faces in balancing their strategic interests with the supernatural threats that arise.
