## Setsuna

**This is a hole in her record and it should be treated as one.** Her yojimbo was
thrown off the island, insulted the host the embassy's welcome depends on, and
accepted a duel with the officer commanding that host's guard — and the first she or
anyone else will hear of the detail is secondhand. She does not know what he said,
to whom, or in front of how many.

What she can act on is the shape. [[Shiba Midori]] was poisoned on purpose, which
makes it two magistrates attacked inside one week. The assassins left evidence
[[Bayushi Monban]] was able to find. And the man sworn to stand between her and a
blade is now committed to stand in front of a different one, for a reason that has
nothing to do with the envoy.
