Faction

Five Pillars of the Thousand Needles

The Five Pillars of the Thousand Needles is a faction significant in the historical and political landscape of the Lion Clan, particularly during the Battle of the Snow Plain. Notable figures, such as Akodo Toronoko, have expressed regret for not eliminating all members of this faction, which has been involved in military conflicts that have influenced the historical narrative shaped by the Lion Clan.

The faction is implicated in the Scorpion Clan's claims to the diamond mines on the Snow Plain, a dispute denied by the Unicorn Clan. Historical records of the battle are fragmented, with personal memoirs from Lion Clan generals omitting mention of Scorpion Clan involvement, suggesting an effort to preserve the Lion Clan's honor. Prominent historian Ikoma Hideri, who could have clarified the events of the battle, was assassinated before revealing critical truths, contributing to the gaps in the historical account.

The group emphasizes the need for substantial corroborating evidence before accepting the Scorpion Clan's claims, underlining the faction's influence on the political landscape and the interplay between history and honor among the clans.
