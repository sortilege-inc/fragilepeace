A form of words [[Bayushi Monban]] uses, and not an organisation — the export filed it as a
faction he represents, with a page that is actually a summary of the [[Castle of the Swift Sword]] arbitration wearing the phrase as a title.

**What it is attached to, every time it is used.** A relationship word. *He is our brother
and first enemy blood.* *My brother in First Enemy Blood.* *My big sister in first enemy
blood.* *Family, and first enemy blood.* It has never once been used to name a body of
people, and nobody but [[Bayushi Monban]] uses it.

**Who he has said it of.** [[Akodo Akihito]], four times across a year, always as *brother*.
[[Shiba Midori]], as *big sister*. And the [[Crab Clan|Crab]] he showed his demon wound to
when he came back, as *family, you and I*.

**The one time he came close to explaining it**, he was asking a Lion whether they had
shared battle with the first enemy — *have we not? Just like your cousin. My brother in
first enemy blood.* The chronicle has read that as kinship claimed through having stood
against the Shadowlands together, which is how his niece who fought at Ponto Pass comes to call him
uncle while being no relation to him by blood at all.

**Held open deliberately.** That reading fits every use and is not the same as knowing what
the phrase means to him — whether it names an oath, an order, something [[Kuni Wardens|Kuni]],
or simply a way of speaking. The owner has not settled it. Nothing in the record should
harden past the usage above until he does.

Distinct from **the first enemy** itself, which the recordings use plainly and often for the
power beyond [[The Wall|the Wall]] — *the first enemy minions of the Foolish One* — and which
the chronicle already treats that way.
