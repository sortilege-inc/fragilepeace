Asako Taishi

Phoenix

What is known

S6A Phoenix scholar, Isawa by blood and raised among the Kaito, travelling the Imperial Roads from east of Morning Glory Castle by way of the Shrine of Durogin and Dragon lands, with no destination written anywhere — Midori reads him as drifting toward the Ikoma libraries. He carries something he will not name until it is authenticated and public, and says everyone he has told has come to harm. Wataru finds he has cast no magic in a day and that there is more to him than shows. Monban sees a presence overshadowing him that is not malignant and is not the kind of affliction a cleansing kihō can lift — the kihō did nothing. He was one sentence from telling Midori everything when the smoke came, and he was taken while under the party's promised protection.
S7Strangled, stabbed in the chest and again under the ribs, bleeding out, and carried away alive by the men who did it. Still under the party's promised protection and now in their hands. He was one sentence from telling Midori what he carried when the smoke came down.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
