Soshi Aisha

Scorpion

What is known

S9A Scorpion courtier on the Path of Splinters, here at the behest of the Imperial Advisor, and she did not know Bayushi Monban by sight. The Advisor opposed the betrothal because the Lion having few enemies is bad for the Scorpion. Her statement of policy: the Lion must have an enemy that is not the Scorpion; the Dragon left to contemplate; the Phoenix kept busy; the Crane kept occupied until the last of them dies; the Crab never allowed to beat the Shadowlands, because a victorious Crab would look elsewhere; the Unicorn kept outside. They have been provoking war between the Lion families for generations. They intend a Scorpion to take the Emerald Championship and think it nearly done. And she offered, unprompted, to manufacture the documentation the Scorpion claim needs.
S21Met Monban on the Path of Splinters behind a fan, denied everything for the benefit of the people walking past, and told him he had been seen. She holds an incriminating docket with his chop on it and his word as a Bayushi behind it. She will produce the document he wants but will not present it — that risk is his, and so is accounting for how he came by it. Anyone who crosses her hangs at the traitor's grove first.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
