Tonbo Higuchi

Dragonfly

What is known

S27A scandalous author, rumoured to be somewhere in the city, and someone's problem.
S28Said by a Unicorn to have gone upriver to Toshi Ranbo. Midori reads that as a deliberate lie to send them the wrong way.
S29A receipt in her pen name, for a talisman holding a wild spirit out of the Burning Sands. She is not merely writing about this trade — she is a customer.
S33The noble author who writes as Hana no Ame — the same woman whose pen name is on the receipt in Setsuna's custody, and Governor Miya Tetsuya's friend. She was the possessed guest — pendant at her throat, feeding a furnace without feeling it, eating a goldfish raw out of the pond. Freed, stable, badly shaken.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
