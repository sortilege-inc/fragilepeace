Centipede Clan

The Centipede Clan is a faction in Rokugan, recognized for its strategic influence and nuanced relationships. It is associated with the Scorpion Clan and operates under the leadership of Lady Daimyo Soshi. The clan is focused on subtlety and manipulation, often engaging in complex political maneuvers to achieve its goals. Members are skilled in espionage and information gathering, allowing them to navigate the intricate web of alliances and rivalries among the Great Clans.

Recently, the Centipede Clan's involvement in a diplomatic conference has been significant, particularly through the actions of Bayushi Monban. He has sought to stall proceedings with the Scorpion Clan and Unicorn Clan envoys. Monban is gathering evidence related to disputed claims over the Snow Plain diamond mines, which the Scorpion Clan claim to own, a assertion denied by the Unicorn Clan. Tensions have increased with the approaching arrival of Kitsuki Kaage, the Emerald Magistrate. Monban's interactions demonstrate a calculated approach to diplomacy as he navigates the political landscape and addresses the complexities of the historical accounts surrounding the Battle of the Snow Plain. The clan's reputation for cunning is reinforced through their efforts to manage narratives and influence perceptions among the clans involved in the dispute.
