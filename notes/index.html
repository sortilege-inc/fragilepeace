Player Notes

Working papers kept from Setsuna's side of the table — what she knows, what she owes, and what is still open.

✎
This is the player's document, not the Game Master's. Nothing here is authoritative about the world — it records what Doji Setsuna has been told, what has been inferred, and what the player intends. The GM's plans are not in it, and no claim here should be treated as confirmed unless it is tagged as such.
❖
Every claim is tagged: TABLE established in play · SOURCE verified in the books or the sheet · HEARD told to Setsuna, truth unverified · OPEN unanswered · PLAN the player's intention · SUPERSEDED no longer true · NOTE a caution.

What Setsuna knows

  • TABLE Matsu Maro was sent to die by her own side. Read off her by Midori at the tea: die on the field or die holding a castle that cannot be held, either suits her superiors. She has her peace now, and she still cannot say why she needed it.
  • TABLE Moto Gaheris's real war is with the Shinjo, not the Lion. Primacy inside the Unicorn, frozen while the whole clan musters against an outside enemy. Peace here gives him back the war he wants — which is why he took it.
  • TABLE Her office does not win rooms; it builds the moment when the Emperor's voice can. Gaheris could take terms from neither a magistrate nor a Lion general. Two hours of groundwork bought Misato one sentence. Deploy Imperial authority last, when it lands, not first, when it gets tested.

Both times Lion against Unicorn on that ground, both decided by single combat between commanders, both leaving a field of Lion dead. She is the only person in the column who knows how the first one ended.
  • NOTE Session 40 is a hole, and so is the road north. No record survives of the night Monban was ejected and challenged, and none at all of the sessions between 2 March and 6 April. Both are flagged where they sit in the chronicle.

The document Setsuna has been drafting in her head since the beginning, and what it is really for. Player intent, not established fact — none of this has been proposed to anyone at the table.

  • PLAN The Toshi Ranbo treaty. Crane formally cede a city they no longer hold in practice — generous-looking, cheap. The Lion take the city, a stipend, and the duty of securing the Drowned Merchant River: honoured in martial terms, employed in a defined role. Crane and Unicorn split the uncapped trade upside on the river the Lion are now obliged to police.
  • NOTE It gives every clan what they say they value and locks the Lion into a permanent economic ceiling. Territory and martial purpose for the Lion, economic position for the Crane, trade access for the Unicorn. A polite knife. A Crane built it — a Crane trained by a Scorpion.
  • PLAN Doji Emika closes it. A Doji–Matsu marriage at the end, folding the personal restoration into the political settlement. This is why Setsuna declined Misato's request — Emika is the one chip she will not spend on the immediate crisis.
  • NOTE The treaty is a peace offering to Matsu Tomoe. Her grandmother: a Matsu who married into the Doji and never stopped being a Lion, furious about Toshi Ranbo lost and furious about the granddaughter she got — one who fears death, refused the sword, and built a life out of courtesy and procedure. The treaty answers both of her needs while refusing her surface demands. I will return your city. I will send Emika back to your family. You will have to accept the granddaughter you got.
  • NOTE The cabinet, and it is leaking. Monban hosts the spirit and wants to go west to release it. Setsuna will not let him, and has filed — without saying so to anyone, least of all to him — that she holds a catastrophic-violence option she could authorise. She is negotiating in good faith and keeping the weapon at the same time without letting either touch the other. Onahime showing herself this session is the deadline moving.
  • NOTE Gaheris is closer to it than he knows. He suspects the Crane of engineering this war. He is wrong about this war and right about the shape: a Crane is quietly rearranging the north to Crane–Unicorn benefit at Lion expense, and that Crane was sitting in his tent saying nothing.

The player is not the Game Master for this campaign. There is no spoiler gate on this page, and nothing behind it that Setsuna does not have some claim to know.
