Castle of the Emerald Magistrates

The Castle of the Emerald Magistrates is a prominent stronghold in the region, serving key judicial and diplomatic functions. It is known for its well-maintained structures and beautiful gardens, including an area called "Virtuous Contemplation," where visitors can enjoy secluded meals. The summer climate brings cool nights, and the presence of Lord Moon adds an aura of secrecy to the castle’s atmosphere. The castle houses various servants and officials, including the Emerald Magistrate, who oversees matters of law and order.

Kitsuki Kaage, an Emerald Magistrate from the castle, will arrive in three days with ambassadors from the Scorpion Clan and Unicorn Clan clans to address a dispute over the Snow Plain diamond mines. This claim, based on Scorpion Clan records, has been denied by the Unicorn Clan. Research conducted in the Kitsu Archives revealed fragmented histories concerning the Battle of the Snow Plain, highlighting significant omissions about key figures and events. The interactions within the castle emphasize the importance of its records and the complex political dynamics surrounding the upcoming negotiations with the Scorpion Clan and Unicorn Clan envoys.
