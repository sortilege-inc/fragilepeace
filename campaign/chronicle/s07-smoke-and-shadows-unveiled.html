Smoke and Shadows Unveiled

Session 7·2025-04-22

The blue demon slipped away out of the fight, as his job was done. The target was either dead or dying. Either way, no longer an issue.

That's a woman. Our target is not a woman. Let her go. They had hold of Doji Setsuna on her Utaku warsteed, and they let go the moment they looked at her. They were working from a description and she did not fit it. What came down on the Emperor’s Road was not a robbery. It was an errand, and the errand was Asako Taishi.

Kakita Kazumi was not in it when it started. He had ridden ahead to forage, largely to get away from the scholar's questions, and came back to the noise with no fatigue on him and no strife. He looked at the smoke once and knew the technique — and would neither confirm nor deny where a Crane learns to recognise it.

Asako Taishi shouted his protection again and it bought him nothing again. Bayushi Monban came down out of the air onto Doji Setsuna's warsteed, back to back with her, and put the flat of a sheathed blade against the ear of the man holding Asako Taishi's pony by the reins — wherever you thought I was, I am right here, and if you make a move I will kill you. It rang the man's helmet and did not shift his grip. Kogarashi did better, rearing and putting a second man in the dust. The archers were blind in their own smoke. They dropped their bows and came in on foot.

Doji Setsuna took the bridle of Asako Taishi's pony out of the hands that had it, walking Kogarashi across between them, and talked the animal quiet. The pony is the size of a large dog and the warsteed looks like it could eat it.

Asako Taishi tried to climb into Bayushi Monban's lap to be protected, which would have made three on the horse, and was dragged off it.

Shiba Midori tried to hook her jitte into a pair of nunchaku and missed, took a calming breath, and then rode clear of the smoke and put herself on guard — I am not convinced these are enemies, but they are not acting very friendly either.

Kakita Kazumi went into the fog on a Skulk and stopped being anywhere, and put a Crossing Blade into somebody on his way past. What came out of the fog, when anything did, was masked in blue-black, and the record from here calls it what the table called it: the blue demon.

They had Asako Taishi held and they were strangling him with the chain of the nunchaku. The blue demon put a yoroi-dōshi into him — the armour-piercer, dressed with the poison Kakita Kazumi had gathered on the road out of Loyalty Castle, eleven deadliness into a man who was immobilised and incapacitated and could not roll to resist. It went in at the chest. It damaged the heart. Then he put Skulk up again and went back into the smoke, and a little later he slipped away out of the fight entirely, because his job was done and the target was either dead or dying and either way no longer an issue.

Nobody saw it. Doji Setsuna had turned for the Castle of the Swift Sword. Bayushi Monban was in the air with rope on his arms. Shiba Midori was in a duel. The party does not know that Kakita Kazumi owns a mask, and it does not know what he did with it. What the record has of the why is not his: an order had been given about this man by somebody extremely motivated to see him gone, and the wounds in him were not hatred.

The rest of the road went on around it.

Asako Taishi began screaming that there were assassins on him and that he was murdered. More smoke went in and the road disappeared entirely. Bayushi Monban ran out of composure. He jumped at a man he had already struck, weeping openly with the paint running off his face — ride on and forget about us, for I have become death — and missed, and the man went down begging, Lord Scorpion, I am not here for you, spare my life, and wet himself in front of his own companions. I do not see a Scorpion here. I see a Kuni Warden. He had not wanted any of it: these are not the enemy. The first enemy is the enemy.

They put him on the ground and started roping his arms behind his back, a proper tie and not a knot. From inside it he announced that he was an Emerald Magistrate and would not be bound. So sorry. But I do not answer to you. He went two range bands straight up with the rope still on him, hauling his captor with him, and headbutted the man's helmet hard enough to ring it. Later he took another of them into the air and impaled him on a broken branch and stood on him to hold him there, and got, for his trouble, the only straight answer anyone gave all day: if you insist on dying for my lord, I will oblige you. But my mission is accomplished. The traitor to the Empire.

Their leader came forward in black armour with no mon anywhere on it, a menpō painted a blue-black that takes the shadows, and the long-chained nunchaku he had been using to strangle the scholar. He put it to Shiba Midori plainly. Little woman, there need be no more death today. Save your own if you insist upon it. Turn around, walk away, and speak no more of this. If you continue, I will be forced to draw my sword — and you will regret that. She read the statement rather than the man, and what she read was that he meant every word of it, including the part about not wanting more death.

She continued.

Something blew the last of the smoke off the road for it, which nobody arranged. They bowed. He sat into his stance and drew and struck in the same motion, two-handed, and Striking as Water took her resistance to nothing and put seven through her. She answered with a void point and opened his arm to the bone, and he did not look surprised so much as reassessing. In the second round she could not outbid him, and the round's own strife tipped her over her composure, which handed him a finishing blow — and hers interrupted his. Fourteen, two-handed, against a man who had strifed out and could keep nothing at all. She took his sword hand, went up onto his shoulders, and drove the katana down; the helmet turned it into his vertebrae and through into his chest. You do not think she could move that fast was the look on his face, and then he slumped.

The four men carrying Asako Taishi dropped his limbs and drew on Bayushi Monban. Sorry, Lord Scorpion. But if you insist, we will give you a good death. In the end they picked the scholar back up and carried him off the road through the undergrowth, breathing badly and bleeding into the forest floor, which was what they had come for and not how they had meant to get it.

What there was to look at afterwards was the clothing: dark blue over basic padded armour, no better than travelling gear, every man masked, every man in a half-helmet, and not one clan crest anywhere on any of them. I did not expect it to go this way at all, Bayushi Monban said. He thanked Doji Setsuna, and they agreed to reassemble at the castle.

Setsuna

They had a description. It specified a man, and it was exact enough that they let go of a Doji on an Utaku warsteed rather than take the wrong person. Someone briefed them on Asako Taishi and did not brief them on the party; they did not know an Emerald Magistrate's escort would be on that road.

Dark blue, matched, over gear no better than a traveller's, with no crest on any of it. That is a company with a paymaster who can outfit it and a reason to keep the outfit anonymous. One of them named the man a traitor to the Empire and named a lord he would die for, which is more than anyone else gave up all day. Kakita Kazumi has seen the smoke tactic somewhere before, and where is worth asking him.

Two Emerald Magistrates asserted Imperial authority on the Emperor’s Road four times between them and it stopped nothing at all. The one thing that moved these men was a sword. She should not plan on the badge working in open country again.

She rode away, and she knew what she was riding away from. Bayushi Monban told her to go and she went, and a man who had claimed her protection that morning was being killed behind her while she did it. Following the instruction was a reasonable thing to do and it was still a choice, and it is the sort of choice a magistrate is supposed to answer for. She will not be the only one who noticed.

Asako Taishi is alive, taken, and still owed the protection she gave him in as many words. Whatever he was carrying, someone wanted it enough to spend a trained company and their commander on it, and to have him killed rather than let him keep it. The commander died on the grass without anyone learning his name.

And there is the thing she cannot account for, because she was not there and nobody has told her: the man she trusts to stand between her and a blade spent that fight somewhere she could not see, and the scholar came out of the smoke with a knife wound in his heart.
