Morning Glory Castle

Morning Glory Castle is located in the Phoenix Clan lands, due east of a notable site, and serves as a stronghold for the Phoenix Clan, recognized for its extensive arcane knowledge. The castle is a significant center for authentication and scholarly pursuits, making it a valuable destination for those seeking wisdom or validation of potentially dangerous discoveries.

Asako Taishi, a Phoenix Clan scholar, hails from the vicinity of Morning Glory Castle. His journeys included traveling along the Imperial Roads to the Shrine of Durogin and crossing into the Dragonlands. His path suggests a wandering quest for knowledge, particularly towards the Ikoma Lands, known for their esteemed libraries. The castle is regarded as an appropriate location for the authentication of potentially dangerous discoveries due to its reputation and the arcane expertise of the Phoenix Clan.
