Ikoma House Of Scribes

Lion

The Ikoma House Of Scribes is a significant structure within the Lion Clan's territory, focused on preserving and recording history and genealogies. It serves as a repository for vital records, including genealogical lines of the Lion Clan, military documents, and personal journals. The house is staffed by diligent scribes, such as Ikoma Ichigo, who meticulously maintain the extensive records. Researchers and historians frequently visit, seeking information on their ancestors and clan affairs.

Recently, Shiba Midori visited the Ikoma House Of Scribes to seek information about Akodo Kayamayako, a missing Lion Clan medic. Ikoma Ichigo assisted in searching genealogies but could not locate Kamiyoko in the records. Ichigo suggested that relevant information might be found in the military library at the Akodo War College. He later escorted Midori to the library, noting heightened interest regarding the magistrates' inquiry into the historical Battle of Snow Plain. The House of Scribes facilitated the mapping of dynastic lines, revealing connections between prominent families, and became crucial in gathering evidence related to recent military disputes and historical grievances of the Lion Clan.
