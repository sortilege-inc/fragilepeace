Clash on the Snow Plain

Session 12·2025-06-10

A Scorpion came down to the parley offering to make peace between them, and both generals refused her. She was not the enemy and she was not an ally, and nobody could read her at all.

The Unicorn Clan gave up the marsh. They pulled out of it deliberately and repositioned, which is not a retreat — a clan that spent a night preparing this ground does not abandon a piece of it by accident. In the Lion camp Matsumura Zane took first aid and put Warrior's Resolve on his own fatigue to get himself back to the honour guard, and Kitsu Somalia called Inari's Blessing to put something fortifying over the line.

Then the two armies met on the hilltop to talk. General Shinjo Kamo stayed mounted through it. Matsu Sakura demanded the Unicorn quit the plain. And a Scorpion came forward — Shosuro Amane — and announced herself as a peace role, offering to broker between them.

Both generals refused her terms.

Kitsu Somalia tried to take her measure while she stood there, and could not. She read for honour and for trustworthiness and got nothing back; Shosuro Amane stayed inscrutable, and then the parley was over. Nobody at the Snow Plain found out what the Scorpion wanted, or on whose behalf she had come down to a battlefield that was not hers.

While that was happening, Ikoma Akuyaku was crossing the plain in the dark. He took a calming breath in water stance and moved up under concealment, and no Unicorn guard saw him do it. He had a route: the flat under cover of night, then the rocks, then a belt of forest that would keep him off the Unicorn scouts' sightlines. It is worth saying plainly what that route climbs. The Old Diamond Mines are up on that forested hill. The battle was being fought downhill of the property.

The rocks beat him. He went at the climb, the rope failed, and he spent everything he had left on not being seen while he fell. He broke his foot on landing, well enough that it might as well have been taken off, and he went out cold in the snow — where the temperature that was going to kill him was also the only reason he did not bleed to death. Before he lost consciousness he put one shot into the Western Watchtower, knowing exactly what that would wake.

Back in camp Akodo Kayamayako told Kitsu Somalia to tighten up: with the guard rotations stretched this thin, Unicorn spies would be coming for the Lion command. In the night there was an alarm. By morning heavy snow had half-buried the tents and remade the ground, which meant every trap either army had laid was under a fresh white lid and none of them were where the maps said.

Then a messenger came in to say Ikoma Akuyaku was alive. Matsu Sakura had written him off. He arrived on a stretcher blue with cold, an arrow in his backside and his foot splinted, and he had the thing they had spent the whole engagement buying: a sketch map of the Unicorn Clan War Camp, and the shape of their scouting pattern, which was tight. Kitsu Somalia put Path to Inner Peace through him to get his fatigue and his strife down far enough to keep answering questions.

What he had to add was about the mountain. Snow had piled on the slope he crossed and slid; anyone coming down that way would be seen doing it; and the same snow was now sitting on top of the traps.

Setsuna

The Scorpion at the Snow Plain has a name, and it is Shosuro Amane. She was not in the line and not in the camp. She walked into a parley between two armies and offered to make peace, and both generals told her no.

Set that beside the ground. The mines are up on the forested hill and the battle was fought below them, and neither army appears to have known they were there. Not the Lion council, which spent two days arguing over that terrain and never once mentioned what was on top of it; not the Unicorn, who prepared the ground overnight and prepared the wrong part of it. The Scorpion knew what the hill was worth. The Scorpion were also the only party there working to keep it quiet.

That changes what Shosuro Amane was doing. A neutral offering to broker peace between two clans is one thing. A Scorpion offering to stop a battle being fought at the foot of a property she knows the value of, and they do not, is a different thing entirely — and it is the reading that explains why she came down at all, why she would not be read, and why she was content to be refused and go.

It also settles the archive. Ikoma Ichigo's account is not edited and there is no cover-up in the Hall of Scribes: the Lion did not write down the mines because the Lion did not know about the mines. The silence is ignorance, and ignorance is harder to attack than a lie and much harder to attack than a forgery.

So the present claim inverts. The Scorpion case rests on having known the value of that hill three hundred years ago — and they cannot lead with it, because saying so says in the same breath that they concealed it from both armies at the time. That is the pressure point. Whatever Shosuro Aishi argues in front of her, he is arguing around a thing he cannot afford to state.
