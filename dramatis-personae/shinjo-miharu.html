Shinjo Miharu

Unicorn

What is known

S48A Unicorn hostage, Harunobu's cousin, and at Setsuna's wedding. Blunt by nature and aware of it. Wants the war over so she can go home. Surrendered to Akihito trusting he would do right by her, and there is evidently something between them she did not deny. Convinced Kazumi cheated in the duel — she fought him once herself and knows he never healed from it. Once had a match to Kazumi floated at her and beat him bloody in the rain instead.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
