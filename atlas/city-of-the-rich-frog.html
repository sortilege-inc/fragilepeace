City Of The Rich Frog

The City of the Rich Frog is strategically located and divided by a river, with various clans exercising control: the Lion on the south bank, the Dragon on the north bank, and the Unicorn on the west bank. At the center of the city is Miya Tetsuya’s Palace, governed by Miya Tetsuya, an Imperial who has held this position for about a decade. The city is noted for its organized structure and serves as a hub of intrigue and trade, particularly in the illicit sale of magical items and artifacts. Local families significantly influence governance, shaping daily life and commerce. The City of the Rich Frog features the largest docks in the region, located on the Unicorn side. The climate supports bustling trade with various shops catering to a diverse clientele. A notable establishment is a Curio Shop off the main wharf, draped in dark cloth that obscures its windows. Inside, the shop is cluttered with strange antiques and curiosities, managed by an elderly proprietor with a long white beard. The Curio Shop serves as a meeting point for those seeking information and engaging in intrigue, containing a mix of Rokugani and Gaijin artifacts. The atmosphere is tense regarding illicit magic, as reflected in the Shopkeeper's cautious demeanor when questioned about spiritually active items. The City of the Rich Frog is known for its vibrant districts, particularly the Unicorn District, where colorful lanterns illuminate the streets. The Docks are recognized as a likely point for illicit activities, where samurai may gather to discuss plans regarding smuggled artifacts. The area is known for its potential dangers and secrets, attracting attention from various factions within the city. A local bodyguard, Kaeru Akiara, highlighted the dangers of The Docks after dark due to criminal activity. A local police officer is reported to be following visitors to the shop, leading to important interactions that uncover receipts linked to a mysterious buyer and a Fox Clan seller. Despite warnings about dangers in The Docks area, visitors continue to plan further investigations there. The city is currently abuzz with rumors about the author Hana no Ame, who is alleged to have traveled upriver, and the sale of Unicorn spirit magic within the city. Miya Tetsuya, who requires formal appointments for meetings, oversees this complex web of intrigue and commerce. The City of the Rich Frog also features vibrant merchant districts and Miya Tetsuya's Tea House, a venue for social and political gatherings. The bustling climate is marked by a mix of samurai and rōnin navigating the streets, reflecting the lively atmosphere amid ongoing regional conflict. The city is a hub for trade and diplomacy, with representation from various clans, including the Unicorn and Lion clans. Local authorities, such as the Kaeru Ronin, maintain order under Miya Tetsuya's oversight. Recently, Miya Tetsuya's Tea House hosted Shiba Midori and notable figures, including Miya Misato, who conveyed an imperial mission to negotiate peace between the Unicorn and Lion armies. They discussed plans to consult representatives from both clans within the city to locate the armies. Arrangements were made for a boat to cross the river to the Unicorn District, a busy area filled with merchants and samurai. Upon arrival, they sought information from local contacts about the armies' whereabouts and initiated tasks, including procuring ingredients for a delicate medical treatment. Additionally, the city is home to the Burnt Theater, which recently experienced a supernatural disturbance, contributing to its complex history. The City of the Rich Frog features a castle that serves as the residence for guests and dignitaries, including the Imperial Miya Tetsuya. The castle includes guest quarters for various individuals, such as Emerald Magistrates and a young lady believed to be a daimyo's niece. Recently, the castle experienced turmoil, with alarms sounding at night and reports of an attack within its walls. Guards conducted searches throughout the castle, checking the Guest Wing without explanation. The following morning, Miya Tetsuya expressed his displeasure regarding a compromising situation involving his niece and demanded explanations from the guests. Shiba Midori, an Emerald Magistrate, reported an assault in her quarters, where she was used as a weapon by attackers who had tied up others, including Miya Tetsuya's niece. Miya Tetsuya declared a lockdown of the island, stating that no one would leave or arrive without his orders until the assailants were found. The Emerald Magistrates agreed to investigate the incident as a courtesy to Miya Tetsuya, and interviews with staff and guests began to establish alibis. The city is currently experiencing unrest due to multiple shinobi attacks, prompting the governor to relocate his niece, Miya Misato, within the castle for her safety. Discussions among courtiers and retainers have revolved around escorting her westward to draw out potential attackers, with plans to join a contingent of Lion soldiers passing through the city. The castle's atmosphere is charged with gossip and strategic maneuvering as various factions seek to influence the situation. Recently, the Emerald Magistrates have confined two Crane suspects following accusations of involvement in the attacks, and an honor guard from the Miya estates in Unicorn lands has been arranged amid concerns for Miya Misato's safety.
