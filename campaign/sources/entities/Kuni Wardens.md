The [[Crab Clan|Crab]] order that hunts what comes over the [[The Wall|Wall]], and whose
Hunter visages — [[Onahime]] and the [[Lady of Decay]] — have been painted on
[[Bayushi Monban]]'s face, beneath the mask, since session 6. The tattoos are a separate
claim and a much larger one, and he has shown those to exactly one person: he opened his
shirt for [[Akodo Akihito]] in a Lion camp and closed it again before he left the tent.

Nobody in [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] lands is looking for a Kuni Warden. That is the whole
of the idea.
