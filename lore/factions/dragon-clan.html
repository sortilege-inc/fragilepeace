Faction

Dragon Clan

In the realm of Rokugan, the Dragon Clan stands as a revered faction, deeply rooted in philosophical teachings and spiritual connections. Nestled within their mountainous lands, which are vigilantly protected by the Dragonfly Clan, the Dragon Clan emphasizes self-discipline, introspection, and harmony with the kami. Their guiding principle, "Neither shall I," serves as a beacon of restraint and contemplation, resonating throughout the Empire and influencing the spiritual discourse among various clans, including the Lion Clan and Unicorn Clans. Characterized by their enigmatic and aloof nature, the Dragon Clan pursues enlightenment and balance with the natural world, focusing on personal growth and understanding of the universe.

The leadership of the Dragon Clan is exemplified by Senior Emerald Magistrate Kitsuki Kaage, who plays a pivotal role in mediating conflicts and maintaining order. Under his guidance, the clan actively engages in political matters, navigating alliances and rivalries to uphold their principles. Key figures within the clan include Kitsuki Wataru, an esteemed investigator known for his technique "Traces of Passage," and Shiba Midori, a celebrated shugenja and winner of the Topaz Championship. Wataru's commitment to justice is evident in his supernatural investigations, such as his examination of Asako Taishi, while Midori embodies the clan's values of compassion and protection, notably intervening in a conflict involving Bayushi Monban of the Scorpion Clan.

The Dragon Clan's influence extends into the political arena, where they recently became embroiled in a dispute over diamond mines claimed by the Scorpion Clan and Unicorn Clans. Kitsu Takeko, a powerful fire-aligned shugenja and daimyo of the Kitsu Family, addressed the Emerald Magistrates regarding the Scorpion Clan's assertion of an ancient agreement linked to the Battle of Snow Plain, a claim that lacks corroboration in official histories. The Dragon Clan's commitment to honor and the preservation of their ancestral legacy is reflected in their efforts to mediate conflicts without resorting to violence.

In their pursuit of understanding historical grievances, aspiring librarian Kitsuko Ayoko, who possesses potential shugenja abilities, guided the magistrates to the Ikoma Hall of Scribes, facilitating the uncovering of crucial historical accounts relevant to the ongoing conflict. The Dragon Clan's dedication to integrity was further exemplified during the arbitration at the Ancestral Shrine, where Kagi ensured that all parties presented their evidence for authentication, addressing conflicting accounts of the Ikoma Akuyaku journals and identifying forgeries. His actions underscored the Dragon Clan's unwavering pursuit of truth and justice, reinforcing their status as a beacon of wisdom and honor within the Empire, striving to maintain order, safeguard the innocent, and mediate between warring factions.

However, the Dragon Clan's reputation faced challenges when Mirumoto Isshin, a prominent bushi of the clan, became embroiled in legal troubles for the alleged murder of a Lion Clan samurai. This incident highlighted the ongoing tensions between the Dragon Clan and other factions, particularly the Lion Clan and the Scorpion Clan. Recently, Isshin was forcibly brought into a courtroom by Lion bushi, creating a public disturbance that drew attention to the clan's intricate involvement in the political landscape of the realm. Bayushi Monban, a Scorpion Emerald Magistrate, asserted jurisdiction over Isshin, further complicating the already strained relations.

As the trial progressed, conflicting documents regarding the Battle of the Snow Plain emerged, implicating the Scorpion Clan in historical conspiracies against the Lion Clan. The proceedings revealed the Dragon Clan's commitment to justice, as they navigated the harsh treatment of Isshin under Emerald Magistrate authority, which included confinement in a harsh cell and sleep deprivation. The Dragon Clan's actions during this tumultuous time demonstrated their resilience and dedication to honor, as they continued to assert their jurisdiction in legal matters and uphold their principles amidst the challenges posed by rival factions.

Recently, the Dragon Clan has further solidified its role in the realm's governance and justice system, exemplified by the active participation of magistrates like Kitsuki Kaage, who is recognized for his persuasive arguments in court. The clan has maintained political alliances with the Crane, Scorpion, and Unicorn clans, while notable members, including Kitsuki Wataru and his son, investigate legal matters related to illicit activities and the protection of magical artifacts. Kaage has departed for Shiro Yogasha to report on a noteworthy legal case and collaborated with Doji Setsuna, a Crane courtier, on legal opinions. The Dragon Clan is also addressing issues stemming from hostilities with the Lion Clan and the increased presence of Shadowlands creatures in Shinomen Forest. A magistrate's son is currently investigating the sale of illicit Meishodo Talismans at the Unicorn docks, highlighting the clan's commitment to maintaining order and integrity. Dragon Clan representatives participate in diplomatic engagements, such as tea ceremonies, to gather intelligence on critical matters, including a renowned author linked to scandalous narratives, further underscoring their influential role in the realm's political and magical dynamics.

Moreover, the Dragon Clan has established control over the north bank of the City Of The Rich Frog under the governance of Miya Tetsuya Miya Amaya. Their magistrates, including Kitsuki Wataru and his son, are actively investigating the illicit appearance of “Meishodo” artifacts within Dragon lands, aiming to halt the trafficking of these dangerous magical items and maintain the integrity of their territory. Their inquiries have led them to a teahouse where various factions, including the Lion and Unicorn clans, converge, revealing the intersection of the Dragon's interests with broader concerns about the illicit trade of Unicorn spirit magic charms and the need to influence an author regarding a potentially scandalous narrative involving a Lion samurai. This proactive stance reflects the Dragon Clan's commitment to addressing issues affecting their standing and the political landscape within the region, as they continue to engage in medicinal practices and diplomatic negotiations, such as the recent tea ceremony at Miya Tetsuya’s Tea House, where Shiba Midori and Kakita Kazumi pledged to accompany Miya Misato in her mission to negotiate a peaceful resolution with the Lion armies in Unicorn lands.

In a recent incident, Shiba Midori reported an attack where she was used as a weapon by assailants who mistakenly believed she was their intended target. Following the attack, her quarters were ransacked, and a guard was slain outside her door. The Imperial Miya Tetsuya, Miya Tetsuya, demanded an explanation regarding the situation involving his niece, leading to a request for the Emerald Magistrates to investigate. Midori and Doji Setsuna agreed to conduct a courtesy probe into the incident. During the investigation, they discovered evidence of infiltration methods, including Bamboo Breathing Tubes and signs of a waterborne approach to the castle. The Dragon Clan's involvement in this investigation underscores their commitment to uncovering the truth behind the attack and restoring order.
