## Narrative

!lede [[Kitsuki Kaage]] cited [[Doji Setsuna]]'s argument as the most persuasive one before him. That is the case closed and closed her way — and the thing she actually asked for is still unanswered. *There has been no response yet to my request to have [[Shinjo Harunobu]]…* Then a tired [[Unicorn Messenger|Unicorn messenger]] found her with a package wrapped in purple. **Your husband sends you a gift.** A parcel arrived; a prisoner did not.

So they turned west. Into [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] country, to find out what the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] are actually doing out there — the siege of [[Gatherer of Wind’s Castle]] in particular, and whether anyone sanctioned it — and to get [[Shinjo Harunobu]] out. As Emerald Magistrates they are entitled to ask the first question, which conveniently takes them to where the second one lives.

Business first. [[Bayushi Monban]] asked [[Kitsu Takeko]] humbly for the return of a certain letter. He also watched her take delivery of one with a hasty sketch in it — the mahōtsukai the party has met before. A childhood friend of his is being sent to the [[Castle of the Centipede]] to try for peace, and if that fails he will go to [[The Wall]] and take his chances against what is behind it. There is more Shadowlands movement in the [[Shinomen Forest]] than anyone is comfortable with.

[[Shiba Midori]] petitioned [[Kitsu Takeko]] formally to take [[Kitsuko Ayoko]] as her yoriki. *Of course you may.* The girl marked the appointment by chasing a badly dressed servant out of [[Shiba Midori]]'s room with a broom — *I shouldn't beat them with a katana, that's not fair* — which is both correct about the katana and a good instinct about the servant.

They rode out with the [[Three Hida Brothers]] alongside them. On the road [[Bayushi Monban]] taught [[Kitsuko Ayoko]] how a court reads a person, and how much of [[Shiba Midori]]'s standing now depends on how her yoriki behaves. [[Shiba Midori]] told them the story of the [[Sword of Hiruma]] — Crab-forged, supernaturally pure, and now lost through treachery. [[Bayushi Monban]] hit the floor with his fist, announced that unless it was the magistrates nobody was to bother him, and went to meditate. [[Ryu]] and [[So]] exchanged a look.

And they made a pact: nobody speaks of their deaths, and nobody speaks of what happened to them at the Battle of the [[Snow Plain]].

The City of the Rich Frog sits where the Three Sides River meets the [[Drowned Merchant River]], which is why it matters. They took a teahouse above the water and were welcomed by [[Ikoma Kotone]], the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] tea mistress, who was pleased to see [[Doji Setsuna]] again. The room held a young Lion woman and a monk in [[Dragon Clan|Dragon]] colours, and the gossip in it had the scandalous author [[Hana no Ame]] somewhere in the city.

[[Doji Setsuna]] worked the room. She offered [[Matsu Koda]] her help with the author problem, and asked the Dragon magistrate about the trade in illicit [[Meishodo Talismans]].

## Setsuna

**She won the argument and did not get the thing.** [[Kitsuki Kaage]] named her reasoning the most persuasive in the case; her husband's custody request has still had no answer. Those two facts arriving in the same morning are the correct measure of what her office is worth: it moves rulings, not gaolers. What actually moved [[Shinjo Harunobu]] last time was [[Kitsu Takeko]] agreeing on the strength of [[Akodo Akihito]]'s service — obligation, not authority. She should keep working that channel and stop expecting the badge to do it.

**The gift is a message and she should read it as one.** He is well enough to send something, permitted to send it, and being handled by people willing to let a parcel through. That last part is the useful information.

**Going west is right and the cover is good.** An Emerald Magistrate asking whether the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] siege of [[Gatherer of Wind’s Castle]] was sanctioned is doing her job, and it happens to put her in [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] country where her husband's family are. Nobody can object to the first thing in order to obstruct the second.

**The pact worries me on her behalf.** They have agreed to conceal their deaths and what happened at the [[Snow Plain]]. She has just spent six sessions in an arbitration where that experience *was her testimony*, given in open court, on the record, in front of [[Kitsuki Kaage]] and both envoys. The secret is already out; what the pact does now is commit them all to a consistent story about a thing several magistrates have heard them describe. That is a harder position to hold than telling it plainly.

Two threads worth a line in the notes. [[Kitsu Takeko]] is receiving sketches of a blood sorcerer the party has already tangled with. And there is an illicit trade in [[Meishodo Talismans]] in this city — which is the second time bound spirits in objects have come near her, the first being the black talisman [[Matsu Morozane]] cut off a Unicorn shugenja's neck.
