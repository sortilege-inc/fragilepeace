Healer’s Tent

The Healer’s Tent is a makeshift medical facility established to provide treatment for injured soldiers. It has a communal atmosphere filled with activity as many soldiers seek medical attention. The tent is often crowded, leading to temporary accommodations being added to expand its capacity. Inside, Akodo Kayamayako, a medic, cares for the wounded, including Matsu Morozane, who is heavily bandaged due to severe burns on large areas of his chest, arms, and torso. The environment is chaotic, filled with the sounds of groans and urgent requests for help.

Matsu Morozane regains consciousness in the Healer’s Tent after being rescued from the snow. Akodo Kayamayako informs him of the severity of his injuries and advises him to rest. The tent becomes increasingly overwhelmed as more soldiers arrive for treatment, requiring additional space and personnel to assist. The atmosphere remains frenetic as the soldiers are tended to throughout the night. Matsu Morozane eventually leaves the tent, visibly injured and supported by a stick, as he reunites with his Lion Clan companion. The tent is a critical location for recovery and care in the midst of the ongoing conflict.
