## Narrative

!lede [[Doji Setsuna]] rode to the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] camp and came back with the
answer nobody in this campaign has ever had: **why [[Shinjo Altansarnai]] refused.** It
was not caprice. It was that the man she was offered had been made to throw away his wife
to be offered at all — and she found that out from the wife.

Morning at the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] camp first. [[Kakita Kazumi]] worked his forms at dawn
and read the place while he did it: the Lion practising their own style and none of them
with his instincts, [[Matsu Tsuko]] out reviewing troops, everybody conspicuously not
looking at him — **and masked [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] moving through a Lion camp**,
which is not where Scorpion belong.

The Lion council could not choose a negotiator without knowing whom the
[[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] would field, so [[Doji Setsuna]] guessed for them, at length and
on purpose obscurely: the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] do not take Imperial mediation very
seriously and will send somebody competent rather than fearsome, so the
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] need not field their worst either — only somebody a little above.
[[Shiba Midori]] backed it beautifully. The officers were baffled, which was the point,
and [[Akodo Kage]] granted that the magistrates *add an interesting complication*: the
process levels the ground in a way that makes overkill worthless. They had been reaching
for [[Ikoma Ujiaki]], who is exactly the strong arm she did not want across the table —
and, to her private relief, not [[Ikoma Anakazu]], whom she wants kept for other purposes.

[[Akodo Osakuan]] — [[Akodo Toturi]]'s right hand, and [[Akodo Akihito]]'s father —
observed that there is something awkward about an Emerald Magistrate present here who is,
by marriage, subordinate to the Lion Champion's authority. [[Akodo Toturi]] considered it,
and did not say it out loud. Not yet.

So she asked leave — as a courtesy, not as permission — to ride and fetch the answer
herself. He gave it. She took [[Kogarashi]] out at speed with [[Kakita Kazumi]] and
[[Shiba Midori]] and a Lion escort, met a [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] escort at the midpoint,
and for the length of that ride she was **not being an Emerald Magistrate**, which she
has needed for weeks.

The [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] are camped at the forest's edge, about equal to the Lion in
numbers and much better placed — around them on three sides, with [[Moto Family|Moto]] and
[[Utaku Family|Utaku]] on the flanks. [[Shinjo Altansarnai]] received them with an advisor
from every family. She knew [[Kakita Kazumi]] and [[Shiba Midori]] already.
[[Doji Setsuna]] introduced herself as newly and gratefully married to her kinsman
[[Shinjo Harunobu]] — **and told her he was free, at the [[Castle of the Swift Sword]],
and that the Lion had praised how he fought.** She had not known he was ever taken.

Then [[Doji Setsuna]] raised [[Asako Ikari]], and asked how much of it the Champion had
known.

Her face changed, and the war finally made sense.

**[[Asako Ikari]] came to [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] lands to kill herself in
[[Shinjo Altansarnai]]'s court.** The Champion's men stopped her, and from her they got
the story: the previous [[Lion Clan|Lion]] Champion, **[[Akodo Arasou]]**, arranging this
match, had ordered one of his own daimyō to set aside and disgrace his wife — the mother
of his child — to clear the ground for it.

*I could not be expected to marry such a man. In doing so I would unravel my own family.
How could I dishonour his wife, and my own mate in the process?*

And she said the thing that makes it worse. **Had the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] simply disclosed
that the groom was not free to wed**, before the offer was made and accepted, she could
have declined politely and none of this would have happened. She freed [[Asako Ikari]],
told her she bore her no ill will, and knows that she and her daughter were guests at her
cousin the Eda's castle for a while. Where they are now, nobody knows. The husband has
never once looked for her.

Her terms are modest to the point of being disarming. The [[Lion Clan|Lion]] leave
[[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] land without further blood. The borders go back where they were.
Captives are released. *I don't wish to punish them. The dead littering the field are
punishment enough.* And she does **not** ask for the concessions the marriage would have
brought her clan — those she is now disinclined to give.

Then [[Kakita Kazumi]] said the quiet part in front of the whole room. She was offered a
man who was **already married**. That makes the offer bad faith, and bad faith voids the
contract entirely. How widely is it known? Nobody knows — **which makes it the single
thing the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] would least like made public.**

[[Shinjo Altansarnai]]'s mouth curled like a cat's. She had understood exactly what she
had just been handed.

[[Doji Setsuna]] then laid out the cost of it plainly, because it is her duty to. If the
Champion brings a formal accusation and it is affirmed, **[[Doji Setsuna]] would be
obliged to demand that the responsible party open his own belly.** And the responsible
party is [[Akodo Arasou]] — [[Akodo Toturi]]'s elder brother — **who is dead**, killed by
[[Doji Hotaru]]'s arrow at the gates of [[Toshi Ranbo]] with [[Akodo Toturi]] standing
there. There is no seppuku to be had from a corpse. There is only the shame, and the shame
lands on the living Akodo.

With the truth on the table they set who would sit at it, and the shape assembled itself.

The arbiter must outrank the negotiators. If [[Shinjo Altansarnai]] and [[Akodo Toturi]]
negotiated in their own persons, **no magistrate alive here outranks them** and only the
Emperor could arbitrate — which is precisely why a peace between Champions belongs at
Winter Court. But if both send stand-ins of lesser rank, [[Doji Setsuna]]'s standing is
enough and she keeps the bench.

And then a [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] advisor found the elegant answer. [[Asako Ikari]]'s
people, the [[Phoenix Clan|Phoenix]], are a **third injured party** — so there is warrant
to seat a [[Phoenix Clan|Phoenix]] to speak for [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] interests. Every
eye in the tent went to [[Shiba Midori]], who accepted with as much dignity as she could
manage over the top of undisguised delight. [[Shinjo Altansarnai]] looked at her for a
full minute. *You will speak for the Unicorn's interests.* She will be briefed on their
position, and the Champion will personally answer for any insult offered to either woman
while their yojimbo is absent.

Two other things came out of that room. The [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] learned that
**[[Shinjo Miharu]] is alive** — held by [[Akodo Akihito]] and never ransomed — which
went down badly. And when somebody joked about trading for [[Bayushi Monban]],
[[Shinjo Altansarnai]] knew him at once, by reputation, to knowing laughter, and reported
that only his horse had been recovered. [[Doji Setsuna]] claimed him as hers and said she
would fetch him from the west when this was done. **So the Unicorn now expect her yojimbo
to turn up on their side of the table.**

[[Doji Setsuna]] did try for a private word and was refused. It would look improper, the
Champion said, to treat privately with a magistrate before an overseen negotiation. So she
said the substance in public instead.

Back at the Lion camp they announced [[Shiba Midori]]. Three Akodo were genuinely
surprised; [[Matsu Tsuko]] smirked; [[Ikoma Ujiaki]] gave away nothing at all. And
[[Akodo Kage]] immediately turned the precedent around — if the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]
may seat a magistrate of appropriate standing, the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] should reconsider
whom they field rather than lean on the Crane on the bench. Which boxes the council
neatly toward [[Akodo Akihito]].

[[Doji Setsuna]] set her household to preparing the ground, and gave [[Kakita Kazumi]] the
security of it — because whoever wrecked this peace and killed the envoy may not have gone
anywhere.

**West.** [[Bayushi Monban]] went down under clubs in a windowless room and woke bound and
slung over a humped beast in the sun, with the spirit inside him basking in the heat. They
staked him out on a rock in an ocean of sand and an old ritualist began drawing the marks
to pull the [[Ifrit]] out of him and put it in a box. *Don't move when we bake you, or you
die.*

He prayed instead, and it came apart in his favour: a shaft of sun, black smoke out of
every pore, and **the [[Ifrit]] free and standing outside his body** — released by him,
on his own terms, exactly as he had promised it. The old man began screaming that the
seals were not ready and it would kill them all. And [[Bayushi Monban]], with his legs
still pinned and nothing else left, asked the spirit to burn him.

It did it gladly. The ritualist went up; a thug went flying and burning; the rest ran into
the dunes. [[Bayushi Monban]] took a club and went out again, and the last thing he saw
was the [[Ifrit]] running the old man down.

He woke a third time with five burned bodies around him and his fox chewing his ropes
apart. He gave the [[Lady of Decay]] the five corpses, and then found out what the fox had
done while he was ill: it had taken his shinies to a river goddess's shrine and traded
them for the water that saved his life — **including his Emerald Magistrate's badge**,
which is a vessel built to hold a fire spirit, now sitting at a shrine to water.

He walked back. Three days, no waterskin, arguing with a fox. He reached [[Khanbulak]] in
late autumn and went over the wall after the gates shut, to steal back his katana — and
found the [[Bayushi Family|Bayushi]] agent packing to leave for [[Otosan Uchi]], **taking
his sword with her**, on the reasoning that he would follow it anywhere and that
[[Shosuro Hametsu]] might pay well for that kind of leash on his most disloyal servant. He
was caught rifling her rooms, fled empty-handed, retrieved the fox from a dog, and went out
into the desert with water and nothing else.

## Learned

- Shinjo Altansarnai: Champion of the Unicorn, and she refused on principle. Asako Ikari came to her court to take her own life and was intercepted, and from her the Champion learned that Akodo Arasou had ordered one of his own daimyō to disgrace his wife to clear the way for the match. She would not build a marriage on that, and says that had the Lion simply admitted the groom was not free to wed, she could have declined politely and none of this need have happened. She freed Ikari and bore her no ill will. Her terms: the Lion leave, the borders go back, the captives go home — and she no longer wants the concessions the marriage would have brought. She grasped the bad-faith lever the instant it was named. She declined a private word with a magistrate on grounds of propriety, and she will personally answer for any insult given to Setsuna or Midori.
- Akodo Arasou: The previous Lion Champion and Akodo Toturi's elder brother. He arranged the betrothal and ordered the divorce that caused this war, and he is dead — Doji Hotaru's arrow, at the gates of Toshi Ranbo, with Toturi watching. No seppuku can be demanded of him. The shame falls on his brother.
- Asako Ikari: Came to the Unicorn court intending to die there. Spared, and told the Champion everything. She and her daughter sheltered at the Eda's castle for a time; where they are now is unknown, possibly with the Phoenix. Her husband has never looked for her.
- Akodo Osakuan: Toturi's right hand and Akihito's father. He was the one to name aloud the awkwardness of an Emerald Magistrate who is subordinate by marriage to the Lion Champion. Toturi weighed it and let it lie.
- Ikoma Ujiaki: The Lion's strong-arm negotiator and the overkill option the council reached for. Gave nothing away when Midori's appointment was announced.
- Matsu Tsuko: Reviewing troops in the Lion camp, and smirked at the news. She stood at the gates of Toshi Ranbo and watched Akodo Arasou die.
- Akodo Kage: Turned Midori's appointment straight back on his own council — if the Unicorn seat a magistrate of standing, the Lion should field their own rather than lean on the Crane. Which pushes them toward Akihito.
- Shinjo Miharu: The Unicorn did not know she was alive. Held by Akihito and never ransomed, which they took badly.
- Bayushi Monban: Freed the Ifrit himself rather than let it be cut out of him, and it burned his captors for him at his asking. Lost his katana to the Bayushi agent, who is carrying it to Otosan Uchi as a leash, and lost his magistrate's badge — traded by his fox at a river goddess's shrine for the water that saved his life.
- Ifrit: Free. Released by Bayushi Monban on the terms he gave it, and it chose to defend him rather than turn on him.
- Kogarashi: Ridden hard, and gladly, for the first time in a long while.

## Setsuna

**The war has a cause and it is not a grievance, it is a fault in the contract.** She was
offered a man who was not free to be offered. That makes the [[Lion Clan|Lion]]'s original
proposal bad faith, which voids it — and converts [[Shinjo Altansarnai]]'s refusal from
caprice into principle in a single sentence. Everything the Lion have done since rests on
an injury they manufactured themselves.

**She handed the opposing Champion the weapon, in public, deliberately.** She could have
kept the legal implication in her pocket. Instead [[Kakita Kazumi]] said it out loud in a
tent full of [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] advisors and they all watched
[[Shinjo Altansarnai]] understand it. That was the correct thing to do and she should be
clear-eyed about what it costs: the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] can now humiliate the Akodo
line whenever they choose, and the arbiter is the person who armed them.

**And the guilty man is dead, which is the worst possible outcome.** If a formal accusation
comes, her duty is to demand a life, and the only life owed is [[Akodo Arasou]]'s, and he
has already given it — to a [[Crane Clan|Crane]] arrow, at [[Toshi Ranbo]], in front of
his brother. There is no justice available here, only humiliation, and it will land on
[[Akodo Toturi]], who has dealt straight with her from the first minute and freed her
husband for nothing. **She needs an off-ramp built before the accusation is made** —
recompense to [[Asako Ikari]], a private acknowledgement, something folded into Winter
Court — because once it is formally on the record she will have no discretion left.

And she should sit with the shape of it. **A [[Crane Clan|Crane]] killed
[[Akodo Arasou]] at [[Toshi Ranbo]].** A Crane now arbitrates the war he caused, having
just dangled [[Toshi Ranbo]] itself in his brother's war tent. If anyone in that camp
assembles those three facts in that order, her neutrality is finished — and
[[Akodo Kage]] is exactly the man who would.

**[[Shinjo Altansarnai]] is not somebody to be worked.** She refused a private word on
propriety, which is the first time in this campaign anyone has declined to be handled.
Everything that landed on [[Shinjo Miharu]] and on [[Akodo Toturi]] — the warmth, the
confidence, the shared register — will read as manipulation to this woman and cost her.
**Deal with her straight, in the open, on the merits.** It is what she respects and, now,
what the bench requires.

**Her bench is on three sides of its own table.** [[Shiba Midori]] speaks for the
[[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]. [[Kakita Kazumi]] runs the security. [[Bayushi Monban]] will
arrive, whenever he arrives, on the Unicorn side, and the Unicorn have been told to expect
him. She arbitrates above all three of them, married into one belligerent and subordinate
by marriage to the other's Champion. [[Akodo Osakuan]] has already said the first half of
that out loud. **She should be scrupulous about the visible things** — seating, order of
speaking, procedure — because that is all anybody will be able to point at, and it is all
she has.

**[[Asako Ikari]] is still nobody's priority and she should be.** She tried to die of
this. She is the living proof of the whole case, she is [[Shiba Midori]]'s clanswoman, and
nobody has looked for her since she left the Eda's castle with her daughter. Find her.
Everything else in this negotiation is argument; she is the fact.

And [[Kakita Kazumi]] saw masked [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] walking through a
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] camp at dawn. Given who a ghost said sent the men who killed the envoy,
that is the most actionable thing anyone noticed this week, and it was noticed by the man
nobody in that camp will speak to.
