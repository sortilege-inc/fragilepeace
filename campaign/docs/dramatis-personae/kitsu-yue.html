Dramatis Persona

Kitsu Yue

Lion

What is known

S8An armoured spirit with a katana and a fresh wound in her side, carrying something she cannot get out. Named by Kitsu Ayoko on sight. She was banished almost as soon as she appeared.
S9Named by Kitsu Taigen as the Kitsu who saved Matsu Sakura at the Snow Plain, and the ancestor he believes is reaching for Ayoko.
S12Argued against waiting, and for acting soon.
S14Kitsu Somalia's bodyguard. Asked to take a duel aimed at her commander, and Somalia heard her ask in two voices at once — the woman present and the descendant behind her eyes. Put Utaku Mangiano on the ground in a single exchange. This is the Kitsu whose intervention Kitsu Taigen's line still owes a karmic debt for, and Setsuna has now watched her fight.
S15Pulled Morozane out of the snow and dropped into a convulsive fit beside him, seen by nobody but him. Put the supply crisis in front of the council, argued against dividing the army and was shouted down, and took command of Somalia's cohort at the hill so Somalia could work as a shugenja.
S16Prayed to live to a good death and called the ancestors instead, without knowing she could. Armoured Lion ancestors crossed the field and cut into the Unicorn; she could not hold them, took a spear and went down. The army now whispers she is a foreign sorcerer. She is not a shugenja and was never detected as one, and the ancestors say it is something about her.
S17Got Somalia out of the snow. The Emperor's heralds arrived mid-working and broke her concentration.
S18Her record leaves more unsaid than it says and then ends on three lines — "I gave my name and I lost my honour in the snow. The sting is in the words the children learn. Come spring, the world changes again." Nothing follows. It has the form of a death poem and nobody records how she died.
S21Her honour is what the party now means to restore, by Imperial finding rather than argument. Setsuna and Ayoko have discussed calling her spirit to attest to or repudiate an account she may have written after the battle.
S26Kitsu Ayoko's ancestor, and by her descendant's account the true hero of the Snow Plain for the Lion. She carried guilt for winning the battle by means she thought unfair, and it was she who did something at the end of the fighting that caused the histories to be lost. She sought out a descendant to correct the record. She also believed she was protecting Kitsu Somalia — and her part in recovering certain Kitsu shugenja techniques was credited to Somalia instead.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
