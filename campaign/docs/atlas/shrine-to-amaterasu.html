Shrine to Amaterasu

The Shrine to Amaterasu is located in the Centipede Lands and is a significant spiritual site within Rokugan. It is characterized by serene surroundings, featuring beautiful golden ginkgo trees. The shrine is dedicated to Amaterasu, the sun goddess, and serves as a location for worship and communion with ancestor spirits. Rituals and offerings are made here to honor the divine and seek guidance from the past.

The shrine plays a role in local gatherings, being referenced by the Emerald Magistrates during their stay at a post road station near a small lake. It is considered one of the most beautiful places in Rokugan, praised for its splendor by notable figures such as Shiba Midori and Bayushi Monban. The shrine is a focal point for spiritual reflection and connection to ancestry.
