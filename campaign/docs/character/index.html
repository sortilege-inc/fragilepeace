A courtier of the Crane, the people closest to her, and the ancestor whose battle she has testified to. Read a dossier, or open the sheet and play.

Crane Clan

Doji Setsuna

Doji Bureaucrat · Courtier · Rank 3

Emerald Magistrate and Personal Advisor to the Imperial Envoy, of Imperial Bloodline. Taught by her father to wield the law as the Kakita wield the sword — one correct argument, like one cut, should be fatal.

Shinjō Harunobu

Hida Battle Leader · Bushi · Rank 3

Setsuna's husband. A Unicorn commander trained in a Crab school, strategist under Utaku Kamoko, and a prisoner of the Lion from the second session until the forty-eighth.

Asahina Jūjirō

Asahina Artificer · Artisan · Rank 2

Master Artisan by the gift of an Imperial consort, and a maker of things with something awake inside them. Betrothed against the grain, and famous far past anything their rank can protect.

Unicorn Clan

Shinjō Anzu

Iuchi Meishōdō Master · Artisan · Rank 1

Harunobu's older sister. A Shinjō raised into an Iuchi school, who binds spirits into objects rather than calling on them, and makes what she makes to be worn on a horse. Not a campaign character.

Lion Clan

Matsu Morozane

Matsu Beastmaster · Bushi · Rank 2

Setsuna's ancestor, and the Snow Plain sessions are played as him. A beastmaster who fights beside a lion and who called the Unicorn commander a cowardly dog in front of the man's own troops; his surviving records are about weather and supply, and almost never about the fighting.
