General’s Tent

The General’s Tent stands as the strategic heart of the Lion Clan's military operations, nestled on a battlefield recently transformed by a relentless snowstorm. This temporary command center, a sprawling structure of thick fabric and wooden supports, is filled with the sounds of urgent discussion and the shuffling of soldiers. Inside, the air is heavy with tension and the scent of damp wool, as military leaders, including the resolute General Matsu Sakura, gather to plot their next moves against the Unicorn Clan.

The tent has been expanded to accommodate the growing number of personnel, including those requiring medical attention from the harsh winter conditions. Flickering lanterns cast a warm glow against the cold, stark backdrop of the snow-covered landscape outside, where treacherous terrain lies concealed beneath the white blanket—terrain that the Unicorn Clan knows well but is foreign to the Lion Clan. The atmosphere is communal yet fraught with anxiety, as soldiers seek guidance and reassurance from their leaders amidst the biting cold that saps their strength.

During a crucial morning meeting, the arrival of Ikoma Akuyaku, a frostbitten scout, disrupts the proceedings. His grim report reveals that the Unicorn Clan remains in high spirits and ready for battle, despite their potential numerical disadvantage. This news prompts urgent discussions about the risks posed by the extreme cold, the careful deployment of troops, and the strategic use of cavalry in the challenging mountainous terrain. As medical personnel tend to Ikoma Akuyaku, the generals deliberate on the best course of action, weighing options that include flanking maneuvers led by Kitsu Somalia and Matsu Morozane, as well as the possibility of parleying with the enemy.

The General’s Tent is not merely a hub of military strategy; it is a vital lifeline for the Lion Clan, coordinating efforts amidst blizzard conditions and the looming threat of Unicorn Clan reinforcements. Here, the fate of the Lion Clan hangs in the balance, as leaders confront poisoned supplies, low morale, and the shadow of a spy within their ranks, all while preparing for an imminent offensive to seize the strategically important Central Hill.
