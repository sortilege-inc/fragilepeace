A [[Dragonfly Clan]] samurai, and the man [[Tonbo Kuma]] was playing go against when the
magistrates arrived at the riverbank shrines in session 32. He excused himself from the
match and took no further part in anything.

He is not the novelist. The export's page for him merged two people with the same family
name — this man, and [[Shinjo Higuchi]], who writes as [[Hana no Ame]] and who was
possessed — and described both of them as one character. The 2025-12-15 and 2025-12-22
recordings keep them apart: [[Tonbo Kuma]] names his opponent at the board, and separately
gives [[Shinjo Higuchi]] as the name of the person asking how people who are not shugenja
reach the spirit world.
