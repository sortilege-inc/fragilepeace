Miya Family

Imperial

The Miya are a prominent faction within the Emerald Empire, serving as couriers and messengers. They maintain critical communication between various clans and officials, ensuring the flow of information across the land. The Miya are known for their speed and reliability, often traveling great distances to deliver important missives.

In the year 1123, a Miya rider inquires about Bayushi Monbon in Otosan Uchi, underscoring their involvement in delivering messages related to key political figures. Recently, a Miya rider carries a Phoenix-sealed missive from an Asako daimyo, which is offered to Doji Setsuna for delivery to Monbon. This occurs amid heightened tensions between the Lion and Unicorn clans and the organization of the Emerald Championship. The Miya play an ongoing role in facilitating communication necessary for navigating clan disputes and alliances, remaining integral to the political dynamics influenced by the Lion Clan's recent military victories.
