Exorcism Vessel

The Exorcism Vessel is an object prepared to address a spirit problem affecting a friend of Asahina Nao. It is intended to facilitate the removal or management of spiritual disturbances. The details regarding its appearance, composition, or specific properties are not provided.

Asahina Nao informed Kakita Kazumi that the vessel had been prepared for an upcoming exorcism.
