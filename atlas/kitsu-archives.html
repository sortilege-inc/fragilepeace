Kitsu Archives

Lion

The Kitsu Archives, a revered repository of historical and ancestral knowledge, is nestled within the imposing walls of Kitsu Keep, serving as a crucial bastion of the Lion Clan's extensive history. This esteemed archive is dedicated to the meticulous preservation of historical records, scrolls, and manuscripts, organized into distinct wings that honor the prominent Kitsu, Akodo, and Matsu families. The atmosphere within the archives is serene, characterized by the gentle rustle of scrolls and the soft echo of footsteps on polished wooden floors, inviting scholars and historians to delve into the rich narrative of the clan.

A prominent feature of the Kitsu Archives is the Ikoma Hall of Scribes, where detailed records of battles, court stories, and significant historical accounts are cataloged with precision. This hall has gained particular importance during investigations, especially concerning the Battle of the Snow Plain, where the Emerald Magistrates sought to unravel the complexities of past events. Guided by Kitsuko Ayoko, an aspiring librarian and adopted member of the Kitsu Family, the magistrates explored the archives with the blessing of Lady Kitsu Takeko. Here, they encountered Ikoma Ichigo, a knowledgeable historian who provided critical insights into missing official records and witness accounts related to the battle.

The Kitsu Archives illuminate the Lion Clan's narrative while revealing gaps in documentation that raise questions about the historical accuracy of certain events and figures. Discrepancies in records concerning the contributions of generals like Ikoma Hideri and Akodo Kagekatsu during the Battle of the Snow Plain have sparked speculation about potential redactions in the Lion Clan's histories. Additionally, the accounts of Kitsu Somalia lack essential details about the battle and the Diamond Mines, further complicating the clan's historical tapestry.

As a central hub for gathering evidence and testimonies, the Kitsu Archives play a pivotal role in the ongoing research efforts of the Emerald Magistrates, who prepare for the arrival of envoys from the Scorpion Clan and Unicorn Clan. The archives house not only official histories but also personal writings, including Ikoma Akuyaku’s journal, which features secret writings related to the Scorpion Clan. This critical information positions the Kitsu Archives as an essential location for uncovering histories that may influence ongoing negotiations and political dynamics among the clans.

Recently, key figures such as Doji Setsuna, Bayushi Monban, Kakita Kazumi, and Shiba Midori convened in the Kitsu Archives to deliberate on the Snow Plain dispute between the Unicorn Clan and the Scorpion Clan. They examined Akiyaku's journal for insights, acknowledging its significance. Monban claimed Scorpion Clan agents were involved in the Unicorn Clan's victory, while others insisted on the necessity of documented proof to support any claims. Setsuna discussed strategies for gathering evidence, including consultations with historians and forgers, while recognizing the time constraints posed by the clans' impending arrival.

The discussions in the archives shifted from academic discourse to practical arrangements, with individuals aligning their efforts towards presenting a unified case. The Kitsu Archives functioned as a critical nexus for political maneuvering and strategic planning among the clans, solidifying its importance in the broader landscape of the game world.
