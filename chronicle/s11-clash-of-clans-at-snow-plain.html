Clash of Clans at Snow Plain

Session 11·2025-06-03

The whole line went forward to make the Unicorn show their commanders. That was the stated purpose. The other purpose was to give one man in the dark somewhere else to be.

Akodo Toronoko laid the field out to be taken. A thin elite centre under her own hand, offered as bait for a frontal charge, and both cohorts thickened to hold what the charge would open — Matsu Morozane on the left where the rice paddies and the hills were, Kitsu Somalia on the right where the river runs, which put a shugenja of the Kitsu within reach of the water kami and was not an accident.

Matsu Sakura's order was to test their mettle and make the Unicorn Clan show who was giving their orders. It was also cover. While the whole line was making noise, Ikoma Akuyaku went into the Unicorn Clan War Camp in the dark.

The first exchange went to the Lion. Kitsu Somalia came off the riverbank into the Unicorn right and put three measures of panic through it. Matsu Morozane took a Lightning Raid straight into the cohort facing him, broke its order, and set the Touchstone of Courage behind it to hold what he had taken. The Unicorn answered hard and both sides lost men, and the round closed with the Lion still standing on their ground and the centre still not committed, which is what a bait centre is for.

In the second, Matsu Sakura gave Kitsu Somalia the lead and she spent Void on it, and the Unicorn right thinned again. Then the Unicorn stopped fighting like cavalry. Red-robed men came forward and threw explosive firebombs into Kitsu Somalia's troops, and their commander turned a whirling incantation into fire and put it across the cohort. That is not a horse clan's battle. That is a prepared position spending prepared weapons.

Ikoma Akuyaku went through the camp unseen, which is what the Ikoma Shadow School is for and what everything else that morning had been arranged to permit.

Out on the field Matsu Morozane did the opposite. He called the Unicorn commander a cowardly dog in front of the man's own troops — a formal challenge, staked on honour and glory. His lion Shigo no Chinmoku went at the Unicorn Officer's horse and drove it off, which put the officer on the ground and made the challenge answerable. Matsu Morozane worked his head before he worked his body, closed his stances down to the ones he wanted, and struck first and struck true. The Unicorn Officer came back at him and took a great deal of him with it.

The battle held there for a moment: both Lion flanks engaged, the centre unspent, the camp compromised. Ikoma Akuyaku moved again in the dark and put another arrow into it.

Setsuna

The Lion Clan ran Ikoma Akuyaku into the Unicorn Clan War Camp at the Snow Plain, and the attack that morning was ordered partly to permit it. That is the case, and the Hall of Scribes does not have it. Ikoma Ichigo's account carries no infiltration and no diversion at all. This one the Lion certainly knew about — a general who orders a diversion is not ignorant of it — so its absence from the record is a decision somebody took afterward, and a narrower one than it looks: it keeps a covert operation out of a victory account, which is ordinary.

The second fact is nearly as good. The Unicorn Clan on that field fought with prepared firebombs and battlefield sorcery out of a fortified camp on ground they had chosen. Whatever the deed says the mines were won by, it was not a cavalry engagement.

The memory gives her both of these from the inside, and neither of them is written down anywhere she can put in front of an envoy. That is the problem to solve.
