## Narrative

!lede Two days left before [[Kitsuki Kaage]] arrives, and the party spent the first of them pulling on four different threads at once.

[[Doji Setsuna]] had written to [[Doji Hotaru]], [[Doji Masateru]] and [[Akodo Masami]] about her husband's ransom. What came back named the man: the force holding [[Shinjo Harunobu]] is under [[Akodo Sakuon]], one of [[Akodo Toturi]]'s principal generals, and both of them are known to be reasonable and honourable. [[Akodo Sakuon]] is also [[Akodo Akihito]]'s father, and [[Akodo Akihito]] is connected to both [[Bayushi Monban]] and [[Kakita Kazumi]]. That is a chain of obligation running from this castle to the man holding her husband.

[[Bayushi Monban]] went to the quartermaster at the [[Akodo War College]] and asked for the rosters, and got a helpful expert who pulled both the outgoing and the returning lists of the Blood of the Lioness. Cross-referenced, they show the army lost about two-thirds of its men to death or other causes. They record valiant efforts by one physic, [[Akodo Kayamayako]], to save as many lives as could be saved. And the quartermaster's own report carries what no personal journal admits: an incident of poisoning or contamination of the camp's water, dysentery and similar diseases uncommon in winter running through the army, and a quantity of food thrown out for being tainted and mouldy.

Then he asked how a journal gets into a library. Personal recollections are gifted on a samurai's death, as part of the death rites, and catalogued by the archivists; military reports are submitted after a battle and matched against the rosters. So he asked what the penalty would be for altering one afterwards — say by a family member who did not care for what was in it.

*I could not imagine such a thing, because it would not be done.*

He pressed anyway, badly — courtesy is not a virtue he has any patience for, and it showed — and the answer closed like a door. *No samurai worth their salt ever suggests something so dishonourable as keeping such a secret, or falsifying the sacred last will and testament of another samurai, no matter how embarrassing the situation.* And then: *No Lion would ever suggest such a thing. If I were you, I would never breathe anything of the sort again.* The reports went into his chest, with instructions to have them copied and returned. *I have a class to instruct.*

[[Shiba Midori]] went after the medic from the other end, because [[Akodo Kayamayako]] is the one who tried to murder her ancestor. She took [[Ikoma Ichigo]] into the [[Ikoma Hall of Scribes]] and they went through three centuries of [[Akodo Family|Akodo]] genealogy — and the Lion keep genealogy well, tracking other clans as thoroughly as their own. There is no such person. *I'm having great difficulty locating this person*, said [[Ikoma Ichigo]], of a woman his own clan's rosters thank by name.

What the genealogy did give them was themselves. [[Akodo Toturi]], [[Akodo Sakuon]] and [[Akodo Akihito]] all descend from [[Akodo Toronoko]]. So do [[Bayushi Monban]] and [[Kakita Kazumi]] — [[Akodo Toronoko]] and [[Ikoma Akuyaku]]'s second son married into the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]], and their third son married out into the [[Crane Clan|Crane]] about a hundred and fifty years later, and a great-great-great-granddaughter of that line married into the [[Kakita Family|Kakita]]. And the Matsu lines run forward to [[Doji Setsuna]], who is an indirect descendant of [[Matsu Morozane]]. The four of them are one family, and were one family in that camp.

[[Ikoma Ichigo]] wanted to know why the whole castle was abuzz about a battle three hundred years dead. He also said why he cared. *To my family's great shame, my ancestor Ikoma Hideri — her record of the battle went missing, and Ikoma Hideri herself perished shortly thereafter.* He had given up hope of her being vindicated a long time ago.

[[Doji Setsuna]] met him between the library and the dining hall and sang him a poem she had made — the traditional story of the [[Snow Plain]] with a second story showing through it — and chose to make the second story obvious rather than subtle. That the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] were made to treat it as a defeat by the [[Emperor Hantei Hantei|Emperor]] rather than beaten into one. [[Ikoma Ichigo]] smiled very broadly. *That is a most fortuitous and likely correct interpretation. The phrasing could use a touch of polish, but you are in the right direction.* Then she suggested that the medic nobody can find might be known under a [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] name, and left it there, and he walked off smirking.

[[Kakita Kazumi]] spent the day trying to forge a confession. Staking testimony costs honour equal to one's own rank and glory equal to the rank of the person impersonated, and is only ever called in if somebody challenges it in court. [[Akodo Toronoko]]'s hand he could have managed — clean and fine. [[Ikoma Akuyaku]]'s defeated him, because it is a deliberate mess. He went back into the [[Kitsu Archives]] to study more of it.

Which is how they found the thing. [[Bayushi Monban]] held a page of [[Ikoma Akuyaku]]'s journals up to a candle, and a second set of writing came up out of the paper.

Invisible ink, on almost every page, in a cipher old enough that a Scorpion of this century knows how it was built. Clean, careful, unhurried calligraphy — nothing like the public hand — as though written slowly, perhaps with the other hand. The plain text reads as bad poetry, something about a cow in a red field under Lord Moon. [[Ikoma Akuyaku]] fed the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] information through his public writings for a career of years and decades, and kept duplicates of everything he sent them in his own journals.

And in among dozens of writings, one unaltered account of the [[Snow Plain]] — in which he takes responsibility for the poisoning, for the intelligence that leaked, and for delivering the lost histories to the Scorpion.

Three agents, then. The one the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] knew about, working with the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]. The one everyone suspected, [[Ikoma Akuyaku]], whose wife defended him to her dying day. And the secret one, the medic. All three [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]], all three inside a Lion camp, and the Lion lost.

[[Bayushi Monban]] worked out what that means and could barely sit still for it. Without the poisoning and the leaks, the Lion would have overrun the Unicorn and the battle would have been finished before the Imperial heralds ever reached the field — and it was those heralds who made it a Unicorn victory. If the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]]'s bargain with the Unicorn was to secure them that victory, they secured it. The claim to the [[Old Diamond Mines|mines]] may be good.

The party agreed not to put [[Ikoma Akuyaku]]'s treason in front of [[Kitsuki Kaage]]. Announcing that a Scorpion fooled an entire clan inside its own house for a lifetime serves nobody.

Then [[Doji Setsuna]] took tea with [[Shosuro Imako]] and made an offer. She played the immaculate hostess and [[Shosuro Imako]] the impeccable guest — *I am sure I can help you resolve whatever vexes you, especially if it is a matter of historicity. That seems to be my specialty here among the Ikoma. They tend to over-elaborate what should be simple.* — and then [[Doji Setsuna]] said that there is a Scorpion chop on a document whose veracity she is not confident of, that it is a mere supposition, hardly worth mentioning, and that should she fail to draw it to [[Kitsuki Kaage]]'s attention, that would surely be worth the Scorpion's notice.

What she wanted in return was a name relayed. Hers, attached to her recommendation, passed up to [[Bayushi Kachiko]] — [[Shosuro Imako]]'s patron, and Imperial Advisor, in an office that has never existed before. *If the decision favours the Scorpion, I will be sure to let her know who is responsible.* Under all the finery the answer was yes.

[[Bayushi Monban]] sat through it with his eyes shut and his tea untouched and his feet tapping, sweating, and left without thanking anyone. [[Shosuro Imako]] saw him out. *Is your tantō shifting in its sheath uncomfortably? You appear to be in some distress.* — *We Scorpion sting. We do not enjoy being stung.* He went to the sparring ground and beat on whatever was there until he had nothing left, shouting about treachery and betrayal and the dishonouring of his ancestors.

[[Shiba Midori]] went looking for a [[Kitsu Family|Kitsu]] who could raise her ancestor for her, so she could put the questions to [[Kitsu Somalia]] herself — what the medic looked like, what scars she carried, how to find her — and ask whether a Phoenix descended from a Lion might be taught to call her own.

In the morning [[Kitsu Takeko]] took tea with [[Doji Setsuna]] and asked what she could do. Two things. Move [[Shinjo Harunobu]]'s custody from the front to this castle — *I understand your concern for your husband. If my spouse were a prisoner of the enemy, I would be equally concerned. I will write to my cousin, [[Akodo Sakuon]], and see what we can arrange. I understand his son [[Akodo Akihito]] was quite the hero of the battle, and took a personal prisoner of his own, also of the Shinjo.* It may take time to hear back from the front. And, on the way out, whether [[Akodo Masami]] is spoken for. [[Kitsu Takeko]] believes not, which is unusual at that age. [[Doji Setsuna]] is asking for somebody else.

## Learned

- Akodo Sakuon: Akodo Akihito's father, one of Akodo Toturi's principal generals, and the man holding Shinjo Harunobu. Reputed reasonable and honourable. Kitsu Takeko calls him cousin and has written to him about the transfer.
- Ikoma Akuyaku: A Scorpion agent for decades. His journals carry a second text in invisible ink and an old cipher — clean careful calligraphy, nothing like his public hand — duplicating everything he ever sent the Scorpion. Among them is an unaltered account of the Snow Plain in which he takes responsibility for the poisoning, for the leaked intelligence, and for delivering Ikoma Hideri's lost histories to the Scorpion.
- Akodo Kayamayako: Thanked by name in the quartermaster's rosters for the lives she saved, and absent from three centuries of Akodo genealogy. Ikoma Ichigo could not locate her. Setsuna's suggestion is that she may be findable under a Scorpion name.
- Ikoma Ichigo: Ikoma Hideri's descendant, and says so — her lost record is his family's shame, and he gave up hope of her vindication long ago. Could not find the medic in his own clan's genealogy. Read Setsuna's poem correctly and told her the phrasing needed polish.
- Akodo Toronoko: The common ancestor. Toturi, Sakuon and Akihito all descend from her; her second son with Ikoma Akuyaku married into the Scorpion, from which Monban and Kazumi descend, and her third son married into the Crane. She was blind for the rest of her life after the Snow Plain, and her husband wrote her records for her.
- Shosuro Imako: Bayushi Kachiko's agent in this castle, and a specialist in historicity among the Ikoma. Agreed to relay Setsuna's name and recommendation to her patron, in exchange for nothing she had to say out loud.
- Bayushi Kachiko: Imperial Advisor, in an office that has never existed before, and Shosuro Imako's patron. Setsuna wants her own name in front of her.
- Kitsu Takeko: Lord of the Castle of the Swift Sword, and Akodo Sakuon's cousin. Agreed to write for Shinjo Harunobu's custody to be moved here. She has now given Setsuna cavalry, an archive, a promise and a prisoner transfer.
- Akodo Akihito: Akodo Sakuon's son, a hero of the recent battle, and holder of a Shinjo prisoner of his own. Kin to Toturi and Sakuon, connected to Monban and Kazumi, and — through Toronoko — kin to all of them.
- Akodo Masami: The Lion yoriki who gave Setsuna travel papers unprompted at Loyalty Castle. Written to about the ransom, and answered. Unmarried, which Kitsu Takeko finds unusual at that age. Setsuna is asking on someone else's behalf.
- Doji Hotaru: Written to for guidance and aid over Harunobu's ransom.
- Doji Masateru: Written to for the same.
- Blood of the Lioness: The Lion army at the Snow Plain. Its outgoing and returning rosters differ by about two-thirds.

## Setsuna

The ledgers held. She said stores are counted, and they were — the quartermaster's own report carries the contaminated water, the winter dysentery and the mouldy food that not one personal journal admits. The sabotage at the [[Snow Plain]] is now a documented fact in the [[Lion Clan|Lion]]'s own hand, in the one kind of book nobody thought to be silent about.

And the quartermaster gave her something better than he meant to. A personal recollection is gifted on a samurai's death, as part of the death rites, and passes through the family before it reaches the archive. When she asked him what the penalty was for altering one, he said he could not imagine it, because it would not be done. That is not a denial. That is a man describing a procedure with an unguarded gap in it and not knowing he has.

Then the thing that changes the case.

**[[Ikoma Akuyaku]] was a Scorpion agent for his whole life, and he wrote it all down.** Invisible ink and an old cipher on nearly every page of a journal that has sat in a Lion archive for three centuries. He poisoned the stores. He leaked the plans. He carried [[Ikoma Hideri]]'s stolen histories to the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]]. And [[Akodo Toronoko]] — his wife, blinded on that field, whose records he wrote out for her afterwards in his own hand — defended him to her dying day.

It cuts in a direction she did not expect. If the Scorpion bargain was to deliver the Unicorn a victory, they delivered it: without those three agents the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] would have finished the battle before the Emperor's heralds arrived, and it was the heralds who made it a Unicorn win. **The Scorpion's claim may simply be true.** She came here expecting to expose a forgery and has instead found the receipt.

Which leaves the shape of the thing rather than the fact of it. Their claim is founded on treason against the clan whose archive she is standing in, committed by a man whose descendants include two of her own colleagues. [[Shoshuro Aishi]] cannot lead with that. Nor can she say it in a Lion castle whose lord has now been generous to her four times over.

The forgery is the one thing that would finish her. They can write what the record is missing because they lived it. An Emerald Magistrate who manufactures evidence in a case she is weighing is done, and so is every ruling she has made, and so is her husband's transfer.

And she should be honest about what she did over tea. She did not offer [[Shosuro Imako]] a favourable recommendation. She offered not to mention a Scorpion chop she doubts, and asked for her name to be carried to [[Bayushi Kachiko]]. That is a magistrate trading the suppression of evidence for a patron's notice, and dressing it as a supposition hardly worth mentioning. The dressing is the part that should worry her. It came very easily.
