Garden Of Virtuous Contemplation

The Garden Of Virtuous Contemplation is a rock garden designed in the classical Rokugani style. It features manicured spaces and an artificial waterfall, creating a peaceful ambiance suitable for meditation and reflection. The garden attracts those seeking tranquility amidst the complexities of court life and exemplifies the values of harmony and balance central to Rokugani culture.

Bayushi Monban, a Scorpion Emerald Magistrate, is often found meditating in the garden, reflecting on feelings of betrayal regarding his fellow Emerald Magistrates and his precarious position within the court. He centers himself before being summoned back to present further evidence. An assistant eventually approaches him to inform him that the court recess is ending, prompting Monban to return to his duties.
