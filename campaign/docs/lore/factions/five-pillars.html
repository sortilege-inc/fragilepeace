Faction

Five Pillars

The Five Pillars are a crucial faction within the Unicorn Clan, consisting of five prominent commanders, including Iuchi Gero, who hold significant influence over the Unicorn Clan forces. They are integral to the Unicorn Clan's command structure, providing tactical leadership and magical support in battle. The Five Pillars are associated with an aura of calm and protection, attributed to their use of talismans and shugenja abilities.

In a recent conflict, the Lion Clan targeted the Five Pillars. Akodo Toronoko, a Lion Clan duelist, killed one Pillar and captured another, while a third was wounded, which significantly affected the Unicorn Clan's command. Despite these losses, the Unicorn Clan advanced strategically to cut off the Lion Clan's supplies by seizing Crystal Pass. Iuchi Gero's protective talismans provided an advantage that helped the Unicorn Clan withstand harsh weather conditions that hindered the Lion Clan. Gero was ultimately slain by Matsu Morozane of the Lion Clan, impacting morale and battlefield dynamics. The loss of Gero and the resulting panic among the Unicorn Clan marked a critical moment in the conflict, though the Unicorn Clan forces regrouped and retreated to their fortified positions. The actions of the remaining pillars and the repercussions of Gero's loss are expected to influence the ongoing war.
