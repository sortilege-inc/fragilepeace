One of the [[Kaeru Ronin|Kaeru]] who keep order in the [[City Of The Rich Frog]] for the
Imperial governor. He declined, with complete courtesy and no give at all, to row
[[Bayushi Monban]] across the [[Drowned Merchant River]] — *I am not a peasant, sir* — and
then directed him to the pleasure district as the nearest place to find someone who would.

He is one of the [[Kaeru Ronin|Kaeru]] who would have duelled [[Bayushi Monban]] over the
insult he gave their lord. Their commander forbade it and said she would give him a proper
lesson herself. [[Bayushi Monban]] asked his name and wrote it down.
