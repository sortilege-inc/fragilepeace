Dramatis Persona

Ifrit

What is known

S33A fire spirit out of the Burning Sands, bound into a foreign pendant. Enormous, and it fought. It also negotiated — asked whether it had to go back into a person, said it would go if there was no other choice, and went into the jar with what looked like grief.
S34Talked down by Midori, who argued against enslaving it while it burned in front of her. It permitted its own sealing. Its box makes whoever touches it irritable and hungry for fire.
S39Agreed to be carried west to the Burning Sands and released there. Until then it will remain inside Bayushi Monban — on condition they leave the water-logged castle at once, which they could not.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
