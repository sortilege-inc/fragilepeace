Unicorn Officer

Unicorn

What is known

S11Rode out under horns, dancing on his saddle with a scimitar turning, and called Matsu Morozane a cowardly dog. Dismounted when Shigo no Chinmoku went for his horse, fought the clash in Water with two scimitars, gave up two fatigue and took four back. The Lion never learned his name.
S12Won the clash — first move, both scimitars, a blade at Morozane's throat and an offer of quarter. Took a crippling injury from Shigo no Chinmoku after the clash was decided, and was dragged off the field by his own troops.
S14The man who won the clash against Matsu Morozane two days earlier. Rode out on an unfamiliar horse to demand he finish it, and was answered with a lion and a deliberate deafness rather than a blade.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
