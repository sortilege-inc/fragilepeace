City Between the Rivers

A river city upstream of the City Of The Rich Frog, and a separate place from it — the export's entry opened by calling the two the same city, and they are not. On the 2025-11-10 recording the route runs from the City Of The Rich Frog to the City Between the Rivers and then south-west toward the Golden Yurts of the Ide; following the river north from here brings a traveller nearer Battle Maiden Castle. The party drew a triangle between the three and put the Lion army somewhere inside it.

Later, with the front moved, it is named again as the logical place for the Lion to have invested.

The Archivist export was taken before this entry existed. What the page holds is drawn from the sessions themselves.
