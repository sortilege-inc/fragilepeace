A shrine on the river west of the Empire, near the spring where the water starts and a
river goddess holds her court. [[Bayushi Monban]]'s fox left his Emerald Magistrate's
badge there in trade for the healing water that saved his life. When he came back for it
the river was running low in its banks, the trees were browning out of season, and the
water spirits were gone. Not in the Archivist export.
