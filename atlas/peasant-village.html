Peasant Village

The Peasant Village is located on a snow-covered plain and serves as a supply point for the Unicorn Clan forces. It features Rice Paddies that create entangling and obscuring terrain, which hinders movement even in the depths of winter. The area experiences bitter cold and heavy snowfall, resulting in challenging conditions for military engagements. The village is inhabited by peasants who may provide supplies for the Unicorn Clan army, making it a strategic target in the ongoing conflict.

The village was the site of a military engagement between the Lion Clan forces and a Unicorn Clan contingent. The Lion Clan forces aimed to seize the Peasant Village to cut off supplies to the Unicorn Clan and replenish their own. However, as the Lion Clan troops advanced, they were met by Unicorn Clan forces that executed a successful ambush, inflicting heavy casualties. Despite attempts to take the village, the Lion Clan forces ultimately failed and suffered significant losses. The village remained under Unicorn Clan control, complicating the Lion Clan's supply situation as they withdrew.
