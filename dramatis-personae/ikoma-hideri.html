Dramatis Persona

Ikoma Hideri

Lion

What is known

S18A Lion historian who survived the Battle of the Snow Plain and was assassinated before he could finish his chronicle of it. His absence is why the record of that battle is fragmentary. Neither Akodo Toronoko's account nor Matsu Sakura's mentions him.
S25His lost chronicle of the Battle of the Snow Plain was in the jar. The account he was murdered before he could finish has been in the ground ever since.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
