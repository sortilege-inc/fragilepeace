A family of the [[Unicorn Clan]]. Their territory, with the [[Ide Family|Ide]]'s, lies
across the river from the contested border, and the [[Lion Clan]] have asked for villages
and positions in it as part of a settlement — ground their army is already sitting on. A
demand for land under occupation is a demand to keep what is held rather than to gain
anything new.
