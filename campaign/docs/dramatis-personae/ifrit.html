Dramatis Persona

Ifrit

What is known

S33A fire spirit out of the Burning Sands, forced into a foreign pendant by a method no Rokugani present could recognise. Enormous, and it hits once, hard. Its void is poor — it could not breach a Threshold Barrier, which is the only reason the evening ended the way it did. It negotiated: it asked whether it had to shrink itself into a mortal again, asked to be freed, and said it would go if there was no other way. It went into the jar, and what the woman holding the jar felt was grief.
S34It did not surrender to persuasion. It gave up because the warding circle held, it had no targets it could reach, and it understood it was going back into a box either way. Its box works on whoever carries it against the skin: an hour of contact left Bayushi Monban stuck in fire and irritable.
S39Agreed to be carried west to the Burning Sands and released there. Until then it will remain inside Bayushi Monban — on condition they leave the water-logged castle at once, which they could not.
S49Free. Released by Bayushi Monban on the terms he gave it, and it chose to defend him rather than turn on him.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
