## Narrative

!lede [[Doji Setsuna]] stopped being the judge of this and offered to be its witness
instead, and the war ended inside the hour.

Before she called the tent back to order she took chapter and verse off
[[Kakita Kazumi]]. He had found the passage [[Akodo Akihito]] was leaning on — a
witticism about a duel without armies being called a war, and about losing a duel by
coming prepared for a duel when the other party came prepared for a war. Akihito was
playing on it and not using it as it was written. [[Akodo Family|Akodo]]'s own teachings
draw a hard line between a duel and a war, and there is a difference besides between
riding to another clan's land to issue a challenge and being the clan whose land is
ridden into. Eloquent, and post hoc.

She offered [[Kakita Kazumi]] the floor to answer it himself. He declined: the
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] do not favour him, having had their favourite son blinded in one eye
by his hand, and his speaking would cost more than it bought. He gave her the passage
and let her carry it.

She was forthright with him about what she actually wanted. She wanted the two clans to
settle directly rather than be ruled on, because a ruling would take honour off whoever
it went against, and she meant to make that plain to them.

So she gave the floor to [[Shiba Midori]], who put the Unicorn objection in one sentence:
it is rather self-serving to walk into somebody's house, challenge them, and then call
their defending themselves consent.

Over the recess [[Shiba Midori]] took tea with the woman who had been sitting at the
[[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]'s elbow all week and who now has a name. [[Iuchi Yukiko]] is one
of the grandmothers of the [[Iuchi Family|Iuchi]], here for [[Shinjo Altansarnai]] as
[[Akodo Kage]] is here for his own Champion — high standing, no authority to bind, and
present so that a daimyō's weight can be felt without a daimyō's word being given. She is
meishōdō, and a shugenja, so she and [[Shiba Midori]] understand the world the same way
and by opposite methods.

She asked one question and kept asking it. [[Shinjo Altansarnai]] sent [[Shiba Midori]]
for the land, the prisoners and the horses; was what was happening in that tent getting
any of them back? Then she said the rest of it plainly. The [[Lion Clan|Lion]] are in a
trap. Two [[Crane Clan|Crane]] are plucking at their fur, and the arbiter looks like
somebody who would rather be right than have a peace — and being right would not bring
back her dead grandchildren. What the Unicorn could give that cost them least was an
acknowledgement of a victory the Lion had already won. Marriages and bloodlines she
advised keeping off the table entirely, or they would all still be sitting there at
Winter Court.

The rumour [[Kakita Kazumi]] had planted was doing its own work meanwhile — that the
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] had opened the marriage negotiation while [[Ikoma Anakazu]] was still
married — and it had reached the entourages of both Champions, which is everyone who
matters.

Then [[Doji Setsuna]] made the move the whole session turns on. She told the Lion that if
both parties would rather reach a bilateral agreement, she would put her name to it as a
witness instead of handing down a decision — a representative of the Emperor attesting
that these were the parties and these were the terms. Not a judge's ruling. And nobody
would need to write to the Imperial legions about an unsanctioned war.

[[Akodo Akihito]] took out a fresh quill and a fresh piece of linen and started again,
briskly, like a man relieved to be doing business instead of pleading. If he did not have
to appeal to an arbiter, and could negotiate with a peer, that was a different matter
entirely. He opened with the borders: back to where they were before, near enough, with
any adjustment left for later.

The prisoners went next. [[Shiba Midori]] wanted them all home; the Lion wanted the
question deferred and wanted paying. They met at five. Five of high standing from each
side stay until the matter is closed at Winter Court — where those five would be expected
anyway — and everyone below that goes home. [[Shinjo Miharu]] and [[Shinjo Harunobu]] are
both of a standing to be among them.

Then the horses, which the Lion meant to keep. An [[Utaku Family|Utaku]] attendant said
the thing that decided it: they would not choose a Lion for a master, and they would not
hold their quality without the training and the bond they are raised to. [[Doji Setsuna]]
helped from the side, remarking to [[Kakita Kazumi]] on how fickle the animals are and how
much [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] help she had needed to learn [[Kogarashi]]. [[Shiba Midori]]
put money on the table rather than argue, opened low, and carried it.

[[Akodo Akihito]] then let slip what the Lion would want in exchange: some redress on the
original marriage contract.

[[Doji Setsuna]] asked him for a private word. Was he certain he wanted anything about
that marriage on a document she was going to notarise? Her honour is staked on what she
attests, and a thing untrue by omission is a thing she cannot attest to — so either she
would be adding the context herself, or he would be bringing her an agreement that did not
contain the passage.

The Lion withdrew it and put something else in its place. The [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]
would acknowledge that the Lion had conducted themselves throughout as righteous,
honourable and correct by the traditions of Rokugani warfare.

That is the trade, and both halves of it are face. The Lion get a victory and their
conduct affirmed, in writing, over the Emperor's witness. The Unicorn get their land,
their people and their horses, and nothing is written down about a bride who would not
come. Everything underneath — the betrothal itself, the fault for the war, the
territorial concessions the match was to have brought, the historical grievances, and
what is owed to [[Asako Ikari]] — is referred on to the Emperor's administration and to
Winter Court.

[[Otoya]] was at her elbow for the drafting. She wrote it out fair, and made it handsome
while she was at it.

Then she bound it. The delegates were made to stand for their clans, so that the three
sitting at that table carried the terms back into them, and [[Doji Setsuna]],
[[Shiba Midori]] and [[Akodo Akihito]] each staked ten honour on the thing holding. In the
doing of it she closed a trap of her own: the Lion were left with a choice between giving
ground and owing her, and she took the debt. Two boons, owed by the [[Lion Clan|Lion]],
banked and unspent.

The treaty was signed. The exchange of horses and prisoners begins within days.

Far to the west, [[Bayushi Monban]] finished what he had started in the troll cave badly.
He was plucked off the roof and held in a fist while the troll woman asked him, in broken
Rokugani, why he had burned the house of people who had fed him and given him somewhere to
sleep. He got loose, drew them out into the open, and the [[The Earthen Queen|Earthen
Queen]] came up the mountain and had them thrown off it. Then the Lord of Fire arrived to
inspect the palace he had been promised, found four of his own spirits unable to say
whether it was suitable, and was walked away from the question by a man who knew exactly
how burnt it was. His fire went down the mountain looking for other things to set alight.

He had gone into a house that took him in, burned everything in it, and had its owners
killed. Honour is internal, and nobody had to see it.

## Setsuna

**She stopped trying to win the case and started trying to end the war, and those turned
out to be different jobs.** Every session since the tent has been spent building a ruling:
the bad-faith lever, the unlawful-duel counter, the divorce that may never have happened.
All of it was sound and all of it would have cost the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] honour to be
ruled against. Offering to witness a bilateral agreement instead threw the entire case
away and got the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] everything they actually wanted inside an hour.
[[Iuchi Yukiko]] said the arbiter looked like someone who would rather be right than have
a peace. She was right, and she said it early enough to be useful.

The trade is clean and it is worth naming exactly. The Lion bought a written victory and
an affirmation of their conduct, over an Imperial witness. They paid for it with the land,
the prisoners and the horses — which is everything the Unicorn came for. Face is the
cheapest currency at that table and she has now spent a great deal of somebody else's.

The lever was her own honour, and that is the part to keep. She did not threaten the Lion
with a ruling. She told [[Akodo Akihito]] what she could and could not put her name to,
and let him work out that a clause about the marriage would oblige her to write down why
it was false. He withdrew it himself. Nothing was accused, nothing was proved, and the
most dangerous fact in the case never had to be spoken aloud in that tent.

Ten honour apiece, from her and [[Shiba Midori]] and [[Akodo Akihito]]. That binds the
three of them and, through them, three clans. It is also the largest personal stake she
has ever put on another party's good behaviour, and two of the three are people she has
spent this negotiation manoeuvring.

**And the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] owe her two boons.** Unspent, unspecified, and taken from a
clan that has just been handed its dignity back in front of witnesses. That is worth more
than anything in the treaty and it is the only thing in this session that is hers
personally rather than the Empire's.

What she gave up to get it is the whole of the case. [[Asako Ikari]] is struck from the
record along with the betrothal, the fault, and the concessions — all of it referred to
Winter Court, where she has now stacked [[Toshi Ranbo]], the [[Phoenix Clan|Phoenix]]
claim, the divorce documentation, and the woman herself. That is a great deal of weight on
one season, and [[Akodo Kage]] will be at it too, with his documentation and his patience.

[[Asako Ikari]] is still nobody's priority, and the agreement just made her officially
somebody else's problem. She tried to die of this. She has still not been found.
