Dramatis Persona

Matsu Sakura

Lion

What is known

S10The Lion commander on the Snow Plain. She refused a contest of champions as premature, asked her officers directly what a day's delay would buy, got no answer she could act on, and sent the battle line across bog to test an enemy already dug in. She is the general Kitsu Taigen's line descends from, and the one Kitsu Yue saved.
S11Ordered a probing attack under the objective of testing the Unicorn's mettle, held her heaviest troops out of it, and gave the lead to Kitsu Somalia in both rounds.
S12Demanded the Unicorn quit the plain and was refused to her face by a general who would not dismount. Wanted to pursue the withdrawal and was talked down. Approved the mountain flank.
S13Ordered the hill in the middle of the field taken despite the snow, and took it.
S14Changed the army's objective the moment the hill was taken — hold it and grind the Unicorn down for every attempt to retake it.
S15Named the crises — enemy initiative, poisoned stores with a third of the army down, broken morale, and a spy who knew the flanking plan. Sent Akodo Toronoko out of the camp to cool off before the council, refused a parley as weakness, approved the village raid, and sent a man with dead feet back out with the order to die well.
S16Refused the assassination in public and ordered Iuchi Gero destroyed in battle rather than by subterfuge. Counted three of the Unicorn's five great commanders killed or crippled. Kept Toronoko off the field and lost the use of her.
S18Gave the most famous battle of her career a single line — that the winter stole the victory. Praised Toronoko and Morozane, allowed Kagekatsu his courage, and omitted both Ikoma Hideri and Kitsu Yue.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
