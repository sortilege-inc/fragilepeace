Grandmother

Session 5·2025-04-08

She came in armour above the waist and a courtier's hakama below, a daishō at her hip and a fan in her hand, and left a pool of blood behind her as she walked. Nobody asked whose.

The Border Waystation sits between the Kitsu and Akodo provinces on a small lake with a Shrine of the Lake Kami on it. Three Kitsu samurai keep the post — two of vassal families, one a pureblood who placed himself, precisely, thirty-seven steps from the line of succession. A Lion gives his ancestry to that resolution and expects it to be useful. The hosts were surprised to be handed several Emerald Magistrates at once and said the papers would be read in the morning.

Over tea the conversation ran the usual course. A magistrate remarked on Doji Setsuna's southern upbringing and hoped the plainer chicken and beef of the waystation would suit; she answered that a narrow palate understands nothing, and told the table about a meal taken with her Scorpion mentor where the diplomacy happened between the courses. Shiba Midori said home is family, and she and Bayushi Monban found they were both missing the same people — a sister neither of them named lightly, and Lady Tsubasa. Shiba Midori said she caught a glimpse of the Moon Maiden in his eyes when he said it. Then she made the case that magistrates are the Emperor's voice and owe the people as much as the people owe them, in a figure about a bonsai: trim too much and the tree dies, trim too little and it is a bush.

Then a cold wind came through a tea house at the wrong season. Shiba Midori felt something and did not like where it was standing, which was next to Doji Setsuna. The three of them went out to the lake, and from there to the station's Shrine to the Ancestors with candles, sake, and clan animals Bayushi Monban folded from paper. Bayushi Monban considered taking off his mask for the rite. Shiba Midori told him not to — not all spirits are trustworthy — and that unmasking would offer a spirit the trust owed to family or to a fellow magistrate. The shrine's monk shifted at hearing an ancestor spoken of as a thing that might not be reliable.

Shiba Midori made the communion. The lights went out, gold ginkgo leaves gathered themselves into a shape, and Matsu Tomoe walked into the hall smelling of jasmine and leaving blood on the floor behind her. You are not Kitsu. Why have you called me here?

Then she saw her granddaughter. Doji Setsuna has never once come to the Hall of Ancestors to pay respect. She keeps a yojimbo. You are so weak that you depend on another's sword to keep your breath? Doji Setsuna bowed to the floor, named the neglect as hers, apologised for a sacrifice unbefitting the woman it was owed to, and asked to be told how to remember her properly. She was told: honour first, before everything, including when honour is inconvenient. She swore in the same breath to restore her husband's honour.

Bayushi Monban showed the scar on his neck. Matsu Tomoe brought her fingers near it and it burned him without being touched. You bear the touch of Jigoku. She ordered him cleansed. She ordered Shiba Midori to cleanse herself as well, for having come to the spirits with a bargain in her hands. Then she said that Doji Setsuna's companions are compromised, that they are walking through Lion Clan ancestral ground, and that she would walk with them and keep her eye on her granddaughter — and went back to ginkgo leaves and jasmine.

Bayushi Monban told the shrine monk to say nothing of what he had seen or heard. The monk agreed at once, and asked for nothing in return.

Setsuna

Three things came out of that shrine and all three are hers to carry.

She has an ancestor walking with the party through Lion ancestral lands and watching her specifically. That is protection and it is supervision, and Matsu Tomoe did not distinguish between them. The standing instruction is honour first, and it was given as a correction rather than as advice.

She has a debt at the Hall of Ancestors that she has never paid, now named aloud in front of two companions and a monk. It is also the only instruction she was given plainly enough to act on.

And her yojimbo is tainted by Jigoku — confirmed by an ancestor, not guessed at — and under an order to be cleansed. Bayushi Monban has not explained the scar. He may not answer failure with seppuku, he is forbidden that, and he is now carrying something that will keep failing him. One monk saw the whole of it and has been asked to be quiet.
