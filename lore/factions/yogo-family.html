Yogo Family

Scorpion

The Yogo Family is a prominent clan within the Scorpion Clan lineage, recognized for their shugenja practitioners and expertise in ancestral summoning. They are viewed with suspicion due to their secretive nature and esoteric practices. The family's influence is evident in discussions about contacting and honoring ancestors, with their techniques seen as valuable yet enigmatic in clan interactions.

The Yogo Family was mentioned during the Kitsu Archives debate regarding a collaboration with Soshi Yamako, a Scorpion Clan forger. This collaboration involved seeking samples of Kitsu Somalia’s writings to aid in creating a forged confession that would implicate the Scorpion Clan in events on the Snow Plain. The Yogo Family's knowledge of ancestral summoning was seen as a potential source of leverage for enhancing the authenticity of the forgery. Their involvement highlights their ties to the political maneuvering and disputes between the Scorpion Clan and other clans, particularly regarding claims from the Unicorn Clan.
