Dramatis Persona

Moto Gaheris

Unicorn

What is known

S42A one-eyed Unicorn family champion on a huge warhorse, leading the army up the southern road. Complimented Kogarashi, tried to reframe the meeting as a Lion surrender, and sat down anyway. Holds Monban and floated ransoming him back.
S43A one-eyed Moto Khan with five hundred cavalry. Opened with terms nobody could accept and gave ground the moment the envoy handed him a way to. His real contest is with the Shinjo for primacy inside the Unicorn, suspended while the whole clan musters against an enemy from outside — which is why peace here suits him. Dealt fairly on the ransom, freed Monban, returned his swords, and signed.
S45Served the audience obnoxiously spiced and named his own suspects — the Scorpion, and the Crane, on the grounds that the new Crane–Lion treaty cut the Owari Plains off from the Unicorn's allies. Had Shinjo Altansarnai in his camp and would not say so; his guards tried to deny she existed and then thought better of it.
