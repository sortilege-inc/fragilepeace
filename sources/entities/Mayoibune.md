The ship that is haunting the [[City Of The Rich Frog]]. It comes back about an hour after
Lord Moon's light touches the water and is gone by dawn, and it has never yet appeared
twice in the same place: the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] docks on the first night, the
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] canals on the second, the [[Dragon Clan|Dragon]] docks on the third.

The [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] samurai who boarded it found no crew aboard at all and heard
wailing coming up through the deck from below, and did not stay. The [[Lion Clan|Lion]]
soldiers who boarded it on the second night have not come back. [[Tonbo Kuma]] went aboard
on the third night and went with it into the morning.

Every ship that could leave the port has left. Trade has stopped, three clans' quarters of
the city are paralysed, and the [[Drowned Merchant River]] howls like [[Gaki-dō]] to
anyone who puts a hand in it.
