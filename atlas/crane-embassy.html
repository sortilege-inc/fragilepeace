Crane Embassy

The Crane Embassy is located in Otosan Uchi and serves as a diplomatic hub for the Crane Clan. It features lush gardens, though these were marred by a fire during crucial negotiations that necessitated an evacuation. The embassy plays a vital role in treaty negotiations, including a recent seven-year truce between the Lion Clan and Crane Clan clans, which ceded significant territory in the Asari Plains to the Lion Clan. The negotiator for the Crane Clan was Akodo Akihito, selected for his proximity to the Totori clan. The Imperial Advisor influences the embassy's operations, having appointed the mediator for the treaty, Otomo Genshin, who is known for being difficult to work with.

The fire incident coincided with critical negotiations, although details about the fire remain undisclosed in public records. The embassy has been a site for political maneuvering, relating to discussions on the treaty's implications for future territorial claims and the involvement of various clans. The beauty of the garden contrasts with the tensions surrounding the negotiations, and the embassy serves as a focal point for the Crane Clan's diplomatic efforts amid ongoing disputes with the Lion Clan and Unicorn Clan clans.
