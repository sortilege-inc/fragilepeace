## Narrative

!lede The night had gone badly. Unicorn cavalry hit the camp and came within reach of killing [[Matsu Sakura]] before the raiders pulled back. The village was still not taken, the food was still poisoned, and leaders were down. [[Ikoma Akuyaku]] came in with a fresh wound in his side, having gone into the [[Unicorn Clan War Camp]] on his own initiative to kill their general [[Shinjo Kamo]] — and [[Matsu Sakura]] refused it, flatly, in front of everyone. [[Shinjo Kamo]] was to be beaten on the field or not at all. She would not take him out of the dark.

[[Kitsu Somalia]] called on [[Tengoku]] and brought its warmth down over the camp, and the hallowed fire of it steadied men who had been shaking for two days.

Then the Unicorn moved. Scouts came in at a run: they had split, and one column was going for [[Crystal Pass]] to cut the supply line. [[Matsu Sakura]] made her decision and made it narrow — *I want [[Iuchi Gero]]'s head.* Their shugenja was carrying talismans that let their army ignore the ground itself, and if the talismans stopped the Unicorn's protection stopped with them. Three cohorts went at it: veterans under [[Matsu Morozane]], archers under [[Ikoma Akuyaku]], armoured troops under [[Kitsu Somalia]]. [[Matsu Morozane]] and [[Matsu Hisoka]] peeled off toward the pass.

It did not go cleanly. [[Kitsu Somalia]] reached for a wall of fire to funnel the Unicorn advance and the working came apart in her hands into loose flame, which panicked them anyway, though not where she wanted it. [[Ikoma Akuyaku]] put a massed volley into [[Iuchi Gero]]'s unit and made no pretence about what that was: *I want them moving around.* [[Iuchi Gero]] walked their talismans across ground that should have slowed them and put supernatural fire through the Lion line. In the snowstorm the Unicorn came at the Lion rear again and knocked [[Matsu Sakura]] off her horse.

[[Matsu Morozane]] tried Call the Wild and it failed, and [[Shiguro Chinmoku]] went in regardless, claws and fangs into the enemy commander. Then the lion took [[Iuchi Gero]]'s horse — savaged it out from under them — and [[Matsu Morozane]] came in with his katana, cut the talisman necklace off, and took the head.

The protections went with it. The Unicorn broke off and withdrew to their stronghold. [[Akodo Toronoko]] came back from her own part of the field having killed one of the Unicorn's [[Five Pillars]] and taken the head for a trophy, with another prisoner besides. The Lion had the day and one day of food.

And in the middle of it the dead had arrived. Armoured [[Lion Clan]] ancestors came across that field and cut into the Unicorn themselves — the working from the [[Kitsu Archives]], the one that will not hold without honour behind it. [[Kitsu Yui]] took a spear in the middle of it and went down. Afterwards she could not steady herself; the battle and the spirits together had shaken something loose in her, and [[Ikoma Akuyaku]] sat with her until she could. [[Kitsu Somalia]] went and asked the ancestors directly what they were doing there, and was told: they had heard her call, and she should take up what was hers.

Then [[Matsu Morozane]] handed [[Kitsu Somalia]] the black talisman he had cut off [[Iuchi Gero]]'s neck, and she felt what was in it. Something bound. Something that could be asked questions about the Unicorn camp, if anyone had the nerve to open the conversation. They agreed to do it that night.

## Learned

- Iuchi Gero: The Unicorn shugenja, killed by Morozane — the lion took their horse, he cut the talisman necklace and took the head. Their talismans let the Unicorn army ignore the terrain and carried supernatural fire. One of them was black, and there is a spirit bound inside it.
- Matsu Sakura: Refused Akuyaku's assassination of Shinjo Kamo outright and in public — the enemy general was to be beaten on the field or not at all. Then ordered a shugenja killed by three cohorts, which she did not consider the same thing.
- Ikoma Akuyaku: Went into the Unicorn camp on his own initiative to kill Shinjo Kamo, and came back wounded and rebuked. Ordered a massed volley into Gero's unit with no pretence of honourable tactics. Sat with Kitsu Yui afterwards until she was steady.
- Akodo Toronoko: Killed one of the Unicorn's Five Pillars and brought the head back as a trophy, and took another prisoner.
- Kitsu Somalia: Drew Tengoku's warmth over a freezing camp to hold its morale together. Her wall of fire failed. The ancestors came because she called them, and told her so.
- Kitsu Yui: Speared and knocked down during the ancestral charge, and badly shaken afterwards by the spirits as much as the wound.
- Shinjo Kamo: The Unicorn general. Survived because a Lion general would not have him murdered.
- Crystal Pass: Where the Unicorn sent a column to cut the Lion supply line.

## Setsuna

The flashback ends here and it hands her three things.

**The dead fight.** Armoured [[Lion Clan]] ancestors crossed that field and cut into a living army, because a [[Kitsu Family|Kitsu]] called them and had the honour to make it hold. That is the working described in the scroll that fell off the shelf in front of [[Kitsu Taigen]]'s daughter, and Setsuna has now watched it done. When [[Kitsuko Ayoko]] dreams the [[Snow Plain]] through somebody else's eyes, this is some of what she is being shown.

The Lion held on and still lost. They took the hill, killed [[Iuchi Gero]], broke the protections and drove the Unicorn back to their stronghold — with one day of food, a poisoned commissariat, a spy they never found and a supply line under attack at [[Crystal Pass]]. Whatever finally decided the [[Snow Plain]], it was logistics and it was betrayal, and the account in the [[Ikoma Hall of Scribes|Hall of Scribes]] describes neither.

And there is a spirit bound in a black talisman, cut from a Unicorn shugenja's neck by her own ancestor and put into a Kitsu's hands to be interrogated. That thread is worth following. The party she travels with now has been dealing with bound spirits and containment vessels in the present, and this is where the memory chose to stop.
