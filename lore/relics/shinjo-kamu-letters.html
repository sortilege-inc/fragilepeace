Shinjo Kamu letters

Unicorn

The Shinjo Kamu letters are a collection of authenticated correspondences attributed to Shinjo Kamu, a Unicorn explorer-statesman who perished at the Battle of the Snow Plains. These letters predate the battle and consist primarily of travel descriptions detailing lands beyond the borders of Rokugan. They are preserved as historical curiosities.

During the proceedings at the Castle of the Swift Sword, Ide Tsubame received a packet from the head librarian that included the Shinjo Kamu letters. The librarian noted that the collection was incomplete and comprised only four secondhand copies of letters. Tsubame sought to enter the authenticated Shinjo Kamu letters into evidence for handwriting comparison against a disputed treaty attributed to him. The letters were described as not providing pertinent evidence related to the case. Later, Shiba Midori referenced the letters when presenting documents related to the Battle of the Snow Plains, highlighting their historical significance while confirming their lack of relevance to the ongoing dispute.
