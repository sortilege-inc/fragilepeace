Emperor’s Road

The Emperor’s Road is a notable thoroughfare that facilitates travel and trade across the region. It includes a way station for travelers to rest and is lined with trees and open areas that provide visibility and concealment. The climate along the road is variable, with the presence of snowflake-like motes observed during certain events. It is frequented by various travelers, including samurai and courtiers, who traverse it for official duties and personal quests.

A chaotic encounter on the Emperor’s Road involved a group of attackers who initiated a melee by deploying a smoke bomb, which created obscuring terrain around Asako Taishi, a Phoenix scholar under the protection of the Emerald Magistrates. The attackers mistakenly seized Doji Setsuna, prompting a scramble as various combatants engaged. Bayushi Monban, a Scorpion bushi, took to the air to navigate the chaos, while Kakita Kazumi utilized stealth to approach unnoticed. The melee intensified as multiple smoke bombs detonated, further disrupting visibility.

As the conflict escalated, attackers attempted to drag Asako Taishi off the road, leading to violent exchanges. Shiba Midori and Bayushi Monban confronted the assailants, with Midori observing the leader of the attackers. The situation culminated in a duel between Midori and the Black-Armored Leader, resulting in the leader's death. The assailants retreated, leaving behind the wounded scholar and the aftermath of the violence on the Emperor’s Road.
