One of the two Kuni Warden Hunter visages painted on [[Bayushi Monban]]'s own face beneath
the mask — Onahime on one side, the [[Lady of Decay]] on the other. He took the mask off in
the trees in session 6 and showed them to everyone below, who took him for a demon and shot
at him. Something bearing her aspect now speaks and shows through him.
