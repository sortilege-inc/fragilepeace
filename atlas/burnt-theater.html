Burnt Theater

The Burnt Theater is a site marked by the remnants of a fire that unleashed a fire spirit during a performance. The structure has been largely cleared, with construction workers and firefighters attending to the aftermath and extinguishing lingering embers. A Shosuro-style scorpion mark is visible on a destroyed wall, indicating a connection to the Scorpion Clan.

Once a prominent venue, the theater hosted notable figures such as Daidoji Shin and Governor Miya Tetsuya. It is now in a state of disrepair, with plans for reopening under the title "The Famous Mediocrity" in about a month.

Crane bushi present at the site recounted that the fire was the result of arson, leading to chaos during the performance. Fortunately, there were no significant casualties, although a few servants remain unaccounted for. The area has undergone purification rites, and the spiritual disturbance from the event is still palpable, casting an unnatural aura over the ruins.
