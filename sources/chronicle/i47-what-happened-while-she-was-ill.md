## Narrative

!note **This was played and is not recorded.** Sessions ran between 18 May and 6 July
2026 for which no transcript exists here, and [[Doji Setsuna]] was largely out of them —
ill in her tent, which is where a character goes when a player cannot be at the table.
Everything below is reconstructed from the recap that opened the session of 6 July, which
is the only surviving account. **No detail has been invented.**

!lede The war moved, the treaty was carried without her, and by the time she was well
enough to stand, two of the four magistrates were gone and a [[Lion Clan|Lion]] hero had
lost an eye.

[[Doji Setsuna]] was ill for about a week. Nobody has a name for it. She was nauseous,
feverish and mostly asleep, and [[Kitsuko Ayoko]] kept her in tea and got her through it.

[[Bayushi Monban]] left. He took [[Kogarashi]] out of the lines one night without asking,
lost the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] riders who went after him, and went up into the
mountains. [[Shiba Midori]] brought the horse back the next day. Somewhere up there the
[[Lady of Decay]] took exception to him — the record says he was *punished*, and the
punishment was cleaning out a troll's cave like a peasant, at real cost to his standing.
He came down the far side of the mountains and arrived at [[Khanbulak]], the walled
trade city at the western end of the Empire's road, with one sword pawned at a bath house,
ill-fitting clothes bought off a stall, and no Emerald Magistrate's badge — lost
somewhere in the sand outside the walls.

That left [[Kakita Kazumi]] as the only Emerald Magistrate in the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] camp
when the treaty had to be presented. He did it in the dead of night, to Lion commanders
summoned out of their beds, holding a document rebuilt out of what people remembered of
it with a scorched Imperial seal attached, no envoy, no witnesses, and a
[[Crane Clan|Crane]] face.

[[Akodo Kage]] called him a liar. Sensei to both [[Akodo Toturi]] and
[[Akodo Akihito]], in front of every Lion officer of consequence.

[[Kakita Kazumi]] answered it the [[Kakita Family|Kakita]] way rather than the
[[Doji Family|Doji]] way, and demanded satisfaction. And the man who took the challenge
was not [[Akodo Kage]] — it was [[Akodo Akihito]], freshly titled Renowned Warrior, who
had wanted a rematch since the Topaz Championship and could not refuse one in that room.

It was not close. [[Kakita Kazumi]] knew every weakness [[Akodo Akihito]] has, held the
initiative from the first exchange, and cut him twice across three already-wounded rings.
[[Akodo Akihito]] lost his left eye, permanently. [[Shiba Midori]] has been treating
him since.

Which is how the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] came to hold [[Battlemaiden Castle]], with the
[[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] army a day or two out and closing, the Lion facing encirclement
on three sides and no appetite for that battle — and with every Lion in that camp
resenting the [[Crane Clan|Crane]] magistrate who humiliated their hero two days ago.

It is late summer. The invitations to Winter Court are going out.

## Learned

- Akodo Akihito: Took the duel because he could not refuse it in that room, and lost his left eye to it, permanently. Freshly titled Renowned Warrior, beaten in front of his uncle, his sensei and every Lion officer of consequence. Midori has been treating him.
- Akodo Kage: Sensei to both Toturi and Akihito. Called Kakita Kazumi a liar to his face over the reconstructed treaty, and is still in the camp. That insult has not been answered — the duel was fought against somebody else.
- Khanbulak: The walled trade city at the western end of the Empire's road, and the gate to the Burning Sands. Where Monban has ended up.
- Kogarashi: Taken out of the lines by Monban one night without asking, and brought back by Midori the next day.
- Bayushi Monban: Gone. Rode west, lost the Unicorn riders sent after him, and was punished in the mountains by the Lady of Decay — made to muck out a troll's cave, at real cost to his standing. Arrived at Khanbulak with one sword pawned and no magistrate's badge.
- Kitsuko Ayoko: Nursed Setsuna through a week of fever and kept her in tea.

## Setsuna

**She was not there for any of it, and that is the whole of the problem.** The treaty she
wrote was carried into a tent full of [[Lion Clan|Lion]] commanders at midnight by the one
magistrate in the party least equipped to be believed — a [[Crane Clan|Crane]] duellist,
alone, holding a document reassembled from memory around a burnt seal. Of course
[[Akodo Kage]] called it a lie. She would have called it a lie.

And the answer to being called a liar was a duel, which is exactly what it should not have
been. [[Kakita Kazumi]] was entitled and he was right. He also took the eye of the one
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] in the Empire who had already conceded that the [[Phoenix Clan|Phoenix]]
were owed, who is the Champion's nephew, and who was the party's natural advocate on the
Lion side. The insult came from [[Akodo Kage]] and the price was paid by
[[Akodo Akihito]], which means the wound is still open at both ends: Kage is unanswered,
and Akihito is maimed and diminished for something he did not say.

Her yojimbo is gone, on her horse, without her badge. Whatever else that is, it means the
[[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] currently has a magistrate's authority unaccounted for somewhere
in the western desert, and the party has no idea where he is or what he has told anyone.

She is, at last, well. Everything she has to repair happened while she was asleep.
