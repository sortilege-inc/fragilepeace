Shinjō Kamo’s Camp

Unicorn

Shinjō Kamo’s Camp is a temporary encampment associated with Shinjō Kamo, a notable figure during the Battle of the Snow Plain. The camp serves as a gathering place for military and political discussions regarding the contested diamond mines of the Snow Plain, claimed by the Scorpion Clan and denied by the Unicorn Clan. It is located near the Castle of the Swift Sword, where important decisions regarding the dispute are made. The area is characterized by rugged terrain, reflective of the harsh conditions faced during the battle.

The camp is noted in historical accounts of the Battle of the Snow Plain, particularly regarding the presence of Shosuro Amane, a Scorpion Clan advisor. His appearance at Shinjō Kamo’s Camp contrasts with his absence from the historical battle records. The significance of the camp is underscored by the upcoming arrival of Kitsuki Kaage, an Emerald Magistrate, alongside ambassadors from the Scorpion Clan and Unicorn Clan clans, who are tasked with resolving claims about the diamond mines. This gathering is expected to address historical omissions and disputes stemming from the battle's outcome, making the camp a focal point for strategizing and negotiation amidst the tensions surrounding these contested claims.
