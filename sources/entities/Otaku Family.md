A family of the [[Unicorn Clan]]. [[Ikoma Tadayoshi]] argued at the treaty table that
they had shamed themselves by withdrawing from the battle on the [[Snow Plain]], and that
breaking the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] afterwards would only finish the Otaku's work for them.
Whether the withdrawal was a disgrace or a judgement is a Unicorn question, and the
[[Moto Family|Moto]] answer it in the way that serves the Moto.
