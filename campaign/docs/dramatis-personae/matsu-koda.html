What is known

S4A Lion bushi of the garrison, large and strong and not quick, who fights with Striking as Water — it strips a man's resistance so the next blow does more than it should. He took Kakita Kazumi apart with it. He challenged over a brother Kazumi lamed and put out of action, and when told the circumstances he apologised in open court to Kazumi and to her both, which says he will take a correction if it is given to him in front of witnesses. He reserved four further grievances before she closed them on custom.
