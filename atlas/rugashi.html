Rugashi

Rugashi is a notable city situated along the Lion Roads, recognized for its well-maintained infrastructure and strategic position within the Lion Clan territories. The climate features hot summers, typical for the region. Rugashi serves as a waypoint for travelers, offering accommodations in the form of castles and villages, with a focus on hospitality, particularly for those bearing imperial authority.

The city is frequented by various clans, including the Unicorn Clan and Crane Clan, who navigate through the Lion Lands. Rugashi is a site where significant interactions and events occur.
