Meishodo Talismans

Meishodo Talismans are magical artifacts associated with the Unicorn Clan, recognized for their connection to unique forms of magic. They are illicitly sold on the black market, reflecting their restricted nature and the sensitivity surrounding their use. The Unicorn are secretive about their magical practices, heightening the significance of the trade of these talismans.

A Dragon magistrate is investigating the illegal sale of Meishodo Talismans at the Unicorn docks, linked to a woman who is researching these talismans for a novel. The involvement of non-shugenja with Meishodo could pose serious repercussions, particularly due to the Unicorn's protective stance on their magical practices. Bayushi Monban, an Emerald Magistrate, expresses concern about the implications of such trades and offers assistance to ensure the safety of those involved in the investigation.
