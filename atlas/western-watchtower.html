Western Watchtower

The Western Watchtower is a military structure located on a hilltop that overlooks the Snow Plain and the surrounding boggy paddies. Its elevated position provides a strategic vantage point for monitoring enemy movements and coordinating defenses. The watchtower is part of the Unicorn Clan's fortifications, serving as a lookout for potential threats and facilitating troop communication. It is constructed to withstand assaults and acts as a rallying point for Unicorn Clan forces.

In recent engagements, the watchtower has played a vital role in Unicorn Clan military operations. As Lion Clan forces launched their attack, the Unicorn Clan troops withdrew to the hilltop to regroup and prepare for a counteroffensive. Sentries at the watchtower remained vigilant, detecting enemy movements and warning against infiltrators. Notably, a Scorpion Clan infiltrator named Bayushi Monban attempted to approach the Unicorn Clan War Camp amidst the chaos of battle. The watchtower's attentive guards significantly hindered his reconnaissance efforts. Monban, while incapacitated, managed to fire a shot at the watchtower in an attempt to alert the sentries and sow confusion, underscoring the watchtower's significance in the ongoing conflict as a focal point for defense and observation.
