The Party

Kitsuki Wataru

Dragon

Kitsuki Wataru, an elderly male magistrate of the Dragon Clan, carries the weight of his years with a distinctive messy bun top knot atop his head. Recently, he has embraced a transformative journey, stepping into the roles of both investigator and monk. His authoritative presence is well-known, particularly in his dealings with the Lion Clan, where he has been actively reporting on military actions, including the capture of several prisoners. At a recent gathering, he shared insights into the customs of the Lion Clan, notably their tradition of drinking only after a victory, and recounted their recent triumph over the Unicorn Clan north of the Aelu River, led by General Akodo Family Sakawan, while the opposing general, Shinju, managed to escape.

Wataru, the son of Kitsuki Kaage, bears a resemblance to his father, a detail noted by those who encounter him. His commitment to his spiritual path is evident in his new role as a monk, where he wields a staff adorned with jingling elements that symbolize his dedication. Under the guidance of Togashi Nun, his monk mentor, he participates in shrine activities at Loyalty Castle, engaging in morning chanting rituals alongside other monks. This desire to learn the ways of a monk has been met with support from Togashi Nun, who is beginning to regain her memories and senses. His integration into the monastic community is marked by an invitation to participate in future rituals at the shrine, signifying a new chapter in his life.

As a Dragon Clan investigator, Wataru is renowned for his keen sense of magical phenomena. He employs his school technique, Traces of Passage, to detect magical activity, allowing him to discern if an individual has recently used magic or has been targeted by it. During a journey to the Castle of the Swift Sword, he examined Asako Taishi, a Phoenix Clan scholar-traveler, using his technique to assess the presence of magic. He concluded that Taishi had not used magic in the last day but suggested that he might have been the target of magic or is currently under an ongoing magical effect. Wataru sensed that there was something more to Taishi than met the eye, indicating a deeper layer of intrigue surrounding the scholar. His actions reflect a steadfast commitment to uncovering hidden truths and maintaining vigilance during the group's travels, even as he celebrates moments of happiness surrounding a recent missive about an inheritance.

Wataru is recognized for his methodical approach and adherence to honor, often employing a jitte, a traditional stick used for examining bodies without dishonor, which underscores his commitment to propriety in challenging situations. During an ambush on Doji Setsuna, his teachings were highlighted when Kakita Kazumi utilized the technique he instructed to examine the slain attackers, enabling her to identify markings and items while maintaining her honor. Discussions surrounding the attackers suggest they may be shinobi rather than common bandits, reflecting Wataru's deep understanding of the complexities of their world. His absence during the investigation of the ambush site was felt by his companions, who recognized his significance in their efforts.

Recently, while at a teahouse in the City Of The Rich Frog, Wataru spoke with Bayushi Monban about his intention to uncover the source of illicit magical items believed to be entering the city, particularly focusing on the trade of Meishodo Talismans in the black market at the Unicorn docks. He mentioned being guided to the area by Tonbo Kuma, the Dragonfly shrine keeper, and expressed concerns regarding the potential dangers these items pose, particularly given the Unicorn's sensitivity to their magic. Displaying a proactive attitude in his duties, Wataru aims to achieve honor and responsibility comparable to his father's legacy. He expressed openness to assistance from Monban and indicated plans to requisition a yojimbo for protection, reflecting his determination to combat the illicit trade while being mindful of the associated risks.

Wataru's investigations have also drawn the attention of his son, who is engaged in similar pursuits within Dragon lands, particularly concerning illicit activities involving "Meishodo" artifacts. This familial connection highlights the sense of duty and responsibility Wataru has instilled in his son, as they both navigate the political landscape intertwined with ongoing tensions among the Lion, Dragon, and Unicorn clans. His son's presence in the City Of The Rich Frog, where multiple factions are investigating similar matters, reinforces the significance of Wataru's lineage and influence in the narrative.
