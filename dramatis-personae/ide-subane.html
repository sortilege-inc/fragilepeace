Unicorn

What is known

S9The Unicorn envoy for the diamond mines dispute.
S22The Unicorn courtier. Presented the Treaty of White Shores Lake from the Unicorn archives, which places the Snow Plain in Unicorn hands. Came plainly dressed into a room full of silk and bowed correctly.
S23Took a recess to read the new evidence properly, then attacked the treaty's legitimacy and the contradictions between the three diaries. She is doing the most careful work of anyone at that table.
S24Found a semi-deliberate watermark on a librarian's letter concealing a message in invisible ink, and disclosed it rather than keeping it. Consulted the Commandant of the Academy on the Shinjo Kamo holdings.
S25Moved to call the librarian to place Monban in the library at unorthodox hours, and refused Setsuna's explanation — the timeline gives a window and a man alone with the exemplars.
S26Argued that a Lion edge means the Lion could have won unaided, so the Scorpion claim should be dismissed. Warned that summoned ghosts can be manipulated like any other witness.
S50On the Unicorn delegation, assisting Midori, and quick to pass her a note when the hostages and the horses went unmentioned.
S51Gave Midori the Unicorn's floor. The marriage must not complete; no territorial concession this time; no indemnity for the return of their own ground; and every horse returned. Everything else is negotiable, and the status quo is acceptable.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
