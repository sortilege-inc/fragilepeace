Shosuro Amane

Scorpion

What is known

S12The Scorpion who rode to the parley on a Rokugani pony with the Unicorn honour guard, announced herself as there to make peace, and was refused by both generals. Kitsu Somalia could not read her at all and had never heard of her.
S18In no personal record of the battle whatsoever, though every one of the party saw her on that field. Her chop is on the treaty that ended it, as a witness, alongside Miya Mimoka's, Matsu Sakura's and Shinjō Kamo's — and a Scorpion had no standing to witness it.
S20The Scorpion who stood with Shinjō Kamo at the Snow Plain, three centuries dead and an ancestor of her clan. Monban has commissioned a feast in her honour at the Ancestral Shrine, praying for a demonstration of truth to silence those who dishonour the Scorpion.
S23Her chop and signature appear on the official treaty as a witness, not a participant. She was in the room when it was signed, which helps the Scorpion more than the Unicorn would like.
S26Manifested in the courtroom as one of Bayushi Monban's own ancestors — blood of his mother. Carries herself exactly as her descendant Shosuro Aishi does. Her first act as a witness was to tell the court, through Kitsu Takeko, that Ikoma Akuyaku is the most accomplished liar she has ever worked with and that nothing he says should be believed.

Each line is what she took from the session it cites, and nothing else. The Archivist's own account of this person is not reproduced here — it blends what she saw with what she did not.
