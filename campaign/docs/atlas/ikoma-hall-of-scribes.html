Ikoma Hall of Scribes

Lion

The Ikoma Hall Of Scribes is a hub for scribes engaged in copying scrolls and documents. It features a lively atmosphere with lectures and the presence of students and soldiers. The hall is integral to academic and administrative activities, particularly in relation to the War College, and serves various clans, including the Scorpion Clan and Soshi Aisha.

A notable event occurred when Bayushi Monban encountered a veiled Soshi Aisha within the hall during a lecture. Their discreet conversation continued on the Path of Splinters, a walkway popular among students. Soshi Aisha warned Monban about the repercussions of appearing with guards at her quarters and mentioned possessing an incriminating docket related to him. Their interaction included jabs regarding Monban's reputation and ended with a reaffirmation of their ongoing bargain, demonstrating the hall's role in significant clan negotiations.

The Ikoma Hall of Scribes, a distinguished archive nestled within the imposing Kitsu Keep, stands as a vital center for scholarly pursuits and the preservation of history within the Lion Clan. This expansive hall is meticulously organized into dedicated wings for the Kitsu, Akodo, and Matsu families, each housing a wealth of scrolls, books, and writings that chronicle the Empire's rich lore and significant events. The hall features a library that houses historical records, scrolls, and treaties, adorned with symbols of the Lion Clan. At the heart of the hall lies a wooden tile emblazoned with the Okoma family crest, symbolizing the legacy of knowledge contained within its walls.

The atmosphere in the Ikoma Hall of Scribes is one of reverent silence, occasionally punctuated by the soft rustle of scrolls being unrolled and the measured footsteps of visitors. The climate is cool and tranquil, influenced by the nearby Snow Plain, creating an ideal environment for study and reflection. Well-appointed scribe desks and private consultation areas facilitate the transcription and preservation of important documents, making the hall a hub for historians, scribes, and courtiers dedicated to the craft of documentation and the crafting of treaties.

The hall plays a significant role in historical discourse, particularly during proceedings involving the Emerald Magistrates. It was here that Kitsu Ayoko, guided by a summons from Lady Kitsu Takeko, engaged with the esteemed historian Ikoma Ichigo. Their discussions delved into the intricacies of the Battle of Snow Plain, where Ayoko presented a scroll detailing the betrothal between Shinjō Altansarnai and Ikoma Anakazu, which outlined crucial land exchanges and the involvement of various negotiators. The absence of the official battle record led Ichigo to recount the events, captivating those present and igniting a lively performance that reflected the tensions of the historical conflict.

Moreover, the Ikoma Hall of Scribes has been a site of unusual phenomena, hinting at a spiritual presence connected to Kitsu Ayoko. This culminated in the discovery of an ancestral scroll that linked her to significant Kitsu techniques, further emphasizing the hall's importance as a repository of knowledge and a catalyst for current political dynamics. The hall's contributions to scholarly collaboration and its role in pivotal debates, such as those surrounding the Snow Plain dispute between the Unicorn Clan and the Scorpion Clan, underscore its significance in shaping the ongoing narrative of the Empire.

Recently, during a court recess concerning the Snow Plain dispute, Ikoma Ichigo was approached by Shiba Midori to authenticate several documents related to the case. Ichigo assisted Midori and Kitsu Ayoko in reviewing the documents, which were laid out in the court chambers, while a Hida scholar observed their activities, highlighting the hall's role as a site of scrutiny. Ichigo examined various treaties, identifying an imperial-sealed treaty as authentic while questioning the validity of competing documents due to vague clauses and discrepancies in handwriting and ink. Additionally, he inspected a provenance box containing scrolls, discovering the lost record of the Battle of the Snow Plain, which he authenticated by seal and style. His findings were crucial, as he later presented further evidence supporting the authenticity of the historical documents, solidifying the Ikoma Hall of Scribes' integral role in gathering and examining evidence that influenced the ongoing legal proceedings.
