Miya Satoshi

Imperial — Miya

What is known

S54The Imperial Herald, and the reason the mission's foundation collapsed. Son of Miya Yoto, who conceded the office to him at the last Winter Court. A traditionalist who loves the job, dislikes rōnin visibly, and appreciates deference to the new title. He holds diametrically opposed positions to his father, who spoke well of rōnin and of the lesser clans. He sealed the treaty, ratified the peace, and told the magistrates the writ they carried was never issued.
