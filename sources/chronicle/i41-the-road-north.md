## Narrative

!note **This is not a session. It is the gap between two of them.** An unknown number

!lede The column went north-west into [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] country with a
[[Lion Clan|Lion]] relief force and the Imperial envoy, and somewhere on that road the
war reached the [[Snow Plain]].

There was a battle there. The [[Lion Clan|Lion]] took it — their commander killed the
[[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] leader in single combat, which is how that ground has always
been decided — and it cost them so heavily that calling it a victory takes an effort.
The field was covered in Lion dead.

The magistrates worked the aftermath. They helped with the triage, and they took down
the last deeds of the dying, which is a magistrate's office and not a physician's: a
death recorded is a death that counts for the family.

Afterwards the column took a castle that surrendered without a fight. Its people were
gone. Its stores were full, unspoiled and unpoisoned, enough to hold a siege for months,
and nothing had been sabotaged on the way out. Whatever moved them out moved them fast,
and it was not an army.

Somewhere in all of this the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] took [[Bayushi Monban]] prisoner.

## Learned

- Snow Plain: The war has come back to it. A Lion victory decided by single combat between the commanders, bought with a field of Lion dead — the same ground, and very nearly the same shape, as the battle her ancestor fought on it three centuries ago.
- Bayushi Monban: Taken prisoner by the Unicorn somewhere on the road north, and carried with their baggage train.

## Setsuna

**She has been on the Snow Plain twice now.** Once as [[Matsu Morozane]]'s memory, three
centuries back, and once in her own body. Both times [[Lion Clan|Lion]] against
[[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] on the same ground; both times settled by two commanders and
one blade; both times a field of Lion dead afterwards. She is the only person in the
column who has seen it happen before, and the only person who knows it did not settle
anything the first time either.

She recorded the dying. That is the office at its plainest — no writ, no intrigue, no
leverage, just a magistrate with a brush making sure a man's last act is entered
somewhere it will be read by his family. Whatever else this stretch cost, that part
was the job.

Nothing about the castle adds up. People who abandon a fortress burn the stores or
poison the wells. These people left months of food behind, intact, and did not damage
the defences. That is not a garrison withdrawing; that is a household running from
something. It is worth finding out what before the column settles in behind those walls.

And her yojimbo is in a [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] baggage train — the man she dismissed,
who would not accept the dismissal, taken by her husband's clan.
