Abandoned Mines

The Abandoned Mines are located in the mountainous region of the Spine of the World, characterized by rugged terrain and steep inclines. These mines are remnants of disused mineral extraction activities and are largely forgotten. The area features treacherous paths, particularly in winter, due to heavy snowfall that blankets the landscape, concealing hazards and reducing visibility. The climate is harsh, with extreme cold and potential for unnatural weather patterns.

During a military campaign, the Lion Clan's forces, led by Kitsu Somalia and Matsu Morozane, utilized the Old Diamond Mines as a strategic flanking maneuver against the Unicorn Clan. The flanking unit encountered the Abandoned Mines while navigating through the mountains. Matsu Morozane discovered a previously uncharted path leading to these mines, not indicated on their maps. Investigating further, the force noted disturbed snow and covered tracks, suggesting recent activity, prompting Matsu Morozane to detach scouts to explore the mines for additional intelligence. The mines served as a potential tactical advantage in the ongoing conflict.
