Tengoku

Tengoku, the Celestial Realm of Heaven, exists as a sacred fire within the Lion Clan, invoked in times of need. This location provides warmth and spiritual comfort, countering the bitter cold and low morale during a harsh snowstorm. Surrounding Tengoku is an environment of deep snow and biting wind, heightening the challenges faced by the soldiers.

During a moment of crisis, Kitsu Somalia, a Lion Clan shugenja, successfully invokes Tengoku, generating a comforting fire that alleviates fatigue and strife among the soldiers, effectively restoring their morale. The presence of Tengoku instills a renewed sense of hope and vigor throughout the camp. As generals assess strategies for upcoming confrontations, the uplifting influence of Tengoku is vital in bolstering the Lion Clan's fighting spirit, even as the threat of dwindling supplies and the advance of the Unicorn Clan looms over them.
