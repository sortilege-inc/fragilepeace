Okoto War College Military Library

The Okoto War College Military Library is a repository of military history and records located within the Akodo War College. It focuses on the documentation and study of past conflicts, particularly those involving the Lion Clan. The library houses various records, including arrival and return rosters, quartermaster reports, and genealogical histories, which are essential for understanding past battles like the Battle of Snow Plain.

Recently, Shiba Midori and Ikoma Ichigo investigated the library for information on the missing Lion Clan medic, Akodo Kayamayako. They found arrival and return rosters detailing significant losses among the Blood Of The Lioness and commendations for Kamiyoko's efforts during the battle. Bayushi Monban later visited the library for quartermaster reports and encountered Akodo Atsushi, a Lion Clan instructor who assisted in the document search but displayed hostility towards any implications of dishonor regarding the Lion Clan's performance. Monban's findings revealed records of dysentery outbreaks and food contamination at the winter camp, highlighting critical issues during the campaign. The library serves as a hub where scholars and military personnel address the intricate balance of honor and truth in their historical records.
