Bathhouse

The Bathhouse is a lively establishment within a House Of Pleasure, known for its vibrant atmosphere and the presence of triplets who serve sake, dice, and fans to patrons. The interior features traditional decor, creating an inviting space for relaxation and socializing. It is frequented by samurai and guests, offering private rooms for discreet conversations and gatherings.

Bayushi Monban, a Scorpion samurai, arranged a private meeting in the Bathhouse, using hand-signs to communicate with a Scorpion Cousin. They discussed information-gathering and potential actions involving an apothecary across the water. Monban expressed a need for poison to target a female and negotiated terms for assistance with his cousin. The Bathhouse served as a backdrop for these dealings, highlighting its role in the intrigue among the samurai.

Monban later awoke in the Bathhouse, disoriented and hungover from the previous night's revelries. He attempted a cleansing prayer to alleviate his intoxication while preparing to leave for The Governor's mansion. The Bathhouse provided a temporary refuge for Monban, allowing him to gather himself before re-entering the events unfolding in the mansion.
