Terminating Tea

Terminating Tea is an abortifacient brewed by Shiba Midori for Doji Setsuna. The tea is prepared with ingredients such as nightshade and other herbs to induce a medical effect while minimizing lethality. The brewing process is described as potentially unpleasant, with a slow recovery expected afterward.

During a tea ceremony at the Governor’s Tea House, Shiba Midori discusses the tea with Doji Setsuna, who expresses a desire to take it before departing for a mission. Shiba Midori advises that the tea would be unpleasant and that Setsuna should consider taking it if necessary. After gathering the required ingredients, Shiba Midori brews the first cautious dose of the tea, ensuring safety in its preparation. Doji Setsuna consumes the tea under Shiba Midori's care, resulting in severe wounding of her Void aspect, consistent with the anticipated physical toll of the abortifacient.
