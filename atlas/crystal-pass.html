Crystal Pass

Crystal Pass is a treacherous mountainous route that serves as a critical artery for strategic movement between regions, particularly during the ongoing conflict between the Lion Clan and the Unicorn Clan. This narrow, snow-covered terrain is not only vital for supply lines but also fraught with dangers, including steep climbs, hidden traps, and the ever-present threat of ambushes from enemy scouts. The pass is sufficiently wide to accommodate military units, yet its harsh conditions—deep snow and biting cold winds—pose significant challenges for any who dare traverse it.

The landscape is characterized by rocky outcrops and forested belts, which necessitate stealthy movement for those navigating its perilous paths. Recently, Crystal Pass has become a focal point for military strategy, as the Unicorn Clan has fortified it to cut off supplies to the advancing Lion Clan. General Matsu Sakura and her commanders recognized the urgency of securing this route, deploying a contingent to maintain their grip on the pass against the Lion Clan's attempts to dislodge them.

In a bold tactical move, Matsumura Zane of the Lion Clan proposed using Crystal Pass as a flanking route to outmaneuver the entrenched Unicorn Clan forces, a plan that received the backing of Kitsu Somalia. However, the Lion Clan, led by Matsu Morozane, faced significant obstacles in executing their maneuvers. Initial attempts, including a Lightning Raid, were met with failure, leaving them isolated and cut off from vital supplies. The ongoing struggle for control over Crystal Pass underscores its strategic importance in the broader conflict, shaping the fate of both clans as they vie for dominance in this harsh and unforgiving terrain.
