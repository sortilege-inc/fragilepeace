Relic

Imperial Seal

The Imperial Seal is an emblem of the Emperor's authority, characterized by its dark blue ink used on important documents. Its presence signifies authenticity and is crucial for political maneuvering, as it can enable dangerous forgeries if stolen. The seal is highly sought after by those aiming to manipulate power dynamics, particularly between factions such as the Unicorn and the Lion.

A recent theft targeted a document bearing the Imperial Seal, revealing its high value. The intruders were specifically searching for this document, indicating its importance. The potential for the seal to be used on a forged message highlights its role in facilitating deceitful actions. The implications of the seal's misuse could escalate tensions among powerful clans.
