## Narrative

!lede In the recess [[Shiba Midori]] went to [[Ikoma Ichigo]] in the [[Ikoma Hall of Scribes]] with [[Kitsuko Ayoko]] beside her, and a Hida military scholar watching every step they took. Then [[Kitsuki Kaage]] laid the whole body of documents out for both sides to inspect, and put [[Ikoma Ichigo]] up to testify on them.

He worked fast and he worked cleanly. The **[[Imperial-Sealed Official Treaty]]** is genuine, and it puts the [[Snow Plain]] in [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]] hands. Of the two competing secret agreements, one is signed and executed and **carries no Imperial seal**, which is the thing that would make it bind. And the clause willing the mines to *the least of the [[Bayushi Family|Bayushi]]* is too vague to do the work being asked of it.

[[Shiba Midori]] then produced the rest of the cache — four large scrolls, an unsigned draft, and **the lost chronicle of [[Ikoma Hideri]]**, the account of the Battle of the [[Snow Plain]] that the man was murdered before he could publish. It has been in the ground ever since. [[Ikoma Ichigo]] examined the jar stopper and the pottery with the scrolls and confirmed the seal would have preserved them, and was given leave to fetch comparison samples from the library. [[Bayushi Monban]] spent the recess sitting in the [[Garden Of Virtuous Contemplation]].

When the court came back, [[Kitsuki Kaage]] set the order of argument and gave it to [[Ide Subane]] first — who moved to call the librarian, to testify that [[Bayushi Monban]] had been in the library at hours nobody has business being there. [[Kitsuki Kaage]] warned her what an accusation like that against an Emerald Magistrate would need behind it.

[[Shoshuro Aishi]] pressed for the signed secret treaty to be ratified anyway. [[Bayushi Monban]] put in [[Ikoma Akuyaku]]'s journals with their confessions of [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] work against the [[Lion Clan|Lion]] at the [[Snow Plain]], and [[Kitsuki Kaage]] said aloud that the weight of it was starting to look like Scorpion collusion with the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]].

Then [[Kakita Kazumi]] stood up and said [[Bayushi Monban]]'s evidence was forged, and invited the court to compare it against his own notes and the library journals.

[[Doji Setsuna]] argued the principles rather than the documents. [[Kakita Kazumi]] argued for the mines to go into Imperial custody. [[Ikoma Ichigo]] came back with his comparison samples and confirmed the cache scrolls as authentic, and [[Kitsuki Kaage]] was visibly relieved to have one body of evidence in the room he could trust — which strengthened [[Shiba Midori]]'s position considerably. [[Kitsuko Ayoko]] testified that the Lion camp held out until the Imperial forces arrived, so whatever the sabotage achieved, it did not win the battle.

Then [[Ikoma Ichigo]] said the last thing. There is no secret treaty on file anywhere. And every one of the [[Shinjukamu Letters]] disappeared overnight, after [[Bayushi Monban]]'s late visit.

[[Doji Setsuna]] got to her feet and told the court that [[Bayushi Monban]] had been returning those documents on her behalf, at her request. [[Ide Subane]] did not accept it, and said what the timeline actually gives you: a window, and a man alone in a library with the exemplars.

[[Kitsuki Kaage]] ruled what he could. One of the two secret documents is forged. The chain of proof is not good enough to say which. Testimonial evidence next session; no final ruling until more authentication is in.

## Setsuna

**She lied for him in front of a magistrate.** That is the fact and she should hold it plainly. The [[Shinjukamu Letters]] vanished after [[Bayushi Monban]]'s late visit; she rose and told the court he had been returning them at her request; [[Ide Subane]] said in reply that the timeline is exactly the window a forger would need. [[Kitsuki Kaage]] has already warned that accusing an Emerald Magistrate requires substantial evidence — which cuts for her now and against her the moment the librarian is actually called.

Her account is now a testable claim. If the librarian's version does not match hers, she is not a magistrate whose yojimbo forged something. She is a magistrate who gave a false account from the floor of an Imperial arbitration.

**Everything else went well, which is the difficult part.** [[Ikoma Ichigo]] confirmed the cache genuine, [[Shiba Midori]]'s Imperial-holding argument is the strongest thing in the room, [[Kitsuko Ayoko]] testified that the sabotage did not decide the battle, and [[Ikoma Hideri]]'s chronicle — the murdered man's own account — is out of the ground and in evidence. The honest case is winning on its own. The dishonest one is the only thing that can now lose it.

**And [[Kakita Kazumi]] denounced [[Bayushi Monban]]'s evidence in open court**, having sat with [[Soshi Yamako]] over plum wine and helped design it. Read it either way and it is worth knowing which: he is cutting the forgery loose to save the party and the Imperial argument, or he is putting the whole thing on [[Bayushi Monban]]. He also invited the court to compare it against **his own notes**, which is a bold offer from a man who can write in several hands.

The shape of the ruling to aim for has not changed and it is now nearly built. The [[Imperial-Sealed Official Treaty]] is real and favours the [[Unicorn Clan|Unicorn]]; the [[Scorpion Clan|Scorpion]] instruments are unsealed, unsigned, or vague; the cache is authentic and Imperial intervention is the documented reason the battle ended. **Imperial custody is the finding the evidence actually supports.** She should stop trying to protect anything else and drive at that.
