Crossroads Fortress

Crossroads Fortress is a strategic location along the well-maintained Lion Roads, serving as a waypoint for travelers in Lion Clan territory. The fortress features robust defensive structures, intended to accommodate and protect the movement of troops and dignitaries. It is set amidst rolling hills, offering good visibility and access to the surrounding areas. The climate is generally favorable, aiding travel. The fortress is a key hub for communication and coordination among the clans, especially during conflicts.

The fortress is monitored by Lion Clan officials, reflecting its role in maintaining order and security in the region. It is common to see columns of Lion Clan soldiers marching north, indicative of ongoing military activities.
